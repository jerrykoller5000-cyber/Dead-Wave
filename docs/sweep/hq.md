# FOB Threshold (the HQ): the sweep

CL-256, 2026-10-24. Against `docs/world-design.md`. The numbers: `numbers/hq.md`. The shots: `review/sweep/hq/` (cloud,
software renderer, a match's prep, noon and night).

**The place.** The middle of the valley, where four trails meet. Its anchor is the blockhouse: 10 × 10 m, the parapet at
8.2 m, the klaxon tower on the roof. Its hatches: the kiosk and the skull window on the west face, the start panel and
the tag wall on the south, the CIF and the armory on the east. The sealed LOCKDOWN door is on the north.

## The placement sweep

1. **Blocking.** The fuel depot's generator stood 0.7 m out from the armory's wall and 1.8 m along it, right in front of
   the roller shutter (N18; `hq-from-east.png`). **Fixed (CL-256):** it now stands on the blank north face, 3 m west of
   the sealed door and 1.7 m off the wall, squared to it with its panel facing out. Nothing else blocks a hatch: the
   armory, the CIF, the panel and the window each have all 12 ways in clear at 0.95 m, and their standing spots are open.
2. **Distances.** The four trails all end at the walls. That is right for the hub every trail leads to (rule 11).
3. **Scale.** The hatches suit him: the armory shutter is about 4 m wide and 2.9 m tall, the CIF window is at chest
   height, and the panel at hand height. The blockhouse reads as a fortified building, not a house.

## The transition sweep

- The plinth meets flat ground all round. Nothing floats, sinks or clips: the numbers find no suspects here except the
  trails ending at the walls, which is intended.
- **C, the look.** From every side at noon the blockhouse reads near-black, with red lamp strips. At night it is a dark
  block with red lights. The look asks for weathered concrete grey, rust, amber lamp glow and faded institutional yellow.

## Against the 15 rules

- **It isn't a FOB (rules 1, 5, 12).** A lone blockhouse stands in an open meadow. Nothing round it says forward
  operating base: no perimeter, generator pad, fuel, comms, vehicles, tents or lights. The story says FOB Threshold fell
  (the clawed LOCKDOWN door, the tag wall of the Gravewalkers), and the yard should show that fight. The blockhouse is a
  good anchor with nothing to reinforce it. This is N19.
- **Negative space (rule 4).** The open meadow round it is good contrast, but the FOB needs a yard of its own to contrast
  with: today the grass runs straight up to the walls.
- **Contrast (rule 14).** At night one amber sodium lamp over the kiosk would be the valley's only warm light: the place
  he comes home to.

## The overhaul (AG-123: Antigravity builds; Claude signs off the placement)

In the hierarchy's order:

1. **The anchor.** Lighter, weathered concrete: rain streaks under the parapet, darker at the foot, the hazard band
   faded. Amber sodium lamps over the working hatches (west, east, south).
2. **The connection.** A packed-gravel apron about 6 m out from the walls that the four trails run into, with tyre ruts
   in from the south trail.
3. **The supporting things.**
   - **The perimeter, broken.** A ring of HESCO bastions and sandbags at 12-14 m, with gaps where the trails come in. On
     the north-east a stretch has burst and spilled its sand where they came through. A sandbag guard post sits at the
     south gap.
   - **The utility side (north).** The generator, moved here, on a pallet with two fuel drums. A cable runs to a junction
     box on the wall, beside a stack of jerrycans.
   - **Comms.** A whip antenna and a small dish on a roof corner. The klaxon tower stays.
4. **The details.** Spent brass and a helmet by the guard post. A burned-out tent frame to the east (the FOB's own).
   Crates and pallets by the armory. A toppled floodlight on the north-east. The FOB's sign ("FOB THRESHOLD · PGB") on
   two posts at the south gap, leaning.

**What must hold:**

- 2 m clear in front of every hatch.
- The trails stay open.
- Nothing goes on the tag wall's face.
- The perimeter has gaps, so the dead still reach the HQ.
- He builds round the HQ, so the perimeter pieces are landmarks: like the fences, they are shot, knifed or dug out to
  make room for his builds (`inTheWay`).
- The night-20 frame budget holds: bake the yard as the house is baked (`world/bake.js`).
