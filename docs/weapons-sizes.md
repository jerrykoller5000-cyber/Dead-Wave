# Weapon magazines and suppressors: real size against the game

Jerry's J7 and J21 ask for the pistol's extended magazine to read as a real one, for magazines to fill their wells, and for rifle suppressors to have believable length and bore. This is the measurement sheet for Grokbot's GB-260 model pass. Product examples below are shape and scale references for fictional weapons, not claims of identity or mechanical compatibility.

## Measuring our models

The `studio/weapon-*.js` builders use local model units. `index.html`'s `WEAPON_SIZE` (around line 21669) scales each model by `real class length × look × MARINE_UPM / model length`; `MARINE_UPM = 1.655 / 1.80`. For a visual millimetre comparison against a 1.8 m marine, multiply a builder dimension by `real class length × look / model length`. The game intentionally exaggerates some silhouettes with `look`. Values below are approximate geometry bounds from the source, not tape measurements on a rendered mesh. `gunExtLook` (around line 22807) scales the visible box/stick magazine body 1.48× along its insertion axis, or all axes of the AA-12 drum 1.22×, with its feed top held in place. The upgrade ratio is artistic; it does not state capacity.

## Visual sign-off for GB-260

Use [Antigravity's AG-164 baseline](../qa/2026-10-31-AG-164/report.md) and [60-shot contact sheet](../qa/2026-10-31-AG-164/sheet.jpg). AG-164 confirms the small pistol extended magazine in first-person reload; its sheet is a baseline for gun placement but does not replace attachment-specific after shots. Render each changed magazine seated and pulled, standard and extended, from side and front in first and third person; check the junction at the magwell and the length below it. Render each suppressor in profile and muzzle view on its own gun; inspect barrel/sight clearance and visible exit. Apply asset-principles geometry, material and context passes at gameplay scale, in noon, overcast, dusk and night, then score silhouette, proportions, materials, attachment and readability at 4/5 or higher. Record before/after shot paths and the load-time/frame-rate result in GB-260's handoff. All game launches go through the crew test slot.
