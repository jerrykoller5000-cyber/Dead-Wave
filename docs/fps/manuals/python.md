# Colt Python (the game's revolver) — knowledge notes (the linked manual could not be read)
Jerry's link: https://www.colt.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/02/python.pdf — a scanned PDF with no text layer. Jerry has
been asked for an upload. THE LOOK STAYS (Jerry, 8 Nov ~05:00Z, J38: "Colt python with rmr and laser light attachment", with a photo of a
stainless Python on a barrel rail with a comp, an RMR and a light; then J44: "Keep the looks I'm just showing attachment points and laser/light combo styles for each gun."):
the photo shows only WHERE the attachments go: the RMR (or the prism, J50) on a plate at the TOP STRAP's rear (over the hammer) and the
laser/light UNDER the barrel on a short rail, both shown only when fitted (GP-20065, GP-20066). The revolver keeps the
blued Smith & Wesson look of his picture (J37, CL-320), the rod two inches short (J36); the operation below applies
where it doesn't change that look (the latch keeps its S&W thumbpiece).
Specs (2020 Python): .357 Magnum, 6 rounds; 4.25- and 6-inch barrels (later 3, 5); about 11.5 in overall with the 6-in
barrel, 46 oz; full-length UNDERLUG enclosing the ejector rod; a VENTILATED RIB on top; a red-insert ramp front sight
pinned to the rib; a fully adjustable rear sight (windage and elevation screws) at the top strap's rear; walnut target
grips (a Colt medallion: none on our model).
Operation: the CYLINDER LATCH on the frame's LEFT behind the cylinder is PULLED BACK to free the cylinder, which swings
out to the LEFT on the crane; load; close it (it locks). Colt cylinders turn CLOCKWISE seen from behind (S&W's turn
counter-clockwise). Double action: the trigger pull lifts the hammer and turns the cylinder; single action: the hammer
thumbed back first. Unloading: the cylinder open, the muzzle up, the EJECTOR ROD pushed fully back (the star lifts the
cases out of the chambers), released. A transfer-bar safety (no external safety).

## Addendum: Jerry's second link (8 Nov), the Umarex Colt Python CO2 replica manual
https://www.umarexusa.com/UMAREX/Product%20Manuals/Manual%20Colt%20Python%20CO2%20Metal%202254032%2007R16.pdf — readable,
but the manual for a BB-firing replica. What it adds for the real gun's handling: the cylinder release is PULLED BACK,
then the cylinder is pushed out to the side (the Python's swings to the left); closing it, it's pushed back until it
clicks; to empty it the ejector at the front is pushed back toward the cylinder (the star lifts the cases out). Its
manual safety and CO2 screw in the grip are the replica's only (the real Python has none). Its figure p.4 names the
front sight, rear sight, muzzle, cylinder, cylinder release, hammer, trigger, safety, cartridge ejector.
