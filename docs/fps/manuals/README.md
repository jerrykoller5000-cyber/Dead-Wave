Jerry: "I do not want Grok Bot to work on these weapons for now ... he can do rough mockups. I want ChatGPT to do a
first-round fix of all the weapons that I provide links to the operating manuals and then you do a second round of
polish. ... Read the manuals be thorough. Use imagination for rest of weapons."

Each file here is Claude's paraphrased digest of the manual Jerry linked (read 8 Nov ~04:15Z through the web tools),
written for modelling and animation: the specs, every visible part and its side, the controls, the operating
sequences, the distinctive details, the figure pages. Parts drawings and photos in the PDFs are images the text tools
could not see: where a digest says [figure only], open the PDF's page yourself (the link is at the top of each file).
Nothing here is copied from the manuals beyond part names; no logos or markings go on any model.
