# Barrett MRAD (the game's sniper) — digest
Source: https://barrett.net/wp-content/uploads/2020/12/MRAD-Operators-Manual-18697.pdf (P/N 18697, read in full as text;
the exploded views p.32-33 and the figures are images [figure only]).
Identification: MRAD (interchangeable barrels, folding stock) and MRAD SMR (fixed barrel and stock); bolt action,
10-round detachable box. Barrels 26 in (660 mm) for the .338 LM / .300 class, 24 in (610 mm) and 17 in (432 mm) for
.308 / 6.5 Creedmoor.
Specs (p.6): overall 49.5 in (1257 mm) with the 26-in barrel, folded 41.6 in (1057 mm); 24 in: 47.5/39.6 in; 17 in:
40.5/32.6 in. Two-stage trigger, 3 lb. Height, width, weight and the bolt throw are not in the text (known: about 60
degree bolt lift, ~7 kg with the .338 barrel).
Parts (p.7 major components, p.32-35 exploded/parts list): the upper receiver (carries the barrel in a barrel
extension, the bolt guides; a full-length top rail in the photos), TWO BARREL SCREWS on the upper (Torx, rear tightened
first; the barrel's index slot at 12 o'clock), the barrel and its threaded, ported MUZZLE BRAKE; the bolt with its bolt
handle knob (lifts up and pulls back; forward and down to lock), the bolt tube with the cocking piece, its lever and
shroud at the rear; the LOWER receiver (grip, trigger, safety, mag well); the pistol grip; the RECEIVER LATCH under the
lower just behind the grip (separates upper and lower, only with the bolt handle raised); the assembly pin on the LEFT
(pushed left); the SAFETY SELECTOR above the grip, reversible to either side, "fire"/"safe"; the trigger (two-stage,
an observation hole in the housing's side); the magazine (10 rounds, ribs on its sides, a letter on its bottom rear, a
floor plate with a tab hole) and the MAGAZINE CATCH pushed FORWARD to drop it; the HANDGUARD with M-LOK slots at 3, 6
and 9 o'clock; the FOLDING STOCK hinged at the lower, its button and latch, folding to the EJECTION-PORT SIDE (the
right); the CHEEK PIECE on top of the stock with its height knob (reversible for lateral offset); the RECOIL PAD on a
base plate with a vertical slider (height latch on the stock's right) and length-of-pull spacers (0.25 in each). No
monopod and no sling points in the text.
Operation: load the mag (10, pressed straight down); bolt handle UP and fully BACK; mag in STRAIGHT (no rock); bolt
FORWARD then DOWN (strips and chambers a round); safety to fire; fire. Cycling: handle up, fully back (the case is
thrown out of the ejection port), forward, down. Single loading through the port. Unloading: safe, bolt up and back,
mag catch forward, mag out, look in the chamber. Folding: close the bolt, press the stock button, swing the stock to
the port side until it locks. Barrel change: bolt back, loosen (don't remove) the two barrel screws, pull the barrel
forward out of the receiver; in: index slot at 12, seat, bolt forward into the barrel extension, torque the rear screw
then the front.
For the model: the game's sniper is an AXMC-style tan chassis today; the MRAD reads as a monolithic upper with a long
top rail running onto the handguard, a squared lower with the grip and a short mag well, a skeletal folding stock with a
tall cheek piece and a thick pad, the big ported brake. Keep CL-319's mechanics (the bolt group on the bore axis, the
open receiver on the right, the next round, boltPoseHook, ejectPort) and J33 (the scope on the rail, not hanging off).
