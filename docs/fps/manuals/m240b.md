# M240B medium machine gun (the game's mounted M240) — digest
Source: https://www.trngcmd.marines.mil/Portals/207/Docs/TBS/W3I0003XQ%20M240B%20Medium%20Machinegun.pdf (TBS student
handout; pp.1-39 read, 39-47 summarised; diagrams are images [figure only]: pp.6-8, 13-17, 19-23, 26-31, 33).
Identification: belt-fed, air-cooled, gas-operated, fires from an OPEN BOLT; eight main components (barrel; buttstock
and buffer; driving spring rod; bolt and operating rod; trigger housing; cover; feed tray; receiver).
Specs (p.6): gun 27.4 lb, length 48.5 in; spare barrel case 12.9 lb; tripod, pintle and T&E 20 lb; system 47.4 lb;
sustained 100 rpm, rapid 200, cyclic 650 (9-10 rounds a second); muzzle velocity 2,750 fps; max range 3,725 m,
suppression 1,800 m. Rear sight: a peep on a sliding leaf, the leaf flat for 200-800 m and upright for 800-1,800 m.
Ammunition: 100-round bandoleers; the M62 tracer has an orange tip, AP a black tip.
Parts: the barrel with its CARRYING HANDLE that is also the changing handle (rotates down to lock), the gas plug and its
collar at the gas end, the gas cylinder tube in front of the bipod, the forward heat shield (snaps onto the barrel),
the front sight post; the receiver (the bipod's ball joint and leg slot underneath, the barrel-locking latch on the LEFT
where the barrel joins); the COVER hinged on the receiver over the FEED TRAY (cartridge stops, a link ejection port
between them); the rear sight on a hinged range plate; the charging (cocking) handle (RIGHT side, pulled back, pushed
home to lock forward); the trigger housing with the safety (a cross-bolt: S pushed one way, F the other) and trigger,
its front notch hooked into the receiver and its rear pinned; the buttstock and buffer (a latch underneath); the bipod
legs that fold into the receiver and spring open. The M122 tripod: one front leg and two rear legs joined by the
traversing bar (a sleeve and sleeve latch, a mil scale, 0 at the centre, 450 left, 425 right), the pintle, the T&E
(traversing handwheel and screw, the elevating handwheel and screw with a +/-200 mil scale) pinned to the trigger
housing's rear.
Operation: load: cover up, the first round on the feed tray against the cartridge stop, open side of the links DOWN,
cover closed; charge: the handle fully back (the bolt locks back on the sear), safe, the handle pushed forward to lock;
fire: F, the trigger lets the bolt fly forward; cases drop out of the bottom, links leave the link port (the right).
Unload: bolt back, safe, cover up, belt off, feed tray up, chamber looked at, cover down, fire, the handle held back,
trigger, the bolt eased home. Barrel change: latch held, the handle rotated up, the barrel pushed forward and lifted
out; in: socket into the receiver, gas plug into the gas cylinder, pulled back to seat, the handle down (2-7 clicks).
Mounting: legs out to the sleeve latch, the T&E on the trigger housing's rear (its pin), the pintle into its bushing,
the gun into the pintle (its pin), the traversing slide onto the bar (slide to the rear, handwheel to the left).

The laser/light (J46, Jerry 8 Nov): "240B gets a PEQ 16": an AN/PEQ-16-type box on the handguard's right rail when
fitted (laser-light.md has the shape).

Standard fit (J52, Jerry 8 Nov: "M40 come with ACOG. Not purchasable attachment for it, also comes with laser light. Launcher can take red dot."): the M240 COMES WITH the ACOG on the feed cover's rail and the
PEQ-16-type laser/light on the handguard's right rail, both always on the gun; neither is bought or fitted in the armory.
GP-20062 leaves both places; GP-20065/20066 put them on for good.
