# AK-47 style rifle, Century Arms owner's manual (the game's KR-7) — digest
Source: https://www.centuryarms.com/media/wysiwyg/pdf/AK47_STYLE_RIFLE.pdf (Century Arms, 2018, 15 pages; text read in
full; the illustrations are images [figure only]). The manual gives NO dimensions, weight or capacity.
Known (not from this manual): an AKM is about 880 mm long, the barrel 415 mm, about 3.1 kg empty, 30-round curved mag,
7.62x39.
The manual's parts figure (Illustration 1, p.6): 1 buttstock, 2 recoil spring cam (the button at the rear of the
receiver cover that releases it), 3 safety lever, 4 receiver, 5 charging handle (RIGHT side, on the carrier), 6 rear
sight, 7 gas tube lock arm, 8 upper and lower handguards, 9 front sight (a post turned for elevation in a block slid for
windage), 10 compensator detent, 11 compensator (the muzzle device), 12 barrel, 13 magazine, 14 magazine release lever
(a thumb paddle behind the mag), 15 trigger, 16 trigger guard, 17 pistol grip, 18 rear sling attachment.
Also named: the dust/receiver cover (locked at its rear by the cam button, its front in a groove at the back of the gas
tube block), gas tube and gas block, the gas piston, the bolt carrier (guide grooves on the receiver rails, a cam track,
the bolt in a large hole), the bolt (rotates in the cam track), hammer and sear, recoil spring assembly, the receiver's
rear plate, the extractor.
Controls: the SAFETY LEVER on the receiver's RIGHT side: up = SAFE (it also blocks the charging handle's travel), down =
FIRE; a click at safe; the lever must stay between the stop under the receiver and the cover. The magazine release
lever is pressed with the thumb. The charging handle on the right, pulled fully back and let go.
Operation: magazine IN = its front hooked into the well first, then rocked REARWARD until it clicks. OUT = press the
release, rock the mag's bottom FORWARD (toward the muzzle) and down. Chambering = safety down, charging handle fully
back and released (the bolt strips a round), safety up. Firing semi: each shot cycles; the manual doesn't name the
ejection side (it's the right, through the receiver's opening under the cover, forward of the handle's slot).
Field strip (p.10-11): mag out, safety down, chamber checked; press the cam button, lift the dust cover off (upward,
rear first); press the recoil spring cam and lift the assembly out to the rear; pull the carrier and bolt back to the
rear plate, lift the carrier's rear and draw them out; rotate the bolt counter-clockwise out of the carrier's cam track.
Sight adjustment: front post turned clockwise lowers it (raises impact); the block slid left moves impact right.
Figures: Illustration 1-3 (parts, safe, fire) p.6; 4 charging handle p.7; 5-6 mag loading/insertion p.7; 7 chambering
p.9; 8-9 mag removal p.10; 10-13 field strip p.11; 14-17 reassembly p.12; 18-19 sights p.13.
