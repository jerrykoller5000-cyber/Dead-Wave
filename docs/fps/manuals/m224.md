Components and weights: the M225 cannon (about 6.5 kg/14.4 lb; about 1 m long; a blast attenuator device at the
muzzle; a carrying handle with the firing mechanism and a trigger/selector (drop-fire or trigger-fire) near the breech
in the hand-held mode; a ball-shaped base plug that seats in the baseplate's socket); the M170 bipod (about 6.9 kg/15.2
lb: a collar that clamps the cannon with two shock absorbers, the elevation mechanism (a crank on a vertical screw in a
tube), the traversing mechanism (a crank on a horizontal screw), a cross-levelling sleeve, two legs with spiked feet
joined by a chain or cable); the M7 baseplate (round, about 6.5 kg/14.4 lb, a rotating socket) or the M8 (small,
rectangular, about 1.6 kg/3.6 lb, for the hand-held mode); the M64 sight (about 1.1 kg, a dovetail mount on the bipod's
left). Total about 21 kg/46.5 lb conventional, about 8 kg hand-held.
Emplacing: the baseplate set and dug in, the cannon's base plug into the socket and turned to lock, the bipod's legs
spread and its collar clamped round the cannon's lower third, the sight on its mount, laid and levelled.
Firing: a round's increments (propellant charges, horseshoe-shaped, on the tail boom) set for range, the fuze set, the
round dropped TAIL FIRST into the muzzle, the gunner's hands down and away; it slides, fires on the firing pin (drop
fire). Misfire: wait, kick the cannon, then the misfire procedure.
Rounds: 60 mm HE (M720/M888) olive drab with yellow markings, a teardrop body, a fin assembly on the tail boom, the
increments round the boom, a point fuze; illumination white body, smoke light green, practice blue; about 1.7 kg, about
350 mm long.
Carrying: one marine with the cannon over his shoulder by its handle or the whole set on a pack frame; hand-held mode:
the cannon with the M8 baseplate carried and fired from the ground, braced by hand.
