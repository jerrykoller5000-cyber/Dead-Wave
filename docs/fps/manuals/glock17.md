# Glock 17 (the game's pistol; no logos or markings) — digest
Source: Jerry's second link, https://www.mcarbo.com/.Owners%20Manuals/Glock-Owners-Manual.pdf — Glock's "Safe Action
pistols, all models, Instructions for use" (print code 31427 / 10 17, ~63 pages; read in full as text; the pictures and
the exploded drawings are images [figure only]: Gen4 G17 p.47, Gen5 p.49, the nomenclature diagram pp.10-11).
(His first link, a Scribd upload, showed only the data table.)
Specs (Technical Data pp.52-57, the table flattened; G17 values): slide 186 mm long; overall about 202-204 mm; width
about 32-33 mm overall, the slide 25.5 mm; height with the magazine about 139 mm; sight radius 165 mm; barrel 114 mm
(hexagonal, right twist); 17 rounds; trigger pull about 26-28 N over about 12.5 mm of travel; rear sight heights 6.1-7.3 mm.
Parts (Gen4 list p.46, the numbers): 1 slide (matte black, rear cocking serrations, the EJECTION PORT on the RIGHT, the
sights); 2 barrel (its squared HOOD at the breech showing in the port, the lugs and the locking cams underneath; the C
models' ports in the slide top); 3 recoil spring assembly (one piece under the barrel); 4 firing pin (a lug at the slide's
rear, in the slide cover plate 14); 10 EXTRACTOR at the breech face on the right, which stands out of the slide when a
round is chambered (the loaded-chamber indicator); 15 rear sight (polymer, a white U/box insert), 16a front sight
(polymer, a white dot) and its screw; 17 FRAME (polymer, steel rails; the accessory rail under the dust cover, labelled
in the diagram); 17a-d the Gen4 backstraps (medium, large, with or without beavertail) hooked at the bottom rear;
19 MAGAZINE CATCH (a flat button behind the trigger guard, reversible on Gen4/5); 21 SLIDE LOCK (the takedown levers on
BOTH sides of the frame, just above the trigger); 22 locking block (under the barrel); 23 trigger housing with the
ejector; 26 TRIGGER with the trigger-safety blade in its face (the blade stands out when forward, flush when pressed);
27 SLIDE STOP LEVER (on the frame's LEFT above the trigger on Gen4 — known; ambidextrous on Gen5); 28 trigger pin;
29 trigger housing pin (rear of the frame); 30 locking block pin (Gen4); 31-35 the magazine: tube, follower, spring,
insert, FLOOR PLATE. Gen4 grip: finger grooves on the front strap, a textured grip (Gen5: no finger grooves).
Controls: the trigger and its blade (no manual safety); the magazine catch; the slide stop lever (down to release a
locked slide); the slide locks (pulled down on both sides together for takedown).
Operation: rounds pressed into the magazine from the front, bottom first; the magazine pushed into the well until the
catch locks; chamber: the slide pulled fully back by the serrations and released (or, if locked open, the slide stop
pressed down). Each shot: the slide moves back ~2.5 mm locked to the barrel, the barrel tilts down over the locking
block, the slide carries on back, the extractor pulls the case and the ejector throws it to the RIGHT, the slide returns
and strips the next round, the barrel locks up. After the last round the slide stays open on the slide stop. Unloading:
magazine out, the slide pulled back (the round ejected), the slide stop pushed up and the slide eased onto it, the
chamber checked, the slide released, the trigger pulled. Field strip: unloaded and the trigger pulled; the slide drawn
back about 3 mm and held, both slide locks pulled down, the slide pushed forward off the frame; the recoil spring pressed
forward and lifted out; the barrel lifted by its cams, forward, then up and back out. Reassembly the reverse (the slide
pushed back onto the rails until the slide lock snaps up).
