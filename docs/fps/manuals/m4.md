# M4 / M4A1 carbine (the game's GW-4) — digest
Source: Jerry's second link, https://archive.org/details/operators-manual-for-rifle-5.56-mm — TM 9-1005-319-10, the
Army's operator's manual for the M16A2/A3/A4 rifles and the M4/M4A1 carbines (June 2010; read through the item's OCR
text; figures are images in the item's PDF, TM_9-1005-319-10.pdf [figure only]). (His first link, Colt's PDF, is a scan
with no text.)
Identification: the M4 fires SAFE / SEMI / BURST (3 rounds), the M4A1 SAFE / SEMI / AUTO. The carbine has the M4
adapter rail (flat-top) with a back-up iron sight; the carrying handle is detachable (a clamping bar and two round nuts
on threaded studs, on the rail's front notch); the buttstock is adjustable with four positions (closed, half, three
quarters, open).
Specs (WP 0002): 29.75 in long with the stock closed, 33 in open; 7.75/8.63 lb with a sling and a loaded mag; 30-round
magazine; 1/7 right twist; 2,970 fps; cyclic 700-970 rpm; effective 500 m point / 600 m area. (Known: barrel 14.5 in.)
Sights (WP 0009): battlesight zero 300 m; the BUIS has two apertures (large 0-200 m, small 300 m+), a windage knob with a
heavy pointer mark, the elevation knob's 300 m mark "6/3"; the front sight post turns to move the strike.
Parts, RIGHT side (WP 0005 p.0005 00-5/6): 1 charging handle (at the rear of the upper, over the stock), 2 back-up iron
sight, 3 M4 adapter rail, 4 front sight assembly, 5 bayonet lug, 6 ejection port cover, 7 magazine catch (the button),
8 magazine, 9 trigger, 10 forward assist. (The rifle list adds the brass deflector, behind the port.)
Parts, LEFT side (p.0005 00-7): 1 compensator (the muzzle device; the blank adaptor hooks behind its first groove),
2 front sight post, 3 selector lever, 4 adjustable buttstock (spring and buffer inside), 5 sling, 6 the stock's
lock/release lever (pulled down to move it), 7 bolt catch, 8 slip ring (the spring ring that holds the handguard/rail),
9 sling swivel. Also: the takedown pin (rear) and receiver pivot pin (front), the gas tube on the barrel, the trigger
guard opened by a plunger (for mittens), a drain hole under the buttstock, an optional vertical grip on the lower rail.
Controls: selector on the left (SAFE needs the hammer cocked); the magazine catch on the right (pressed to drop the mag,
the mag pushed up until it catches); the bolt catch on the left (bottom pressed = lock the bolt back, top pressed =
release it); the charging handle at the rear (pulled back, released, never ridden forward); the forward assist on the
right (tapped to seat the bolt); the stock's lever on the left underside of the stock.
Operation (WP 0006): load a magazine from 10-round stripper clips in a filler (three clips); insert it straight up until
the catch holds; chamber: charging handle fully back, released, forward assist tapped; or from bolt-locked-back: mag in,
the bolt catch's top pressed (the bolt flies home), forward assist. After the last round the bolt locks back on the
catch. Reload: mag out (catch pressed, mag pulled down), new mag up, bolt catch top. Clearing: SAFE, mag out, charging
handle back, the catch's bottom pressed, the bolt eased onto it, the handle forward, look, then the catch's top. Immediate
action (SPORTS): slap the mag up, pull the handle fully back, observe the ejection, release, tap the forward assist,
squeeze. Burst: three per pull. Cases leave through the port on the right.
Field strip (WP 0013): clear; sling off; handguards off (the slip ring pressed down, the halves pulled out) or the lower
rail pivoted away; the takedown pin pushed (from the left) and the upper pivoted down on the front pin; the pivot pin
pushed; the receivers apart; the carrying handle off; the charging handle and carrier pulled back and out; the bolt out of
the carrier (the cam pin turned); the buffer and spring out (the retainer pressed); the stock: fully extended, the lever
pulled, slid off the receiver extension.
