# AA-12 (the game's Breacher-12) — digest
Source: Jerry's link, https://defensereview.com/auto-assault-12-shotgunfrag-12-high-explosive-round-comboweapon-system/
(DefenseReview, June 2005), plus the two items it links on the same site (the Oct 2004 AA-12 article and the USMC
Warfighting Lab FRAG-12 fact sheet, Mar 2005). Press articles, not a manual: thin on the outside shape (their photos and
the SOF magazine scans are images). Knowledge notes fill the shape and are marked.
Identification: the Auto Assault-12, Military Police Systems (MPS), Tennessee; from the Atchisson Assault-12, reworked
by Jerry Baber. Standard: 18-in barrel, about 10.5 lb empty; CQB: 13-in barrel, about 10 lb. Full-auto 12 gauge at 300
rpm (the trigger finger gives single shots and doubles). Magazines: an 8-round box, 20- and 32-round drums. Stainless
steel and high-impact plastics. The "constant-recoil" system: the bolt group never hits the back of the receiver, it
runs out on a long two-stage spring, so the felt recoil is low and the muzzle barely climbs. Said to need no lubrication.
FRAG-12: a fin-stabilised 19-mm warhead in a 3-in 12-gauge case (HE, HE-FA, HE-AP), arming at 3 m, impact-fused, 200 m.
Shape (knowledge, not in the sources): about 38 in (965 mm) long with the 18-in barrel; a tall, boxy, flat-sided receiver
made of two stamped halves joined along a horizontal seam with rivets/bolts, ventilated along its top; a carry handle on
top with the rear aperture at its back and the front sight post ahead on the barrel shroud; the charging handle in a
slot on the LEFT side under the handle (it does not reciprocate); the ejection port on the RIGHT; open bolt, gas operated;
a square-ish forend/handguard round the barrel's shroud; a fixed polymer stock in line with the bore with a thick rubber
pad; the pistol grip and a large trigger guard; the drum (a big round drum under the receiver, in front of the grip) or
the box in the well with a catch at its rear; the safety on the left above the grip. Finish: black (some tan/OD
furniture).
For the game: the Breacher-12 is already this layout (CL-313, CL-318: the 62 mm receiver, the handle slot on -x and the
port on +x, the drum 89 mm thick); this card refines it to the AA-12's look: the receiver's seam and fasteners, the
handle's sights, the vented top, the constant-recoil stroke for the shot animation (the bolt group and spring travel
long, the muzzle steady).
