# Micro UZI (the game's Wasp) — knowledge notes (the linked manual could not be read)
Jerry's link: https://www.scribd.com/document/444064419/... — Scribd served only its landing page (41 pages, a semi-auto
conversion manual). Jerry has been asked for an upload or another official copy. General knowledge:
Specs: about 250 mm with the stock folded, 460 mm extended; barrel 117 mm; about 1.95 kg empty; 20- and 32-round
magazines in the pistol grip; 9x19; cyclic about 1,200-1,250 rpm (the Micro's light bolt).
Parts and sides: a stamped boxy receiver; the TOP COVER (the feed/cover plate) with the COCKING KNOB on top (it rides in
a slot along the top cover; it does not move when firing: drawn back to cock, then pushed home); the EJECTION PORT on the RIGHT side of the
receiver with the bolt visible behind it; the magazine well IN the pistol grip with the magazine catch at the grip's
bottom-left heel; the GRIP SAFETY on the grip's back strap; the FIRE SELECTOR on the left above the grip (S / R / A:
safe, semi, auto); the trigger in a squared guard at the front of the grip; a folding wire stock that folds to the
RIGHT along the side; rear and front sights on top (the front in protective ears on the barrel nut); the barrel nut at
the front holding the short barrel.
Operation: mag into the grip from below until it clicks; the cocking knob pulled back (about the bolt's travel) and
released forward (it locks back on the sear: open-bolt); fire; cases out of the right port; unload: mag out, cock,
look, ease. Field strip: the cover's rear catch pressed, the top cover lifted off rearward, the bolt lifted out, the
barrel nut unscrewed, the barrel out the front.
