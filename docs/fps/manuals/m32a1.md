# Milkor USA M32A1 MSGL (the game's launcher) — digest
Sources: https://www.milkorusa.com/product/m32a1-usmc-40mm-v-02/ and the brochure it links
(milkorusa.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/Milkor-32a1.pdf). No manual online (an operator's manual ships with
it). The photos on the page are the source for shapes.
Identification: 40 mm six-shot revolving launcher, semi-automatic, the cylinder advanced by gas (an index mechanism),
double-action trigger, fires 40x46 LV and 40x51 MV.
Specs: 648 mm with the stock collapsed, 739 mm extended; height 292 mm; width 165 mm; barrel 203 mm (8 in; the
brochure has an ambiguous 260 mm row); cylinder 140 mm long; 7.0 kg with the sight (6.6 without, 7.2 with the M2A1),
8.3 kg loaded; six rounds in 3 s or less; progressive-twist rifling.
Parts: the six-chamber cylinder (140 mm), the barrel ahead of it, a STAINLESS receiver with a Picatinny rail on top,
the M2A1 reflex sight (a quadrant reticle, battery, on a Picatinny base; ~0.4 kg), a QUAD-RAIL forend (rails all round),
an ambidextrous safety selector, the double-action trigger, an angle-adjustable collapsible stock with a recoil pad.
Internal: firing-pin block, cylinder alignment pin. Finishes: matte tan, green or black (NP3 on the steel parts).
Operation (known, not on the page): the frame hinges open (the barrel and forend swing away from the cylinder on the
M32's front hinge), the cylinder is wound against its spring (the spring housing at the cylinder's front), rounds go
into the chambers from the rear, the frame closes; each shot the gas index turns the cylinder to the next chamber.
