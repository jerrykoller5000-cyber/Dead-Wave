# UZI (the game's Wasp) — digest
Source: Jerry's fourth UZI link, https://archive.org/details/firearms-mi-uzi-submachine-gun — "UZI SMG Operation Manual,
Cal. 9 mm Parabellum", adapted from FN Herstal's factory manual (1980 or later, ~47 pages; read in full through the
item's OCR text; the figures and the exploded view pp.22-23 are images [figure only]). It covers the FULL-SIZE UZI with
the folding metal butt; the Micro and Mini sizes are not in it (the Wasp is Micro-sized: micro-uzi.md's numbers for the
size, this manual for the parts and the operation; the three other UZI links were scans or a cover page).
Specs (full size, p.9): 3.57 kg without the magazine (metal butt); 650 mm long with the butt extended, 470 mm folded
(635 with the wooden butt); barrel 260 mm, 4 grooves; sight radius 309 mm; 650 rpm; magazines of 25 and 32 rounds (the
25 weighs 0.5 kg loaded); 9 mm Parabellum at about 400 m/s; effective to 200 m.
Parts and sides: the BODY (receiver; the bolt wraps round the barrel's rear); the TOP COVER on top, released by the COVER
CATCH at the front of the rear-sight seat (pressed back; the cover's rear lifted, then the cover drawn back and up), the
COCKING LEVER and HANDLE on the cover, drawn fully back with the LEFT hand (it also closes the ejection opening against
dust when back); the GRIP SAFETY in the back of the pistol grip (squeezed to cock and fire); the CHANGE LEVER (selector)
on the LEFT above the grip, a knob pushed to S (rear, safe), R (middle, single) or A (front, auto); the MAGAZINE CATCH, a
knurled button in the grip pressed with the left thumb; the magazine into the PISTOL GRIP (two grip halves on two
screws); the trigger in its guard; the folding metal BUTT hinged on the body's rear face by a hexagon screw, folding
FORWARD UNDER the body (unlocked by pressing the plate on its right side; the butt plate's tip struck with the palm to
unfold); the diopter REAR SIGHT on the cover with a tilting flap "100"/"200" and protectors; the blade FORESIGHT in its
housing with protectors (adjusted with a screw and nut); the BARREL NUT at the front holding the barrel, the BARREL CATCH
in the front of the foresight housing (pressed back to free the barrel); two handguard halves on two screws; the
ejection opening (the right side, known; the manual doesn't say). Accessories: bayonet, blank adaptor (in place of the
barrel nut), magazine filler, sling.
Operation: magazine filled round by round, pressed home; inserted with the gun held by the grip (grip safety squeezed),
tilted to the right, pushed up into the grip until the catch clicks. Cocking: the lever drawn fully back with the left
hand (the bolt held back on the sear: OPEN BOLT), released (it springs forward on its own; it doesn't move when firing),
the selector to R or A. Firing: the trigger lets the bolt fly forward, it feeds, chambers and fires at the end of its
travel; R = one shot per pull, A = while held. The case leaves the ejection opening. Unloading: S, magazine out (the
thumb on the catch), chamber looked at, the selector to A/R, the trigger pulled to ease the bolt home, S. Stoppages:
cock, magazine out, another in, fire. Field strip: the cover off; the bolt and return spring out (the bolt's front raised,
out forward); the barrel catch pressed, the barrel nut unscrewed, the barrel drawn out FORWARD.
