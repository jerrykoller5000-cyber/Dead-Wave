# Stevens Model 320 pump shotgun (the game's shotgun) — digest
Source: https://archive.org/details/stevens-model-320-manual (Savage Arms manual P/N 108782, 06/15 Rev 2, 10 pages; read
through the item's OCR text; the figures were not readable as text). The original: Savage's PDF named in the item.

Identification: Stevens 320 pump-action, 12 and 20 gauge, Field Grade and Security Grade. The gauge is stamped on the
barrel's side. Capacity 5 in the tube (the Field Grade 28-inch ships with a plug limiting it to 2). The game's is the
Security Grade look: short barrel, pistol-grip stock (J34, black, Jerry's call).
Specs in the text: only the 28-inch Field barrel, the 5-shell tube, the screw-in choke (Field). Overall length, weight,
length of pull: [figure/spec page only]. Known for the Security 12 ga (not from this text): 18.5-inch barrel, about 39
inches overall, about 7 lb, ghost-ring or bead sights by model, a vented heat shield on some.
Named parts: the safety button at the REAR OF THE TRIGGER GUARD (a cross-bolt, pushed right = safe, left = fire, a red
band shows on fire, clicks in both); the forend (the sliding pump); the slide lock (a lever that frees the forend when
it's locked forward, near the guard); the trigger; the ejection port on the receiver's side (the right on a right-hand
gun); the magazine tube with its spring-loaded end; the locking nut (the tube cap, unscrews counter-clockwise, holds the
barrel); the barrel's retaining lug that slides over the tube; the bolt/breech face, seen when the forend is back; the
white magazine-spring retaining cap (inside).
Operation (what moves): pull the forend straight back = the bolt opens, the chambered shell is extracted and thrown out
of the ejection port; push it forward = the bolt closes and locks, chambering the next shell from the tube. The trigger
releases the forend's lock when fired (keep forward pressure while firing). The slide lock frees a locked forend
without firing. Loading the chamber directly: forend back, a shell laid in the port and thumbed into the chamber,
forend forward. Loading the tube: shells thumbed into the tube under the receiver, five. Unloading: safe, cycle until
the chamber and tube are empty, feel the chamber, press the tube's follower. Barrel off: action open, the locking nut
off counter-clockwise, the barrel pulled forward; on: forend forward about two inches, the lug over the tube, seat,
nut hand-tight.
For the model and the hands: the cross-bolt safety in the guard's rear (left/right), the slide-lock lever by the guard,
the ejection port with the bolt face, the loading port under the receiver, the tube cap nut at the muzzle end of the
tube, the barrel's retaining lug ring over the tube, the forend on its action bars. The pump rests forward (J-answer
8 Nov), racks back about 3-3.5 inches on a 12 ga.
Figures (no page numbers in the OCR): 1 safety on safe, 2 on fire, 3 forend/slide lock open, 4 forend locked forward,
5 shell into the chamber, 6 loading the tube, 7 the locking nut, 8 barrel removal, 9-10 the plug, 11 barrel insertion.
