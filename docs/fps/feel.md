# First-person feel: the yardstick (D-119, Claude, 7 Nov; Jerry: "more like an FPS feel")

What the reviews measure against. A target, not a rule; where a number is off, the review says by how much.

- **Aiming (ADS):** in about 0.18-0.25 s for a pistol or SMG, 0.25-0.32 s for rifles and shotguns, up to 0.4 s for the sniper
  and heavies; out a little quicker. The sight comes straight up the centre line, no swing round from the side. (J7: today's is too slow.)
- **In the view at the hip:** the gun sits low and to the right, filling no more than about a quarter of the screen; the
  support hand on the fore end (not round the side); the stock out of frame or just at the bottom edge.
- **Aimed:** the sight on the centre, nothing of the gun above it but the sight itself; no stock in frame; the hands still.
- **Motion:** a walk bob of a centimetre or two, a run bob larger with the gun lowered and turned a little (a sprint pose for
  the long guns); sway lags the turn slightly and settles; landing dips once. Nothing pops: the elbows, wrists and fingers
  move smoothly every frame (J8).
- **Firing:** the shot and the tracer leave the muzzle (J10); the kick goes up and back and recovers in about 0.1-0.2 s;
  heavier guns kick more; the flash at the muzzle.
- **Reloads (Jerry: "all reload animations and movements from arms while reloading. Go for realism."):** each gun's real
  manual of arms: the support hand goes to the magazine release or the mag, strips it, takes a fresh one from the belt (off
  screen, low left), seats it with a push, then the charging handle, bolt or slide where the gun has one (only on an empty
  reload); the gun tilts toward the work and comes back; the shotgun loads shell by shell, the revolver swings its cylinder,
  the launcher's six-shot drum swings out to the left (an MGL: unlatch, swing, eject, load each chamber, wind, close; GP-20039). The arms move as arms do (shoulders and elbows follow the hands, no snapping). Times near
  Jerry's references (2-5.6 s, docs/fps/feel.md's sources: his GLBs). The counts on the HUD stay true (J12).
- **Switching:** lowers one gun and raises the next without a jump.
- **The body in view:** the arms' texture never pops (J6); the balaclava stops at the ears (J5); sleeves and gloves as in third person.
