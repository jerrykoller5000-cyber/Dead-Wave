# The Signal: the story bible, v2 (D-44)

Owner: Claude (CL-74, P-86). Rewritten 2026-10-01 from Jerry's answers on the narrative (the valley, the PGB and its
Gravewalkers, the twelve of FOB Threshold, the survivors on the roof, the floatplane). The first version is in
`docs/archive/story-v1.md`. Every line here is a first draft for Jerry's red pen. Names are Jerry's (2026-10-01).

**The rule for telling it (Jerry):** no walls of text. The history is in the world: little things on the map that show
who lived here and what happened to them. Words come in short pieces: a field note, a radio line, a survivor talking.

## The short version

A valley deep in forest, ringed by mountains, with a lake in the middle and rivers running into it. Everyone who has
ever come here has died.

Under the lake, in a white rock the PGB calls **the Marrow**, something very old is waking up. Its voice is
**the tone**. The tone runs through water and rock, and it raises the dead. Three weeks ago it took **FOB Threshold**,
the twelve-strong base the PGB built to lock the valley down. One marine parachutes in to find out what
happened, get the relay talking again, and hold out for the floatplane on night 20.

## 1. Who and what

- **The PGB, the Paranormal Ground Branch.** Unofficially, the **Gravewalkers**. Motto: *"Against What Should Not Be."*
  Strange things have been turning up all over the world, and these are the people sent to put them down. Hard,
  quiet, military. The marine is one of them.
- **Ridgeline.** The PGB command on the far side of the mountains: the voice on the relay. Tired and decent.
  It can't send a team in (it sent one). It can send supply drops (one call a day) and, on night 20, the floatplane.
- **The marine.** The player. Never named, never unmasked (D-66). He doesn't talk; the world talks to him.
- **FOB Threshold.** The HQ: a cold, industrial forward operating base the PGB dropped into the valley, built at the
  edge of an anomalous zone, with the relay, the sample window and the supply terminal. It fell three weeks ago. It is
  sealed (section 5).
- **The Cordon.** The great wall the PGB built round the valley. Nothing leaves.
- **The Marrow** (was "the chalk"). The soft white rock under the lake, pale as bone. The heart sleeps in it.
  The sixth cave, the Marrow cave, sits right on top of it.
- **The heart.** The thing asleep in the Marrow. Old beyond anything in the files. Never seen, only heard.
- **The tone.** The heart's voice. It travels through water and rock, not open air. It raises the dead and drives
  them. Every night it is louder.
- **The guardian.** There is only one. It is the heart's own body: it comes up through any cave, which is why every
  guardian looks the same. While the tone sings, the heart rebuilds it, so it can't die. It protects the heart.
- **The Pit and the ring.** The sinkhole under the lake, ringed with eight carved stones. The ring is what held the
  heart asleep.
- **Heron**. The floatplane: a military amphibious transport. It lands on the lake and taxis to the dock.

## 2. The history: everyone who came here died

Told only through the world (section 7) and in pieces (the relay, the notes, the survivors). Never all at once.

1. **The first people.** They found the heart long ago. They carved the eight stones, set them round the Pit, and
   sealed the Marrow cave with a carved stone door. They are buried in the barrow under the hill cave. They died here.
2. **The settlers: Coldwater.** A mining town came for the iron. The mine went deep and broke toward the
   Marrow. The town died in one winter. The last of them walled the mine shut with iron bars, buried their dead in
   iron-banded coffins (they had learned the tone can't get through iron), and left. Coldwater became a ghost town.
3. **The trapper.** The last man in the valley, a settler's grandson, living alone the old way: horseshoes on every
   door, an iron-lined root cellar. He warned people off. He is dead now; his camp is still standing.
4. **The hikers.** Ghost-town stories brought a group of hikers. They explored the caves and cut the old iron bars off
   the mine to go deeper. That opened the way. One stayed outside, waited for his friends, and walked out to the
   rangers when they didn't come back. He never saw the dead.
5. **The rangers.** Wilderness rangers (not Army Rangers) ran a search and rescue with the last hiker. The hiker and
   all but one ranger died in the caves. The last ranger got over the pass and reported what he saw.
6. **The PGB.** The report was flagged: the PGB has had a file on this valley for a hundred years.
   They built the Cordon, dropped in FOB Threshold, and sent twelve Gravewalkers. Four quiet days. Then, at 02:14 on the fifth night,
   the tone came up on every channel, the signals tech pulled the relay's fuse to shut it out, and the post was
   overrun. Nine died. Three hid (section 5).
7. **Now.** Three weeks of silence. Ridgeline sends one marine.

## 3. How a run tells it

- **Insertion.** He parachutes in. The relay panel on the HQ board: "RELAY · SILENT".
- **Act 1 · Boots on the ground (nights 1-3).** No voice yet. The world speaks: the field notes of the twelve
  (section 6) and the places themselves (section 7). The first poke into a cave and the first swim toward the Pit each
  get their line.
- **Act 2 · Get the relay talking (nights 4-10).** Repairing the relay is the day's goal. From the next morning
  Ridgeline speaks one line a morning (section 4) and offers Tonight's call. Survivors can be found from night 3.
- **Act 3 · The wave (nights 11-20).** Fog Night (14), Swarm Night (17), the siege (18), the colossus by day. The
  relay turns from what happened to what the heart is, and then to Heron.
- **The way out (night 20).** He calls Heron from the board. Flares at the dock; she comes in low over the trees,
  lands on the lake and taxis in during the last push. He holds the dock and boards.
- **The secret (for players who look).** `docs/specs/secret-quest.md`, with the changes in section 9.

**Skulls and Cash.** Every risen skull carries a faint rune mark, proof the tone was in it. He drops them through the
sample window; the PGB pays for them and uses the bone for research and weapons (the rune finish is the first
thing that comes of it). Cash is requisition credit: the terminal is a supply terminal, and buying is ordering a drop.

**Why the alarm and the tone differ.** The alarm is only noise: it pulls the dead that are already up toward the sound.
The tone makes them: it raises them and drives them. The alarm attracts; the tone creates.

## 8. The Hollows

- **What they are.** Under five of the six caves runs a **warren**: the tunnels where the dead drag the living down
  toward the Pit. All five together are **the Hollows**. The PGB boards at each mouth name them (section 6).
- **Why only five.** The sixth, the Marrow cave, is the heart's own: sealed at its mouth by the first people's door.
  The only way to the heart is through the warrens' rune doors (the secret).
- **What the dead want.** The living. They drag them below to the Pit, and the Pit raises them. Whatever they were
  carrying comes down with them: FOB Threshold's crates and kit, a ranger's radio, the hikers' packs.
- **The husks.** Bodies hung in a pale, veined sack from the roots, half turned: a hiker's boot through the skin of
  one, a ranger's jacket in another. Some husks hold two or three bodies grown together: the pit makes amalgamations,
  and that is where the brutes, the spiders and the rest come from. The longer below, the stranger.
- **The dog tags.** Nine, one for each of the twelve who died (three lived): root 2, shale 2, iron 2, wet 2, hill 1.
  Each tag is a name and one last thing about them, written when the tags are made (CL-100).
- **The iron warren** is where it started: the settlers' old iron wall, cut open; the hikers' rope and their lights.
- **The rune doors.** A sealed slab at the back of each Deep, carved like the Pit's stones, humming toward the lake.
  E on one: "It's warm. It's singing."
- **The rune shards.** Five, one in each warren's strongbox. Each shows one glyph of the word and its place.

## 9. The secret: what the word is, and the Hush

**The word.** The heart's tone is its call, and the call has a shape: five of the eight glyphs, in an order, over and
over. The stones round the Pit echo it at night. It is never the same twice (a new word each run).

**Why it silences.** Play a sound back at itself, half a beat out of step, and the two cancel: the way noise-cancelling
headphones work. The relay on the mast is the biggest voice in the valley, and its ground line runs down into the rock
and the water, the way the tone travels. Send the word through it out of step and the tone is cancelled across the
whole valley until dawn, when the heart sings again. That night the dead come at half strength, and the guardian
falls back to the heart to protect it: the one time and place it can be killed.

**The Hush.** The same trick, small. A box Ridgeline talks him through building from the relay's spare board: it
listens to the tone and plays it back out of step, so for a few metres round him the tone goes quiet. The heart and
the guardian sense the living through the tone; inside the Hush he isn't there for them. Its battery lasts one delve,
and the HQ charges it once a dawn.

**How he learns the word** (the clues are the twelve's, not a ranger's):
- Ridgeline's line 14: "almost a word". The board shows five empty marks.
- Brandt's pencil on the watchtower rail: "Count them from the tower. Five." Brandt on the roof: "the stones blink. In
  order." From the tower at night the five stones flare in turn.
- The rune shards in the Hollows, one glyph each.
- Maybe (try it and see): the first time he stands on the tower at night, "The stones are blinking. In order."

**Glyphs.** Carved symbols, like letters. Each of the eight stones has its own: the eye, the cup, the moon, the star,
the ladder, the crown, the drop, the gate.

**The true ending.** The guardian dies in the heart; the tone dies for good; every dead in the valley drops where it
stands; the lake goes flat and clear; birdsong. Ridgeline: "Ridgeline. Your tone's gone. Everyone's tone is gone.
Heron's coming to get you. Walk to the dock." The badge "Silence", the rune finish, and the rune pistol on the dock in
later runs.
