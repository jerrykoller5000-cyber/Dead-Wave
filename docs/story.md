# The Signal: the story bible (D-44)

Owner: Claude (CL-74, P-86). For Jerry to read; ChatGPT puts the words into `ui/strings.js` and on the board (GP-66).
Every line here is a first draft for Jerry's red pen. Written 2026-09-30.

## The short version

Three weeks ago the emergency relay on the mast went silent. The medical convoy never arrived; the three camps
stopped answering on the same night. One marine parachutes in to find out why, holds the cabin that becomes his HQ,
and gets the relay talking so a boat can come for him on night 20.

The dead are answering a signal: a tone from the runes around the sinkhole under the lake (the Pit). Every night it
sings louder, and more of them walk out of the six caves and up out of the water. That is the dead wave. Something in
the chalk cave guards the source, and it can't be killed while the tone sings.

Two endings: **the boat** (hold the dock on night 20 and board it), and **the secret** (silence the tone for one
night, kill the guardian, and the lake goes quiet: D-56).

## 1. Who and where

- **The marine.** The player. Never named, never unmasked (D-66). He doesn't talk; the world talks to him.
- **Harbor Nine.** The coast station at the other end of the relay: a dispatcher's voice, tired and decent. It can't
  send people in; it can send supply planes (one call a day, D-53) and, on night 20, a boat.
- **Medic-4.** The medical convoy that left the depot on the ridge road with antibiotics and a doctor, and never
  arrived. Its wreck is the convoy (the medical cache). The doctor got out; see the survivors.
- **The camps.** The ranger post, the hikers' camp, the trapper's camp. All three went quiet in the same minute.
- **The Pit.** The sinkhole under the lake, ringed with carved stones. The source of the tone.
- **The guardian.** The thing in the chalk cave. It drags the curious into the dark. It is the tone's watchdog, and it
  never leaves the dark for long.
- **Places that already say it** (the props, `assets/world/`): the silent emergency relay station on the mast, the
  evacuation landing at the dock left in haste, the failed medical supply convoy and a stripped utility truck on the
  road, the three camps, the fuel sheds, the watchtower.

## 2. How a run tells it

- **Insertion.** He parachutes in. The relay panel on the HQ board: "RELAY · SILENT".
- **Act 1 · Boots on the ground (nights 1-3).** No voice yet. The props and the camps speak (section 4). The first
  poke into a cave and the first swim toward the Pit each get their line (P-11, P-12).
- **Act 2 · Get the relay talking (nights 4-10).** Repairing the relay is the day's goal. From the next morning, Harbor
  Nine speaks one line each morning (section 3) and offers Tonight's call. The survivors can be found from night 3.
- **Act 3 · The wave (nights 11-20).** The relay's lines turn from what happened to what the Pit is, and then to the
  boat.
- **The way out (night 20).** He calls the boat from the board; flares at the dock, a horn, a lamp on the water during
  the last push (CL-73). He holds the dock and boards.
- **The secret (for players who look).** Written in `docs/specs/secret-quest.md` (CL-79). This bible only plants it:
  relay line 14, and the stones seen from the tower.
