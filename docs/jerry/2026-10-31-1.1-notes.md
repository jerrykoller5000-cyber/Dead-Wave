## First person: the weapons, the hands, the feel
- J3. Hands still do not grip the pistol accurately in first person. The hands clip through the pistol. That is not the way one would grip a pistol in reality.
- J4. Wasp FOV on zoom in first person too close. Sights need work in first person to be usable.
- J5. Wasp clips through player cam on reload in first person.
- J6. Akimbo Wasps look awful in first person. The left one is barely visible and the right is only visible if you look down. Guns are not in the player's hands (they pop out of the hands while looking down). Need a better akimbo first-person crosshair. Same with pistol akimbo, and likely the revolver akimbo.
- J7. Extended mag for the pistol looks bad: tiny, nothing like a real pistol magazine.
- J8. Lasers do not move with the recoil of the guns; they stay straight when they should move with the weapon.
- J9. Lasers turn off during reloads, which they should not.
- J10. GW-4 red dot could use some work. FOV for the GW-4 is wrong when shooting from the hip and reloading.
- J11. The hand placement on almost every weapon is inconsistent. Wrists look weird.
- J12. All the weapons in first-person hip fire sit up and to the left. Too much of the screen; should look like a traditional FPS.
- J13. Revolver sights unusable: the hammer is in the way on aim.
- J14. Someone who knows what they are doing needs to do a complete revamp of how weapons look and are handled in first person: the aim, the hand placement and movement, the reloads, how the weapons look up close, and the FOV.
- J15. "My God the hands. It's the bane of my existence in first person. They look so wrong and always in the wrong spots."
- J16. Launcher needs better first-person sights: the ability to arc rounds a little, maybe a leaf sight (less arc than the mortar or the grenades).
- J17. Health pen animation could be much better; it looks bad in first person. No health pen while akimbo.
- J18. Minigun looks good; could use a little red dot. Placement is great. Hands not even close.
- J19. Flamethrower shakes the player too violently in first person.
- J20. Breacher-12 recoil a little too violent in first person. Sights unusable and inaccurate.
- J21. Due diligence on the size of the suppressors and magazines: most look smaller than the magwell; some rifle suppressors too small.
- J22. "If someone doesn't figure out how to get these hands and wrists to look good I'm going to implode."
- J23. Smooth the weapons jumping around when moving in first-person ADS: they jump and jerk.
- J24. Need a smoother FPS feel. Right now it is disorienting and jerky.
- J25. FPS combat feels off.
- J26. Weapons wheel brings the player into first person when in third person. It should open without the camera change (and from first person without a transition).
- J27. Using the armory (and every menu) in first person sometimes makes the cursor disappear; Esc is needed to unlock it.
