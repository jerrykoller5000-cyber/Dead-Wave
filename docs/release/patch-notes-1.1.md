# Grave Shift 1.1

The valley has a longer memory now. Follow its leads by day, hold the line by night, and see who is still out there.

## The story and the missing hikers

- After the first day, a new lead arrives each dawn. The duty board, radio and places you search carry one trail from Threshold's lost fuel through the relay and six supply sites. What you finish changes the world around you.
- Six missing hikers have names and faces on the trailhead posters. An ambulance radio log points toward their camp; a camera found there shows the next clue. Pike, Brandt and Okafor can be found where their own trails end.
- The Hollows have a rebuilt descent. Bring a spare board from the relay to the HQ table and wire it into the Hush. It charges each dawn; carry it lit to get past what waits below.

## The world

- Threshold, the supply sites, trails, landmarks and cave mouths have had a placement and detail pass. The trapper's path reads as a place to search; the first steps into a cave now lead through rock and earth before the dark.
- Storms come less often and hold off for the first two days. Wind moves through in spells, bending the trees and grass and driving the clouds and leaves.
- The medical convoy keeps the marks of your visit: the ambulance doors stay open, its cooler gone, its cab radio within reach.

## The dead

- The full cast of zombies has had a silhouette, material and motion pass. The screamer, feral and armored dead read sooner in a crowd, by day or by flashlight.
- The first night presses in together.

## The marine and the guns

- First-person sights, hands and reloads have been brought closer to the guns. The sights stay steadier while running, and an empty gun reloads on its own.
- The marine's unarmed run, ladder climb and blade strikes have new body-led motion. From his eyes, a knife or sword now crosses the view toward the target.
- Brightness, vibrancy and first-person field of view can be set to suit your screen.

## Sound

- The alarm's metal sting is back over the klaxon before the fight begins. The guardians, guns, footsteps, field terminals and several places in the valley have refreshed sounds; the night still leaves room to hear what matters.

## The screens

- The supply terminal is quicker to read: fewer tabs, a clear look at what can be bought, prices and unlocks together.
- Field screens and prompts have a more settled terminal look. Pickups and prompts stay in view from the marine's eyes, and mission panels work in either camera view.
- Late-night Cash has been rebalanced so the final nights still ask you to choose what to buy.

## Speed

- Crowded nights have had a scene pass, with less work spent on distant history props and the HQ yard.
