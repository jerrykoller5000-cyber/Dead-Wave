# Grave Shift 1.1 — Jerry's play list

1. Start a fresh run and check the first day: the goal, Training Ground and first supply terminal should be clear without a mission lead arriving too early.
2. Browse the supply terminal with and without Cash: useful items, prices and unlock conditions should be understandable at a glance.
3. Reach the second dawn and follow the fuel lead: the HQ should be dark until the can is poured, then its lamps, hum and radio should return.
4. Work through the relay: the breaker, tuner, lever and vault should be usable in both views, and the mast light should answer the reboot.
5. Bring the relay's spare board to the HQ table and wire it: the Hush should be yours at the next dawn, and lit at the Hollows it should let you go down past the dead.
6. Enter and leave the Hollows: the descent should stay underground, the camera should stay inside the rock, and the route back should be legible.
7. Jack the ambulance at the medical convoy: good pumps should lift it a notch, misses should drop it, and the cooler should come free at the end.
8. Return to the ambulance after the run: its doors should remain open, the cooler should be gone, and the cab radio should play the hiker search log.
9. Follow the missing hikers to their camp: six named faces should appear on the trailhead posters; the camera should show its short clue and lead onward.
10. Search beyond the hikers' camp for Pike: his hiding place should make sense from the trail and he should come home once his thread is finished.
11. Follow the other survivor leads to Brandt and Okafor: each should be found where that search ends, with the camp's dead cleared.
12. Walk from the FOB to the trapper's path, river, bridge and a cave mouth: approaches, scale, solid ground and the shift into the cave should feel natural.
13. Play through day three into bad weather: the first two days should stay storm-free; later wind should rise and fall, with trees, grass, clouds and leaves responding.
14. Fight in first and third person with a rifle and a blade: sights, hands, reloads, prompts and swings should stay readable, and the dead's different shapes should stand out in motion.
15. Carry a run through night 20 with sound and music on: the opening alarm sting should hand off to the fight, key sounds should stay audible, the larger crowd should stay responsive, and Cash should still call for choices.
