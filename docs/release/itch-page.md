## Title

Grave Shift

## Tagline

Scavenge by day. Hold the dead by night. Make it to extraction on night twenty.

## Page text

FOB Threshold fell. You're the Gravewalker sent back into the valley. In daylight, bring in supplies, find survivors, repair the relay and build a line around HQ. Prep has no countdown: when you're ready, sound the alarm at the HQ panel. At night, the dead come out of six cave mouths. Hold through twenty nights, then call Heron to the lake.

The six supply sites each have a task to solve before their stores open: a wrecked medical convoy, a ranger cache, a chained utility truck, a trapper's path, a hikers' kit and a fuel depot. Explore the unsealed caves by day to enter the Hollows, search for the missing Gravewalkers' tags and follow what they left behind. Kill the risen, collect their marked skulls, bank them at the HQ sample window for Cash, then order weapons, ammo and defences from the supply terminal.

This 1.0 build is single-player. A run does not save midway.

## Controls

- WASD or arrow keys: move. Shift: run. Space: jump. C: crouch. V: slow time for a few seconds.
- Mouse: aim. Left click: fire or place a build. Hold right click: zoom in toward the cursor (in first person, aim down the sights). 5: switch between first and third person. Scroll: zoom. Hold middle mouse and drag: turn the camera.
- 1: night vision. 2: flashlight. 3: laser. 4: holster or draw.
- R: reload (or rotate a build). F: knife. Hold G and release: throw a grenade. H: use a MedPen.
- E: interact, including supply sites, the HQ sample window, the supply terminal and the alarm panel.
- Hold Q: weapon wheel; tap Q: switch carried guns. Hold B: build wheel; tap B: enter or leave build mode. T: repair a build. X: scrap in build mode.
- Tab: full map. Esc: pause, Settings and Tips. F11: toggle fullscreen.

## Download and start

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit. Download the Grave Shift Windows installer and run it to install for your Windows user account; administrator access is not required. Launch Grave Shift from the installed app. The installer is not signed, so Windows SmartScreen may ask before the first run: choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”. The game opens fullscreen; F11 switches it.

Your profile is stored at Documents\Dead-Wave\profile.json. The folder name is still Dead-Wave even though the game is Grave Shift.
