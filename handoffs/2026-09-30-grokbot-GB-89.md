# grokbot - GB-89 (R5, P-61): a colossus walks a trail by day - 2026-09-30

Changed:
- **The wanderer bounty.** On the odd nights from 7 (never a colossus night or a guardian night: 7, 9, 11, 13, 17, 19, 21 ...), the prep posts one colossus out on a worn trail, beside the ordinary bounties.
  - It's a GB-57 post with `kind: 'wanderer'`, one guard, and the reward `max(150, bountyRewardFor(d))`: 150 on nights 7-13, 300 from 14.
- **How it walks:**
  - It keeps to a stretch of trail at least 45 m from the HQ (`wandererTrails()`), end to end and back, at 0.6 of its speed.
  - If it hasn't gone 1 m in 3 s it turns back (the re-path).
  - It uses the normal zombie movement (steering, the walk cycle, heavy footsteps), with its target set to the next trail point. It walks through the no-zombie grace.
- **Waking and ending:** it wakes on the guard rules (a hit, walking within 18 m, a heard shot, the alarm) and then fights as a colossus. Its kill pays `bounty-done` with the reward into the bag, on top of its own skulls. At the alarm an open one goes (`bounty-expired` 'alarm').
- `getBounties()` rows now carry `wanderer`, and `bounty-posted` carries `wanderer: true`.

What the player notices:
- Every other day from night 7, a colossus walks slowly along one of the far trails, with heavy footsteps.
- He can loot round it (it ignores him until he comes within 18 m, shoots it, or fires where it can hear; a suppressed gun carries only about a third as far) or bring it down by day for about 150 skulls (300 from night 14) plus its own. If he leaves it, it's gone at the alarm.
- The HQ board doesn't list it yet: `ui/bounties.js` only knows the POI kinds, so the row waits for GP-68.
