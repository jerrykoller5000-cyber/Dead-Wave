# CL-133: the marine, smooth and fluid (Claude, 2026-10-08)

Jerry: "Make all of the Player's movements and animations smooth and fluid." One task, solo. The crew's story v3
cards wait.

## What was wrong (read from the code, measured in the real renderer)

- **Pops at every change of state.** The pose picks one branch a frame (standing, walking, in the air, swimming) and
  each wrote its own angles outright: the chest's twist and roll, the head's pitch, yaw and roll, the hip roll, the
  arms. The first frame of a walk went from the standing pose straight to the clip's; a jump put the head at a new
  pitch in one frame; the first frame of a dodge roll and the frame after it were two different men; a death started
  with straight legs. Only the lean, the side lean, the leg yaw and the body dip were eased.
- **Shift was a switch.** Cadence, stride, knee bend, lean, the gun's bounce and the arm offsets all flipped between
  the walk's and the run's values the frame Shift went down or up (only the clip blend eased).
- **A strafe reversal turned the chest in a frame.** The chest's counter-twist read the legs' target yaw, which flips
  when the velocity flips, while the legs themselves took a quarter second.
- **Frame-rate bound eases.** `x += (t - x) * Math.min(1, dt * r)` on the NVG flip, the idle and airborne arms, the
  hold weights, the hand roll, the carry arm, every recoil decay, the reload offsets, the water wade: slower at 30 Hz,
  faster at 144, a snap on a slow frame.
- **The start was a lurch.** The velocity's exponential approach has its hardest kick on the first frame.
- **His root snaps up every ledge, deck edge and stair** (STEP_UP) and the whole body and the camera's look point went
  with it in one frame.
- **The landing squash started part way up a fixed sine**: a short fall bent the knees 50 degrees on the landing frame.
- **Into water his body flipped 20 degrees in a frame**; the walk cycle's phase unwound to 0 while standing so every
  start led with the same foot.
- **The gun was welded to the chest**: no weight in it on a start, a stop, a landing or a turn.

## What changed (index.html)

- `_lerpK(rate, dt)` and `_decayK(rate, dt)`: the frame-rate free forms. Every ease above now uses them.
- `MOVE_FEEL` (the knobs) and `GUN_LAG`. `XFADE` and `poseXfade(key, dt)`: a post-process cross-fade on the final rig.
  On a change of state the last frame's joint angles, height and pitch are kept and faded out over the new state's
  length (standing 0.16 s, walking 0.16, the air 0.11, swimming 0.2, the roll 0.1, the death 0.12). Smoothstep. The
  states themselves are untouched; the death branch fades too.
- **The run blends**: `POSE.gaitRun` (rate 10) is the one weight for cadence, stride, knee base and swing, the body's
  constant dip, hip roll, lean, the gun's bob and roll and the arm offsets, the clip blend included.
- The chest's counter-twist and the head's follow read the eased leg yaw (`POSE.lowerYaw`), and the chest lags a fast
  turn a little on the move too (`turnLag * 0.1`, standing it was 0.15).
- **The wish**: `wishVX/Z` eases toward the wanted velocity at 22/s (40/s on a release, 22 in the air), and the
  velocity follows it at the old PHYS rates. A start is an S-curve (2.3 m/s at 80 ms, up to speed inside half a
  second), a reversal arcs through the stop, a release stops him as before (0.24 m/s left at 0.3 s). Anything that
  zeroes the velocity outright zeroes the wish; the roll sets both.
- **The step glide**: the part of a frame's root rise that the ground under him does not explain (a hillside is
  continuous: `sampleHeight` here minus `sampleHeight` at the frame's start) is taken off his body and given back at
  16/s (`POSE.stepGlide`; 2 cm to 60 cm count; teleports do not). The camera's look height (`camLookYS`) follows his
  root at 14/s instead of nodding with every step; the camera's own position already eased.
- **The landing squash** runs over its own length from 0 (`POSE.landDur`).
- `POSE.bodyPitch` eases his pitch into and out of the water (6/s). The cycle's phase stays on one turn and is kept
  where he stopped. The airborne and walking leg splay ease instead of snapping to 0.
- **The gun's inertia** (`gunLag`, `updateGunLag`): a damped spring (17 rad/s, 0.85) on his acceleration (2.2 mm per
  m/s^2) and his turn (4.5 mm per rad/s), capped at 3.5 cm, added to the hold's anchor in torso space (0.6 of it to a
  shouldered gun's pocket). The gun still aims from where it is at the reticle. A run's start lags it 2.8 cm back; it
  is at rest within a second of standing.
- `TT.smoothDbg` for the tests and the crew.
