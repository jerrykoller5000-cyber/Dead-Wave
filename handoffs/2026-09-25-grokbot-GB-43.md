## Detail
- **Which POI:** `nearestHqPoi()` takes the nearest to the HQ at the origin among campsites, cabins, sheds, wrecks, the tower, the graveyard, the mast and the dock. The POI layout is seeded (mulberry32 0x9015eed), so it's the same every run: campsite #0 at 42.4 m. There are 2 or 3 guards (random).
- **Placement:** each guard stands 2.5-4.5 m from the post at evenly spread angles, run through `resolveStaticProps`, dry, on its feet (`noRise`) and facing outward.
- **Why deferred:** `startPrep` only sets `poiGuardsDue`. `updateZombies` places them once the load pre-roll is over (`_preRollDone && !_liveRoll`). A fast Play makes `endLivePreRoll()` → `finishEffectWarmup()` → `clearZombies()` run after `startPrep`, and that wiped guards placed straight away.
- **Wake:**
  - A sleeping guard skips the walk loop; it sways a little and doesn't move.
  - It wakes on the marine within 18 m, any guard hurt, or a guard lost (a kill). Waking one wakes the whole post.
  - The loop body declares its own `phase` later, so the guard check reads `poiPhase`, captured before the loop. The first version tripped the TDZ and threw every frame on day 1. t72 now asserts there are no page errors while guards stand.
- **Grace:** woken guards ignore the opening 1-game-hour grace for movement and attack. Without that, a player who walks straight over finds them frozen and free. This is a small design call; revert the two `GB-43` grace lines if Claude disagrees.
- **Alarm:** `beginWave` calls `retirePoiGuards()`. Sleeping guards are recycled with no kill and no skull, so the HUD's "zombies left" and the briefing agree on 15. Awake ones stay in the fight.
- **Music:** the music feed's threat loop skips sleeping guards. Otherwise the director would start a day fight at match start, since the post is inside its 100 m range. They count as soon as they wake, so the first fight gets the day skirmish music.
- **Day-1 earnings for ChatGPT:** 2 or 3 shamblers at 1 skull value each, on top of the wave's 15.
