# GB-138 - The Watchman MG made real - Grokbot, 2026-10-06

From Jerry's playthrough 1: "Watchmen MG never got created." It was in the code (CU-67: blueprint $140, places for $80,
500 of 1,000 rounds, E to man it, T to shoulder it, t141), but on it you were really firing the rifle hidden in your
hands, its belt could never be refilled, and the game called it a mortar. Now it works as its own gun.

## Changed
What the player notices:
- **The rounds come out of the Watchman's own muzzle.** Before, they came out of the hidden rifle in his hands (he
  kneels about 1 m behind the gun), with that rifle's recoil. Now each round leaves the flash hider with the gun's own
  spread (M240_SPREAD 0.012), aimed at the reticle. Its own tripod no longer stops its rounds (`p.ignoreBuild`). Damage,
  rate of fire and range are unchanged (18 a round, 0.09 s, 0.35 s at 110 m/s).
- **A muzzle flash on the gun** (a short cone at the muzzle for 0.05 s plus the effect light). It uses the AK's report
  in place of the M4's, and the dead hear it like the minigun (heardShot, GB-105).
- **It has its own feed.** Once the Watchman plans are bought, the kiosk's Ammo tab sells **7.62 belt** (+300 for $48,
  cap 900): the existing loose `'7.62 belt'` reserve, which nothing used since the minigun went to belt boxes. Its shop
  line says it feeds the Watchman MG. **R on the gun changes the belt** (3.2 s, no firing during it), topping the belt
  up to 1,000 from the reserve. An empty belt starts the change on the next trigger pull if there are rounds in reserve.
  With none, you get BELT EMPTY and a line saying where to buy it. Leaving the gun mid-change cancels it and feeds nothing.
- **The HUD shows the belt while you're on it**: "Watchman MG", the belt count, "belt / 1000 · reserve Bullets",
  "Reloading" during a change. It goes back to your own gun when you get off.
- **Its own banners**: manning it said "MORTAR MANNED · … shells", and shouldering it said "Carrying the mortar". The
  Watchman now has its own (placeholder copy, keys below).
- **Brandt's roof M240** fires from its real muzzle when that line clears the HQ, as before otherwise, and flashes
  there. His damage, bursts and targets are unchanged.
