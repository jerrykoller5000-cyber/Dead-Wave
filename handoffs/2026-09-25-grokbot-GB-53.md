## Detail

### What was wrong
Before this, every night from 2 on was about 90% shamblers (the HORDE_MULT swell). The same sprinkle of specialists made up 9-12% of the horde every night, dealt out evenly. Night 12 felt like night 5, only longer: no set pieces, no rests and no build-up.

### What each night is now (the full table is in docs/specs/difficulty.md)
- **Teach**
  - Night 1: D-29's fifteen, unchanged.
  - Night 2: runners, the first ferals in two packs.
  - Night 3: the lake wakes (drowned) and the first leapers.
- **Build**
  - Night 4: Ember Night; two caves; the first soldiers; brutes in the last push.
  - Night 5: spiders, and a colossus.
  - Night 6: guardian night; the first spitters.
  - Night 7 (rest): forty claw up in the treeline; one pack of the first bombers.
  - Night 8: Ember Night; a bomber pack of ten; the first screamers.
  - Night 9: runners from two caves, eight packs of ten.
  - Night 10: brute night down one cave, and a colossus.
- **Test**
  - Night 11 (rest): surround; the first demons.
  - Night 12: the guardian on Ember Night, with demons.
  - Night 13: artillery (twenty spitters behind soldiers).
  - Night 14 (rest): the lake surge (seventy drowned) and sixty risers.
  - Night 15: the spider nest and a colossus.
  - Night 16: Ember Night, demon night.
  - Night 17 (rest): surround, the fast ones.
  - Night 18: guardian night, the siege (brutes behind a bomber screen).
  - Night 19: the gauntlet (three caves, the treeline, 3 s breathers).
  - Night 20: the last stand (Ember Night, a colossus, three caves, the treeline, the lake).
- **Past 20**, nights 13-20 come round again, on the old day curve.
