# CL-141: pink screamers and the missing flashlight in the Training Ground (Claude, 2026-10-09)

Jerry: "1. Sometimes screamers look pink when spawned in training ground. 2. Player does not actually start with
flashlight during training ground, it's says to buy it from gear menu but is absent from there."

## 1. Pink screamers (index.html, updateNightRims)

Not the spawn: the night rim (CL-83). In the dark it lights every special kind's whole body in its own eye colour
(`bodyMats` emissive, 0.14 at full night, 6 to 60 m). The screamer's eyes are hot pink (0xf373ce), so its whole body
turned pink. "Sometimes" is the Training Ground's light switch: lights off, the room is midnight (CU-94). Measured
on four screamers 12 m away: lights on, emissive 0; lights off, `f373ce @ 0.14`. The same happened outside at night
to brutes (red), spitters (green) and the rest; the screamer just shows it most.

Now the rim is moonlight (0x9fb2cf) with a fifth of the eye colour in it (`nightRimHex`, cached per colour), same
strength. Measured after: lights off, `b4a8cf @ 0.14` (cold grey-lavender). The dead still stand out in the dark and
keep a hint of their kind's colour, but nothing changes colour. `NIGHT_RIM_EYE` (0.2) is the knob if Jerry wants
more or less of the eye colour back.

## 2. The flashlight (index.html, enterTraining)

D-68 made the gun light standard issue and took it off the kiosk (the shop skips it; the strings say "every gun
starts with a flashlight"). enterTraining still had GP-154's `gearOwned.flashlight = false`, so in the Training
Ground key 2 said "FLASHLIGHT NOT OWNED · buy it at the kiosk", for something the kiosk no longer sells. It is now
`true`. Checked in the game: on entering, owned; key 2 turns the light on (intensity 6 lit, 42 in the dark), again
turns it off; no banner.
