## How

**Which deaths get a body.** `beginCorpse` adopts the body (`corpseBodyFor`) before it lets go of `z.mesh`. A crawler, a window climber, one still rising, or bodies switched off get none, and a body the zombie already had is released.

**The kill.**
- `corpsePush` turns the death into m/s the same way a hit is turned (`motionPower`, before armour).
- For a shell, it adds the pellets of the same shot that are still flying into the body: a 3 m ray from each unspent pellet of that shot id against the body's cylinder. Those pellets still fly on to whatever is behind, exactly as before.
- `corpseThrow` then calls `body.kill` at the hit height along the hit, with at least 1.5 m/s.
- Under 5 m/s it uses the hit's own kind (bullet, pellet, blade), so a rifle round in the back folds it forward.
- From 5 m/s it uses `blast`. A limp body hit as a pellet only folded where it stood, 0.8-0.9 m back even at 1.3-1.6× the power. As a small blast (the whole body, a little up) it goes 1.2-1.8 m at 6.5 m/s.
- If the pool is full of mid-reaction bodies, `kill` refuses and the topple plays (`killRefused`).

**While it falls.**
- `stepCorpseBody` runs follow, update, apply each frame, and moves the corpse by the body's drift through `resolveHardBuildCollisions` (`corpseMove`).
- It freezes on the body's `settled` event (points under 0.08 m/s for 0.5 s), if the pool puts it to sleep, or at 4 s at the latest. In t91 they settle on their own.
- `freezeCorpse` computes the matrices once, then sets `matrixAutoUpdate = false` on every joint and mesh under the corpse (marked `_corpseFrozen`). The root stays live so the corpse can still sink away at the end.
- `finishCorpse` makes those joints live again, and gives the body back, before `resetZombieMesh` returns the mesh to the pool.

**The throw into the one behind.** While a thrown corpse (5 m/s or more) is still moving (over 0.4 m/s, in its first 1.5 s), the first live small zombie within its radius + 0.45 m, and not behind the throw, gets `reactToHit(…, 'crush', 0 damage, push 120)`. That is the trample's 6 m/s crush: its body falls, or with no body `maybeKnockDown`'s crush gives `knockT` 1.2 s. It happens once per corpse.

**Today's topple.** With no body, a throw of 5 m/s or more slides the corpse along the hit for 0.3 s, slowing linearly: 1 m at 5 m/s, 1.6 m at 6.5, capped at 2 m. The same shove check applies. The topple freezes once it has landed and stopped bouncing (0.4 s after the fall).

**t91 checks:**
- An AK round in the back of a shambler facing him:
  - it dies on its body;
  - it settles on its own (the body's settled event, not the 4 s cap) within 4 s, with the pelvis within 0.35 m of the ground;
  - its head ends past its hips along the shot;
  - its joints are frozen and the body sleeps;
  - the slot is back;
  - after clearing, the mesh goes back to the pool with live joints.
- A close shell kill:
  - the body takes it at 5 m/s or more;
  - it ends 1-2.6 m back (the pelvis), away from him;
  - the zombie behind is knocked down by that corpse, checked 150 ms after contact, because a body that went down is up again about 2.2 s later.
- Killed by the first pellet, it is still thrown by the whole shell.
- An AK kill throws nobody.
- With bodies off: the topple slides 1-2 m, the zombie behind gets `knockT`, and the corpse freezes.

**Notes.**
- A weak rifle kill from the front can still fold forward, the way a shambler leans. A hard one throws it back. If Jerry wants every frontal kill to go over backwards, that's preset tuning (the zombie presets' death tone and the bullet kind's spread), GB-70 territory.
- A thrown body isn't stopped by walls in its own physics. The corpse's position is (`resolveHardBuildCollisions`), but a limb can end up through a thin wall.
