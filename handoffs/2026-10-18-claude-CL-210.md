# claude — CL-210 The marine and the survivors inside the frame budget — 2026-10-18

Changed:          **The marine and the survivors draw as a few skinned meshes** (new studio/marine-skin.js), the way
                  CL-138 did for the dead. Their rigid parts are baked into SkinnedMeshes whose bones are the joint
                  groups the pose code already turns:
                  - The plain parts go into one mesh, with their colour, roughness and metalness per vertex (a node
                    material reads them).
                  - The camo and detail-mapped parts get one mesh per material, drawn with that same material, so a
                    camo change shows at once.
                  Nothing else in the game has to change:
                  - The source meshes stay where they were and keep their own `visible`; they are only taken off the
                    render layers.
                  - Each frame, `syncRigSkins()` (in renderFrame) checks every part for a change: shown or hidden,
                    material, colour, its place on its joint, its geometry. Any change bakes him again.
                  - A part that keeps changing (three bakes in three seconds) is handed back and draws itself. Today
                    that is only the gloved hands, which the grip pose changes.
                  - What hangs on the gun mounts is never baked.
                  The survivors also merge parts that share textures (their cap, pack and carrier), and their live
                  cloth is baked while they stand still.
                  - **Marine:** fresh 76 meshes → 25. In the game at the HQ, about 100 → 52: 32 on him and 20 on the
                    guns he carries, which come under the gun budget.
                  - **Survivors:** Okafor 101 → 14, Brandt 127 → 16, Pike about 100 → 13.
                  - **The HQ frame:** 565-607 draws, down from 649 on 10-15.
                  `?rigskin=0` turns it off, for measuring.
Files:            studio/marine-skin.js (new), index.html (makeMarine bakes at the end and takes `skin: false`;
                  makeSurvivorFigure bakes with `mergeTextured` and `bakeLive`; renderFrame calls syncRigSkins;
                  TT.rigSkinDbg), studio/inspector/subjects.js (the counts skip meshes taken off the layers),
                  docs/budgets.md (the new numbers and how it works).
Tests:            The suite's fake three has no SkinnedMesh, so the bake does nothing there and the checks see the rig
                  as before:
                  - Pass: t36 22/0, t123 6/0, t256 10/0, t258 4/0, tinspect 22/0.
                  - t1 is still red for GB-187's reasons.
                  In real three (the cloud, WebGL2 through SwiftShader):
                  - Inspector shots, old and baked side by side, for the fresh marine, Okafor and Brandt standing,
                    and for the marine and Pike posed (an arm raised, a knee bent, the head and chest turned). Each
                    pair is identical.
                  - A match through the insertion: no errors. After a walk the marine had baked 9 times in all and
                    no more, so nothing kept changing but the hands.
Not verified:     On Jerry's GPU, and in WebGPU proper (the cloud runs the WebGL2 back end). Frame time is AG-82. The
                  burial: the diggers' cloth should hand itself back as they dig (the rule above), but I have not
                  watched it.
Contract changes: None. A mesh on a baked rig with `layers.mask === 0` is drawn through `rig-skin` meshes. Code that
                  needs a part drawn on its own sets `userData.noRigSkin` before makeMarine returns.
