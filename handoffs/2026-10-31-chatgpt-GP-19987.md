## Quest design answers (before implementation; rule 17)

- **Why does this quest exist?** The day's new lead names a concrete problem in the valley. Its interactive mission is available when that lead opens at dawn under `game/story-chain.js`.
- **Why should the player care?** A mission's action and reward should follow the lead they have received. An accidental early fuel fill must not advance or spoil the generator mission.
- **How does it connect?** The existing lead-to-site mapping connects the generator, relay, medical convoy, missing hikers, truck, ranger cache and trapper path to the story chain. The READY briefing is the place to review a tracked lead before setting out.
- **What changes afterward?** The completed action advances its site and can complete its lead. Before opening, the site's state and save record stay untouched; the player can inspect the world prop but cannot fill, jack, cut, open, take or claim.
- **What makes it memorable?** The cause is presented on the Branch's briefing before the player can act in the world; the mission prop becomes useful when its lead is issued, instead of silently consuming an action early.
