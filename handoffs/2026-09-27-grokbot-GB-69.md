## How

`laserSteadies()` is true when all of these hold:
- `lasersEnabled`;
- `gearOwned.laser`;
- the current weapon isn't melee;
- `weaponMeshes[currentWeapon].userData.laser` exists (only the chainsaw has `null`).

This is the "switched on and on this gun" part of the beam's own condition in `updateLasers`. It leaves out the beam's visibility gates (reloading, swimming, the mortar, place mode), because no gun fires in those anyway.

The factor goes on `cone` only, after all the other multipliers. Climb and side use their own terms and are untouched.
