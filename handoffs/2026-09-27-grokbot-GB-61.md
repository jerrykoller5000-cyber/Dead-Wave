## GB-58 follow-up: t80 (8) and his rounds through walls

- **Symptom.** After GB-61, t80 check (8), "three wall pieces between them stop his rounds", failed 2 runs in 3 with 25 to 49 damage. It had passed before.
- **Not GB-61.** The zip only runs from day 2, and t80 is day 1.
- **Cause (logged with a temporary hit log, now removed).** Every round that hit came from a muzzle 3 to 4 m south of him, past the end of the walls, and flew round them. His gun's mount had a scale of (1, 6.73, 6.73). In tools/tests/fakethree.mjs, `Matrix4.makeBasis()` is a no-op and `getWorldScale` returns the local scale. The two-hand hold builds the gun's rotation with `_hm.a.makeBasis(x, y, f)`, so headless it reads last frame's mount matrix and the scale drifts. It depends on the rig's history, so it comes and goes, and once drifted it stayed off 15 s or more. Real three.js is fine. The request to Claude (fakethree's owner) is sent.
- **t80 while that waits.** shootAt holds the trigger only while his muzzle is within 2.5 m of him. Check (8) now asks for 40 ticks of fire (up to 3 s extra), after waiting up to 10 s for his gun to be with him. When the rig is off, the failure says so and prints the rig state. That is not a weaker check: the same rounds hit the same walls, and a rig that never settles fails.
- **A real bug found on the way (fixed).** updateProjectiles ran the zombie pass before the structure pass. dt is capped at 0.05 s, so an AK round can step 5 m in one frame and a sniper round 6.5 m. On a slow frame, a step that crossed his wall and a zombie within that distance behind it hit the zombie. Now his pieces (not railings or wire) and the house are found first, and a body only counts if the round reaches it before them. This covers plain and piercing rounds, and turret rounds still pass his pieces. The build loop now runs once per round per frame, not twice.
- **New t80 (9).** One round stepped 13 m in a single call, from 2 m in front of him through the wall line to the spider. Plain and piercing rounds are both stopped. A control round from past the wall hits.
- **Other t80 flakes seen under 4 parallel copies:** check (6) (tight 25 to 36% against the < 25% limit) and once check (1). These are GB-56 AI timing under load, and they pass alone and in the full suite. They are not changed here, and they are on my list.
