Jerry: "Major problem right now. When playing the regular game, The player is getting attacked by invisible zombies and
the blood stains on the ground pop in and out."

## Why (both mine, CL-140)
The cull hides a top-level object that is out of view and too far to cast a shadow into it, by laying an accessor over
its `visible` that answers false. Things whose visibility is game state are left alone through a skip list.
1. **Invisible zombies.** Zombies, the dead, builds and gibs were put on the skip list once a second (every 60
   frames). Anything in the scene but not on the list in that second could be hidden, and a skip-listed object was
   never looked at again, so a hide laid on it before it was listed stayed on for good. Zombie meshes are pooled and
   warmed ahead (CL-140): a mesh hidden while it sat in the pool, or a zombie that rose far off and out of view, came
   back as a zombie the cull kept invisible while it walked up and hit him.
2. **Blood stains.** The stains are one instanced mesh with culling turned off by its owner. The cull measured its
   reach once from its bounding box, which three.js keeps from the first time it is asked: at load, when every stain
   slot is empty at the world's middle. So the cull saw a 0.5 m ball at (0, 0) and hid every stain at once whenever
   that point was out of view (or, past 80 m, always), and showed them again when it came back in.

## Fix
- Zombies, the dead, builds and gibs are listed every frame (`cullLiveSet`, a few hundred set inserts).
- Anything listed, pooled, flagged, an instanced mesh, or set `frustumCulled = false` by its owner is never hidden,
  and if the cull had hidden it before, it is shown again on the spot.
