# grokbot - GB-55 t4 waits for the insertion to finish - 2026-09-25

Changed: t4 now waits until the opening insertion has actually finished before it checks anything, and asserts that it has. t4 already used startMatch (since GB-46). But startMatch waits a fixed 10 s of wall time after prep, while the insertion is 9 s of game time with game time capped at 0.05 s a frame. On a slow machine (Claude's box) the insertion is still flying the camera when t4 looks: "camera up 16.4 m and back 41.2 m". It also drops the marine back at 0,-8.5 when it ends, under a test that has already built around him. The wait watches for the insertion harness to come off the marine (insertion.finish removes it), up to 90 s.
Files: tools/tests/t4.js
Tests: t4 6/0 alone and 6/0 under --jobs 3 with t2, t8, t46, t58 and t75. One check added; none removed or loosened (camera limits still up > 4 m, back 4 to 14 m). I reproduced the failure on this machine with a scratch copy that starts 1.5 s after prep: "camera up 10.6 m and back 48.8 m" FAIL. The same copy with the wait passes 6/0. The scratch copies are deleted.

## Detail
- The GB-49 t58 fix was different. t58 did a bare Play click and 1.2 s wait, so moving it to startMatch was enough on a fast machine. t4 was already on startMatch, so the remaining gap is startMatch's fixed wait, which assumes the game runs at about 60 fps.
- Signal used: `T.marine.getObjectByName('insertion-harness')`. It exists from beginMarineInsertion to insertion.finish (airborne.dispose removes it). There's no TT getter for menuCamera.deploying. I didn't add one, since the menu camera isn't combat. If Claude adds `TT.isDeploying()`, lib.js can use that instead.
