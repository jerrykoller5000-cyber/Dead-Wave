# CL-118 · The valley stays outside · Claude Fable 5.1 · 2026-10-02

Jerry, up after the overnight performance pass: "a bunch of environmental effects still present in the training ground, particularly the lightning", and "still worth chasing: the terrain's 530k triangles, the trees, the shadow pass, the High preset". ChatGPT has the room's audio in GP-137; this is the sim and the terrain only.

## What was wrong

The Training Ground is indoors, 640 m past the map's edge, but `updateWorldAnimations` ran the whole valley there every frame. `enterTraining` stopped the rain once, and then the shower timer started a new one: with it came the lightning (the flash on the screen, the thunder, and a strike that does 70), the wind and its sound, the birds, the dust motes, the falling leaves, the lake ripples, the clouds, the tree sway, the minimap draw.

## What changed (index.html only)

1. **The room's own frame.** `updateWorldAnimations` branches at the top: when `training.active`, `updateWorldAnimationsIndoors(dt)` runs instead. It keeps what a fight or a build in there uses — the flamer's stream, the banner, the HQ hatches (the room's copies follow them), acid pools, build bars, doors, spike traps, fire pools, the shadow LOD, the sky dome's position, tree fade/fall bookkeeping — and holds the storm off: `weather.raining = false` and `weather.timer ≥ 8` every frame. `updateWeather` and `updateLightning` still run, so a shower or a bolt already in the air winds down (the rain sound stops with the intensity) and nothing new starts. The leaves and tree batches before `tick` are skipped in the room too.
2. **The terrain in wedges.** One 266k-triangle mesh became 60 wedges (12 around × 5 bands) that share `ground.geometry`'s attributes: every edit of the surface (the shovel, a site levelled, the land's tint, `computeVertexNormals`) still goes through `ground.geometry` and shows in all of them. Each wedge has its own index and bounds, so the renderer culls the ones out of view. The outer band (past 222 m, beyond the land) uses every second ring and column — a quarter of its triangles — with a strip of half-steps along the seam so it stays watertight. `ground` itself stays in the scene with `visible = false`, the geometry's keeper; the wedges have an empty `raycast` so a ray still asks `ground`. Measured at the HQ in prep: terrain triangles drawn 266k → 132k. The shot of the valley is pixel-identical to the one mesh apart from the HUD counters.
3. **`trainingWarmCompile`'s cap** went from 4 s to 10 s (ChatGPT asked for it gone; without a cap a hung compile freezes the game for good, and a real GPU compiles the room in well under a second, so it only ever fires as a last resort).

## Looked at and left

- Trees: Cursor's batching (TB) already merges every tree beyond 52 m into cells and keeps the near ones live; the shadow LOD (`updateShadowLOD`) already limits casters to 42 m. The armory rack is already flattened per material per gun (`rackGunCopy`); its ~95 draws are 11 guns × their materials and the pegs. Nothing cheap left there.
- Bloom already renders at half resolution inside three's BloomNode. The High preset is full-scale with 4× MSAA at up to 1.5× pixel ratio; that is a choice, not a bug — Medium (0.65 scale, no MSAA, 1024 shadows) is the fallback if it still hitches on his GPU.
