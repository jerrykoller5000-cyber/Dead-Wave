`platformHeightAt` loops over every deck with a `cos` and a `sin` each, no early-out, so it costs per deck (about 45 ns each: 1.2 µs over 27 decks) and the cellar added 27. The new decks come from `game/trapper-path.js:1273` (the cellar's six decks and the roof cap's patch decks, all pushed into `platforms`); the other `platforms.push` sites are the same in both trees.

Sum, roughly: 1.1 to 1.2 µs more per lookup × the 3 to 4 lookups a body makes in the grown stages × 48 bodies ≈ 0.16 to 0.23 ms a frame, which is the three stages above. `resolveStaticProps` is called for every body each frame and reads a grid that is rebuilt from trees, rocks and worldSolids every 12 frames (`propsNear`); 170 more `worldSolids` makes both the rebuild and the lookups dearer (about the +0.03 of the barricades stage). `platformHeightAt` is also called by the marine, projectiles, drops and effects (anything on the ground), which is outside these stages and was not counted here: the other marks that moved (r:cull +0.13, `drops+hud`, `w:banner..normals`, `r:render` +0.27) are not explained by this.
