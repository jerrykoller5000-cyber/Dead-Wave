# GB-171 Frag grenade rebuilt M67-style (Grokbot)

Jerry: "this grenade could use some work".

Changed:
- studio/frag-grenade.js (new): `makeFragGrenade({ THREE, r, tall })`. Smooth lathed body 1.12x taller than wide, 28 segments. Paint is in vertex colours: olive drab with broad tone patches, a faint yellow marking band, a dark equator seam, a few scuffs. Fuse is one lathe (collar, 3 thread ridges, head, cap) plus a striker block. Spoon is a bent strip that follows the body skin from over the fuse down the +x side, narrowing, with the end turned in. Safety pin through the block, pull ring (torus), safety clip on the spoon. Named meshes: body, fuse (fuse-block child), spoon (clip child), pin, ring; also in `userData.parts`. 3 shared materials per THREE. Built at unit radius in an inner scaled group, so the caller's origin/pivot and scale are untouched.
- index.html:
  - import;
  - in-hand grenade (grenadeHand) = makeFragGrenade r 0.05 (same origin, spoon side +x, grenadeHand.userData.parts);
  - thrown grenade = makeFragGrenade r 0.11;
  - Bug fixed: the startup pool warm-up filled the 'grenade', 'shell' and 'mortar' projectile pools with a placeholder 6x6 ball. Every throw reused it, so a thrown grenade (and a mortar round) never showed its real model. The warm-up now uses the real builders for grenade and mortar; shell is no longer prewarmed (its builder is inline at its one use).
- studio/frag-grenade.test.mjs (new): named parts, in-hand bounds near the old ones (w/d 0.085-0.13, h 0.11-0.17, bottom ~-0.056, centre near origin), thrown scales with r, spoon stays on/just outside the skin.
