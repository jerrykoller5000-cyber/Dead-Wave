# grokbot - GB-90 (R5, P-64): survivor bounties - 2026-09-30

Changed:
- **Survivors at camps.** From night 3, each campsite bounty can hold a survivor. The chance is 0.5 (`survivorChance`), and it happens at most once per camp per run: a camp that has held one, rescued or not, never does again.
- **Where they wait:** 1.5 m from the fire, facing it.
- **The look is a stand-in:** a bare `makeMarine()` (bare head and torso, no gear, no gun) until CL-75 swaps the look in.
- **Nothing can hurt them.** They're scenery, in no zombie, hit or target list, so nothing targets or damages them.
- **Pressing E:**
  - `actionTarget()` gives `'survivor'` only within 2.2 m and once none of the post's guards is alive. Before that, E does nothing and no prompt shows.
  - After that, E takes them in: the figure goes, `survivor-rescued` `{ style, camp, day, count }` fires, and they're added to `getSurvivors()`.
- **Ending a wait:** the alarm or the next day's posts end a wait still at the fire (`survivor-lost`, reason `'alarm'` or `'new-day'`).
- A run reset clears the survivors and the used camps.
- **Data:**
  - `getBounties()` rows gain `survivor: { style, state } | null`.
  - `bounty-posted` carries `survivor: true|false`.
  - TT: `getSurvivors`, `survivorDbg`.
- **Prompt:** it uses the key `survivor.rescue`, placeholder copy "E — Take the survivor in". I sent ChatGPT a request in handoffs/requests.md for the real copy.

What the player notices:
- About half the camp bounties from night 3 have a person sitting by the fire.
- Clear the guards, walk up, press E, and they're with you. Nothing else changes yet: their help comes in GB-91, the board line in P-66, and names on the victory screen in GP-69.
