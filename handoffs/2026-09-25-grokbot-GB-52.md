# grokbot - GB-52 The knife and the chainsaw toned down - 2026-09-25
Changed: Blades and the saw now do less to the bigger kinds (a size multiplier on every blade and saw hit: military and spider 0.8, brute 0.6, demon 0.5, colossus and guardian 0.35; the rest 1.0). The knife recovers more slowly (0.42 -> 0.55 s) and so does the machete (0.40 -> 0.50 s), and the machete now bites at most 3 per swing (was every body in its half circle). The saw does 14 a tick (was 17) in a narrower arc (minDot -0.05 -> 0.20, about 93 -> 78 degrees each side), bites at most 3 bodies a tick (was all of them), and its gas and heat matter: 2.5 s of gas a second while cutting (1 s running free), and 8 s flat out in bodies overheats it, stalling it until it has cooled (about 1.7 s). Its shredding no longer takes the head off anything in about a second whatever its health: a part comes off per 18 % of health taken, the head only below 35 %.
