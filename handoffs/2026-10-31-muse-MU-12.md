## 1. How placement works today (read this first)

Two layers decide where everything is, and CL-285's move touches only the first:

- **POI layer (seeded, deterministic).** `planPOIs` (index.html:3634) rolls every POI from `mulberry32(0x9015eed)` (index.html:3635) via `findSpot(rMin, rMax, maxSlope, sep)`, in a fixed order, each accepted spot pushing to `taken` and constraining the next. Campsites: 3 × `findSpot(40, 115, 0.22, 42)` (index.html:3658) — so all three camps currently land **40–115 m** from origin. Cabins 60–160 m, tower 90–170 m (then overridden to `{x:-25, z:-125}`, index.html:3710), graveyard 70–150 m, sheds 3 × `findSpot(45, 130, …)` (index.html:3693), wrecks 2 × `findSpot(50, 140, …)` (index.html:3699), mast `findSpot(80, 165, 0.22, 40)` (index.html:3704). **Consequence for the move:** changing the campsite range to 110–160 m re-rolls every POI after it in the sequence (cabins, tower, graveyard, dock, bridges, sheds, wrecks, mast all shift), unless the camps are moved by a post-roll override like the tower's (index.html:3708–3710). Rule 10 sign-off covers the seed; the override pattern is the low-blast-radius option and has precedent.
- **Site layer (anchored offsets).** The seven objective sites are *not* independent positions: `assets/world/objective-props.js:21` (`SITES`) anchors each to a POI plus a fixed offset, resolved by `objectiveSitesFrom(POI, height)` (objective-props.js:40). Moving an anchor moves its site, its approach point, its map marker and its mission dressing together — *except* where a mission re-derives its own spot from the camp (trapper yard, §2.7) or from another POI (truck cutters at the iron cave, §2.5; hikers' tree, §2.4; fuel pump/generator, §2.1). Camp index → style is fixed by build order: `buildCampsite` sets `variant = siteIndex % 3` and `style = ['ranger','hikers','trapper'][variant]` (assets/world/campsites.js:372; index.html:5641, 5652), so **campsites.0 = ranger, campsites.1 = hikers, campsites.2 = trapper** on every run. The site anchors follow the same indices (objective-props.js:21): ranger-cache→campsites.0, hikers-cache→campsites.1, trapper-cache→campsites.2, fuel-depot→sheds.2, wreck-salvage→wrecks.1, medical-convoy→wrecks.0, radio-repair→mast.

The chain itself: `game/story-chain.js:7` (`LEADS`) — generator→`objective:fuel-depot` (day 2), relay→`objective:radio-repair` (3), medical→`objective:medical-convoy` (4), missing→`objective:hikers-cache` (5), truck→`objective:wreck-salvage` (6, after missing), search→`objective:ranger-cache` (7, after missing), oldman→`objective:trapper-cache` (8, after search), below→`site: null` (9, after oldman). `siteOpen(site)` (story-chain.js:86): a site with no lead is always open; a lead's site opens once its lead is open *or done*.
