## Pricing decision

Only guns (except chainsaw), gear, magazine upgrades, pairs, machete and perks receive the night multiplier. Later quotes round upward to the next 5 Cash. The amount displayed is exactly what is charged and reported; prices reset with a fresh run. Example M4 prices: night1–3 160; night4 180; night10 275; night20+ 435. Minigun: 420 -> 465 -> 715 -> 1135 at those same stages.

Ammo, hand grenades (12 each), MedPens, repairs, construction/fortification plans and placement, Field Intel (120), and the agreed chainsaw package keep fixed prices. There is no extra inflation on repeated essentials, no direct Cash reward, and no hidden change to skull value. Preserve the satisfaction of valuable specialist kills; price permanent equipment against the richer waves instead of silently weakening those drops.

The starter-pistol/medicine reductions are grounded in the model: night3's upkeep drops from173 to107 against160 estimated banked Cash. Night7's rest-night upkeep drops from345 to315 against417, preserving a breather with improvement money. Late nights retain appreciable surplus for replacing a damaged base, buying additional equipment, or saving for stronger fortifications. The ramp is deliberately capped outside this task's twenty-night scope.
