## How

- `buildOnMyStorey(b, band)`: the piece's height span `[y, y + rise or pieceHeight]` overlaps `storeyBand()`, the same band `pickOnMyStorey` uses.
- `buildReachable(b)`: a segment from his chest (y + 0.9) to the piece's `edgeMid`, at a height clamped into the piece, must not be cut by any other build before t = 0.97 (`segmentHitsBuild`, as `objectiveReach` does). Railings and wire don't block, since you reach over them.
- `onMyStorey(b)` = both. `nearestOnMyStorey(lim, damagedOnly)` replaces nearest-in-2D out of build mode for T (`findRepairCandidate`) and X (`sellNearestBuild`, and the new `findScrapCandidate`).
- `scrapTarget()` (build mode: the red outline, and T/X on the ghost) now takes `pickOnMyStorey()` only if `onMyStorey`, then falls back to `topmostOnMyStorey(gx, gz)`. The old `|| pickBuildOnRay()` and `topmostInCell` fallbacks were what reached through a floor, so they are gone.
- `upgradeTarget` is not T or X, so it is deliberately unchanged.

**t6 re-base (acceptance says `--review` if re-based).** t6 used to ask, while standing on the ground, that aiming at the deck overhead targets the platform. That is exactly what P-4 forbids. The check now asks it standing on the deck (same aim, still expects `platform`). A new check asks that from the ground the same aim does *not* target the deck. He then goes back to the ground for the wall scrap and refund checks, which are unchanged. Nothing was deleted or weakened: 20 checks became 21.
