# GB-161: Training wear fixes: sign fit, locker collision and door, back-wall fade (Jerry, direct ask)

Grokbot, 2026-10-12. Status: review. This follows GB-159 and GB-160.

## Asked
Jerry sent three screenshots with three problems:
- The DANGER sign's text ran off its sides.
- The player walks through the lockers.
- With a high camera behind the range's back wall, the props hung on that wall float in view.

## Done
1. **Sign text fits** (world/training-wear.js):
   - Every printed sign line is measured and shrunk to fit inside a 20 px margin. A sign's lines share one size, and headers stay clear of the peeled corner. The DAYS WITHOUT INCIDENT board is fitted the same way.
   - `textTex` in world/training.js (BUILD ROOM, LIGHTS, NO BUILD ZONE and the other labels) now shrinks text to 90% of the canvas width too.
2. **Locker collision and the dented door:**
   - The locker bank is one blocking solid. world/training.js `keepOut(p, r)` pushes him out sideways or forward, never back into the wall, and index.html `trainingKeepPlayer` calls it.
   - The middle door is on a named hinge (`training-wear-locker-door`) with a damped spring (`WEAR.DOOR`: rest 0.5 rad, min 0.22, max 2.2). It settles back ajar and never shuts.
   - Three things set it swinging:
     - he walks into it (`bumpDoor`, called from `keepOut`)
     - a round hits it: it has its own `lockerDoor` solid that follows the swing; index.html `trainingRound` calls `tg.wear.hitDoor`
     - a blade: index.html `trainingMelee` calls `tg.wear.cutDoor`
   - Zombies never enter the range, so nothing changes for them.
3. **Back-wall fade** (world/training-cutaway.js, GP-197's file): the build-room shell (the infirmary's back wall included) already fades this way. I added a `rangeSouth` side: the range's back wall and everything on it (the lockers, the door by name, the monitor, the rack, the board). It fades to 0.06 when the camera-to-player line crosses z = -12, and the camera ignores those solids as it does for the other faded sides. Signs on the side walls are flat panels facing into the room, so they don't show from outside anyway.
4. world/training.js `setLights` now calls `wear.update(0, lit)`, so the flickering strip goes out at the same moment as the rest. GB-159 had left t219 failing on this; it passes now.
