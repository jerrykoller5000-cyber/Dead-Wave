Jerry: "Right now walking around the main game the framerate fluctuates like crazy and is not stable. Can you please
do a mega performance sweep of just walking around the map and trying to get a stable 60?"

## Where the time went, and what changed (all in index.html unless said)

1. **Every mesh tested twice a frame.** The renderer tests every visible mesh against the camera, then again against
   the sun for the shadow map: 2,245 visible meshes for ~500 drawn, a bird's eight parts and a rabbit's twenty each
   on their own. `sceneCull()` (in renderFrame) hides a top-level object whole when it is outside the view and too far
   to throw a shadow into it, and hides a small thing (under a metre) past sixty metres. It never touches an object's
   own `visible` (an accessor sits over it and remembers what the owner sets); trees, bugs, the dead, builds, rocks,
   effect pools and the sky are left alone, since their visibility is game state. `?cull=0` turns it off;
   `TT.cullDbg` counts. ~210 of ~270 top-level objects hidden on a typical frame, ~0.2 ms to decide.
2. **The marine's shadow: one draw, not eighty.** `core/skin-merge.js` (new, with `core/skin-merge.test.mjs`) merges a
   rig's parts into one SkinnedMesh whose bones are the parts themselves, so whatever moves, it follows exactly. The
   marine keeps drawing his own parts; a shadow-only merged copy casts for them, drawn by the sun's camera alone
   (three copies the view camera's layer mask onto the shadow camera, so the copy answers to the shadow camera's
   own Layers object instead). Rebuilt when his visible parts change and stay changed for a second (a hat, the
   sleeves, a magazine out), at most every two seconds, ~8 ms. Guns keep their own shadows. `?shadowproxy=0`.
3. **Wildlife, one draw each.** Rabbits (20 meshes), birds (10), fish, frogs, squirrels, turtles: `skinMergeGroup` at
   the end of each make*Mesh. Ears, tails and blinks still move (the parts are the bones). ~120 draws at the HQ.
4. **The flow field (the dead's pathing) resampled the whole map as he walked.** Every 0.4 s, and whenever its 160 m
   grid had moved a cell, all 16,384 cells were height-sampled again: 10 to 17 ms, every 24 frames, the steady
   stutter. The grid now slides: the cells still covered are copied across and only the new strip is sampled
   (~2.5 ms). Checked against fresh samples after a walk: 0 error. It is also not rebuilt while no zombie reads it (a
   guard asleep at its post does not), and the first one is built at match start, under the insertion.
5. **The gun held twice a frame.** `holdWeapon` (an arm solve and a matrix pass over the whole marine, 0.6 ms) ran
   twice every frame so a shot's recoil could be applied; now the second pass runs only on a frame that kicked.
6. **Bodies built mid-fight.** A zombie body is ~15 ms to build (CL-138's merge). The pool held one of each kind
   after the pre-roll, so the first wave built nine shamblers in combat. 26 bodies for the first nights are built
   behind the menu now, one a frame (`TT.zombiePoolDbg()`).
7. **Shaders compiled mid-walk.** Two pipelines, 30 to 60 ms each, the first time a tree stood between him and the
   camera (the canopy's see-through variant) and the first time a leaf fell. Both drawn once in the pre-roll now
   (`warmFrame('tree-fade')`).
8. **Ground cover past 150 m** no longer tested on High (it was unlimited; 283 chunks).
9. **`TT.profDbg`**: where a frame's time goes, phase by phase. `TT.profDbg.on = true`, play, `TT.profDbg.report()`
   gives the average and worst ms per phase and the worst frames with their top phases. ~35 marks through tick(),
   renderFrame and updateWorldAnimations; off, each is one test. It found everything above.

## Not done, for whoever is next

- **The marine's own draws (86 + 21 shadow)** are a quarter of a frame at the HQ. A full merge (parts drawn as
  skinned meshes per material, not only the shadow) would halve that, but the dressing room recolours his item
  materials and the hit flash paints them, so the merged copies would need rebuilding on every wardrobe change.
- **The HQ group: 132 draws.** CL-117 baked its still parts; what is left is hatches, racks and lettering.
- **A 90 to 140 ms hitch in `drawMinimap`, once a minute or so, only on the camera's hidden page** (the browser
  recreating the canvas, I think). Watch for it on a visible screen; if it shows, the bake could be drawn to the
  minimap at a lower rate, or as a texture.
- **The JS heap grows to ~1.1 GB on a long-lived page** (530 MB fresh). Something retains; major GCs then cost
  30 to 60 ms. Not chased.
- `npm test` not run (no shell on Jerry's machine). `core/skin-merge.test.mjs` passes (5). Any t-test that counts a
  rabbit's or the marine's meshes, or walks the scene's visible objects, may need its numbers looked at.
