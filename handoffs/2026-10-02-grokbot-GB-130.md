# GB-130 - Grokbot - per-weapon hand anchors, marine-proportional gun sizes, stowed guns refit (2026-10-02)

Jerry's task, with his three follow-ups: (1) every player gun true to the marine with the hands on each model's
real grip / support points, minigun untouched, GB-129 shouldering kept, muzzle on the crosshair; (2) the rifles,
shotguns and launcher read small, the Uzi reads right: use the Uzi as the reference, and refit every stowed gun
(hip, pack holsters, slings) snug, no floating, no air gaps, no clipping; (3) pistol, revolvers, AK, M4 still
tiny: judge by eye against the Uzi, quality over speed.

## Changed:
- index.html, WEAPON_HOLD: explicit gun-space anchors per model (grip = pistol-grip centre line 42% down,
  trig = trigger blade, fore = support point read off each model; pistol/revolver support hand cups the firing
  hand; shotgun on the pump with pumpDy; launcher/flamer on their front grips; chainsaw top of the wrap handle).
  hold/twist and sh.butt/sh.eye unchanged (gun space, so they follow the size). Minigun row unchanged.
  Uzi fore z 0.25 -> 0.18 and sniper fore z 0.40 -> 0.35 so the support arm reaches at the new sizes.
- index.html, holdWeapon: pump line reads H.pumpDy (default -0.045 as before).
- index.html, after `const weaponMeshes`: MARINE_UPM (1.655 u = 1.80 m), WEAPON_SIZE {len, m, look}
  (factor = m * look * UPM / len, applied once before the furniture/off-hand/carry copies are cloned, so
  akimbo, holsters, slings, armory and survivor copies follow), BLADE_SIZE (knife, machete; same formula).
  look = the by-eye multiplier against the Uzi (Uzi 1.0): the marine is stylised (gloves ~1.25x a real hand,
  broad armoured shoulders, chunky Uzi) so the slim guns read toy-sized at bare real length.
- index.html, CARRY_FIT + fitCarriedGun/fitCarriedGuns (same block) and ONE hook line at the end of
  applyCarryPlan: every stowed copy is sized to s x its in-hand size (s = 1 for all: the spots were built for the
  old oversized models at 0.68-0.85, a sling shrinking some again by CARRY_SLING_SCALE) and moved inside its spot
  by a measured offset ([+x side, -x side], spot units) so it lies snug. holsterBand = the pack-holster offset
  used while the shell bandolier or 40 mm belt crosses the pack. Minigun has no entry: its carry is untouched.
  buildCarryRig, placeSecondary, spots, shells, straps, bands: untouched (split agreed with ChatGPT).
- index.html, knife: KNIFE_REST_POS / mount z 0.04, KNIFE_SLIDE 0.2 so the handle stays in the left hand.
- index.html, updateLasers: beam length divides by the gun's world z scale; muzzle flash divides by sizeK.
- index.html, TT.getWeaponSizeDbg(): { upm, sizes, blades, carry, factors }.
- tools/tests/t189.js (new): the stowed guns (split out of t188 for the 180 s limit): in-hand size, gap and
  clipping per gun and spot, minigun carry untouched.
- tools/tests/t188.js (new): sizes (class range, look, model scale, akimbo copy, measured length = m x look),
  blades, anchors on the models, trigger reach, support anchors, hands (SKIP on the stub page), barrel to
  reticle, knife handle in hand.
