# CL-226: night 20's spikes are pipelines; bench --prof journals who each one is for

**AG-105's census (CL-223's bench, night 20 High, the 5080):** 53.5 fps median, 1 % low 25.7, tick 14.65 ms. Of
the ~30 frames over 25 ms a run, 21-26 built a render pipeline (`newPipelines: 1`): 22-48 ms in `r:render` and about
10 MB of heap each (the node builder). They come all night (frames 72-644 of a 30 s sample), so the load's warm-up
can't be the whole answer: something in the fight keeps making pipeline states, or keeps handing one back (three
releases a pipeline when its last user moves to another state) and building it again. GC is 1-2 frames a run.
docs/budgets.md, "2026-10-20, later".

**The tool.** `tools/bench.mjs`'s PROF_START wraps three's `_pipelines._getRenderPipeline` and `_releasePipeline`
while measuring: each pipeline built goes in a journal (frame, the object and three parents, its type, the material
type and name, transparent/side/depthWrite/blending/alphaTest, the camera: the scene's or another, and whether its
vertex or fragment shader was new); releases are counted. PROF_END groups them, and `prof.pipelines` reports `made`,
`released`, `withNewShaders`, the top 15 groups with their first frames, and the first 12 built.
