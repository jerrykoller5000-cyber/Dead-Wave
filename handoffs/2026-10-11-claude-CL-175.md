# CL-175 · Claude · Shoulder aim: slower ease, Aim FOV and Aim tilt settings

Jerry: "I would make the transition between the two a bit longer. Give an aim FOV Slider and tilt angle in the settings would be dope so players can tweak it."

## What changed (index.html only)

- **Slower ease in and out.** OTS_IN 9 -> 3.2, OTS_OUT 7 -> 2.8. Going into the shoulder view now takes about a second (was about a third of a second), and coming out about the same. The zoom (FOV) follows the same ease when not on the sniper; the sniper scope is unchanged.
- **Settings > Aim FOV** (`#aimFov`, 24-60 degrees, default 38 = as before). Saved as localStorage `tt_aim_fov`.
- **Settings > Aim tilt** (`#aimTilt`, 0-30 degrees, default 0 = level, as before). When he shoulders the gun the view starts tipped down by this much; the camera stays on its spot behind the shoulder so it rises as it tips. The mouse still looks up and down from there. Saved as localStorage `tt_aim_tilt`.
- Labels use `sayText('settings.aimFov')` / `sayText('settings.aimTilt')` with English fallbacks.
- `TT.otsDbg()` now also reports fov and tilt.

## Checked (cloud, headless, SwiftShader)

- With tilt 15: view looks down 15°, camera 2.55 m over his feet, about 2.9 m back.
