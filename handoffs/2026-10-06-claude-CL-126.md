# claude — CL-126 The walk and the run less goofy; heavy breathing after a run — 2026-10-06
Changed:          Jerry's playthrough 1: "Run and walk animation a little weird, he kinda bobs back and forth in a funny way right now. Legs and body rotation look goofy and a little rigid. Needs heavy breathing after running on idle."
                  - The wobble. CL-84's walk and run clips are a loose, free-armed gait. Over one run stride they rolled his head 17 degrees side to side, swung his chest 25 degrees, rocked his spine and hips, and lifted and dropped him 15 cm. With a rifle up, that read as a wobble. Now (GAIT_FEEL):
                    - the head takes a third of the clip's roll, turn and nod, so it stays level and on the aim;
                    - the chest turns a third as much with a gun up, and 80% unarmed, where the arms swing;
                    - the spine rocks at 40% and the hips at about two thirds;
                    - the forward lean keeps its average but only half its swing;
                    - the bob is three quarters.
                    Measured over a run stride on the test page, the head's side-to-side tilt is 6 degrees (was 15), its swing off the aim 2.5 (was 5), and its rise and fall 10 cm (was 13).
                  - The legs' turn. Walking sideways or at an angle, the legs turned up to 60 degrees off his aim, which made a strafe look like a twisted run. They now turn at most 49 degrees.
                  - Out of breath. Running winds him, filling over about 6 s of running; standing drains it over about 9 s, walking over about 14. Standing winded he breathes at twice the rate. His chest heaves, he leans in a little, his shoulders rise with each breath and his head nods. You hear each breath in and out (AudioSys.playerBreath: a quiet in through the mouth, a lower push out). Rested, nothing changes.
                  - GAIT.feel = false (TT.gaitDbg) gives CL-84's gait as it was, for comparing.
