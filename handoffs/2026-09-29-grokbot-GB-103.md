# GB-103 — R2 P-117: the horde fights the defences

Changed:
- A turret that fires on a zombie now brings that zombie, and every zombie within 6 m of it, after the turret for 8 s. The timer resets each time the turret shoots again. This covers the light, heavy and flame turrets (anything with a range).
- Brutes, demons, and one in four of the other wave spawns are now defence seekers. Each goes for the nearest turret within 45 m before the marine. With no turrets up, nothing changes.
- A zombie that came for the defences, whether drawn or a seeker, hits builds 1.5x harder. A shambler's blow on a turret is 9 instead of 6. Everyone else hits builds as before.
- What stays the same:
  - The marine is still the target for everyone else.
  - In reach he always wins: the attack check is unchanged.
  - A lure still pulls harder than a turret.
  - Bosses, bombers, spiders and screamers keep their own jobs, and sleeping POI guards and bounty posts aren't drawn.
- A turret the zombie can't reach is dropped. That means one where the straight walk crosses the cabin, or one it has made no headway on for 5 s while not fighting anything. A wall in the way gets fought, and that counts as headway. The zombie goes back to the marine on the flow field for 10 s. I added this after the first turret-base nightsim left 13 zombies stuck on night 13.
- What the player notices: turrets no longer win a night by themselves. Brutes and demons walk straight at the guns, whatever a gun shoots at comes for it with its pack, and turrets get broken and need repairs or covering fire.
