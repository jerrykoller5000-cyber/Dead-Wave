## Decisions I made
- Wall-clock safety caps of 90 s (or 20x the window) so a stuck page still fails rather than hanging.
- Corner tight-% samples once per 0.1 s of game time, not once per poll, so a busy PC does not inflate the ratio. Threshold stays `< 0.25`.
