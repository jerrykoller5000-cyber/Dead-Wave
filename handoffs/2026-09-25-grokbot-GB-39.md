## Detail
- The problem (audit GB-A1, S1): on day 1 the wave walks out of its cave, the player shoots at it, and a round that goes past or through a zombie into the mouth counted as a poke, so the guardian came out and killed him in the middle of the fight he was supposed to be having.
- **Round judged at the end.** `updateProjectiles` used to call `noteCaveMouthHit` the moment a player round crossed a mouth. Now it only stores `p.cavePokeCi`. Every removal path runs `endRoundPoke(p)`: life out, zombie hit, pierce spent, tree, landmark or solid, build, bug, wildlife, terrain and out of bounds. There were 12 splice sites; most splice directly, so all 12 needed it. `endRoundPoke` pokes only if `!p.hitZombie`. Both zombie damage paths (single hit and pierce) set `p.hitZombie = true`. A round that crosses a mouth and then hits rock or ground still pokes, as before.
- **Busy caves.** `caveBusyWithWave(ci)` is true only in the wave phase, in either of two cases:
  - The cave is an assault cave (in `wavePreview.caveIndices` or `caveByIndex`) and `waveSpawned < waveTotal`.
  - Any live zombie stands in the cave's mouth volume (`pointInCaveMouthShot`).
- **Where the guard runs.** `noteCaveMouthHit` checks it before logging a hit, so explosives are covered too. `triggerCavePoke` also checks it, so every path is guarded.
- **Prep is unchanged.** A clean miss into an empty mouth still brings the guardian (D-26 as built by GB-35). GB-44 will turn the first poke into a warning.
- **t69 checks:**
  - A round that damages a shambler in a free cave's mouth doesn't poke.
  - A clean miss into the same mouth does.
  - Mid-spawn, the assault cave is busy, and a bullet or explosive note returns false and leaves the cave unused.
  - A different empty cave is still pokeable mid-wave.
  - Once the wave is all out and the mouth is clear, the assault cave is not busy. One zombie in the mouth makes it busy again. With the mouth cleared, it pokes normally.
- **t69 diagnostics.** The miss check prints the positions and the poke state when it fails. That doesn't change what it asserts.
- **Numbering:** t69 was free (checked tools/tests and the LOG).
