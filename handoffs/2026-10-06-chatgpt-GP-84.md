## Integration notes

- UI polls the owners' snapshots at most five times per second; it never advances the battery, infers clearance, grants rewards, or changes world/combat logic. Text nodes change only when their value changes.
- Battery displays remaining whole seconds; stir includes a textual warning/countdown and meter. Existing guardian escape prompts retain ownership of the kick-free instructions.
- Pickup receipts queue for reading instead of replacing a tag immediately. Tags show only stamped identity and the story-bible line, without a reward counter. Reset/leave clears transient notices; duplicate receipts are ignored within a run.
- Strongboxes show their actual prize plus the learned symbol/place. Unknown glyphs remain unknown; no access to the secret word.
- The new HUD sits in the existing notice stack so interaction prompts cannot cover it. Surface objective cards hide while that underground panel is visible; the HQ board retains its normal scroll and keyboard behavior.
