## Detail

### What a day's bounty does
- **Posting.** `startPrep()` sets `bountiesDue` from night 2. `updateZombies` places the guards once prep is really on: after the load pre-roll, not while the alarm sequence runs, and not while a card has the game paused. So Next Night straight from the Night Complete card posts nothing that day, and "yesterday's" then counts as none.
- **Picking.** The candidates are every campsite, cabin, shed and wreck, plus the tower, the graveyard and the mast. The rules exclude the POI nearest the HQ (day 1's post), yesterday's posts, the dock (its post is on the water) and anything standing in water. Posts are picked at random, and a second post has to be at least 25 m from the first.
- **Guards.** `placePostGuards` (split out of GB-43's `spawnPoiGuards`, which now uses it with 2-3 shamblers as before) puts them in a ring 2.5-5 m round the post, asleep and facing out. The first guard is the specialist on nights 8 and later.
- **Waking.** `wakePoiGuards(reason, post)` wakes only that post's guards on "near" (18 m) or "hit". With no post given (the alarm on day 1), it still wakes every guard, as before.
- **Clearing.** In `killZombie`, right after the post's `poi-cleared`, `settleBounty` pays out. The bag grows by one skull worth the reward, a `skull-pickup` {count 1, value, carriedCount, carriedValue, bounty: true} goes out, the pickup sound plays, and then `bounty-done` {kind, index, reward}. The guards' own kills are untouched (normal skulls, pooled below 8 as usual from day 2).
- **Ending.** `expireBounties` runs on my own listener for `alarm-started` (so I did not edit hqStartWave), and again in `beginWave` for waves started without the alarm. Every guard of an open post goes, and `bounty-expired` {kind, index, reward, reason} is sent. There is no `poi-cleared` and no reward, and the wave keeps its plan's total.
- **Run reset.** `run-reset` empties the list and "yesterday's".

|---|---|---|---|
| 2-3 | 1 | 3-4 shamblers | 25 |
| 4-7 | 1 | 4-5 shamblers | 60 |
| 8-13 | 2 | 5-6, one a brute | 150 |

Two posts from night 8 means up to 300 skulls a day on nights 8-13 (GP-41's night 10 pays about 1,500) and up to 600 from night 14 (night 20 pays about 3,800): 16-20% of the night, at the top of D-38's 8-20%. The number of posts is my choice (the board says "one or two"); one post all the way would halve that.
