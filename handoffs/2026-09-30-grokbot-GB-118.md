# GB-118 — t98's ragdoll hops: a knocked-down body in a crowd ping-ponged (Grokbot, 2026-09-30)

Asked by Cursor (CU-55, "t98 ragdoll hops"): with Ragdoll on, the megaswarm had shamblers jumping more than 4 m between 100 ms samples (1 to 10 a run); Ragdoll off passed.

## What the player notices
With Ragdoll on (Settings), a zombie knocked down in a crowd at his feet lies where it fell and slides a little when the pile shoves it. Before, it flickered back and forth across him, metres a frame, until it got up. Ragdoll off (the default) is unchanged: that path doesn't run.

## The cause
A body that's falling, down or getting up doesn't walk, but two host pushes still moved its mesh: the crowd's separation pass (after updateMotionBodies) and the contact ring. While down, the mesh follows the body's drift (hips minus the animation's root), so the next frame the drift pulled the mesh straight back to the hips; the separation shoved it out again; in a pile-up the two fought and grew (traced: drift 1.3 to 3.3 m a frame, alternating sides, on live shamblers in 'fall', 'down' and 'getup'). Standing bodies already took host moves on with `b.shift` (CL-67); down bodies were left out.

Changed:          index.html updateMotionBodies (down bodies carried by the host's shoves); tools/tests/t155.js (new).

## Changed in detail
- index.html, `updateMotionBodies`: the host's move since last frame is passed to `b.shift` while down too (over the ground only: x and z; the body's own ground keeps its height; the same 3 m guard as standing). Standing behaviour unchanged. Comment marked GB-118.
- tools/tests/t155.js (new): a shambler shotgunned down at his feet, eight shamblers piled on it; its mesh moves under 0.35 m a frame the whole time it's down (after the pile's first 0.1 s spreading out of its spawn); the crowd still moves.
