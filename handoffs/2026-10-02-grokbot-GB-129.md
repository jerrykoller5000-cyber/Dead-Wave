Grokbot, 2026-10-02, ~04:50 CT.

## Changed:
- Long guns (m4, ak, aa12, shotgun, sniper, launcher) are shouldered now. Before, the hold placed the pistol grip
  28 cm in front of the shoulder and the butt ended up where the gun's length put it: 3-6 cm outside and 4-7 cm
  behind the gun-side shoulder joint, inside the arm/back (box: /workspace/shoulder-shots/diagnosis-before.txt).
- New in holdWeapon (index.html, after solveArmIK): SHOULDER_HOLD + holdShoulderGun():
  - The butt-plate centre (per gun, WEAPON_HOLD[gun].sh.butt) is put on the shoulder pocket, torso space
    (0.20, 0.33, 0.17): just inside the shoulder joint, on the vest. The gun pivots about the butt.
  - Barrel exactly on the aim: the gun is pointed from where its MUZZLE is at aimTarget (refined twice), not from
    the grip. Measured 0.00-0.01 deg off (was 0.8-1.0).
  - Firing hand IK onto the grip, support hand onto the forend as before (pump on the shotgun, reload path to the
    pouch unchanged). A reach guard slides the gun back along the barrel if a pose asks for more than 0.54 m.
  - The marine's arms (0.567 m shoulder to palm) are too short to reach the grips with the butt in front of the
    shoulder, so while a long gun is shouldered both shoulder groups roll forward (armRG +10 cm fwd, 4 cm in,
    2 cm down; armLG +6 cm fwd, 3 cm in), eased with the hold and put back exactly for any other weapon.
    No gun scaling, no model changes.
  - Twist (bladed stance) for long guns 0.7 -> 0.8.
  - Recoil kick and reload tilt now pivot about the butt (stock stays in the shoulder while firing/reloading).
- Cheek weld ONLY while RMB-zoomed (Jerry's correction): weight = the zoom's own easing (camera pull-in
  camDistCurrent 8 -> 7.2 for most guns; scopeAmt for the sniper), smoothstepped. With it the stock rises up the
  pocket (to y 0.40) and the head bends about the base of the neck (0, 0.40, -0.02) to bring the gun-side eye
  toward the sight line (per-gun sh.eye), max 0.5 rad. Not zoomed: head pose untouched (position and rotation
  exactly the pose's), stock still in the shoulder, both hands on. holdWeapon runs twice a frame; the weld is undone
  and redone each call (idempotent).
- TT.getShoulderDbg() -> { w (roll weight), ads, rawAds, cheek (rad), hold: SHOULDER_HOLD, holds: WEAPON_HOLD }.
- Watchman MG / m240: a tripod build, never in his hands - skipped (noted in WEAPON_HOLD).
- Pistol, revolver, uzi, akimbo, minigun, flamer, chainsaw, melee: untouched code path (same branch as before).
