# claude — CL-116 The light budget (why the frame rate was so low) — 2026-10-02
Changed:          Every point light in the scene is computed for every pixel of every frame, on or off, and the game had thirteen, nearly always dark. Now four real point lights (a fixed pool) stand in for all of them: each frame the brightest lights near the marine are copied onto the pool, and the rest sit on a layer the camera never renders. The set of real lights never changes, so nothing recompiles when one comes on. The Training Ground's dark box and the floor around it are now unlit (cheap) materials.
