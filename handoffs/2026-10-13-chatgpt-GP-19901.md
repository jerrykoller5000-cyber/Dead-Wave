## Feature and drill catalogue

The exact 60 names and objectives, player controls, scoring and tuning guide are in review/range-GP19901/README.md. Each of the exact twelve existing weapon keys has five distinct configurations. Ownership/upgrade eligibility is read from the current equipment system; weapon browsing never starts a run.

The session owns five temporary copies of the actual metal plates, art, rings, silhouettes and hinges. It folds and hides the original bank during drills and restores free shooting on exit. Saw plates are on the target side at z=1.15 and within the unchanged 3.1m reach from legal feet; flame layouts fit the actual stream. Multiple plates can be pierced by one revolver/sniper round or affected by one selected-launcher blast. Launcher contact against range solids is tested only while a drill owns the range.

PREPARING → COUNTDOWN → ACTIVE → COMPLETED / DISQUALIFIED / ABORTED. Targets stay down for eight full seconds. The start cue, timer and first rise share the same transition; only fully raised plates score. Feet crossing +0.12m beyond the yellow line, actual wrong-weapon attacks (including misses) and actual throwable releases immediately VOID a countdown or active run. Switching alone is allowed. Launcher's own 40mm is legal when selected. A player who moves out of the legal start area while plates are lowering cannot arm the countdown there.

Attack creation captures immutable run/phase/weapon/shot/activation tokens. Different shotgun pellets remain eligible; duplicate callbacks, old activations/runs and pre-start/ended tokens are rejected. Continuous scoring accumulates actual contact time from a per-frame contact union, with caps per activation and an efficiency ceiling of 100%. The range-only flamethrower idle clock is clamped to avoid spending a tank through catch-up ticks after countdown; ordinary combat flow remains unchanged.

Scores reuse head/bull 10, ring 5 and plate 2 with the existing lane multipliers [1,1.5,2,2.5,3]. Configured clear/speed/pellet/collateral/efficiency bonuses and missed/wrong-zone deductions are explained before starting. Wrong-zone shotgun deductions occur once per discharge/target. Completed timed runs save their earned score even if not every plate was cleared. VOID and abort never save. Shot accuracy counts successful discharges, not pellet totals; continuous weapons show contact efficiency.

Top ten and personal best use existing localStorage conventions under tt_range_drills_v1. Boards separate weapon/drill, a full configuration hash/version and fitted loadout/skill signature. Ties resolve by higher score, shorter time, better efficiency, earlier timestamp and lexical run ID. Storage failure is reported; results stay available in memory. Firing configuration or upgrades changing during a run aborts it rather than mixing records. Drills use one gun and restore the previous paired setting.

Main tuning: ui/range-drills.js. Session scoring/lifecycle: ui/range-session.js. Persistence: ui/range-records.js. Visual adapters: ui/range-targets.js. UI/copy: range-console.js, range-drills.css and strings.js. Integration: range-feature.js plus the focused live hooks. Fuel Saver uses 48 fuel units; the objective copy now matches that tested budget. No CDN or build step.
