## How the comparison works

The builder numbers are in local model units. `index.html`'s `WEAPON_SIZE` (around line 21669) scales a model by `real class length × look × MARINE_UPM / model length`; `MARINE_UPM = 1.655 / 1.80`. The estimates below remove that world-units factor and report the *visual* millimetres against a 1.8 m marine: builder millimetres × `real class length × look / model length`. This preserves the intentional `look` exaggeration. Bounding boxes are approximate from the builder literals and must be checked against actual renders. The standard/extended output is driven by `gunExtLook` around `index.html:22771`: 1.48× on box/stick magazines, 1.22× all axes on the AA-12 drum; the feed top stays anchored. These multipliers affect the asset silhouette, not the game's capacity.
