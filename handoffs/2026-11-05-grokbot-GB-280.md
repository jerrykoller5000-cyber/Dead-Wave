# GB-280 - live brute get-up arms (Grokbot, 2026-11-05 ~10:10 PM CST)

## Summary
Done. Studio-keyed get-up on the live brute now plants hands mid rise and returns arms near idle at the end. t330's new hands-near-ground check passes (twice on the real renderer).

## Root cause
1. **Stale bone matrices (primary):** `skinZombieMesh` sets `matrixAutoUpdate = false` on joint groups. `restore()` put position/quaternion back but left a stale local `matrix`, so the next `pose()` sampled AB from the previous keyed world pose. Hips drifted tens of metres under the mesh; dbg points matched targets on that drifted skeleton while the visible `zombie-skin` kept showing bind/walk arms (fists up at chest/head).
2. **Knocked used the spawn-emerge arm pose:** `rising` included `knocked`, so every knockdown frame forced `armBase - 1.4` (claw up). Get-up keys are offsets from the animated pose; at u≈1 (zero offsets) arms stayed raised instead of idle.
3. **Aim mapping:** live used current-pivot→S[end]; studio uses S[a]→S[b] (+ lastW). Aligned.
