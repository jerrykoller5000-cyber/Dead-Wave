# claude — CL-128 Music: each night its own — 2026-10-06
Changed:          Jerry's playthrough 1: "Better Music deserving special attention each night." His reference: "BEST OF CHIPTUNE MIX" (Final Discovery, youtube.com/watch?v=zjyQWpJc6qk). Asked what he likes in it: "Everything".
                  - Before: nights 2 to 20 were all First Blood's song, re-keyed and sped up (tools/hordes.py), so the same hook came back every night.
                  - Now every night has a chiptune song of its own: 18 new songs, for nights 2-13 and 15-20. Night 1 stays First Blood; night 14 is Fog Night and keeps its own song. Each night has its own:
                    - hook and answer, riff and bridge line;
                    - chords, key and mode (aeolian, dorian, phrygian, harmonic minor);
                    - tempo, drum groove (d-beat, shuffle, breakbeat, half-time, four on the floor, gallop, march, blast);
                    - bass line (octave bounce, gallop, walking, syncopated, First Blood's engine), arpeggio pattern and lead voice (square, 25% or thin 12.5% pulse, with its own vibrato).
                  - Each song is written for its night's plan. Some examples:
                    - the Ember nights are phrygian and drive harder;
                    - the guardian and brute nights are slower and heavier, in harmonic minor;
                    - the rest nights bounce in dorian;
                    - Artillery marches;
                    - Swarm is a 148 bpm blast beat.
                  - The run climbs from 108 bpm on night 2 to 152 on night 20. Night 20, Last Stand, comes home to First Blood's key and chords, and its climax sings First Blood's hook over the new drums.
                  - The tunes come from tools/nights.py: a composer with fixed rules (a seed per night makes the choices inside them), checked on every song:
                    - the hook is a two-bar call and a two-bar answer that starts the way the call did and ends long on the tonic or the fifth;
                    - the notes on the beat are chord tones and the ones between step along the scale;
                    - no leap is over a sixth inside a bar;
                    - the riff is a one-bar figure carried through its chords;
                    - the bridge climbs on long chord tones to a step from home.
                  - They are built on First Blood's voices and cut into its seven sections, so the game's section player follows each night's wave as it follows night 1's: quiet in the stalk, the drops at contact, the climax for the last few.
