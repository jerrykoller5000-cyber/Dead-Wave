## The prize roadmap (game/relay-vault.js)

The vault is a mystery box. Each tier is a pool, and the lift brings up something from it that he does **not** have yet. Guns come up loaded with their reserve full; a second gun makes a pair; kit goes on; plans unlock the build.

- If the tier's pool is all his already, the draw reaches up a tier, then down.
- Only if there is nothing left anywhere does it send cash.
- The final day is the red katana, whatever he has.
- The vault opens once a run, so the day is the gamble. Waiting also means going without the radio's daily calls and the sites it reveals.

| Days | Tier | What can come up (only if he doesn't have it) | Items | Cash if nothing's left |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1–3 | Footlocker | Uzi, shotgun, revolver | 1 | $120 |
| 4–7 | Quartermaster | M4, AK, chainsaw, machete, plate carrier, helmet, laser sight, gun flashlight, pistol pair, light turret plans | 1 | $200 |
| 8–11 | Armoury | AA-12, sniper, flamer, launcher, night vision (with a helmet if he has none), knee pads, Uzi pair, revolver pair, flame turret plans, mine plans | 1 | $320 |
| 12–15 | Heavy stores | Minigun, launcher, sniper, AA-12, heavy turret plans, mortar plans, Watchman MG plans | 1 | $480 |
| 16–19 | Black cache | Minigun, AA-12, launcher, sniper, flamer, heavy turret, mortar and Watchman plans, night vision | 2 | $700 |
| 20 (final) | The red katana | The red katana | 1 | — |

The work gets harder alongside the prizes (relayDayPlan, unchanged from CL-184):

| Day | Reboot | Zombies at once | Then one every | Most in all |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 s | 4 | 6 s | 7 |
| 5 | 34 s | 8 | 5.1 s | 14 |
| 10 | 51.5 s | 13 | 4.0 s | 25 |
| 20 | 86.5 s | 23 | 2 s | 48 |

On the lift:
- A gun comes up as the gun itself, a clone of the one he'll hold, on a rack over its case.
- The katana comes up on its stand.
- Anything else comes up in the crate.
- The black cache's second item goes in a crate beside the gun.

The banner names what came up, e.g. "FOOTLOCKER · Revolver". The pre-CL-185 tiers (cash, med pens, restocks) are gone.

## The rest

1. **Floor clip (fixed):** the floor deck stopped at the inside of the front wall, and the ramp's deck began past the doorway. That left a 0.3 m strip at the threshold with no deck, where he dropped to the terrain under the dock. Once he was down there, the floor was above his step-up and he walked under it. The floor deck now runs out under the doorway onto the top of the ramp.
2. **Ramp:** it is now a wedge that runs out flush into the grass:
   - 4.2 m long (was 3), about 15°;
   - grooved concrete, with a yellow pipe handrail on posts each side;
   - its foot must sit within 0.2 m of the ground where the vault is placed;
   - the side solids now go as far as it is too high to step onto.
3. **Model** (studio/relay-vault-model.js):
   - Weathered concrete painted on canvas: pour lines, rain streaks, cracks, moss at the foot.
   - A rusted roll-up door with a stencilled "07", worn hazard paint along its foot and a bloody handprint dragging down.
   - Over the door: a corrugated tin awning on struts. Beside it: a caged bulkhead lamp.
   - Striped steel corner guards on the dock.
   - On the walls: an aircon box with its fan, and a conduit running down to a junction box.
   - On the roof: a dish, a whip aerial with a red tip, and a coil of wire along the parapet.
   - By the ramp: sandbags and a rusted drum.
   - Inside: shelving with a few empty boxes, and a cable reel.
4. **Placement:** 1.5 m farther out all round. The vault is now at least 9.5 m from the cabinet and from the mast, and its ramp at least 5 m from the mast.
5. **Dirt path:** a trodden ribbon on the ground from the cabinet to the ramp's foot. It follows the terrain and has ragged edges.
6. **The card flash (fixed):** CL-184 hid the radio's tracker card a frame after the runtime painted it. A MutationObserver on #objectiveHud now sets the class before the frame is drawn.
7. **Sato's tree:**
   - The note is always at the foot of a tree 9–26 m from the cabinet, on roughly the cabinet's side. It is a new tree each run, and a rock only if there is no tree.
   - Next to it: a boonie hat dropped crown-down, and a folding field stool knocked on its side.
   - Four raw claw gouges down the trunk at shoulder height.
   - A faint trail of boot prints runs from the cabinet to the tree, at 40% opacity.
8. **The note is a sketch:** a pencil drawing of the breaker box (fuseSketch in ui/relay-panel.js).
   - Its four switches are drawn, with circled 1, 2, 3 over switches 2, 1, 4 and a dashed arrow looping through them.
   - Switch 3 is crossed out, and there is a smudge in the corner.
   - There are no words, and the panel has no title.
