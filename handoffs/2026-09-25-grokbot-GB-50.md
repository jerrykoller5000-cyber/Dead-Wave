# grokbot - GB-50 Zombies hold a contact ring; a hit lands (knockback, stumble, knee) - 2026-09-25
Changed: Zombies can no longer stand inside the marine: every zombie is held on a contact ring round him (its radius + his 0.42 m + 0.04 m) before the wall and prop collisions run. A zombie's hit now lands: he slides back along the blow (about 0.38 m for a shambler, 0.97 m for a brute), stumbles for a beat (body lurch, walk slowed to 70 percent for 0.32 s, aim thrown off 0.06 to 0.14 rad and easing back over 0.4 s, a camera jolt along the blow), and a heavy hit (a brute, a boss, or 22+ damage) puts him on one knee for 0.55 s (walk at 45 percent at worst, never stopped; at most once every 3 s).

## Detail
Why it clipped: zombies steer straight at the marine's position and nothing kept them out of him; the attack check only needs them within reach, so a crowd walked into and through him. The hit's shove was added to velX/velZ, which the walk code lerps back to the input speed at accelGround, so it moved him a few centimetres at most.

The contact ring (updateZombies, just before the soft barricades): if a zombie at his height (within 1.6 m) would end the frame closer than touching, it is put back on the ring along the line from him to it (or its old position, or a per-body angle if it was dead centre). The builds, props and cabin collisions still run after it, so the ring never pushes a body through a wall. The attack reach (radius + 0.42 + 0.15) is outside the ring, so bodies on the ring still swing: t75 spawns five (three shamblers, a feral, a brute) inside him, and over 3.5 s the worst gap is +0.024 m, all five stay at arm's length, and 19 blows land. Skipped during a scripted kill (the guardian's grab and the tentacle own the marine then). The cave-chase guardian is a rig, not in zombies, so its grab is unaffected (t59 48/0, t67 9/0).

The hit (damagePlayer, only when a zombie or other attacker with a position hits him on the ground):
- Knockback velocity kv = min(7, (1.6 + dmg x 0.14) x (1.6 for a brute, tank or boss)), x0.7 when the armour soaks most of it. It rides on top of the walk and bleeds off at 7/s, so the slide is kv/7 m: shambler (6) 2.44 m/s = 0.35 m (t75 measured 0.38), military (12) 0.47 m, brute (16) 0.97 m measured, guardian (25) 0.73 m (1.0 m if it counts as a boss), colossus slam (26, boss) capped at 7 m/s = 1.0 m. Walls and props still stop him.
- Stumble: the existing body lurch (hitReaction) plus 0.32 s at up to 30 percent slower walk, an aim offset of 0.04 + dmg x 0.004 rad (cap 0.14) that eases back over 0.4 s (it rotates the aim point round him, so the shot really goes off a little), and a camera jolt of 0.18 + dmg x 0.014 along the blow and a little down, gone in about 0.3 s. The general addShake stays as it was.
- Knee: heavy attacker or 22+ damage, with health damage taken, at most once per 3 s: 0.55 s down and straight back up (hips down 0.42 m, knees bent, torso forward), walk at 45 percent at the bottom of it. He can always move, aim and shoot.
- Rolling still makes him untouchable (unchanged).

Numbers to tune if Jerry wants more or less: HIT_KNOCK_DECAY 7, the kv formula, HIT_STUMBLE_S 0.32, HIT_AIM_S 0.4, HIT_KNEE_S 0.55, HIT_KNEE_CD 3, HIT_KNEE_DMG 22, all next to hitReaction in index.html.
