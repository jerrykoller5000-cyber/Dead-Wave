# GB-134 - The grenade's arc, and the launcher's - Grokbot, 2026-10-06

From Jerry's playthrough 1: "Hold G: an arc grows from a short lob (a tap) to the longest throw at 3 s; where terrain
cuts it, the arc shows that. The 40 mm launcher gets an arc like the mortar's."

## Changed

- **Hold G to throw.**
  - G down starts a hold. G up throws, and the throw is set by how long G was held (game/arcs.js throwSpeed).
  - A tap is a lob: horizontal 4, up 4.5, about 3 m on the flat.
  - At 3 s it is the longest throw: horizontal 17, up 7.5, about 19 m. Holding longer stays at 3 s.
  - The old fixed throw (12 / 6, about 11.5 m) sits at about 1.85 s of hold.
  - In some cases G works as before, at once: the holy grenade is ready, both hands are full, none are left, it is on
    cooldown, or a throw would be refused anyway. Their banners and clicks are unchanged.
  - A bare throwGrenade() (the rabbit's TT hook) still throws the old 12 / 6.
  - The hold is dropped if the game pauses or ends, he goes into place mode, swims, mounts or carries the mortar, or
    the window loses focus.
- **The grenade's arc (while G is held).**
  - 18 dots and a ring trace the flight updateGrenades will fly: gravity 16, from the eye height along the aim's x/z.
  - It stops where the ground first takes it, the first bounce, found by bisection on the real terrain. A rise in the
    ground cuts it short there, which is the "where terrain cuts it" part.
  - A thrown grenade only meets the ground in flight (updateGrenades sweeps solids for shells only), so its arc checks
    only the ground. The arc never claims a wall stops it.
  - The arc grows as the hold grows.
- **The launcher's arc (40 mm in hand, drawn, not in build/place mode, not on the mortar, not swimming).**
  - The shell's own flight from the muzzle: vy = dir.y x speed x 0.35 + 3.2, gravity 16.
  - It ends at the first thing its contact fuse meets (the ground, a world solid, a rock or a tree, all gathered near
    the path first) or at the fuse.
  - Like the mortar's colours: yellow dots with an orange ring, or green when a zombie is within 1.9 m of where it
    ends. The reticle's LOS classes are not touched.
- TT: grenadeArcDbg(), startGrenadeCharge, releaseGrenadeCharge, GRENADE_THROW, grenadeThrowSpeed, flyArc.
