# claude — CL-120 The crouch: no clipping through the ground — 2026-10-05
Changed:          His boots stay on the ground on a hillside, standing, walking, crouched and crouch-walking. GB-125 had already put the crouched legs on planted feet, but only against level ground: the body stands on the lowest ground within 0.3 m of him, so on a slope every boot was about 0.1 m into the hill (12 degrees; more on steeper ground), standing as well as crouched. Now, in updateMarinePose: (1) each foot's ground is read under that foot (footGround: its rise over his own ground, in the leg's space, and the hill's pitch under the sole); (2) his hips go with the lower foot, so on a hill the downhill leg stands as it does on the flat and only the uphill knee bends (eased in the world, so the step's few cm of root bounce don't shake him); (3) the crouch's leg IK aims each foot at its own ground and lays the planted sole along the hill; (4) standing, walking and running, a planted boot that would be in the hill is lifted onto it by the knee (only ever up; swinging feet are left alone). Only on open terrain: on a deck, a build, the HQ roof or the tower nothing changes. On the flat nothing changes either (every offset is 0 there).
