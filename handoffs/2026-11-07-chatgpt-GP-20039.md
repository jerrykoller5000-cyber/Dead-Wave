## Mechanism and reference

Military family. The existing six-chamber model and six-round gameplay favor a swing-out MGL over a single-shot break-open launcher. Reference: Milkor USA's [M32A1 brochure](https://www.milkorusa.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2017/06/Milkor-32a1.pdf) for the cylinder arrangement; no reference model or footage was copied. The carrier pivot is gun-local `(-0.14, -0.02, 0.12)`, axis `+Z`. When fully open the drum center is `(-0.28, -0.02, 0.12)`; its chamber axis remains parallel to the bore. The drum's rear and front faces stay at local `z ≈ 0.055` and `0.185`, between the rear receiver ending at `0.049` and the forward frame starting at `0.265`, so its path cannot pass through those frames.

Key poses by normalized reload time: 0–0.06 unlatch; 0.06–0.20 swing left; 0.22 eject fired brass; 0.28–0.78 insert and index each chamber; 0.78–0.85 wind; 0.86–1 close and latch. Duration is `0.80 + 0.40 × inserted rounds` seconds before skill multipliers. A zero-to-six gameplay probe observed ammo/reserve `0/6 → 2/4 → 6/0`, then hinge and latch both returned to zero. A three-round partial probe observed five chamber caps visible with ammo/reserve `5/1` at 0.55. The `t201` test also checked that one, three and six fired rounds each eject exactly that many cases.
Holding fire cannot fire the launcher with its cylinder open; the close and latch phase must finish. The shotgun retains its per-shell interruption.
