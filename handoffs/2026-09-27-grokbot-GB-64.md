## How

**Why he dropped off.** The crew spot was always 1.1 m behind the tube, re-aimed every frame as the tube swung, and `updateMortar` then set his height with `playerGroundY` at that spot. A one-cell deck is 2.04 m wide (hw 1.02), so a tube in the middle of a deck row put the spot 3 cm past the edge on any bearing across the row. `playerGroundY` then answered with the ground under the deck, and he dropped about 2.4 m (t89 measured the ground 2.39 m under that spot).

**The surface.**
- On a gun platform, a tube stands on the emplacement pad (`deck2`, 0.29 m above the planks). So "the tube's surface" is the walkway (`deck.deckY`) of the platform or floor in its cell (`mortarSurfaceY`), and otherwise the ground under it.
- On a deck, the crew spot must be within 0.05 m of that surface.
- On the ground it must be within 0.6 m, because slopes vary.
- "Clear" means that no BUILD_BLOCKS piece other than the tube cuts the line from the tube to the spot at knee height, or the spot's own column from 0.25 m to 1.6 m.

**The sweep.**
- `updateMortarArc` checks the spot for the wanted bearing before it swings the tube. If there's no room, the tube keeps its last bearing and `mortarNoRoom` makes the arc 'blocked'. The banner shows once per 1.5 s when he enters that state.
- `fireMortar` refuses while `mortarNoRoom` is set: empty click, no shell spent.
- The crew pull in `updateMortar` only takes a step if the step lands on the tube's surface. That guards the chord between two good spots on an L-shaped deck.

**Mounting.** `mountMortar` plants him at the spot, including its height. If the tube's current bearing has no room, it tries the bearing that points the tube away from him (the spot is then on his side). If that fails too, it refuses with the banner and he doesn't move.

**Stairs.** `onStairsRamp(b)` checks that he's inside the flight's ramp footprint (5 cm slack) and between the foot and one storey up. `toggleStairs` refuses to fold (not to lower) while that holds, with an invalid sound and a banner.

**Strings.**
- `mortar.manned`, `mortar.controls` `{shells}`, `mortar.overhead`, `mortar.blocked` `{name}`, `mortar.noShells`, `mortar.buyShells`, `mortar.carrying`, `mortar.shouldered` and `mortar.carryHelp` already existed and are now used.
- So are `build.message.stairsUp`, `build.message.stairsDown` and `build.message.stairsSafe`.
- The E prompts (`E — Man the mortar`, …) are the HUD prompt line, which GP-45 is rebuilding, so I left them alone.

**t89 checks:**
- A deck one cell wide and three long, with a mortar in the middle.
- The crew spot along the deck is on it; across the deck it is off the edge.
- 3 s of sweeps: still manning it, within 0.05 m of the deck throughout, the tube held on the bad bearings, and it never pointed where the spot was off.
- Aimed across the deck: red, and it won't fire (shells kept). Aimed along the deck: free again.
- A tube pointed at the rim gets turned on mount, and he stays on the deck.
- A ground mortar with a wall on the edge where he would kneel refuses the mount, and he stays put.
- Tier-2 stairs refuse to fold while he's on the ramp and fold once he's off.

**Decision for Jerry (small).** On a lone one-cell deck, a mortar can only point diagonally: that's the only way the spot 1.05 m back stays on 2 m of planks. That follows the board ("use the crew spot only if it is on the tube's surface, otherwise refuse"). If that feels too strict, the options are a shorter crew offset on decks (the tube's own collision radius 0.6 + his 0.42 is 1.02, so there's only 3 cm to give), or telling players to put mortars on 2x2 decks.
