## How it works

- **The game, not a copy.** `index.html?inspect` (also `?inspect=<subject>`): the disclaimer and the caracal are skipped
  (opening.js, disclaimer.js), the warm-up's pools, compile and fights are skipped (the Training Ground compiles its own
  scene on the way in), and `studio/inspector/inspector.js` is imported. It walks the marine into the Training Ground
  (bypassing the menu's callsign and fullscreen), hides the room behind a backdrop and takes the camera
  (TT.setShotView), the clock and the input.
- **index.html's part** is small and inert without an inspector: `inspectHost()` (the builders only reachable inside the
  page, by name, each `null` if it goes missing; a few hooks: enterTraining, placePlayer, the pointer, render, loop,
  state), and in `tick()`: `if (inspectHook && inspectHook.held()) { …draw only… }`, `dt = inspectHook.scale(dt)`
  before `realDt` (so slow motion slows the aim too), and `inspectHook.frame(dt, realDt)` right before
  `applyShotView()`. `TT.inspectHost` is on every debug game, so the suite's check attaches without `?inspect`.
- **Subjects** (catalog.js): built from the game's own tables (ZOMBIE_TYPES, WEAPON_ORDER, UPGRADE_TRACKS, SKULL_LOOK,
  FISH_KINDS, CAVE_DESIGNS) plus a hand list. Each has an owner, so notes go to the right agent. Adding one is an entry.
- **The marine's moves** (marine.js) are the player's input (keys dispatched from the page, `TT.keys`-free: the game's
  own `keys` map for held movement, the mouse button through `setMouseFireDbg`, the right button through
  `setZoomHeldDbg`) or the game's debug hooks where there is no key. Walking is on a treadmill. Holds use the game's
  own (`GAIT.holdU`, `reloadClipDbg.hold`, `handsDbg.knifeHold` / `drawHold`, `holdGrenadeAnim`).
- **The rewind** records the subject's whole rig every frame (902 nodes for the marine, 900 frames, 36 MB at most),
  and puts a past frame back exactly; play restores the present first.
- **Light** is the game's sun (shadows and all), sky light and fill, re-aimed just before the draw; nothing is added,
  so nothing recompiles. The studio grows with what is in it (the cargo plane, a camera far back), the grid keeps 1 m.
