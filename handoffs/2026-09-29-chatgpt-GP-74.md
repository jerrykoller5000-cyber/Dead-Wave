## Behavior

- Idle time advances only during active simulation. Any movement input, aim input, shooting, reload, knife/gesture, damage reaction, jump, crouch, roll or building cancels it. Menus, deployment, scripted kills, water, ladders, mounted/carried mortar, knockdown, death and victory exclude the idle sequence; pause freezes it. Reset removes idle props and dropped butts.
- First 5 seconds retain the existing weapon-ready breathing pose. Bored movement fades in after the threshold.
- Pack sequence begins after 25 seconds: reach to vest, raise open pack, extract a cigarette, bring it to the mouth and light it. It takes 4 seconds. Each lit cigarette then burns for a full 60 seconds; the next pack sequence follows automatically.
- Right hand raises/lowers in an 8-second puff cycle; the smoking hand stays at the mouth across the lighting handoff. Cigarette paper shortens during its lifetime. The pack is unbranded; no player-facing text is added.
- Primary and off-hand weapons hide during packing/smoking and return on the action frame. No input delay is added. The idle pose runs after the existing weapon IK and before the reaction body.
- A lit cigarette falls under gravity from the hand, then ignites one 16%-scale existing flame patch for up to 4 seconds (rain can shorten it). Existing dark scorch decal remains after it expires. Water extinguishes the cigarette. This small cosmetic flame does not damage the player, ignite zombies/trees, or spread. Ordinary flamethrower behavior remains intact.
- Spent cigarette butts are extinguished. At most 8 dropped props are reused; butts disappear after 8 seconds. Smoke, fire and scorch use their existing capped pools. No new dependency, shader, texture asset, gameplay RNG seed or world layout change.
