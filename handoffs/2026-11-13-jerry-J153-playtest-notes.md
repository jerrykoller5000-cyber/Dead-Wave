  Carry rules for the rework (from how soldiers carry):
  * A slung long gun lies FLAT against the body or the pack: the receiver's flat side against him, the magazine/drum facing AWAY from his body (or down), the sling short and tight from the shoulder.
  * Slings: across the chest (patrol), muzzle down on the support side or across the back muzzle up/down. Two-point slings run from the rear sling point to the front, snug.
  * Holsters: pistols vertical on the hip or thigh, muzzle down, grip up and slightly rearward toward the drawing hand; chest holsters grip toward the drawing hand, muzzle down or angled across. Never sideways or upside down.
  * Nothing floats: every holster/pouch/sling seated on the belt, vest or leg panel with visible straps.
  * Every combination (two long guns, long gun + pistol(s), akimbo, revolver, heavies) placed by these rules, checked from four cameras, standing/walking/crouched, no clipping (the arms included, note 20).
