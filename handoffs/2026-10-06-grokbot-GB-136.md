## The rules (D-77, Jerry's playthrough 1)
- One leg: `z.speed = z.baseSpeed * 0.75`, `z.hopping = true`.
- No legs: `z.speed = z.baseSpeed * 0.35`, `z.crawling = true`, `z.hopping = false`. The crawl speed is unchanged; Jerry gave no number for it.
- No limbs (both arms and both legs): `z.hp = 0`, `z.limbless = true`.
  - Every live limb loss comes through damageZombie, whose `hp <= 0` check kills the body on that same hit.
  - The losses inside killZombie (the death gore) happen to a body that is already dying.
- Losing arms alone changes only its damage (as before), not its walk.
