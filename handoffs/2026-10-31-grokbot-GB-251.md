## The size lead, corrected
My "95 kB bytecode" was the function's source size, not V8's bytecode: Cursor's CU-187 static pass puts updateZombies' bytecode at 41,120 bytes (under the 60 kB default). The flag probe still cut its garbage by about a quarter and the split by about 45%, so size is part of it in practice (likely V8's other size heuristics for big functions, e.g. inlining and OSR budgets), but the cause is not the 60 kB limit as I first said. Each half is now about half the old size, so around 20 kB of bytecode by CU-187's ratio (not measured by me). CU-187's --trace-opt read is the one to say which halves get optimized.
