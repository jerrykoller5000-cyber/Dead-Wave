Defects under 4, part by part (look or feel calls, reported):
- Recoil continuity (all guns): the whole kick lands in the first frame (0 to peak in under 16 ms) with no rise. A real kick reaches its peak in about 20-40 ms, and the snap reads as a pop. Feel call.
- m4 recoil: 9.4 deg and 8 cm per shot is a shotgun-lite shove. A carbine is 2-4 deg and 1-2 cm, back in ~100 ms; here it takes ~250 ms. Halving it is a feel change; proposed.
- Minigun recoil: it bucks 2-8 deg in an irregular rhythm while spinning. A real M134-style gun shakes at high frequency and barely climbs; it should be a fine tremor (under 1 deg, ~20 Hz) plus a slow push-back. Feel change; proposed.
- Minigun in first person (firing-fp-minigun.png): the barrel cluster sits dead centre at the bottom and covers what you're shooting at, and no hand is on screen. It wants to sit lower and right, with the carry handle and the trigger hand in view (Claude's first-person framing; the heavies are carried close).
- Flamer in first person (firing-fp-flamer.png): 1) the flame is a blown-out white-yellow blob that whites out the middle of the screen, where a real flamethrower stream is a rope of orange fire with a dark smoky edge, thin at the nozzle and widening; 2) the grey block at the right is the front grip with no glove on it. Look calls.
- Flamer reload: the right hand drifts 2.8-3.3 cm off the grip mid-reload (GB-228 logs). Small; noted.
