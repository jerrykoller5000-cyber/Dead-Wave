## How it works
- `otsAllowed()`: right button held, in a run, not paused or in a menu, not building or placing, not the sniper, not
  on the mortar or the Watchman or carrying one, not swimming, on the tower ladder or rolling, no cinematic, not the
  Training Ground's wake-up.
- On: the view's heading starts at where he faces (aimYaw), pitch 0. Mouse movement turns it (OTS_SENS 0.0022 rad a
  pixel x Look sensitivity), pitch -0.72..+0.62 rad. The right-button press asks the browser to lock the pointer (it
  needs a user gesture) and letting go releases it.
- The camera: a pivot 1.6 m over his feet (a crouch lowers it), 0.62 m to his right; the camera 2.9 m behind it along
  the view and 0.2 m up, pulled in when the ground behind would cut across; it looks level along the view. Blended over
  the usual camera by OTS.w (eases in at 9/s, out at 7/s). It is laid on after the scope block, whose saved orbit
  position starts the next frame, so the usual camera's smoothing is never disturbed.
- Aim: the ray runs from the camera through the screen's middle (the same picking as the mouse: zombies, ground,
  Training plates, a far point at the sky), so he faces and fires at what is under the crosshair.
- Movement: W/S/A/D are along and across the view while shouldered (they are screen-relative otherwise, as before).
- Cursor's CU-114 pose still runs underneath (the zoom, and what the blend comes from); at full weight it is replaced.
