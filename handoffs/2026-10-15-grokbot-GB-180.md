# GB-180 Weapon redo pass (Grokbot, 2026-10-15)

## What changed
Pistol (studio/weapon-sidearms.js makePistolModel), rebuilt:
- The slide's bottom is lowered to local -0.044, so it overlaps the frame top (0.012) by 6 mm and there is no gap.
- The frame's rear is flush with the slide's at z -0.09. The dust cover runs to 0.294, 14 mm short of the slide's front.
- A new beavertail/tang sweeps back under the slide's rear and blends into the back strap.
- The guard is smaller, its front is at 0.104 instead of 0.122, and a root block joins it to the grip's front strap.
- The trigger is a solid curved blade in the middle of the guard (z 0.061 to 0.080), with a safety tab.
- The grip keeps the same centre line and rake (hand anchor unchanged). It is 0.084 deep instead of 0.074 and 52 mm wide instead of 46, with finger swells and a full-depth bottom. The grip panels and checkering moved out to match.
- The rail is longer (0.1 at z 0.2) and sits in the frame. The light hangs from it by a clamp block with a cross-bolt, and its body, head and lens are bigger.
- The magazine baseplate is square, 46 mm wide and flush with the grip.
- The sights sit on dovetail bases, and the rear one is a U notch with two ears.
- The barrel hood (on the frame, so it stays put on reload) shows in the ejection port, with dark edges around the port.
- tools/tests/t242.js now uses the new grip constants: d 0.084, the new points, HW 0.026 and R 0.009. I checked offline that the magazine is 18 mm inside the grip and the baseplate sits 11 mm below it (allowed: 0 to 15 mm).

All guns with a wire trigger: the trigger is now a solid tapered blade and the guard a moulded band.
- M4, AK, AA-12, shotgun and sniper in studio/weapon-longarms.js.
- Launcher, flamer and M240 in studio/weapon-heavy.js.
- Uzi in studio/weapon-sidearms.js.
- The Uzi trigger moved 14 mm forward, the flamer's 8 mm back and the M240's 6 mm back, to put each in the middle of its guard.
