## Changed
- **One shot, 20 m.** One shot into a cave mouth brings the guardian out; before, it took three
  hits in 1.5 s. An explosive still counts. The marine has to be within 20 m (was 45) and in front
  of the mouth with a clear line, and not a build in the way. It stays once per cave per day.
- **The chase.** After the `aggro` screech (0.35 s) the guardian races out of the mouth straight
  at the marine at **27 m/s**. A flat-out sprint is 11.8 m/s (`PHYS.run`), so he can run but
  cannot gain ground: from 12 m out he is caught within about a second. Nothing can hurt it; it
  is a prop, not a zombie. It runs flat out, arms reaching, and kicks up dust. Climbing up high
  only buys up to 6 s, then it takes him anyway.
- **The leg grab and the drag.** On contact (1.5 m) the grab sounds play (roar, hurt, scream,
  gore) and a `cave-guardian` event goes out with `phase: 'grab'`. He goes down on his back, and
  the guardian turns and hauls him by the ankle back to the mouth at about 6.5 m/s. The drag
  takes 1.2 to 4.5 s, plus a 0.45 s grab beat. The camera eases from the gameplay view to a low
  shot behind and to the side and follows the two of them in, with the mouth ahead. Boot dust and
  a blood trail follow him.
- **Then the thrown-out cutscene.** At the lip the kill's own timeline picks up at the fade
  (t = 2.2). He is pulled on into the dark under the black, the off-screen kill sounds play at
  3.05 and 3.6, and the aftermath runs unchanged: it walks out holding the remains, throws them
  onto the apron, then the burial.
- **No crawl-in snatch on this path.** The snatch's grab beats (`stir`, `grab`, `drop`) are
  switched off for the drag variant, and its arm-out-of-the-dark animation never runs.
- **The walk-in grab is unchanged.** `beginScriptedKill('cave', cave)` with no third argument
  plays exactly as before (t36, t41). Walking into a mouth during a chase ends the chase and plays
  the walk-in grab. `abortScriptedKill()` (resetGame, the harness) also removes a running chase.
