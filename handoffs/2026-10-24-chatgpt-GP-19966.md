## D-89 review

**Reference and construction.** The action is a distant, circling flock: a readable wing silhouette, flap, short glide, then another flap while the birds bank together without locking their wing phases. Jerry's [Animated Bird, Pigeon](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/animated-bird-pigeon-797d27b68af3453e865149435df6aa30) reference has separate glide and flap loops; [White Eagle Animation Fast Fly](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/white-eagle-animation-fast-fly-30203bf39e5145f19c79e83c550139d3) is a faster flight reference. Those pages establish the motions to compare, but I could not verify exact beat/glide timing from their previews. Our structural poses are wings raised, wings near level, wings down, and a partly held glide; each bird varies its phase and rate. The art family is **settler and civil** wildlife within the natural world: a dark, unadorned silhouette against the mountain sky.

**Gameplay camera evidence.** `handoffs/GP-19966-shots/after-1.png`, `after-2.png`, `after-3.png` are sequential real-renderer views from the title camera. They show spread formation and changing wing angles; `qa/2026-10-24-AG-123/gp19966-flock-gpu.png` is Antigravity's closer live GPU view. The title camera is the flock's actual display context.
