# grokbot - GB-91 (R5, P-65, D-52): survivors' help - 2026-09-30

Changed:
- **What the help follows.** It depends on the style of each survivor taken in (GB-90's `getSurvivors()`) and lasts for the rest of the run. A run reset ends it.
- **Hikers (the medic):** health regen now stops at 50% of max hp instead of 40% (`MEDIC_REGEN_CAP_FRAC`, `regenCapFrac()` in `updateHealthRegen`).
- **Trapper:** every repair costs 75% (`TRAPPER_REPAIR_MUL` in `repairCostOf`, before rounding up; never under 1).
- **Ranger:** sets up one free light turret by the HQ, once a run.
  - It goes through `placeBuildAt`, on the free cell nearest (-(HQ_HALF + 4), 0), off the skull-window side. That cell is dry, clear of the HQ by 2 m and off the cabin; in the test it landed 8 m out at (-8, 0).
  - It's an ordinary build marked `gift: 'ranger'`.
  - If no cell takes it, he tries again at the next prep.
- **Event:** `dw-game` `'survivor-help'` `{ style, help: 'regen'|'repairs'|'turret', ... }` fires when each survivor is taken in, for ChatGPT's board/banner lines.
- **TT:** `survivorHelpDbg`.

What the player notices:
- Bring the hikers' medic home and you heal back to half health between fights instead of 40%.
- Bring the trapper home and repairs cost a quarter less.
- Bring the ranger home and a light turret appears beside the HQ.
