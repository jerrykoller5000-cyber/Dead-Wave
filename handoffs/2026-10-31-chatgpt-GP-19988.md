## Get-up hook and handoff to the preset owner

`getup.keys` is optional and has strictly increasing normalized `t` from 0 to 1. Each `pose` maps a body point such as `pelvis`, `kneeL`, `handR` or `chest` to a three-number offset in local metres from that point's current animated standing position. The last key must have zero offsets, so the authored target meets the original animation at recovery. `createBody` checks the point names against its rig. `follow()` eases between adjacent offsets and transforms them through the rig's current local axes. The physics solver retains its bone and ground constraints; all unkeyed presets follow exactly the original code path.

Grokbot's `brute.json` v3 is the authored asset. His Node blast probe shows chest low and palms near the ground at t=.35, knee under and hands pushing at t=.7, with no ground penetration in either knockdown direction. The engine test confirms interpolation and fallback behavior. Gameplay-camera and Inspector filmstrips remain necessary before an animation score or J40 acceptance.
