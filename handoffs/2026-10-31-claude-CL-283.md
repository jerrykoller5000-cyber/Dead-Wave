## J36: "a sound like rattling chains when the player walks or runs"

Muse's MU-10 (handoffs/2026-10-31-muse-MU-10.md): there is no gear or chain layer; the rattle is in the footstep takes
that play on ordinary ground, the pack's gravel steps (walking) and gravel stomps (running), each with bright ringing
gravel ticks that, once a footfall, read as a chain. Those eight takes are made again with their top off: two 12 dB/oct
low-passes at 3 kHz, each levelled back to its own mean loudness (+0.4 to +1.3 dB). Above 4 kHz they are 13 dB quieter
on average and their peaks there 15 dB lower; the crunch below stays. The originals are in Claude outputs/cl283/orig/.
tools/pack-make.py now takes `lp` (Hz) on a folder or a take, and docs/audio/packs/player-builds.json has `"lp": 3000`
on player/footstep-dirt and player/footstep-run-dirt, so a remake from F:\Sound_Effects keeps it. The pack files stay
git-ignored (pk-*), in the builds only.

Not changed: the metal boots (real clinks, but only on metal: the HQ roof, the tower deck, the cellar), the grass under
the trees, the water. If Jerry still hears chains, the next suspects are those (MU-10 section 4).

## J37: the goggles by day

Down by day, the tube's picture is flooded: a second SVG filter (index.html `nvgImageTubeDay`, beside GP-165's
`nvgImageTube`) lifts every value toward a pale green-white, the shadows grey-green, with a wide glare round the bright
parts; the eyepiece overlay (GP-166) is on as at night. Night is unchanged. `canvas.nvg-washout` is the class, toggled
in updateNvgVisual (which already runs every frame), so dawn turns the night tube into the glare and dusk back.
First use of the day filter may cost one slow frame (the night tube's was warmed at load, CU-35); left as is.
