## Where it is, and why there

The training ground has one doorway, between the range and the build room, and that is where a light switch goes. The plate sits
on the range side of that wall, 0.55 m downrange of the hazard frame (x = RANGE.minX, z = DOOR.maxZ + 0.55), its lever at 1.4 m.
From the spawn it is about 3 m ahead and 8 m to his right, in view from the play camera, and he walks past it on the way to the
build room. Its E reach (1.7 m round a point 0.8 m off the wall) starts as he steps out of the doorway and doesn't reach any other
station: the terminals are on the far wall, and the panel and the skull window are in the build room.

Up is on. Off, the lever drops, the strips go out (glow 0 and the tube dulled to grey: white with no glow still caught the night's
light like a lit tube, 229 against the wall's 37 in a close-up) and a small amber lamp on the plate glows so he can find it again.

## What "off" does

advanceSkyClock holds the training clock at 0 (midnight) instead of 0.5 (noon) while `training.lightsOff`, so updateDayNight runs
the real night: the sun, sky, moon, ambient and fog of a night outside, nightReadK, the goggles' phosphor and the flashlight's
night strength. I checked what else reads isNight: audio returns early in training, the night lockdown keys off the wave phase
(the terminals stay usable in the dark), the wildlife isn't updated indoors, and the match-clock hours only feed the grace period,
which is off in training. One side effect: dropped loot magazines are cleared when isNight changes, which is existing behaviour
at every dusk.

Every visit starts lit: a `dw-game` listener for the 'training' event (enter or leave) puts the lights back on, and on leave it puts
the clock back to noon first so the night doesn't follow him out to the title (resetGame sets the title clock anyway).
I kept out of enterTraining, updateTraining and leaveTraining because ChatGPT is working in them for GP-154/155.
