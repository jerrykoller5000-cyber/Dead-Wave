# GB-71 — R2 P-16: one breather and a surge on the test nights

Changed:
- Nights 11-20 (the test act) no longer stop and start between every push. The early pushes run straight on into each other; there is one real breather, just before the last push. It waits for the field to thin (the night's own lull, once 5 or fewer are up) or 45 s at most with a full field, then the last push comes as one surge.
- In that breather the cave eyes dim to level 1 ("they're gathering"); they flare back to 2 as the surge starts. The player gets a clear "hold on, here it comes" beat instead of four or five small pauses.
- Teach and build nights (1-10) are exactly as before: a breather between every push, at most 30 s with a full field.
- New `dw-game` event `wave-push { day, push, pushes, last, lull }`, once per push as it starts, for the music's surge and a HUD line. `getWaveDirectorState().pace.straight` says whether the night runs straight.
- Horde sizes, the mix and the push sizes are unchanged (t78 still passes all of its plan checks).
