## WebGL shader error

The native links that failed both belonged to the wood and leaf draws for `forest:oak:1`. `buildForest()` supplied a storage `mat4` and a tint `vec3`. The r175 GLSL fallback in `vendor/three/three.webgpu.js:54035` emitted the matrix at slot 4 and the tint at slot 5. The matrix occupies slots 4–7. The renderer subsequently tried to use the unlinked program. The same matrix input also produced invalid size-16 vertex bindings in the other tree scenery draws.

The review copy passes four interleaved `vec4` columns on WebGL for the forest, understory, wall-band plants and shortcut logs. Each group still uses its existing instance count, transforms and tint. WebGPU retains the storage-matrix nodes. The paired WebGL capture shows previously missing trees across the valley. The `Shader Error 0` and `Shader Error 1282` messages, and the associated GL invalid-value and invalid-operation messages, cleared. Both pages had zero uncaught exceptions or GPU context loss.

The separate UV warnings reproduced before and after. The armory rack's folded geometry requests missing `uv1`, and the bomber's `bomber-gut` geometry requests missing `uv`. The exact objects, generated GLSL and console evidence are in `review/GU-5/shader-before-webgl-valley/result.json` and `shader-candidate-webgl/result.json`.
