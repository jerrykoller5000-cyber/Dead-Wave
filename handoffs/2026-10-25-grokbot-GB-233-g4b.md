Defects under 4, part by part (look calls, reported):
- Spikes: a tidy planked board with 25 tiny cones about 2 cm high. From the gameplay camera (high, three-quarter) the cones vanish and it reads as a plain wooden floor tile. Real improvised spike boards use long nails or rebar standing 8-15 cm proud. Taller, thicker, sharper spikes would read from above.
- Wire (vs a real double-apron or post-and-wire fence): the barbs are X-shaped crosses about 10 cm across, like tank-trap jacks in miniature. Real barbs are small twists (2-3 cm) on twisted strand, and razor wire is a coil. The strands are straight rigid bars with no sag. The gameplay read is fine (4), but Construction is 3.
- Lure: the build's hp read -5 in the world probe while it was still standing. Either it's a timer, not hp, or a build below zero isn't being removed. It needs a look in the code before it's called a bug.
- Barrel (vs a 55-gallon steel drum): 1) the two dark bands sit where a wooden barrel's iron hoops go; a steel drum has two rolling ribs pressed into the shell, the same colour as the drum. 2) Even matte-red faces with no wear at the rims, where the art direction wants bare metal at worn edges. The yellow hazard band reads well (Readability 5).
- Mortar (vs the M224 60 mm): 1) the tube is glossy black chrome with a blown-out highlight; a real tube is matte olive or black phosphate. 2) The bipod clamps about a third down the tube, as on a real one, so construction is right.
- Light turret: 1) the gun is a short stub, a third the length of the head, as with the heavy turret. 2) The six thin spikes splayed round the column read as debris, not legs, because they don't reach the ground plate's rim. 3) The glossy dark finish.
