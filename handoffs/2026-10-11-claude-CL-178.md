# CL-178 · Claude · The insulated boots are on a skeleton up the radio mast

Jerry: "Instead of a line man lets have it be a skeleton that is wearing military fatigues and the boots are attached to his feet. You climb up and can reach them, but like you said. If you climb during a storm there is a 1 in 3 chance per strike you get killed by lightening. As soon as the player gets the boots though he is protected."

## What changed

- **world/lightning.js** (mine):
  - `MAST_PERCH`: the layout in the mast's own frame.
  - `LIGHTNING.MAST_ODDS = 1/3` and `mastStrikeFinds(rand)`.
  - `buildWornBoots(T)`: the same boots, standing side by side.
  - `buildMastPerchParts(T)`:
    - a small grated work platform on the -z face (where the cable ladder runs), about 12.5 m up, left of the ladder, with struts and a rail
    - a soldier's skeleton sitting on its edge in olive fatigues and a patrol cap, slumped against the mast, his lanyard still clipped to the leg
    - his legs hang over the edge with the trousers tucked into the boots, and bare bone feet underneath
  - The parts are flat meshes, so the host merges them into one draw.
- **index.html:**
  - `buildRadioMast` merges the perch (`mast-perch`) and stores `POI.mast.gy`.
  - `ensureInsulatedBoots` now puts the boots on his feet instead of on the ground. GP-119's rubber work mat at the foot is gone with them.
    - `userData.noCull` is set, because their visibility is their state; the scene cull was hiding them and could have shown them back after they were taken.
    - The pickup is unchanged and still works by reach: from the ladder near the top (feet about 10.4 m up) he takes them; from the ground he can't.
  - **The mast's cable ladder climbs.** `mastClimb`, `mastLadder()`, `onMastHigh()`, `letGoOfMast()` and `updateMastClimb(dt)`:
    - Walk up to the ladder face and hold W. W/S go up and down at 2.4 m/s, with a rung sound every 0.4 m.
    - The climb tops out at 12.35 m with his hands by the platform, and he steps off at the foot.
    - It sets `climbingTower` as well, so gravity, jumping, rolls, the shoulder aim and the land clamp all stay out, the same as on the tower ladder.
    - `updateTowerClimb` returns early while on the mast. The tower's siege and collapse no longer count the mast as the tower.
    - `sendMarineToSpawn` resets the climb.
  - **Strikes on the mast.** In `strikeLightning`:
    - While he is more than 1.5 m up the mast, each un-forced strike finds him 1 time in 3 (force `'mast'` always finds him). The bolt comes down to the mast's tip.
    - Without the boots it kills him outright, armour and all; he lets go and falls.
    - In the boots: the GROUNDED banner, and nothing happens.
    - The record gets `mast: true`. Off the mast, nothing changed.
  - Pickup banner: new key `pickup.insulatedBootsMast` ("Pulled off a dead soldier's feet. Lightning can't touch you now."), English fallback.
  - Dev console:
    - `mast` puts him at the foot of the ladder.
    - The `boots` text was updated.
  - Debug hooks on lightningDbg: `mast()`, `climbMast(y)`, `leaveMast()`.
- **world/lightning.test.mjs:** two new tests: the 1-in-3, and the worn boots and perch layout within reach.
- **tools/tests/t162.js:** see Review.

- Screenshots:

  - from the ground, the shoulder aim looking up shows the skeleton on the platform
  - close-ups of the boots on and off look right
- No page errors. Main script passes node --check.

## Review: t162 expectations changed

t162 (mine, CL-92) expected the boots at the mast's foot. It now expects:
- them high up the mast on the skeleton (more than 10 m), and out of reach from the ground under them
- reaching them still puts them on
- a second match: up the mast in the boots, nothing; without them, dead at full HP and armour, and off the ladder

The rest is unchanged.
