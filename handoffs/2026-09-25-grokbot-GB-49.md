# grokbot - GB-49 t58 mortar arc check fixed (the test was wrong) - 2026-09-25

Changed: The test was wrong, not the game, so I fixed t58 and changed no game code. It had two stale assumptions. First, it started the match with a bare Play click and a 1.2 s wait, so the insertion was still flying the camera: 94 m out, with jumps of 88 m a frame, which caused 2 of the 3 fails. Second, it measured "the view" as camera-to-marine. The follow-cursor pan (camPanX) now slides the camera toward the reticle, so aiming west put the camera 4 m west of him and the proxy swung about 40 degrees. That gave dot = -0.69 (-0.46 once started properly), while the real camera heading gave 0.00. I also added one check for the case the GB-24 clamp exists for: aiming straight back at the camera now holds the arc at dot -0.16 (the clamp is 0.55 pi, 99 degrees).
Files: tools/tests/t58.js
Tests: t58 9/0 (was 5/3; one check added, none removed or loosened, the -0.25 limit is unchanged). t33 4/0 and t54 32/0 also pass.

Why each fix is right, not a loosening:
- The start: lib.js says the insertion owns the marine for about 9 s after prep. Mounting a mortar during it put the insertion camera in the check. Real players can't mount during the insertion.
- The view: the game clamps the tube against camYawCurrent (the camera heading). camera.getWorldDirection is that heading, including elevation, which the test ignores by using x and z only. Camera-to-marine includes the pan offset, which isn't the view.

They pass today, but any of them that reads the camera or the marine within about 9 s is exposed to the insertion in the same way. Owners may want to move them to startMatch when they touch them.
