## How it decides
- The loadout is the Armory's four slots. Before any gun has gone through the Armory (a debug grant), it is the first two owned of each kind, in weapon order.
- In hand: `currentWeapon`, unless he is unarmed (U) or the gun is put away (swimming, the mortar, the smoke break). That gun comes off him: off his back, out of its holster, or out of the hip holster for the pistol. Akimbo takes two of that kind.
- A holster stays on him, empty, while its gun is in his hands. The shotgun's bandolier and the launcher's belt stay on, empty, while those guns are carried.
- Stages: magazine tops from the average of the carried magazine guns' spare magazines (the belt's 2 pouches; 5 with the carrier's 3); shells 10 loops, 40 mm rounds 6, belt boxes 2, tanks 2, each by that gun's ammunition against its cap; grenades one for one, up to 4. Something left always shows at least one.
- It refreshes five times a second and only rebuilds when something changed; a fitted suppressor is copied onto the gun on his back too.
