## Why
CL-143 opened build mode in the Training Ground, so he can build a mortar or a Watchman in the build room. But in the
Training Ground, E goes through `trainingActionTarget` / `trainingDoAction` (CL-115) instead of the outdoor
`actionTarget` / `doAction`, and those only knew the room's terminals and the door keypads. So nothing offered
'mountMortar', and the 'dismount' / 'setMortar' that actionTarget returns while manning or carrying one fell through
trainingDoAction too.

## Fix
- `trainingActionTarget`: outside build/place mode, the nearest mortar or Watchman in reach (`nearestMortar(2.6)`,
  the same reach and floor check as outside) is offered as 'mountMortar' when no terminal or keypad is nearer.
- `trainingDoAction`: 'mountMortar', 'dismount' and 'setMortar' go to `interactMortar()`, as outside.
