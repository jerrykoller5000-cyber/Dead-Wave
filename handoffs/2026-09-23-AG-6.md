### FPS note

`raf=timer` runs the frame loop at the OS timer rate (~5 fps) by design — it is the headless-safe test mode. The raw fps numbers (4–5 on both runs) reflect the timer, not the GPU. The reliable comparison is `frameMsAvg`:
- **Batching ON:** 0.096 ms/frame batch overhead, max 0.8 ms
- **Batching OFF:** 0 ms batch overhead (no batch system runs)
- After ignite: avg rose to 0.148 ms (2 dirty trees being re-written) — still well under budget.

The "25% far FPS gain" figure from the script summary is noise on a 4–5 fps timer baseline and should be ignored. Real-gameplay FPS comparison requires a running match with `raf=requestAnimationFrame`.
