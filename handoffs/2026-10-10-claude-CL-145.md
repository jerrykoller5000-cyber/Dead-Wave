# CL-145: ElevenLabs prompts for every zombie sound, and the z-sounds folder (Claude, 2026-10-10)

Jerry: "give me a text prompt for every zombie sound effect. I am going to feed them to eleven labs. And then ... an
organized folder in z-sounds where I will eventually drop them off for you to put back in the game. It gives you four
variations per prompt so keep that in mind."

No game code changed. New: `z-sounds/` at the project root.

- `z-sounds/PROMPTS.md`: 112 prompts, numbered, each with its seconds, loop flag and target folder.
- `z-sounds/<group>/.../_prompt.txt`: one folder per prompt; the four takes go in beside it (any file names).
- `z-sounds/manifest.json`: id, folder, seconds, loop, prompt, and the game hook each one replaces.

Coverage (from ui/zombie-audio.js profiles and core/audio.js):
- 14 types x idle/chase/attack/hurt/death (70), plus leaper move, demon charge, colossus step.
- Per-zombie extras: screamer call, spider spit and acid splash, bomber burst, guardian roar and slam, cave guardian
  growl, screech, cave groan, in-walls.
- footsteps (deadStep kinds + heavyStep): shuffle, barefoot, waterlogged, boots, hop, crawl, heavy, huge.
- impacts: flesh-hit, flesh-hit-heavy, headshot, gore-small, gore-big, body-fall.
- horde: emerge, groan-near, distant (loop).
- build-attacks: hit and break for wood, stone, sand, wire, metal, turret.

Voices are asked for dry and close (the game pans, filters and reverbs them); cave guardian scripted sounds carry their
own cave echo. Takes will be picked at random per play, pitched a little per zombie (the vocalizer's personality).

## Next (when the takes are in)

A sample-bank loader for z-sounds (like FOLEY), then each hook in the manifest plays a take when its folder has one and
falls back to today's procedural sound when it doesn't, so they can arrive a folder at a time.
