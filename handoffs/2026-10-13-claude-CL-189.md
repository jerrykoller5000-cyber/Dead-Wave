## 1. Akimbo, backing away (index.html, AKIMBO_CARRY)

The high carry (guns up by the face, elbows in) now only comes on with forward travel. How much of his travel is
along his facing decides it (`fwd: [-0.3, 0.6]`: none at or below -0.3, all at 0.6 and up): backing straight away
both guns stay out on what he is backing from, and the hands reach `retreatReach` (7 cm) further toward it, the arms
nearer straight; strafing is in between; running forward is the full carry as before. `TT.akimboDbg.retreat()`.

## 2. The jump (studio/marine-air.js, new; marine-air.test.mjs, 5 tests)

GP-172's air pose bent both legs the same small amount whatever he did. Now the shape comes from how he left the
ground, mixed by how much of each his velocity is (in his legs' frame), and runs over the flight on
u = vy / jump speed (1 leaving, 0 the top, -1 landing):

- **Straight up:** both knees come up together over the top (not quite in step) and reach back down to land.
- **Forward / a running leap:** a stride in the air. The leg that was forward as he left the ground leads: its knee
  drives up and then reaches out to land on; the trailing leg is left behind with the heel up, then comes under.
- **Backward:** the hips sit back (the chest leans back), both knees come up in front, and the feet reach back
  under him for the landing.
- **Sideways:** the leg on the side he is going reaches out that way (the hip opens), the other tucks under; he
  leans into it a little.
- **Off a ledge** (no jump): no tuck; the legs are already reaching down.
- **Free arms** (unarmed, carrying the mortar): up as he leaves, out over the top, forward to meet the ground; on a
  leap the arm opposite the leading leg swings forward; jumping back both come forward; sideways the lead arm opens.
  With a gun up the hold stays on the aim, as before.
- **Take-off and landing:** the load and the landing absorb (GP-172) now lean into the way he is going (forward,
  back on a jump back, over on a side jump).

Knobs: `TT.airDbg.AIR` (every key pose, the arms, `on: false` is GP-172's pose for comparison);
`TT.airDbg.jumpNow()` launches a jump for the camera (the Space path is unchanged).
