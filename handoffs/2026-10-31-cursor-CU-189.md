## What moved the camera
`otsAllowedBase` treated an open wheel like a menu and turned the first-person camera off, so the view eased out (OTS_OUT, about a second) and eased back in when the wheel closed. Third person does not have that camera on, so opening the wheel does not switch it into first person. The ease out, and back in on close, was the transition in first person. The wheel now keeps the camera the way a device panel does (CL-258): the pointer lock is let go, the mouse does not look, and the view is not blended.

The third-person aim zoom is no longer dropped just because the wheel opened, so a held right-click does not ease out either.
