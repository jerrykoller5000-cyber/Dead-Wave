# GB-74 - Streaks heal (P-22, D-52)

Changed: From the 5th kill of a streak, each of the marine's own kills gives back 1 HP, and 2 HP from the 20th. It only fills to 70% of max: past that it does nothing and never takes HP away. Turret, trap and wire kills neither count nor heal, as before. Natural regen still stops at 40%, and a medpen is still the way to full. From 5 kills up, the combo line now ends with "· kills heal". The player will notice that a hurt marine who keeps killing claws his way back to 70%. On a 20+ streak that's quick: 2 HP a kill.
Files: index.html (STREAK_HEAL_CAP_FRAC, streakHealHp and streakHeal beside the streak powers, one call in registerKill, the combo line, TT hooks), tools/tests/t97.js (new)

## How
- **The heal:**
  - `registerKill()` calls `streakHeal()` right after `combo += 1`, so the kill that makes 5 is the first to heal.
  - `streakHealHp()` returns 2 at 20 or more, 1 at 5 or more, and 0 below that.
  - `streakHeal()` does nothing when there's no heal due, the game isn't live, or HP is 0. Otherwise it fills `playerHp` up to `PLAYER_MAX_HP * 0.7` (so 84 with Vitality at 120) and calls `updateHpHud()`.
- **Defence kills:** `killZombie` only calls `registerKill` when `!info.defense`, so they never heal. This path is unchanged.
- **The combo line:** `(streakHealHp() ? ' · kills heal' : '')` is appended to the existing multiplier text. It only shows from 5, which is when the line has a multiplier anyway.
- **t97:**
  - the 4th kill from HP 30 doesn't heal; the 5th makes it 31 and the 6th 32;
  - the 19th kill heals 1 and the 20th heals 2;
  - from 0.5 under 70% it stops at 70% exactly; at 70% nothing happens, and at 85 nothing happens;
  - a defence kill leaves the combo at 10 and HP at 30;
  - the combo line says "kills heal" at 6, and `streakHealHp` is 0 at 3;
  - regen from 35 stops at 40 over 4 s;
  - no page errors.
