# GB-273: the walls' cull (docs/specs/sector-cull.md, D-112 order 2)

Grokbot, 2026-11-03. CU-232/CU-233's stone is the default (Cursor, 2026-11-02), so this is on by default with it;
?wallcull=0 turns it off (for the A/B), ?walls=hedge has no occluders (the hedge is under the eye).

## Changed
- game/wall-cull.js (new): wallSegmentsFromBand() turns each band's solids into straight segments of at most 6 m,
  broken wherever the solids break (a gap, with the solids' own clearance, so never across one). Each segment's top is
  conservative: the lowest ground under it + 1.75 m where the stone kit's breach wave (ang = at/60, > 1.15) comes within
  1.2 m, else + 2.5 m (the far slab's floor; near courses are 2.8+). createWallCull(): a 16 m segment hash; 8 m cells
  with their boxes; a cell is hidden when all four top corners are under the sightline over a segment between it and
  the eye; two hidden reads to hide, one seen read to show; the cells within 24 m every frame, then up to 64 more
  round-robin that are unsettled or whose last test was from over 0.5 m away. Typed arrays and a stamp: no garbage a frame.
- index.html (by section):
  - imports: the wall-cull import after the stone kit's.
  - sceneCull: `let wallCull` beside cullStats; at the top of sceneCull the update from the camera, timed into
    cullStats.wallMs and its own PROF line `cull:walls` (r:cull is marked before it, so the two add up as before);
    when a unit's sphere is first measured, a static one (r <= 10 m, no userData.noWallCull) joins its cell, and one that
    later moves over 0.1 m leaves the walls' cull for good; `out` also when its cell is hidden. Hiding is sceneCull's own
    cullHide (visible false), so the eye's pass and the shadow pass both lose it, and the owner's visible is kept.
  - after the bands are built (before inHQThicket): build the occluders when STONE_WALLS and not ?wallcull=0.
  - TT.wallCullDbg(): cells, hidden, tested, segments, ms.
- game/wall-cull.test.mjs (new, node --test): the spec's t-test rules. tools/tests/tgb273.js (new): in the game.
- Backup qa/2026-11-03-GB-273/index.before.html; edit script qa/2026-11-03-GB-273/edit.cjs.
