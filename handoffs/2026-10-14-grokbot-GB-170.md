# GB-170 CIF relaxed hang: palms to the outer thigh, thumbs forward (Grokbot)

Jerry's feedback on GB-169: in the CIF the palms faced up and forward. Cause: poseDressPreview turned `ud.wristLG/RG`, which the figure doesn't have. makeMarine's userData has elbowLG/RG, and each wrist hangs off `elbow.userData.wrist`, so the wrist turn never ran and the palms stayed in the rig's build pose (facing +z, forward). The in-game empty-hands idle (poseHands) already found the wrist through the elbow and was correct.

Changed:
- index.html poseDressPreview: wrists looked up through `elbowLG/RG.userData.wrist`; same turn as the idle (y +1.15 left / -1.15 right, x 0.05), so palms face the thigh, thumbs point forward, backs of the hands face out. The dwCifPose hook is unchanged.
- tools/tests/t254.js (new): in the CIF preview and the unarmed idle, for each hand in the figure's own space: palm normal points toward the midline (n·medial > 0.7) and thumb tip is forward of the palm centre (z > |x|). Mirror sign checked per hand from its body x.
- handoffs/2026-10-14-grokbot-GB-170/patch170.mjs, relax.mjs (shots).
Tests: t254 8/0 (before the fix, CIF 4 fail: normal ~(0,.3,.95) forward; idle 4 pass). t240, t229, t215 pass (flaky under load, pass alone).
