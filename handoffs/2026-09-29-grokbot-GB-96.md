# GB-96 — R2 P-100: the shotgun earns its place against spiders

Changed:
- Shotgun pellets do 1.25x their damage to spiders (the wall-climbing spitters, `spider: true`). A full shell into a spider on a wall now does a quarter more; a pellet that was 8.3 on a spider (9 less its 0.08 armour) is 10.4. Nothing else changes: pellets on every other kind, and every other weapon on spiders, do what they did. The headshot bonus scales with it (it is a share of the hit).
- My call under Jerry's "use your best judgement" (2026-09-26): damage, not spread, so the edge is the same at every range and the shotgun's feel doesn't change.
