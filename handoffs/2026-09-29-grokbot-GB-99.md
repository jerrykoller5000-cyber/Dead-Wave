# GB-99 — R2 P-20: the brute's head line

Changed:
- The headshot line is now per kind. Everyone keeps 0.78 of `hitH` except the brute, which drops to 0.74 (`HEAD_LINE_BY_TYPE = { brute: 0.74 }`, read through `headLineOf(z)` in `damageZombie`). A bullet or pellet hitting a brute at 0.74-0.78 of its height, which is its chin and lower face, used to count as a body hit and now counts as a headshot.
- What the player notices: aiming at a brute's face now scores the headshot (the extra damage, and the head can come off) far more often. Before, a shot at the middle of its head sat right on the line and roughly one in ten walking frames it counted as body.
- No other kind changes. The brute's body shots (0.74 and below) do what they did, and plates (GB-75) still take 0.45 of body hits.

Files:
- index.html (`HEAD_LINE`, `HEAD_LINE_BY_TYPE`, `headLineOf` and the headshot test in `damageZombie`)
- tools/tests/t111.js (new)

Tests:
- Both inline scripts pass `node --check`.
- t111 (new) 5/0. The brute at 0.72 is a body hit, at 0.76 a headshot (well over 1.5x body; a headshot at full health can take the head off, so the check doesn't pin the exact number), and at 0.90 a headshot. A shambler still treats 0.76 as body and 0.80 as head. `headLineOf` gives 0.74 for a brute and 0.78 for everyone else.
- Follow-up, 05:50 CT: the full suite caught one t111 fail. A headshot at full health can take the head off by chance, which reads as 999999 damage, and the 0.76 check also required 0.76 and the crown shot to match exactly. It now checks that each shot is a headshot on its own (more than 1.5x a body hit). The expectation is the same, the check is just no longer flaky. The fixed t111 passes 5/0.
- t93 and t37 pass. t90's perf check fails, and it also fails in Cursor's CU-78 baseline.
- How I picked 0.74: I sampled where each kind's head mesh sits while walking, as fractions of `hitH` at p50.
  - Brute: chin 0.746, centre 0.848 (p10 0.78), crown 0.95. At p10 the centre is on 0.78, which is the bug. 0.74 sits just under the chin, so the whole head now counts as head.
  - Shambler: chin 0.75, centre 0.86. 0.78 is fine.
  - Feral: chin 0.62, centre 0.776 (p10 0.71). Half its head shots count as body.
  - Leaper: centre 0.59, crown 0.75. This is under 0.78, so a leaper can never be headshot.
  - Spider: centre 0.71. Demon: centre 0.805.
  - For most kinds the shoulders and raised arms come up to head height, so a line based on height alone can't separate head from body cleanly.
