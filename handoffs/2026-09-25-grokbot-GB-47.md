## Detail
- The t71 failure on the tree: "all of them reached him (8 left)" and the bag count. The new diagnostic showed 8 skulls flying 5 m from the marine, phase prep, finisher over.
  - CL-41 starts prep when the finisher ends, and GP-34's `showDawnSummary` pauses the game at that moment.

  - each frame's step is `max(the old speed step, distance x realDt / timeLeft)`, where realDt is dt / 0.32 during slow motion.
  So every skull lands by its deadline, whatever the distance. The pickup rules are unchanged.
