# CL-179 · Claude · Mast follow-up: better skeleton and boots, ladder climb, electrocution smoke and fall, storm command

Jerry: "1. Let us improve the quality of the skeleton model and the boots. 2. Let us have a proper climbing animation for ladders. He should be unarmed when climbing. 3. I would like smoke to come out of the player after being electrocuted and when he falls off the ladder he falls spread eagle and lands on his back. 4. "storm" command triggers a rainstorm."

## What changed

- **world/lightning.js (mine):**
  - `buildRubberBoot(T)`, one boot merged into a single vertex-coloured mesh:
    - a tapered shaft with creases at the ankle
    - a black top band and pull loops
    - a rounded foot, toe and black toe bumper
    - a sole with a heel and tread lugs
  - `buildWornBoots` uses it, so each boot is now one draw instead of four.
  - `buildMastPerchParts` was redone:
    - **Skull:** cranium, face, sockets, brow, cheekbones, nose hole, teeth, dropped jaw.
    - **Clothes:** patrol cap with band and brim; jacket open on a spine, sternum and five pairs of ribs; dog tags on a chain; collar, shoulders, a patch, pockets; belt and buckle; a pouch.
    - **Limbs:** sleeves to the elbow with bone forearms (radius and ulna), palms and fingers; thighs with cargo pockets, knees, trousers bloused into the boots; shin bones, ankles, foot bones and toes under the boots.
    - **The rest:** a curved lanyard with a carabiner; the grating has slats, side rails, a toe board and a rust bloom.
  - Still plain meshes, which the host merges into one draw.
- **index.html, the ladder climb (every ladder: mast, watchtower, HQ):**
  - `ladderNow()` and `onAnyLadder()`.
  - `poseLadder()` takes over updateMarinePose while he is on a ladder:
    - He faces the rungs (`player.rotation.y` is the ladder's yaw).
    - He climbs hand over hand on a two-rung cycle, driven by the height actually climbed, so standing still on the ladder holds the grip.
    - The hands are placed on the rungs by `solveArmIK`; the reaching hand clears the rung. The opposite foot steps up; he looks up and leans in.
    - On the tower and HQ ladders, whose rungs are on his line, his body stands 0.34 m back off them.
    - `poseHands` closes his fists while climbing.
  - `updateLadderHolster()` slings the gun on any ladder, keeps it slung (a number key can't bring it back mid-climb) and gives it back when he steps off.
  - `fireHeld` is off on a ladder. The idle cigarette is not eligible on the HQ ladder.
- **index.html, the fall and the smoke:**
  - A lightning kill up the mast calls `startMastFall()`. `updateMastFall` runs in the game-over path, because the usual movement stops when the run ends: gravity, and a drift away from the mast.
  - `poseMastFall`:
    - he tips onto his back, arms (by IK) and legs flung wide
    - he lands flat with dust, a shake and a thump, then a little bounce
    - his limbs twitch for about 1.6 s
  - The burial fade waits about 2.4 s longer after a mast fall, so you see him land and lie there.
  - `startZapSmoke` and `updateZapSmoke`: any lightning strike that hurts him leaves dark and pale wisps rising off his chest, thick at first and thinning over 6 s (8 s after a mast kill). The smoke keeps going after he dies.
  - Both reset on spawn.
- **index.html, dev console `storm`:**
  - rain at full strength at once for 90 s
  - a fresh set of lightning strikes, even if it was already raining
- **Debug hooks on lightningDbg:** `fall()`, `smoke()`, `ladderNow()`, `holster()`.
- **world/lightning.test.mjs:** one new test (the boot is one mesh, yellow and black, standing on its sole).
