# CL-131 The Watchman MG, carried and fired - Claude, 2026-10-07

From Grokbot's GB-138 report: shouldered, the Watchman showed the mortar, its barrel only turned, nothing came out of it,
and it borrowed the AK's report.

## Changed
What the player notices:
- **Shouldered (T), it is the Watchman.** He carries the gun on his right shoulder with the tripod's legs folded forward
  under the barrel, the muzzle forward and up. Before, the mortar's tube was on his shoulder.
- **His arms while carrying either load.** They used to hold up an empty rifle grip. Now his right hand is up on what he
  carries and the left arm swings as when he has no gun. This goes for the mortar too.
- **The barrel tips with the aim.** Manned, the gun elevates on a hinge at the cradle (20 deg down to 35 deg up) while the
  tripod stays level; the rounds still leave the muzzle. Brandt's roof M240 tips down at its mark too.
- **Cases and links.** Each round throws a spent case down out of the receiver and a link out to the right (the belt
  feeds from the can on the left). Only one piece in four tinks as it lands, or a burst would be a wall of tinks.
- **Its own report** (`AudioSys.fireWeapon('m240')`): deeper and heavier than the AK, a bolt clack in front and a long
  low tail, each round a touch different. Brandt still uses the turret shot.
- **Brass is brass again (every gun).** The case tint added a random part of 0x181008 to the packed colour, which carried
  between channels: cases came out lime, pink and red as often as brass. `brassTint()` nudges each channel on its own
  (spawnCasing, the revolver's and the launcher's dumps, and the Watchman's).
