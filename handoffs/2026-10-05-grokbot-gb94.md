## What the player notices
- The pace varies. Most of a pack shambles or jogs, and about 1 in 8 sprints. Ferals are still the fast ones and brutes are still slow.
- No more conga line. A stream out of a cave mouth fans out on open ground and arrives from the front and both sides. Near the house, a build, a drop, deep water or anything the flow field routes around, they follow the field as before. Inside 4.5 m it's always the field, so they crowd him tightly.
- They aim at where he's going. Ferals and military read up to a second ahead; shamblers barely do. Running in a straight line gets cut off.
- They group up, then rush. At 7-14 m a body slows to 0.6x for up to 1.4 s while a pack is on its way. Once 4 are in that ring, everything within 14 m rushes at 1.3x for 1.3 s (at most once every 7 s). Two or three by themselves, or POI guards, never wait.
- Lunges: shamblers, drowned, ferals and military burst at 1.75x for 0.35 s when 1.6-4.2 m away, every 2.2-4 s.
- After a hit, a body more than 3 m away ducks sideways (and a little back) at 0.55x for 0.45 s, at most every 2.5-3.5 s. Brutes, demons and bombers don't flinch, and nothing in his face does.

## Decisions I made (Claude to confirm)
- TT spawns keep the old walk (z.ttSpawn means no GB-94), unless a test calls TT.hordeDbg.join(z). About 20 older checks place zombies and hold or time them, so a random pace would make them flaky. Every real spawn (waves, caves, ground rises, screamer calls, POI guards, bounties) joins.
- The slot changes only the heading. tx, dist and the type logic still use him, so a leaper still leaps at him, a demon still charges at him, a spitter still creeps from 15 m of him, and a bomber still sprints from 14 m. Flank, weave, spit, smash and boss tactics keep their own steering.
- The room nudge steers but never moves a body, so a test that holds a zombie still (speed 0) stays still.
- Waiting needs a pack: 4+ muster-type bodies between 7 and 30 m, and never POI guards. The first version let a lone POI guard stall at 12 m; t74's 1.2 s "come for him" line failed once under load.
- Below (the warrens) and the heart fight are untouched (updateBelow returns before the join).
- The wave director's spawning is unchanged (sizes, cadence, cap). "Bigger hordes" comes only from the crowding.

## Decisions I made (Claude to confirm)
- The machete's damage falls by 39% (85 to 52) to keep "twice the knife" exact. Jerry's rule, but it is a big nerf on top of the reach and cone. With the cone halved it is now narrower than the knife's. Its edge is double damage, 3 bodies a swing, and the quicker recovery.
- The stagger is a trait rolled once per zombie (28% duck, 72% stagger), not a fresh roll per hit, so each zombie behaves consistently. The exclusions stay as they were. Brutes and demons keep their weight; a bomber that flinches would be odd.
- The 0.9 s stagger cooldown means automatic fire slows a zombie (about 0.4 s of every 0.9 s at 0.3x) but can't freeze it in place.
- Nights after 20 stay at 19%. The night is the day counter (`day`) when the zombie first walks.
- spawnMedDrop and medDropChance stay: the warm-up and t35 still use spawnMedDrop, and medDropChance documents the old odds behind ZOMBIE_MED_DROPS = false.
