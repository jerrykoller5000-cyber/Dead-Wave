# GB-75 — R2 P-26: brutes wear plates

Changed:
- The brute (the only `armored` type) now shrugs off bullets: rounds, shotgun pellets, the knife and machete, and the chainsaw do 0.45 of their damage (was 0.72 from its 0.28 armour). An iron cave's extra armour still adds on top, to 0.65 at most.
- Blasts (the launcher, grenades, mortar shells, bomber blasts) and fire's own hits (the flamer's stream, burning ground, the flame turret) do 0.9 of their damage (was 0.72). The burn afterwards is untouched: it never went through armour and still doesn't.
- Every other zombie takes exactly what it took before.
- What the player notices: an AK mag into a brute pack barely dents it; the flamer or the launcher is the answer (P-29's scouting line from ChatGPT will say so).

- index.html (`spawnZombie` copies `armored`; `PLATE_CUT`, `PLATE_SOFT`, `plateResist` before `damageZombie`, which uses it for armoured zombies; `fire: true` on the flamer's hit, the ground-fire tick and the flame turret's hit)
