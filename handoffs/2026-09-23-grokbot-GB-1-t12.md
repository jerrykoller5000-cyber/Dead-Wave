## Fixes

1. **Callsign + prep** — Play and `tryPlace` need a live match.
2. **Wait out insertion (~9s)** — until `player.position` sticks after `p.set`; otherwise aim and teleports are overwritten mid-test.
3. **Turret on pillar** — `placeBuildAt('heavy', …, { piece: pil })` instead of side-aim `tryPlace`. A flat aim ray often hits terrain before the post (`aimGroundDist` clips the pick), so the ghost never sees the pillar. Same `resolveTarget` branch as a good reticle hit.
4. **Floor / turret heights** — use `T.ruleFor('floor').height` instead of hardcoded `0.25`.
5. **Roof / stairs floors** — `{ forceLv: 1 }` so they land on the walls while the marine is still on the ground (plain place boardwalks at 0).
