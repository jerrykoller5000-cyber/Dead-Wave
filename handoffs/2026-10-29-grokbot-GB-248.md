## What changed (index.html only)

**The feral's lunge** had no tell: hzLungeT only changed its speed, and D-87 caps a feral at his run, so a lunge
looked like its run. Now, against docs/animation-principles.md:
- Anticipation: a 0.18 s coil (hzCoilT) before the 0.35 s lunge. It slows to 35 % of its pace, drops into its
  knees, folds forward, draws both arms back and keeps its head up on him.
- The strike's line: the spring snaps out fast (ease-out over the first 30 %) and holds. The body stretches flat
  along the lunge (torso about 1.1-1.2 rad, from 0.5 in the run), both arms thrown at him (about -1.5 to -1.8 rad,
  from -0.5 to -0.7).
- Follow-through: a 0.24 s recovery (hzLungeRec) eases it back into its run.
- The lunge's speed, reach (LUNGE_IN/OUT), length and cooldown are unchanged; a stagger cancels a coil. Only the
  feral coils; the shambler's and soldier's lunges are as they were.
- GP-19850's applyFeralClaws owns the feral's arms and palms after the poses, so the arms' lunge pitch is added on
  top of it at its call site, and the claws' palm-down hold rides along. game/feral-motion.js is untouched.

**The colossus's slam** snapped both arms from -0.1 to -2.5 rad in one frame and froze them there. Now:
- Wind-up (0.75 s, the same length): it sinks into its legs (hips down 12 cm, knees bent) and heaves both arms up
  and back from wherever its walk had them, slow in to 0.6, shoulders out, torso arched back (-0.25). It holds them
  high, then from 0.82 brings them down before the hit (-2.6 to -1.6 rad at the damage frame).
- Follow-through (slamFollowT, 0.5 s, new): the fists drive on to the ground, the torso folds over them (+0.55),
  the knees take it, a held beat, then it settles into its walk (slow out). It stays planted at a quarter of its
  pace through it.
- One committed attack at a time: its claw swing no longer starts inside the slam's wind-up or follow-through (in
  the first after-pass a swing snapped the raised arms to -1.4 mid-wind-up), and a slam waits for a swing to finish.
- The slam's damage, radius, shove, build and house damage, timing and cooldown are unchanged. The AI's own arm
  snap (two lines setting -2.4) is gone; the pose owns the arms.
