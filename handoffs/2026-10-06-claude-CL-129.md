# claude — CL-129 The dead: better models and animation, the crawl and the hop — 2026-10-06
Changed:          Jerry's playthrough 1: "Better models and animation for all zombies. They need to be more engaging visually and behaviorally ... Zombies with lower extremities blown off need to crawl and have a crawling animation ... Zombies with one leg blown off need to hop towards player at 75% speed." GB-136 made the rules (z.crawling, z.hopping, the speeds, no limbless bodies); this task is the look and the motion.
                  - The crawl (no legs). It lies flat on its belly and hauls itself with its arms, one then the other. An arm swings out ahead with the elbow up, plants, and drags the body up to it while the other comes round. The chest rolls onto the pulling side, the hips lift a touch on each haul, and the head is tipped back to look at you. With one arm left it drags itself with that one, the body yawed toward it. Before, it was the walk cycle laid on its front.
                  - The hop (one leg). Two hops a stride on the leg it has: up with the knee tucked (the hips rise and fall about 18 cm), down with the knee taking the landing, a lurch forward on each landing, the body leaning over the good leg, and both arms out for balance. Before, it walked on one leg.
                  - The face. Every walker kind (all but the spider, the screamer, which has its own head, and the bosses) gets a jaw hanging open below the face, each with its own gape (the feral's widest). It has the dark of the mouth, a row of teeth top and bottom, and on about half of them blood down the chin and a stain on the chest. This replaces the thin slit they had for a mouth.
                  - The body. Strips of torn cloth hang from the hem, front and back, three to five of them. About a third show ribs through a torn chest (not the brute or the military, who wear plates and webbing). The shambler, the drowned, the military, the spitter and the bomber sag forward at the chest, with the head dropped and jutting and one shoulder lower.
                  - None of the new parts glow, so the zombie compactor folds them into the head's and the chest's merged meshes. They add vertices, not draw calls (t209 checks it).
                  - The reach. Closing on you (inside 5.5 m and moving), a shambler, drowned, military, spitter, bomber or brute brings both arms up and out at you. The arms are never quite together, and the elbows flex in and out like grasping hands. The reach blends in as it closes and the attack swing still takes over.
                  - The twitch and the lurch. Every few seconds a head jerks to one side and back, and now and then a moving shambler lurches forward and to a side as if it had nearly gone over.
