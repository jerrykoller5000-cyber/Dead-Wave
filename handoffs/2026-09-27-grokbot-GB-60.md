## How

- The recall: `beginWaveFinisher` arms `skullRecallUntil` on every night, not only when `skullKeepDay()`. `SKULL_KEEP_DAYS` still keeps day 1's no-pooling and no-expiry rules. The flight itself is GB-42/GB-47's: real time, home within 1.5 s, bagged with `skull-pickup {recalled: true}` before the finisher hands over, so the dawn banner counts it.
- The safe spot: `skullSafeSpot(x, z, y)` runs in `spawnSkullDrop`.
  - Over the sinkhole (within `LAKE_HOLE.grabR` + 1.5 m of its centre), the skull is moved out along the line from the centre to `grabR` + 1.5 m.
  - In a cave mouth, using the mouth's own frame (the same one `playerInCaveGrabBand` uses): if it's in front of the mouth by less than 2.05 m, or up to 12 m back in the tunnel, and within the mouth's width plus 1.5 m, it's set down 2.55 m out, keeping its sideways offset.
  - The height check uses the body's own height (`py` from killZombie), so a kill in the tunnel counts as in the mouth even though the ground reading there is the hill above it. A skull up on the hill over a mouth is left alone.
- t85 (night 3):
  - A skull over the sinkhole ends up more than grabR + 1.16 m out.
  - On all 6 caves, skulls dropped in the band (0.3 m and 1.5 m in) and in the tunnel (6 m in) end where every point he could pick them up from (within 1.1 m) is outside the band.
  - A skull on the hill over the mouth stays, and one in the open stays.
  - The last kill arms the recall on night 3. A skull left 30 m out is in the bag within 2.6 s with `recalled: true`, none are left on the field, and the dawn banner's skull count includes them.
