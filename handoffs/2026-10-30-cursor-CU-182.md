## The regression I made, and its fix

CU-181's `Box3.setFromObject` used each geometry's own bounding box moved by the matrix (three.js's non-precise way). On the test page that is not the box the old vertex walk made: the zombies' hit heights and the head line moved, so t111 (`feral: a round through its head is a headshot (20.00 vs chest 20.00)`, then leaper too) and t76 (`a walker walks (phase +2.9, idle 0.00)`) went red in CU-182's first full run. Both were green with the pre-CU-181 stand-in swapped back in for one slot hold, and green on the real renderer. CU-181's own 18-check run did not include them: neither names a box.

Fix: the stand-in makes the same box as before, vertex by vertex through `matrixWorld`, in scalars (the matrix read once, no `Vector3` per vertex, no kept box). The old allocation, not the walk, was nearly all of the cost: day 1's prep on the test page is 18.4 to 18.9 fps (frame 53 to 55 ms) and `r:cull` 1.31 ms, against 5.1 to 5.3 fps (188 to 196 ms) and 118 to 125 ms before CU-181. The suite was then rerun whole, which is the result above: t76, t111, t74, t222 green in the chunks.
