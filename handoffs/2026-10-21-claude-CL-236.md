# CL-236: the stowed guns, the holsters and the smoking props stay out of the marine's shadow proxy, and cast

AG-110 traced why `marine-shadow` still rebuilt 2-3 times in 30 s of night 20 after CL-234 (11-12 ms each):
(a) `carryGunCopy` set `g.userData.proxySkip = true`, then later `g.userData = { carryKind: kind }` wiped it, so every
stowed gun went back into the proxy; (b) the holster shell (`open-molded-holster`) is remade when the pistol moves
between hand and hip, and nothing above it had the flag; (c) with the idle props out of the proxy, the cigarette, pack
and lighter (and the folded minigun and revolver copies) cast no shadow at all, because only the proxy cast for them.

Changed:          index.html only. carryGunCopy: one `g.userData = { carryKind: kind, proxySkip: true }` (the early
                  line that was wiped is gone), and every visible opaque mesh in the copy casts its own shadow.
                  buildCarryRig: every carry spot's group (slings, holsters, pairs, hip, hipPair) gets `proxySkip`, so
                  whatever is made under it (shells, copies) stays out of the proxy. The idle props group keeps
                  `proxySkip`, and its meshes cast (the MeshBasic ember and smoke don't).
