## Detail

### Why
Jerry's item 3 asks for the grab, the drag, the chase and the pit tentacles to be "as polished as they can be". Every beat posed its joints straight off its own timeline, so wherever two beats met, a joint jumped. Examples: the chase handing over to the drag; the fight-to-limp switch (poseMarine(1) to poseMarine(0) in one frame); the tentacles going from sway to reach. The player also flipped 180 degrees at the start of the pit kill.

### What
- rigDamp: each joint eases toward the pose just written. It takes the shortest way round and is never faster than RIG_MAX_TURN, 12 rad/s. Rates: arm 11, leg 15, torso 7, head 9, jaw 16, claw 14. The marine's limbs use 12, his torso and head 10. The tentacle segment rate is 24 - 13*i/N, so the tip trails the root (follow-through). The cut under the black (t 4.4) takes the new scene's pose as it is.
- beginScriptedKill records the joints as they were on screen before the clean-stance reset, so the grab eases out of the run pose. Before, the arm jumped about 1 rad in the first frame.
- poseMarineGrabbed replaces the three poseMarine calls (snatch, drag, pit). Fight blends to limp.
- Player heading: the snatch eases over 0.45 s, the drag turns with the grab, and the pit turns with the yank.
- The dragging guardian leans into each heave.
- Tentacles break the water staggered by 0.07*(i%4) s, with an easeOutBack overshoot. They blend from sway into reach properly (tentSnap/tentBlend). Before, they jumped at w 0.06 and the second arm froze.
- Chase: footfall dust, addShake 0.16, and a body bob. The guardian's legs damp at rate 60 so the fast strides stay crisp.
