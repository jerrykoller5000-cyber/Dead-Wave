## 1. The chainsaw today

- **Buying:** a weapon like the others — `WEAPON_PRICE.chainsaw = 190` Cash (index.html:22689), bought at the kiosk, owned in `weaponOwned`, in `WEAPON_ORDER` last; armory mods: the bigger **tank** (`tank` slot, index.html:23397; `sawTankFitted` → 1.5× capacity, index.html:22775). It does not count in the carry-slot rotation that excludes it (index.html:26309).
- **Starting:** GP-188's starter pull — `createSawStart/stepSawStart/sawStartPose` (game/chainsaw-animation.js:1–24): one pull (~1.02 s timeline), phase `off → starting → ready`; interrupted starts finish a fresh pull. The saw can only bite when phase is `ready` (index.html:52254), with fuel > 0 and heat not over (below). Equipped-but-not-started it still idles audibly (`chainsawEngine(sawWant && !hold, …)`, index.html:20738).
- **Fuel:** `sawFuel` in **seconds of cutting**: base 60 s (`SAW_FUEL_BASE`, index.html:22752), 90 s with the tank fitted. Burns 1/s running, ×(1 + 1.5) while biting (`SAW_BITE_FUEL`, index.html:22759; drain at index.html:52277). Refuel: kiosk gas pack (+45 s, $18, index.html:22728/27770; explicit `buyAmmo('chainsaw')` path), objective/shop grants fill it (`if (w === 'chainsaw') sawFuel = sawFuelMax()`, index.html:27733), gathering picks up gas into the same pool. HUD shows "Gas N / MAX · Running/EMPTY" (index.html:48875). Saved/restored with the run's weapon state (index.html:59535).
- **Heat:** the second limiter (GB-52): biting climbs 1/8 per s (8 s flat-out in a body), running 1/40, cooling 1/3; at 1 it overheats — won't start until cooled to 0.45 (index.html:52224ff, 52278ff).
- **The swing:** it is a *hold*, not a swing — hold fire with phase ready → `chainsawTick()` every `interval 0.12` s (index.html:22618, 52295ff). The WEAPON_STATS: damage 14, melee, auto, **reach 3.1, arc** (index.html:22618).

## 2. What it hits

`chainsawTick` (index.html:51342), each tick, in order:

- **Zombies:** within `reach + z.radius` and in front (`dot > arc`), nearest first, **at most 3 bodies a tick** (`SAW_MAX_BODIES`), each taking `14 × dmgMult × meleeSizeMul` with gib bursts (`damageZombie(..., {kind:'chainsaw'})`, index.html:42368ff for the gib path). Biting sets `sawBiteT = 0.3` (the fuel/heat "in a body" window).
- **Trees:** every standing/stump tree within `reach + trunkRadius` and `dot > 0.35` takes `damageTree(t, …, 1, …)` (per tick, not per second). `damageTree` (index.html:53387) sheds leaf pieces by health and, at health out, `beginTreeFall` — **yes, he fells trees with it today**; it is the felling tool (shots also damage trees, but the saw is the deliberate one). A felled tree: falls, stump stays, canopy drops, and the trunk becomes a **log**: `addLogSolids` (index.html:54032) lays a chain of `kind:'log'` solids along the fall line (0.8 m spacing, radius trunk+0.2, heights from the lie) — "solid to the player, zombies and bullets" (comment index.html:53755).
- **Log life:** the log (solids and its lying trunk mesh) stays `TREE_LOG_LIE = 120` s (index.html:53757), then the tree-update sinks it and `removeLogSolids` (index.html:54050) splices the solids out. `restoreTree` also removes them. **Both transitions fire `dw-log`** (`state:'down'/'gone'`) — and today's flow field both reads `worldSolids` as blocked cells *and force-rebuilds on `dw-log`* (MU-14 §2; the field's dynamic layer). So a fallen log already blocks the dead for two minutes, unprompted — the shortcut feature is about *marked, permanent* logs and a *player-initiated* reopening, not a new blocker.
- Bugs (`meleeBugs`) and Training dummies (`trainingMelee`, index.html:51342ff tail) round out the hit list. It does not cut rocks, builds, or logs today — a log in the arc is just a solid his body resolves against.

## 3. Its noise today

**The saw has no hearing radius.** `SHOT_HEARING` (index.html:43684) lists nine gun kinds; chainsaw is not among them, and the saw never calls the noise paths: not `shotHearingRadius` (index.html:43688), not `stir.shot` (index.html:56946 — gunshots only), not `belowFight.noise` (index.html:56667 — explosions). What hears the saw today: **nothing in the zombie systems**. The dead react to the saw only by being bitten (damage aggro) or by sight. `AudioSys.chainsawStart/chainsawEngine/chainsawDryPull` are player-side sound only. So "time and noise that draws the dead" (W2.1) is a genuine addition: the natural plug is a *periodic* noise while biting (the saw is loudest under load) — e.g. every tick or every 0.5 s of cutting, `stir.shot(SAW_HEARING, …)`-shaped with a chainsaw entry (radius a design call: louder than the AK's 50 feels right for a running saw under load; an idle-revving saw should be quieter — two radii, or one radius only while `sawBiteT > 0`). Who hears: POI guards sleeping at posts are the ones a shortcut-noise should wake (that's the tactical price), plus stir for below. Grokbot owns the numbers; the hook point is `chainsawTick`'s biting branch.

## 4. Where "cut through this log" plugs in

The spec: certain fallen logs (marked — across trails/gaps, W2) can be cut through by the saw, taking time, making noise, opening a gap for him *and the dead*. Every piece has a home today:

- **Which logs:** the feature needs a marker on the tree/log record — the candidates are logs lying across trails (`distToPath` is the test every other system uses, MU-14: a felled tree whose log line crosses a PATHS polyline within its half-width). Two ways to choose: (a) *any* log lying across a trail can be cut (emergent — he fells where he likes), (b) W1/W2 pre-place marked logs across designed gaps. (a) is nearly free and matches "marked fallen logs" if "marked" means *visibly marked when near* (a prompt is a marking). **Recommend (a)** with the prompt as the marking; pre-placed deadfall (b) is W1 world-building that reuses exactly the same cut code when it lands.
- **The prompt:** a *context* target like the fuel can/Hush (MU-12's `actionTarget` verbs): "Cut through — hold E (chainsaw)" when within ~2 m of a cuttable log's midspan with the saw owned (equipped? owning should suffice; the hold can auto-equip or require the saw in hand — require `currentWeapon === 'chainsaw'`, it's the honest read). Standing *in* the gap he wants to open is where he stands to cut it.
- **The hold:** the objective claim's hold-E pattern (`objHold` machine, MU-12 §3) with a saw twist: progress runs only while the saw is running (phase ready, fuel left). **Time:** ~4 s of biting is my proposal (long enough to be a decision, short enough not to be a chore — about 8% of a base tank: `4 × 2.5` fuel ≈ 10 s of gas). If fuel dies mid-cut, progress *holds* (like the pour) rather than resets.
- **The cut:** two kerfs — the gap doesn't need the whole log removed: on completion, remove the log solids within ±1 m of his position (`t.logSolids` are individual `addSolid` records with positions — splice those, leave the two log ends as stumps of log), split the lying trunk *mesh* at the same place (the trunk is part of the tree's grown geometry: the cheap version hides the midspan by collapsing that mesh section the way leaf pieces collapse, or accepts a two-piece look by scaling — Grokbot's visual call; the *solid* removal is the functional part and is exact).
- **Noise:** §3's biting noise, whole cut — that's the price that draws the dead to the new gap, which is the feature working as designed (W2: "a cut opening a gap for him *and the dead*" — symmetric).
- **Telling the dead's field:** `removeLogSolids` already fires `dw-log` (`state:'gone'`) and the fine field force-rebuilds on it. The plug is to make the *partial* removal fire the same event (a new `state:'cut'` or reuse `'gone'` with the remaining count) — the field doesn't care why solids changed, only that they did. **And** this is exactly GB-264's blocked-cells source: when MU-18's valley field lands, its topology delta for a cut is "these cells unblocked" derived from the same solids list. One event, both fields.
- **Skill/progress hooks:** sawing already has its feel systems (bite window, heat, chain advance `advanceChainsawChain`) — the cut reuses `sawBiteT`-style feedback; a cut shouldn't spike heat to overheat in 4 s (heat bite rate gives 0.5 in 4 s — fine unmodified).

**What it must not break:** the log's 120 s sink timer (a cut log's ends should sink on the same schedule — keep them on the tree's record), `restoreTree` (a restored tree clears all its solids already), and the felling direction choice (he aims the fall; a log across the trail is often *his own doing* — that's fine, cutting his own log back open is the loop).
