## How the songs loop
- **Nights and the heart.** ElevenLabs made each song from the composition plan in
  review/elevenlabs-music/manifest-music.json: seven sections of whole bars (stalk 8, dropA 16, riffB 16, break 8,
  dropA2 16, bridge 16, climax 16) at the song's own tempo, and the lengths came back exact. So the song is already on
  the section player's grid. Each take sits about 24 ms late (beat tracker, calibrated on the old exact-grid songs),
  so it is shifted forward by its own measured offset (the median, 25 ms, where the beat was too loose to measure).
  Each section loops on its own in the player (loopStart/loopEnd), so its last 25 ms are cross-faded into the 25 ms
  that lead into its start: the wrap sounds like the music carrying on. A climax that rings out at the song's end loops
  only its full-volume bars (12 of 16 on the songs that fade; 16 where it stays up). The flow is the old one: stalk while
  the horde is out of sight, dropA/riffB/dropA2/bridge in rotation once one is near, the break in a lull, the climax for
  the last 3. The heart keeps its phases (1: dropA/riffB, 2: dropA2/bridge, 3: climax).
- **Day fights.** These were plain prompt tracks with no plan (2:00, an intro and an ending). The real tempo was
  measured (112.3 / 120.2 / 129.2 / 139.7 / 152.0 BPM), and the loop is the longest stretch of whole bars whose start
  and end sound most alike (chroma + timbre, a bar either side), at least 16 bars, starting after the intro and ending
  6 s before the take ends; the end is nudged (±10 ms) to line up the waveform, then cross-faded. Only the loop is
  saved, so it loops natively from 0 like the chip loops did (no sections, so no decoded memory).
- **Loudness.** Each song is set to the old songs' loudness at their gains (old nights -12.7 LUFS at 1.6, old chip
  loops -15.25 LUFS at 3.0), so the mix is where it was.

## What the game plays now (music.json)
- waveByDay: day 1 → elevenlabs/night-01 (First Blood), day N → elevenlabs/night-NN for 2-20. Day 14 → night-14
  (Fog Night), and specials.fog → night-14 too.
- dayFight.tracks: elevenlabs/day-horde-1..5, same horde sizes [1, 4, 9, 16, 26].
- fight_heart: the name core/audio.js uses for the heart, now on elevenlabs/heart_sections.ogg.
- sections: only the new songs and calm_day (the old night songs are left out of sections on purpose: the player loads
  every sectioned track that is not a wave track, so listing them would decode all of them).
- Everything else (pools, stings, calm, menu) is unchanged.

## Backup
- assets/soundtrack/music-old.json is the old music.json, byte for byte. Every old song file is still in
  assets/soundtrack; nothing was moved or deleted.
- "Switch music (old - new).bat" at the game's root: if music.json is the ElevenLabs set it copies music-old.json
  over it, otherwise it copies music-elevenlabs.json. Reload the game after. Note for the crew: an edit to music.json
