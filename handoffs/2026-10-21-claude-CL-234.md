# CL-234: the marine's shadow proxy leaves out what comes and goes

**What AG-109 showed (CL-232 in):** night 20 High: 54.2 / 56.4 / 58.6 fps, 1 % low 28.7 / 39.7 / 40.9, no hitches, one
zombie-skin build a run. Of the 10-18 frames over 25 ms left, the biggest repeat was the marine: `marine` 11-14 ms
and ~12 MB of heap, then a `marine-shadow` node build the frame after: the shadow proxy (CL-140: one SkinnedMesh
casts his ~80 parts' shadow) rebuilt three times in 30 s. It rebuilds when the set of his visible parts changes and
stays so for a second. Standing still in the bench, he goes into the smoking idle: the gun is stowed (a copy appears on
the hip, chest or back), the pack, lighter and cigarette come out and go away again.

**Changed (index.html):**
- `marineProxySkip` also skips anything under a node with `userData.proxySkip`: the stowed gun copies
  (`carryGunCopy` marks them) and the idle props (`marine-idle-props`, marked after createMarineIdle). They cast their own
  shadows, as his weapons in hand always have.
- `TT.marineShadowDbg()` adds `builds` (how many times the proxy was built) and `lastDiff` (the parts that came and went
  before the last build), so the bench can say what still changes it.

Tests (cloud): t270 5/5 on the real renderer (the page boots and draws), t146 43/43, t218 21/21 on the test page.
Not verified: night 20 on the 5080 (AG-110: the proxy's builds and lastDiff, and the spikes).
