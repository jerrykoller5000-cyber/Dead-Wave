## Cause
updateMarinePose always posed both arms at the gun hold (ARM_L_BASE / ARM_R_BASE + ELBOW_*_BASE) in every branch: idle, walk/run and airborne. With a gun, holdWeapon() then IKs both hands onto the grip, so this was never visible. Unarmed, weaponMount is hidden, so holdW fades to 0 and no IK runs, which left the arms in the empty gun hold reaching forward: "arms straight out".

## How
- The new block runs at the end of the arm posing in updateMarinePose and blends (POSE.relax, eased at rate 9) from whatever the branch chose toward the relaxed targets. Because it's a blend, switching weapons, holstering and drawing fade smoothly.
- relaxWant = unarmed && !swimming && !mortarMounted && !mortarCarried && !climbingTower && !gameOver && no knife anim && no gesture (throw/jab) && the gun isn't in his hand during a marineDraw move. So during a U-holster stow the arms stay in the hold until the gun leaves his hand, then relax. On a draw they come up as soon as the gun is in his hand.
- Targets: shoulder pitch U.x - swing, twist +-U.y (thumbs forward), out from the body +-(U.z + sway), elbow U.elbow minus a forward-swing bend. Walk swing = POSE.thighL/R * 0.75 (run 0.95, elbow -0.95 more), so the arms move opposite to the legs. Idle sway is +-0.03 rad pitch, with the arms out of phase, plus +-0.012 out.
- Frame order is unchanged: reload/knife offsets, holdWeapon, marineDraw and marineIdle still run after updateMarinePose and override as before.
- t183: armed, the grip is in front of the shoulder. Holstered, each fist is >0.4 m below its shoulder, 0.4-0.8 m off the ground, within 0.2 m fore/aft of the shoulder and 0.22-0.5 m out to the side. The idle sway range is 0.01-0.2 rad. Walking, the arms swing >0.25 rad, L/R are anti-correlated (< -0.6), each arm is anti-correlated with its own leg (< -0.4), and the arms never pitch up forward. Drawn again, the gun hand is out front.
