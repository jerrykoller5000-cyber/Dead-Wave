# GB-257: Lasers that ride the gun (J8, J9)

Grokbot, 2026-10-31. index.html SHA-256 C01E428BC41472E0EA449013543CCB5295AEBF81F8ED2D8F2630B15C95E4E51D (updateLasers only). Backup of the start: qa/2026-10-31-GB-257/index.before.html (418412EB..., the GB-256 page). No git.

## The cause
- J8: updateLasers aimed the beam from the muzzle at the aim point (weaponAimPoint) every frame and turned it in the gun's space to face that point, so when the gun kicked or swayed the beam swung back onto the cursor instead of following the barrel.
- J9: the beam's on-condition had `!reloading`.

## The change (index.html, updateLasers)
- The beam leaves the muzzle along the bore (the gun's local +Z, or -Z if the muzzle sits behind the origin) every frame and stops on the first material along that line (laserStop, unchanged). Its reach is the aim point's distance x1.25 + 1 m, so at rest it meets the cursor where it did and a kicked beam can run on to what it now points at.
- `!reloading` is out of the on-condition: the beam stays on and rides the gun through the reload, in both views.
- Exact-line edits (qa/2026-10-31-GB-257/edit.mjs), LF kept, no BOM, the three old CRLF lines untouched.
