# GB-100 - Zombies and the director go for the nearest living player (R2, P-103)

Changed: Every zombie now picks the nearest living player once a frame (`zTarget(z)`, which calls CU-63's `nearestPlayer(x, z, { alive: true })`) and chases, steers, flanks and bites that player. The 42 per-zombie `nearestPlayer(z.mesh.position...)` reads inside `updateZombies` now go through it. Zombie hits (melee, leaper, demon, colossus, the burning touch) land on the player they reached: `damagePlayer(..., p)`. The flow field is still centred on the local marine, but every other living player on the grid is also a destination, so a zombie walks to whichever player is nearest on foot. On the director side: ground risers stay clear of every player, the wave picks the cave nearest any player, the deeper spawn at the mouth and the "never on top of him" hold check the nearest player, the screamer's pull works from the player nearest the screamer, and the spawn log's `pd` is the distance to the nearest player. The first-seen card (GB-111) is for the marine at this screen, so it reads `localPlayer`. What the player notices: nothing when playing alone, which is exactly the goal. With a second player (a dummy for now), the zombies near him go for him and leave the marine alone.
