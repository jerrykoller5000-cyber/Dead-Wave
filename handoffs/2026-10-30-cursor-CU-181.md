## What changed

- `Box3.setFromObject(o, precise = false)`: for each mesh, `geometry.boundingBox` (made when missing, or when the position attribute's `version` or `array` is not the one it was made from), `applyMatrix4(matrixWorld)`, union. It walked every vertex of every mesh with a new `Vector3` each. `setFromObject(o, true)` is the old walk, and a geometry with no `computeBoundingBox` still takes it.
- `BufferAttribute.version`: moves with `needsUpdate = true` and with the setters (`setX`, `setY`, `setZ`, `setW`, `setXY`, `setXYZ`, `setXYZW`, `set`, `copyArray`), so a kept box notices an edit made that way.
- `BufferGeometry.computeBoundingBox`: a scalar min and max (no `Vector3` a vertex), and it records the version it saw. `_xf` (`translate`, `rotate*`, `scale`, `applyMatrix4`, `applyQuaternion`) refreshes a box already kept, as three.js does.
- Instanced meshes keep the old meaning (the base geometry's box, not the instances').
