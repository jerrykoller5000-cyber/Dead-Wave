### How the nights were played
- `node tools/nightsim.mjs [nights] --jobs N --dt 0.0333 --cap 900 --out qa/nightsim/x.json [--god]`. Each night runs on its own fresh page of the test build (fakethree, like npm test): a real match start, a wait for the insertion to end, then setDay(n-1), startPrep and hqStartWave. The game clock is pinned to fixed 1/30 s steps and the loop runs as fast as the machine allows, so a 14-minute night takes about 10 minutes of real time. Every night is capped at 900 s of game time and also has a real-time cap. A night that hits the cap is ended, flagged "capped", and keeps its partial data. All times in the tables are game time.
- The marine is scripted. He stands at the HQ window, just off the west wall of the house, with the loadout a player plausibly has by that night and no turrets or walls. His reserve ammo is topped up. He aims at the nearest zombie he can see past the house. He uses his close gun under 5 m (kept to 6.5 m), knifes while reloading when something is in his face, and walks back to his post when he's knocked off it. Godmode is off. A hit that would kill him is logged as a death (with the cause and what was round him), and he's set back to full HP so the night can go on.
- Column meanings. Pushes: the pacing pushes, with their start times. Breathers: the lulls between pushes, in seconds. Worst pile-up: the most zombies within 1.5 m of one zombie. Crowd on him: the most within 2.5 m of him. Inside him: bodies overlapping his and the deepest overlap. Stuck: moved under 1 m in 15 s while more than 8 m from him. Walkers and spit holders are counted apart; a spider holding its band and skittering sideways counts as a spit holder, which is intended behaviour. Lost: the night stalled (everything spawned and no kill for 60 s), so what was left was counted and removed. Damage taken: HP and armour hits, total. Knees: GB-50 knockdowns on him.
- Caveats. One bot and one post, with no building, turrets or movement tactics. Deaths swing a lot from run to run on the same night, because one early crowd on the wall snowballs: night 19 went from 0 to 109 deaths across the final four runs, and night 16 from 0 to 96. Use lengths and spawned totals as the solid numbers, and deaths and damage as rough ones. The final runs are listed below.

### The fixes (index.html, zombie AI; combat only)
1. **Spit holders need a line** (the `tactics === 'spit'` block; in practice only spiders use it). Twice a second, while the spider is inside its hold range, it checks whether it can actually be shot. It holds and skitters only with a line; otherwise it keeps coming along the flow field. After coming round a corner it keeps coming for 1.2 s (lineGo), so its skitter doesn't carry it straight back behind the corner.
2. **Flankers don't flank through the HQ** (the `tactics === 'flank'` block, plus a new slab-test helper, `segCrossesHouse`). A flank point is off to the player's side, so flankers steer straight at it with no flow field. When the point was across the house, they walked into the middle of a wall and stood there. Now, if the house is on either leg (zombie to point, or point to player), the flanker goes for the player and the flow field takes it round.
3. **No blind back-off.** A spider inside its back-off distance (6.5 m) backs off only when it has a line. Before this, it backed out of range, walked back in, and rocked there.
4. **A line with room, and a straight back-off.** The line check is now two lines from its body to his gun height, 0.45 m either side (his rounds leave the gun, not his chest). The back-off goes straight away from him instead of reversing the flow-field heading, which at a corner took it back round the corner.
