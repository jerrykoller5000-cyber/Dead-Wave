## Detail
- The warning. `triggerCavePoke` runs every check it had before (phase, modal, range and line of sight, grab band, GB-39 busy cave, no chase running). If the run has not been warned yet, it calls `caveWarningBeat` instead of starting the chase. That:
  - publishes `cave-guardian` `{ phase: 'aggro', warning: true }`, so CL-22's screech plays as before;
  - opens the eyes with `caveWarn(cave, 2)` (staring, pulsing, seen from 140 m, a burst of dust);
  - nudges the camera with `addShake(0.45)`, scaled down to 40% at 30 m.
  It does not mark the cave used.
- After 3 s the eyes go back to the level the director had (`c.pokeEyesPrior`). If `warnActiveCaves` resets the mouths during those 3 s (the alarm, say), `reapplyCavePokeEyes` records the director's new level and keeps the eyes open until the 3 s are up.
- The grace. Pokes within 2.5 s of the warning return false, so one burst of fire can't be both the warning and the chase. After that, the next poke of any cave brings the guardian out.
- Once a run. `startPrep` now calls `resetCavePokeDay(day === 1)`. Day 1 clears `cavePokeWarned`; later days keep it. Each day still clears the used caves and the day's warning record.
- The chase (GB-A8). It moves in steps of at most 0.4 m so a thin wall can't slip between frames. Out past the lip:
  - `caveChaseSteer` looks 3.5 m ahead through `propsNear` for the nearest trunk, stump, rock or landmark solid on its line (the same height test the zombies use) and bends away from it;
  - `resolveStaticProps` pushes it off anything it still brushes;
  - `caveChaseSmash` tests each build near it with `pushOutOfBuild` (so decks it runs under, open doors, spikes and mines don't count). Any build it touches takes `damageBuild(b, hp + 1, 'guardian')`: chunks, the break sound, `removeBuild`, and whatever stood on it comes down. That adds camera shake if he is near, and 0.14 s at 30% speed.
  Inside the cave it runs straight as before.
- t59 now covers:
  - the first poke only warns: aggro with warning:true, warned, eyes at level 2, shake > 0.15, cave not spent;
  - the rest of the burst is ignored;
  - the eyes go back to the prior level after 3 s;
  - the second poke brings the chase (aggro with warning:false);
  - it goes round a 0.9 m solid on its line (closest 1.35 m or more, steered > 0);
  - a wall put down 2.5 m ahead of it gets smashed;
  - the second cave comes straight out (already warned);
  - the run stays warned after `startPrep`.
- t69: the round that hits a zombie also doesn't warn. The first clean miss is the warning; the next one, after the grace, brings the chase.
