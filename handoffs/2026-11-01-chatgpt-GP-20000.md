# ChatGPT — GP-20000 WebGPU foliage bundle pilot — 2026-11-01

Changed: Added an opt-in WebGPU `THREE.BundleGroup` for ground foliage chunks (`?bundles=1`). It uses `static=false` so wind and trampler uniforms refresh, and rerecords after chunk visibility, geometry/membership, or graphics-preset changes. `?bundles=0` and the default use the previous path; WebGL never creates the bundle. The pilot did not improve frame cost, so it is not enabled by default and no further bundle rollout is proposed.
Files: `index.html` (foliage chunks); `review/GP-20000/day5-{off,on}-{1,2,3}.{txt,json}` and `night20-{off,on}-{1,2,3}.{txt,json}` (benchmark logs and parsed JSON).
Tests: Extracted inline module `node --input-type=module --check` → exit 0. Twelve real WebGPU `tools/bench.mjs` runs through the machine-wide game-test slot, High preset, `--prof --cpu`, 12 measured seconds each: three alternating `?bundles=0`/`?bundles=1` pairs for day 5 and three for night 20. All twelve exited 0. Mean paired bundle-on minus off: day 5 reported draws -15.67, `r:render` +0.07 ms, `PassNode.updateBefore` total CPU +0.09 ms, 1% low -2.43 fps; night 20 draws -20.33, render +0.41 ms, PassNode +0.35 ms, 1% low -3.07 fps. FPS stayed at the 60 fps cap in both scenarios; no throughput benefit was measured. The 12 source JSON lines are preserved in the review folder. `npm test` not run: the coordinated full suite belongs to Cursor/Claude.
