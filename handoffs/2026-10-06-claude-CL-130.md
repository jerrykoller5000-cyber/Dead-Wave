# claude — CL-130 The dead: each kind its own sounds — 2026-10-06
Changed:          Jerry's playthrough 1: "We need unique sounds for the different zombies."
                  - A throat for each kind. The voices were sawtooth tones sliding about: they read as machines, and the kinds blurred together. Each walker kind now speaks through deadVoice (core/audio.js), which works like a throat:
                    - a buzz (a sawtooth and its twin a few cents off, for a ragged, doubled voice);
                    - shaped by three formants of a vowel that can move during the sound (an "uh" moan, an "ah" snarl, an "oo" through water or a gas mask, an "ee" shriek);
                    - with a rasp (the buzz chopped at 9-80 Hz: from a death rattle to a growl), a slow pitch wobble, a breath of noise, and for the drowned and the military a lowpass (water, the mask).
                  - Each kind has three voices in DEAD_VOICES: at rest, hunting you and swinging.
                    - Shambler: a low "uh-oo" moan, a rougher "ah" when it hunts you, a short bark on the swing.
                    - Feral: a rattling snarl, then three quick barking snarls in a row when it hunts.
                    - Leaper: a hiss with a thin "ee" in it, and a rising shriek when it jumps.
                    - Drowned: a gargled "oo" under water, with its bubbles.
                    - Military: a moan muffled by the mask, with its mask breathing and dead radio.
                    - Brute: a deep "oh" bellow, and a roar on the swing.
                    - Spitter: a wet, phlegmy "eh", and a hawk before it spits.
                    - Screamer: a thin wail.
                    - Bomber: a belching "oh", with its fizz and ticking.
                    The spider, the demon, the colossus and the guardian keep what they had.
                  - Their feet. Before, only the heavy kinds were heard walking. Close up (inside 13 m), the rest are heard on their feet too: a shambler's scuff with every other foot dragged, the feral's and the leaper's quick bare patter, the drowned's squelch, the military's boots, a hopper's thump and a crawler's slapped hand and dragged body. One step sound at a time across the horde, so a crowd is a texture, not a din.
