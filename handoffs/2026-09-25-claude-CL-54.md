## The ideas, best first

1. **Tonight's scouting report (small; Grokbot + ChatGPT).** GB-53 already freezes tonight's plan at prep (caves, pushes, the trick). Put it on the HQ board and the minimap: "East Cave, two pushes, runners". Building then has a point: wall the side they come from. Cheapest idea here, and it makes the day plan the night.
2. **Bounties on the HQ board (medium; ChatGPT + Grokbot).** One or two a day, only on the board (no pop-ups, per GP-39): "Clear the trapper's camp before dusk, +40 skulls", "Bring back the ranger's radio part". The guards already exist (GB-43); a bounty just picks a POI and pays for it. It gives each day a destination.
3. **Noise draws company (medium; Grokbot).** Gunfire in daylight calls a few roamers from the nearest treeline; a knife kill makes no noise. Exploring gets a little tense, and the toned-down knife (GB-52) gets a job: the quiet option.
4. **Finds worth the walk (medium; Grokbot + ChatGPT).** Rare crates at the far POIs with things the kiosk doesn't sell: an extended mag, a scope, a flare gun that lights a cave mouth for a night. That's a reason to go far instead of looting the same near sheds.
5. **Damage that carries over (small; Grokbot).** Builds keep last night's damage into the day and show it (cracks, a leaning wall), so repairs at the kiosk are a real morning decision. The repair systems are already there (prep-repairs).
6. **Hunting and fishing (medium; Claude, the world and wildlife).** Deer and rabbits already live in the woods and fish in the lake. Meat as a small heal, or for sale at the kiosk. It's a calm day loop that fits the new ambient day track.
7. **Weather days (medium; Claude).** A fog morning (short sight, a bonus crate somewhere), a storm afternoon (lightning shows zombies between the trees). Variety for the same map, and it pays off the sky work.
8. **The horde gets restless (small, but it's a design change; Jerry's call).** Prep has no clock by design. If that ever feels too loose, a gentle push after a long wait: a few scouts probe the fence. It stays optional; say no if the no-clock day is the point.

For Saturday, 1 and 2 would show best: both use systems that already exist, and both make the day lead into the night.
