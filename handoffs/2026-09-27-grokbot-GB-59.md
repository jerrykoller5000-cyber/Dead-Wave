# GB-59 - The fog cull (CU-42 brought back)

Changed: At night, a zombie past the fog's far edge (plus 8 m) is no longer drawn. It still walks, paths and attacks. It shows again once it is 4 m back inside that line, and also when it's killed or when the switch is off. Some zombies are always drawn: the guardian, a motion body that has the blow, anything mid-attack or charging (and for 2 s after), and a spitter holding its firing line. By day the fog reaches 640 m, so in practice nothing is culled. The player should notice nothing except the frame rate: these zombies were already solid fog colour.

## How
- **Where the line sits:** `fogCullPrep()` runs once per updateZombies. The line is `scene.fog.far + 8` for linear fog (FogExp2 or no fog means no cull), and it takes the camera's forward vector.
- **Depth, not range:** `syncZombieFogCull(z, dt)` runs right after `syncZombieFarLod(z)` for each live zombie. It measures depth along the camera's view axis, because three fogs by view depth. A zombie 90 m off to the side can be only 50 m deep and still visible on screen, so range would pop it (t96 checks this).
- **Hysteresis:** a zombie hides past the line and shows again at the line minus 4 m. It only writes `mesh.visible` when that state changes (`z.fogHidden`), so nothing else's visibility is touched each frame.
- **Always drawn:**
  - `typeKey === 'guardian'` or `guardianPlanned`;
  - `z.body.awake`;
  - `attackWindup`, `swingT`, `_pendingAttack`, `chargeWind` or `chargeT`, which also set `fogKeepT = 2` so a zombie clawing a far wall doesn't blink between swings;
  - a spitter holding its line: `lineClear` with `lineT > -0.5`. The spit code only re-checks the line while the spitter is inside its hold range, so a line that has gone stale means it isn't holding.
- **What it builds on:** the far LOD (`ZOMBIE_FAR_M` 40 m) already drops shadows, freezes joint matrices and skips limb animation. The cull adds the draw skip (the renderer doesn't walk hidden subtrees), which is where CU-42's 897 draws went.
- **After death:** `killZombie` un-hides the mesh, so a corpse is there when he walks up to it. Pooled meshes come back through spawnZombie (visible = true) on a fresh zombie record.
- **The switch:** `TT.setFogCullDbg(false)` turns the cull off and un-hides everything, for before/after measurements.
- **t96 at night (worldTime 0, fog far 65, line 73):**
  - near and inside zombies are drawn; one past the line is hidden;
  - the off-axis zombie is drawn;
  - a hidden zombie still walks (over 0.5 m in 1.5 s);
  - 2 m back inside the line it stays hidden, and at 6 m it's drawn again;
  - the guardian, an attacker and a spitter with its line are drawn; the attacker hides after the 2 s hold, and the spitter hides once its line goes stale;
  - a zombie killed while hidden leaves a drawn corpse;
  - the switch works off and on;
  - by day (line 648) everything is drawn;
  - no page errors.
