## 1. The eyewear (new: `ui/marine-eyewear.js`, `ui/marine-eyewear.test.mjs`; index.html makeMarine)

**Why it no longer fit.** CL-97 placed the four styles as fixed boxes for the old head. GP-105/GP-163 rebuilt the
face about 3 cm further back (the face under the eyes is at z 0.14; the lenses were at 0.176 to 0.192), so every
style floated in front of the face. The lower edges also ran into the balaclava over the nose (its top edge rises to
y 1.40 there), and the goggle strap was a square box round the head.

**Now** each style is built from the head itself (the HEAD_RINGS skull from marine-face.js, its face depth, the mask's
top edge, the nose, brows and ears):
- **Teardrop shades (aviators):** gold wire rims on a gently curved front, a straight brow bar and an arched lower
  bridge, nose pads on little arms resting beside the nose, wire temples along the side of the head, over the ear,
  hooked behind it.
- **Classic shades (wayfarers):** thick acetate frames with the top outer corners lifted, a keyhole bridge, two
  silver studs, thick temples running straight back over the ears.
- **Wraparounds (pit vipers):** one mirrored shield on the skull's own curve with a nose cut, a white bar on top,
  thick white temples.
- **Ballistic goggles:** foam-backed frame sealed round the eyes on the face's curve, one smoke lens with a nose cut,
  a strap round the head just above the ear tops, clips where it meets the frame.

Lens to eye: 14 to 26 mm by style (it was ~35 to 50). Nothing inside the skull, the mask, the nose or the ears; the
only touches are the aviator nose pads on the nose and the ear hook behind the ear, as real glasses sit. The
helmet's front rim (y 1.48) clears all four. The NVG still hides them when down. In the game each style is 2 to 4
meshes after makeMarine's merge.

The module keeps its own copy of HEAD_RINGS (so index.html never depends on an export from marine-face.js);
**`ui/marine-eyewear.test.mjs` fails if marine-face.js changes the head**, so a future head change says "refit the
eyewear" instead of floating them again. It also checks nothing is inside the head or the mask and the lenses are
10 to 30 mm off the eyes (the old ones fail that). Run: `node --test ui/marine-eyewear.test.mjs` (passes here).

## 2. The Armory window (`ui/armory.js`, `ui/armory.css`)

Looked at in a page of its own with the real armory code, styles and text (pictures stand-ins), at 1920x1080,
1600x900, 1280x720 and phone width. Three things fixed:
1. **At 1280x720 the window ran off the left edge and Done / Move to shelf were below the fold** (you had to scroll
   inside the window to finish). The Armory alone is now centred with a darker backdrop, and its button bar stays
   stuck to the bottom of the window whatever the workbench holds. Below 800 px tall the header and the 3D preview
   shrink a little.
2. **A stored gun for the other kind of slot was a dead button** (greyed out; "pick a matching slot first"). Now a
   click on it carries it into a free slot of its kind, or, with none free, selects that kind's first slot so the
   next click swaps it in.
3. **Each stored gun says which slot it goes in** (a small PRIMARY / SECONDARY tag; gold when it fits the selected
   slot).
New text keys with English fallbacks (ChatGPT's to word in strings.js): `armory.kindPrimary`, `armory.kindSecondary`,
`armory.carryOther` ("Carry this in a {slot} slot").
