## Foot slide and crouch speed (Jerry's call)
The crouch speed is unchanged at PHYS.crouch = 3.6 m/s. A planted foot moves back at step x rate / (PI x stance) = 0.25 x rate / (PI x 0.55). The step rate follows the ground speed, so at 2.2 m/s or less the feet don't slide at all (accelerating, slowing down, or slowed by water/webs/skills). Above that the rate is capped at 15 rad/s (about 4.8 steps a second), and at full 3.6 m/s the planted foot keeps up with ~60% of the ground speed. Before, it was ~35% in the crouch, and the standing walk is ~20%.
**A crouch speed of about 2.2 m/s (PHYS.crouch: 2.2) would remove the slide completely** at this step rhythm. Keeping 3.6 m/s without a slide would need ~24 rad/s (~7.6 steps a second), which reads as a scurry.

## Diagnosis (before)
1. He was knee-deep in the ground. Crouched, both boots and most of the shins were buried: soles 0.25-0.26 m under the ground standing still, 0.27-0.35 m crouch-walking.
2. The knees went through the ground while crouch-walking (knee joints at -0.04..+0.04 m).
3. Cause: the crouch dropped the whole body 0.36 m but bent the legs (knee +0.85, thigh -0.16) only enough to take up ~0.06 m of it, and nothing kept the feet on the ground. The thigh offset was also fed back into an eased value every frame, so it crept to about -0.9 rad.
4. The torso hardly leaned (0.14 rad), and the head tipped DOWN with it (+0.05 on top of the lean).
5. The crouch-walk was the standing walk cycle (same cadence and stride) sunk underground: no planted foot, no heel-to-toe.
6. Unarmed and crouched, the GB-124 arms hung straight down from the lowered shoulders. Also, resetReloadAnim was overwriting their elbow bend every frame.
