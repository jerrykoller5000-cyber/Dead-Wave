  now call reloadMagPose: the magazine is seated, in the palm, or hidden in the pouch — nothing else. The shotgun's
  loading shell is hidden, in the palm (pouch -> port) or pushed into the port with the hand on it.
- holdWeapon (Claude's; only the reload paths):
  - non-clip support branch: `else if (reloading && RELOAD_PLAN[w])` -> reloadSupportTarget moves the support target;
    after the IK the magazine (foot in the palm, tipped a little away from the well) or the shell is placed in the
    hand (reloadMagToPalm / reloadShellToPalm). The old branch stays for the minigun and flamer.
  - akimbo: the shared `pairReload` dip is replaced by akimboReloadDip per gun (right gun 0.06-0.50, left 0.48-0.92)
    and akimboReloadAnchor (each grip anchor moved so that gun's magazine foot — the revolver: under its cylinder —
    lands on top of its own side's pouch, muzzle tipped up 0.5 rad while there). The other gun stays up and aimed.
Per gun now (single): reach under the well -> pull the magazine out in the palm -> pouch (stowed, the fresh one taken;
hidden only there) -> under the well -> push it home -> pistol: racks the slide (hand on the slide 0.68-0.90) / sniper:
works the bolt (0.62-0.94) -> back to the hold. Revolver: hand opens the cylinder, fetches a loader from the pouch,
stays under the open cylinder through the rounds, closes. Launcher: pouch -> drum each shell. Shotgun: pouch -> shell
in palm -> into the port -> back to the pump, each shell.
Akimbo approach: "one gun at a time, seated against the body" — no free hand, so each gun in turn goes to its own
side's magazine pouch, well onto the pouch (the fresh magazine there), and comes back; magazines stay seated and
visible throughout. Simplest that reads, nothing loose to float.
