## How

- **tryPlace.** It checks placeRefusal first ("CAN'T BUILD THERE" plus the reason as a key), then the Cash (NOT ENOUGH CASH, build.message.cost), and only then `ghostValid` (No room). The shovel skips the `ghostValid` step, because its first swing at a fence or a camp is free; its paid dig says NOT ENOUGH CASH when short. Setting down a carried mortar is free and no longer needs the Cash.
- **placeRefusal.** For a piece at level 1 or up, or on a pillar top, that isn't a modifier or a wall-top cap: if his feet are within 1.2 m of the piece's base (ghostYAt) and he's inside its footprint, it's 'you are standing there'. Edge pieces use the same strip test as on the ground. On the ground, his cell was already refused through overlapsBlocked; inTheWay now names it.
- **refusalText(why).** It matches a refusal against the build.reason.* texts (case-insensitive). A landmark goes through build.reason.landmark with build.obstacle.<kind>. A tree, stump or rock goes through build.reason.natural and build.obstacle.* once those exist. Anything else is capitalised. Strings come from dwText, and a missing key falls back instead of throwing.
- **The ghost.** updateGhostPreview sets placeHint to the reason (or build.reason.funds when broke), and refreshPlaceBanner redraws only when it changes. The drag path clears it.
