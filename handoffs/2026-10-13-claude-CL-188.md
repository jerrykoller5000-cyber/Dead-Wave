# CL-188 · Claude · The marine's shoulders, and how he carries two guns

Jerry (2026-10-13, three screenshots): "The transition of the shoulders to the arms, and the shoulders to the body
look bad. Also for akimbo his elbows are too bowed out and not natural. I think when running forward with akimbo and
not firing he should bring his elbow into his body and the guns more vertical and closer to his face. Then a half way
of that for walking akimbo."

## 1. The shoulders (studio/marine-body.js, `liveShoulder`)

The sleeve's cap (GP-106's 'tailored-upper-sleeve' form and the seam on it) was rigid on the arm. Raised for a gun it
swung up off the shoulder as a ball, with a crease where it left the shirt: from behind, from the game's camera,
every time he aims. It is now worn by the torso and the arm together, the way CU-98's shinShaft wears the boot: built
with the arm hanging (its bind), held in the torso's space, and before each draw (and each shadow draw) each vertex
follows the arm's turn by its weight — 0 at the top of the shoulder, which stays on the collarbone, smoothly to 1 a
hand's width down the upper arm, which goes with the bone; the outside of the cap leans a third more toward the arm.
Nothing is recomputed while the arm doesn't move. Each live piece has its own group so mergeRigidMeshes leaves it
whole (it still gets the box-projected camo UVs); the shadow proxy (CL-140) skips it (`userData.liveShape`, it casts
its own) and the AO bake (CL-186) shades it by neither the torso nor that arm (`userData.aoOwners`).
review/marine-CL188/shoulder-raised-arm-before-left-after-right.png: the ball is gone; the sleeve runs on from the
shoulder.

## 2. Akimbo (index.html, AKIMBO_CARRY, in holdWeapon)

- **Presenting** (standing, firing, aiming, reloading): the elbows were aimed by the two-handed hold's poles, out
  wide, with the hands as wide as the shoulders. The poles are now points: each arm's IK lays its elbow toward
  `presentElbow` (0.22, 0.14, 0.12 torso space, under and inside the shoulder), and the hands come in to 0.15 inside
  the shoulders (was 0.10).
- **The carry** (two guns out, on the move, not shooting): the hands come up by his collarbones, the elbows down and
  in against the chest (`elbow`), the muzzles lifted 1.25 rad so the guns stand up past his face. Running is the
  whole carry, walking half of it (Jerry's "half way"), standing none; the trigger, the aim or a reload brings them
  straight back to presenting and they stay there for half a second after the last shot. Eased (rate 7).
- `POSE.locoK` (updateMarinePose): on the move, eased; the carry reads it.
- Knobs live in `TT.akimboDbg.AKIMBO_CARRY` (run, walk, rate, hold, at, up, presentX, elbow, presentElbow);
  `TT.akimboDbg.carry()` is the current weight. `TT.setDualOwnedDbg(w)` gives a pair without the kiosk (the camera).
