# grokbot - GB-84 (R3, P-48): one mod per gun - 2026-09-29

Changed: each gun has one mod slot.
- **Extended mag** (every gun in `EXT_MAG_PRICE`): x1.5 magazine and calibre cap, as before. New cost: the reload takes x1.25 while it's fitted. That's `EXT_MAG_RELOAD_MUL`, one constant, applied in `startReload` before Quick hands/akimbo/streak. The shotgun is exempt because it already pays shell by shell.
- **Heavy barrel** (M4, AK, Uzi, minigun; `HEAVY_BARREL_PRICE` 95/100/70/140, the same as their ext mags, through `equipmentPrice`):
  - Half the climb (`HEAVY_CLIMB_MUL`, in `aimDirWithSpread` and in the new `recoilClimb`, which `recoilSpan` now uses).
  - Double the movement term of the cone (0.45 to 0.9 at a run; `HEAVY_MOVE_SPREAD_MUL`).
  - A 0.45 s draw (`HEAVY_SWAP_DUR`): the arm dip uses the new `swapDur`, and `fireCooldown` holds fire for 0.45 s. Today's draw is animation only, so without that the "slower swap" would cost nothing.
- **Owning and fitting.** Buying either mod fits it and un-fits the other. `extMag[w]` now means fitted; `extMagOwned`, `heavyOwned` and `heavyFitted` are new, and all of them reset with the economy. `fitWeaponMod(w, 'ext'|'heavy'|null)` switches free between owned mods. `buyExtMag`/`buyHeavyBarrel` on an owned mod is a free refit with no purchase event.
- **Taking the ext mag off never loses a round** (Claude's ruling, 11:20 PM CT). The loaded magazine keeps what fits and every other round is repacked into spare magazines of the new size (`refitMagazine`, both ways). What the smaller cap can't hold goes back to the reserve over its cap, like any over-cap reserve: buys and pickups add nothing until it's spent back under. t136 now checks a full AK: 405 rounds before, 405 after, and 375 in the reserve against the 252 cap; it's the same with the mag back on and off again.
- **Reads and events.** `weaponMods(w)` returns `{fitted, ext, heavy, canExt, canHeavy}`, and `weaponMods()` returns all guns. The barrel's purchase is `purchase-delivered` `mod:heavy:<w>`.
- **Kiosk.** The existing ext-mag row reads OWNED from "bought" (one-word change). There's no heavy-barrel row: the Upgrades rows are GP-62's.

What the player notices:
- An extended mag makes that gun's reload a quarter slower: the AK goes from 2.0 to 2.5 s, the M4 from 1.55 to 1.94 s.
- Nothing else yet. The heavy barrel can't be bought from the kiosk until GP-62 adds its row and the switch (TT only today).
- Once it's on sale, a barrelled AK barely climbs on a long burst, but it's sloppy on the run and there's a beat before it fires after a swap.
