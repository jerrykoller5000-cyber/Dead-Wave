## 1. One place, one loudness
- Layout: every sound is a folder, every take is `<sound>-N.ogg` in it (zombies: `zombies/<type>/<sound>/<type>-<sound>-N.ogg`).
  371 folders, 1,348 takes; 328 folders have 2-4 takes. 20 categories.
- Evening (`/home/claude/c162/norm.py`, cloud): one-shots by their loudest 400 ms (K-weighted, EBU R128 momentary),
  loops by gated integrated loudness, all to -20 LUFS; look-ahead limiter at -1 dBFS, at most 12 dB of limiting (a take
  that would need more is set quieter instead of squashed); up to 48 dB of boost for the near-silent ones (all the
  suppressed-gun takes came in around -60). Before: -66.7 to -1 LUFS. After: -30.9 to -20, 1,182 within 1 dB.
  Batch 2 and 3 cut as CL-161 (convert2.py): leading silence off, 0.2 s tail; loops cross-faded 0.6 s. Mono Vorbis q5 48 kHz.
- The game is unchanged in level: core/takes.js evens each take to its folder's target when it decodes it.
- Deleting works live: tools/serve.py `/__zsounds` lists the folder at each start (and on a Training Ground refresh);
  names starting `_` or `.` are skipped, so `_prompt.txt` and `_HOW TO PICK TAKES.txt` are safe. An emptied folder
  falls back to the synth. index.json (1,348) is only the fallback list.
