## 1. How a run starts today

2. **The insertion (the opening flight).** `beginMarineInsertion` (index.html:44779): a cargo plane crosses (t<5 s), a parachute opens at 0.25 s, he descends over 6.3 s from 28 m up drifting from (−10, ·, −3.5) to the ground at **(0, gy, −8.5)**, lands (dust, footstep), recovers over ~1.1 s (weapon returns), the chute collapses and fades. The camera watches canopy + plane first, then settles to the gameplay destination. `finish()` puts him **exactly at (0, gy, −8.5)**, zero velocity, grounded (index.html:44858). `resetGame` also calls `insertion.finish()` if one is running (index.html:60387).
3. **The orders card.** Shown *in his hand during the drop* (CL-204): `ordersCardShow().update(t)` displays from t 0.7 s to 5.4 s, any key/click dismisses (index.html:44755/44805). The same lines live on the HQ briefing. **This already matches W3's "orders card in his hand as now"** — the drop-in changes where he lands, not the card.
4. **Where he lands and why there.** (0, −8.5) is 3.5 m south of the HQ's south face (the house is a 10 m square at the origin, MU-15 §3), inside the flattened spawn apron (20 m + feather, MU-16 §1), the settled terrain core, the 15 m tree-clear spawn, and the HQ yard where every trail's ROOT is (MU-16 §4). In short: he lands **in the HQ yard because the whole first day is staged in the HQ yard**, and the flight's job today is only to put him there cinematically.

**The literal move (a new landing constant, e.g. (−35, −35)-ish in (−x, −z)):**

- index.html:41608 (`SPAWN_X/SPAWN_Z`) and its readers (index.html:41635, 41665) — co-op seating and fallbacks follow the constant automatically if it's kept single-sourced.
- index.html:44779–44858 (the insertion rig): every hardcoded (0, −8.5) — start ground `gy`, drift endpoints, landing dust, `finish()`'s seat — plus `beginHunt`'s focus/destination (index.html:60328) and the menu camera's default seat (index.html:60283, 60610). - The landing clearing itself: it must exist in the world builders — flattened (the apron pattern generalised, MU-16 §3), trees/rocks/foliage cleared, and *excluded* from scatter like spawn is (a `landDist < spawnClear`-style guard at the new spot, or the chute lands him in a thicket). Two bends at 40–60 m: `wavyLeg`'s bow cap (5.5 m / 20% of leg) gives gentle waviness, not two deliberate bends — W3 likely wants an authored waypoint (MU-16 §4's S-waypoint note).
