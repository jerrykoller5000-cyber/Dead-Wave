# GB-107: Fighting below (Grokbot, 2026-10-01)

Started after CU-71, CL-99 and CL-91 (all done). P-138, hollows.md 4 and 11.

## What the player notices
- **Sleepers.** Every alcove CL-99 laid out has a dead lying on its back (11 in the iron warren). Each warren has its own: iron has soldiers and a brute among its shamblers, wet has drowned, and so on. They wake when:
  - a shot is heard (GB-105's radius for his gun; a suppressor cuts it to 0.3, so a suppressed rifle wakes only the near ones; a grenade or launcher blast is heard like the launcher),
  - his gun light holds on one for 1.5 s within 8 m,
  - he walks within 2.5 m of one,
  - a round or a hit lands on one in its sleep.
  A woken one gets up (0.8 s) and wakes its neighbours within 4 m 0.7 s later.
- **Awake, they hunt him through the warren.** They follow the warren's own nav grid, never stand in the rock, wind up and swing (their damage, as topside). Wet warrens slow them to 0.8. They take the warren's cave role and the damage table (D-62), react to hits, and pay skulls when killed (killZombie, as topside).
- **Nests.** A dark pulsing mass where CL-99 put each nest, 400 HP. Rounds stop in it; blasts and fire count double. While he's within 20 m (same depth) it sends a new dead up out of the floor every 12 s, at most 3 of its own alive at once. Blown, it bursts (debris, shake), sends no more, and pays a crate: a MedPen and a grenade, each if he has room (CU-80's grantHaul).
- **Set pieces, once a run.** Stepping into the Deep's chamber brings its set piece:
  - Iron: the mine crew, a brute foreman and four soldiers.
  - Shale: the flankers, two groups of four from either side.
  - Wet: six drowned rising out of the floor.
  - Hill: the barrow king (a colossus) with two soldiers and two shamblers.
  - Root: the climbers' knot comes only when he opens the strongbox; six drop from the roof and land.
- **The clear.** The Deep is cleared (`hollow.markCleared()`, the cave's `cleared: true`) when the set piece is dead and the strongbox open.
- **The cap.** Never more than 24 awake below. Past it, the rest lie stirring and get up as room is made.
- **Coming up** takes the warren's dead away. **The dead stay dead for the run:** a second delve finds only what was left, and the set piece doesn't come back.
- **Skull pay below (GP-83's half-a-night rule, contracts "The Hollows' haul").** Each warren's roster (its sleepers plus its set piece) splits half the same day's night skull value between them with `planSkullPayouts` (night value = the cashDrop sum of `waveComposition(day)`; on day 1 that's 7 of 15 over 16 bodies). A nest's dead pay nothing because they keep coming. The streak, late-night and blood-moon multipliers still apply on top, as for any kill.
- Topside's turrets and mines stop while he is below (hollows.md 2). Before this they could fire at a warren body sharing their x,z 400 m down.

## Decisions I made
- The set pieces are built from kinds that exist (hollows.md 3 says so). The hard hats on the mine crew and the barrow king's bier aren't there: they're dressing.
- The dead stay dead for the run per warren, so a full clear pays once (the half-a-night budget would mean nothing if the warren refilled each delve). A new run brings everyone back.
- Nest dead pay 0, following hollows-loot.js ("never credits ... respawning enemies").
- Set-piece bodies count against the cap like the rest. If it's full, the rest of the set piece waits in the dark until there's room.
- The wet warren's knee-deep slow is 0.8 on the warren's dead. The marine's own slow is the runtime's.
