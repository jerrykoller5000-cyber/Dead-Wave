## How it moves now

- **Pivot first.** Each planted boot turns toward the aim, critically damped (rate 16, crouched 13), as far as it can
  from where it landed: 0.8 rad (46 degrees) for the boot on the side he turns toward, which turns on its heel, and
  0.5 rad (29 degrees) for the other, which turns on its ball so its heel swings out. Crouched it is 0.74 and 0.48.
  The point it turns on stays exactly where it is.
- **A step only past that.** A boot lifts only when the aim is more than 0.06 rad past its pivot. On a turn from
  square the trailing boot's pivot is smaller, so it steps first and the leading one pivots meanwhile.
- **Slide, then lift.** The step's first 32% is on the ground: the boot turns about its ball (or heel) and slides 12%
  of the way, then lifts as high as before and lands. A flick far behind (over about 70 degrees, none past 140)
  shortens the slide to nothing, so a 180 degree snap still steps at once (GB-149's quick far steps are kept).
- **Steps run ahead of a turn that is still going.** A step taken while he turns lands up to 0.4 rad past the aim
  (0.3 s of the turn), so a slow turn doesn't step every few degrees.
- **The knee over the toes.** The leg IK's pole is 70% the boot's own forward, not the hips' front, so the leg turns
  at the hip with the boot (less twist at the ankle and in the new shin shaft, CU-98).
- **Sinking into the turn.** On planted boots his hips swing round over them; where a leg could not reach its boot
  he sinks by what it lacks (8 cm at most), so the boot holds instead of slipping.
- The aim, the chest, GB-149's twist cap and catch-up, and the root are untouched (t226 38/0).

## The idle bug this found

t226 failed in the test build after this change, one case: the boots read 132 degrees apart. Traced it: GP-74's
bored idle (studio/marine-idle.js, after 5 s with no input) ran after the turn IK and added to his hips' sway and his
knees' bend. In the real game that slid the planted boots a few centimetres back and forth while he idled. In the
test build, whose three stand-in never copies a quaternion back into .rotation, it threw the IK's knee away. Now,
while the turn steps hold his legs, the idle layer publishes its sway (ud.idleSway) and the turn tilts his hips by it
over planted boots. The weight shift is kept, the boots stay put, and t226 passes in both builds. Off the turn steps,
the idle layer poses as before.

## Changed test expectation (review)

studio/marine-turn.test.mjs "support boots stay planted in world space" asserted that a planted ankle never moves.
A boot now pivots on its heel or its ball, so it asserts that one of those two points stays exactly where it was,
and that the boot never leaves the ground. Every other unit test is unchanged and passes. New: small turns pivot
without a step; bigger turns take few steps and still settle on the aim; a step slides before it lifts and a flick
lifts at once; the heel or ball pivot by role, and the knee bends toward its toes.
