# CL-147: Jerry's training clips in the game: keypad, security door, zombie alarm (Claude, 2026-10-10)

Jerry: "I have some new sounds for training if you could put them in." From `Z-Sounds\My Replacement Clips`
(his originals are left there untouched).

## Files (assets/sfx/training/, 48 kHz stereo WAV)
- `keypad-press.wav` from Keypad_Press.wav, cut to 0.3 s (the beep is 60 ms; the rest of the 2 s was silence), faded.
- `security-door-open.wav` / `security-door-close.wav` from Security_Door_Open/Closed.wav, a 0.13 s fade at the end.
- `zombie-alarm.wav` from Alarm_Zombie.wav, a 0.12 s fade at the end.

## The player (core/audio.js)
`AudioSys.clip(name, v = 1, pan = 0)`, `AudioSys.preloadClips(names?)`, `AudioSys.clipState()`. A table `CLIPS`
(name → file, level, pitch jitter) next to `foley()`: each clip is fetched and decoded once and played through the
same fx bus and panner as foley. Asked for before it has loaded, it plays on arrival if within 0.25 s, else it's
skipped; a missing file stays quiet. Levels: keypad 0.55, doors 0.8, alarm 0.5 (one line each to change).
This is the loader the z-sounds voices can use too (CL-145).

## Where they play (index.html)
- Keypad: every press at a keypad (`trainingDoAction('trainingDoor')`, replaces nvgClick). While the door is locked
  (zombies in the room or on their way), the press is followed by the usual refusal buzz.
- Door: when the shutter starts to open or shut, from the door's position (`trainingDoorSound`, from the security
  onChange): a press, or the interlock slamming it when a deployment starts. Not on entering or leaving (the resets
  are quiet).
- Alarm: when a deployment is started from the panel (`trainingSpawn` accepted), with the 5 s countdown.
- Preloaded on entering the Training Ground.

## Checks
In the game (camera eval): all four decoded (0.3, 0.98, 0.98, 1.0 s), none failed; entering played nothing; range
keypad → keypad + door close; again → keypad + door open; a deployment → alarm + door close (interlock); a press
while locked → keypad (+ refusal). Not heard by ear (no speakers on the camera); levels are a guess for Jerry to judge.
