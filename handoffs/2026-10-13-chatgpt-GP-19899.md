## Recommended candidate families

Ordinary vault: sandbags (built/scenery), barricades, low deck railings, low wooden world fences, concrete checkpoint blocks, single fuel drums, loose low crates/cases, cemetery benches, camp tables/shed/trapper benches, low headstones, low Coldwater foundation sections, low/narrow rocks, fallen trunks, low rubble/pile remnants. Window traversal: unbarred player-built windows with actual opening clearance. Training furniture: low cot/bed if enclosure already open, cabinet only with clear worktop/landing.

Different movement: onto platforms/decks, taller crate stacks, vehicle bonnets/beds and higher ruin stubs are mantles; ground floors, tiny stump/debris and spikes/mines are ordinary stepping/jumping/hazard traversal. Low build wire is already vaultable but retain deliberate hazard treatment; tall concertina fences, security/zombie/FOB/Cordon gates, map boundary, solid walls, pillars, tall equipment and turrets should not be ordinary vault targets.

Cheapest first implementation scope: built/scenery sandbags, barricades, low railings and concrete roadside blocks; validate actual obstacle height, headroom and a clear permitted landing before starting animation.
