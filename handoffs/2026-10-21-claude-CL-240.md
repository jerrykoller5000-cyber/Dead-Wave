# CL-240: a steady page, to ask whether the computer itself has a 3-second clock (measurement only)

AG-114 (`bench --prof --gc`, CL-239): it is not the garbage collector. Main-thread GC was 40 ms in 30 s (about 50
scavenges of 0.7 ms, 0.02 ms a frame) and none of it fell inside a spike frame (0/11, 0/9). The spike frames start on
the wall clock: 31644.1, 37639.0, 40644.0, 46643.9, 49640.3 ms in one run, 33228.0, 35228.1, 41228.1, 44228.2,
50228.0 in the other, whole seconds apart to within ~5 ms (mostly 3 s). bench's own 1 s sampling drifts more than that,
and nothing in the game runs on a 1 s or 3 s clock (GB-218; the crew panel polls every 10 s, the test slot every 5 s).
With every phase of the frame 2.5-3x slower at once (GB-218), the likeliest cause is on the computer: another program
waking on a timer, or Windows moving the browser's thread to a slower core for a moment.

Allocation, for the record (AG-114's `--alloc`): 769.6 kB a frame (45 MB/s), `updateZombies` 259 kB of it. It costs
under 1 ms of GC a second, so it isn't what holds the 1 % low.
