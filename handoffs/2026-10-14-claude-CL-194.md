- Only 'up' plates are traced, so the hinge never tilts the plane under a trace.

- **ui/range-drills.test.mjs 60→65:** the count follows the five added drills, and nothing was weakened (it is 45 since GP-19913).
- **For Jerry: the dive's bullet time inside a timed drill.**
  - The session clock runs on the game's dt (`rangeFeature.tick(dt)`). The dive slows that dt to ×0.28.
  - So during a dive, a timed drill's exposures and its clock last about 3.6 times longer in real time, while the aim runs at full speed. A dive helps the speed and accuracy records.
  - `busy` (`!grounded`) stops a drill from starting in the air, but not a dive during one.
  - Options:
    - (a) No bullet time during a timed drill. The dive still goes, at full speed. This is one line in tryRoll, mine.
    - (b) The drill clock runs on real time.
    - (c) Leave it as a skill.
  - **Jerry chose (a), and it is in.**
    - `startDive(…, { slow: false })` gives a dive with no bullet time and no charge to the meter.
    - tryRoll passes it while `rangeFeature.ruled()` (a drill's countdown or run). Free practice keeps bullet time.
    - This was merged by hand onto GP-19916's jump-to-dive edits, which were live in the same two lines (startDive's options, tryRoll's call). Both are kept.
