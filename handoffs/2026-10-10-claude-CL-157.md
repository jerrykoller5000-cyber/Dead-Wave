## CL-157 · The flashlight in the Training Ground (index.html, trainingHideWorld)
- Cause: the gun light (a SpotLight) aims at `flashlight.target`, a plain object in the scene. Going in, trainingHideWorld hides the valley's top-level objects more than 150 m from the room and shadows their `updateMatrixWorld`; the target was one of them (it sat where he had last aimed outside). From then on its matrix never moved, so the light kept pointing at the old valley spot (a bright patch high on the hangar's far wall) and lit nothing he aimed at. A fresh SpotLight with the same settings lit the floor fine, which is how this was found.
- Fix: `keep.add(flashlight.target)` in trainingHideWorld. Camera: lights off, flashlight on, a pool on the floor where he aims, in the range and the hangar.
- Not changed: the sun's target is hidden the same way when he goes in (it was in the valley that frame). Noted for whoever owns the room lighting; changing it would change how the room looks.
