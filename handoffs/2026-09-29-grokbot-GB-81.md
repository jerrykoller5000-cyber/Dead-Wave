# grokbot - GB-81 (R3, P-38): a crate pick brings the plane over the mast - 2026-09-29

Changed: Tonight's call now delivers crates, and drops are earned (D-49).
- **The random crate** (`updateSupplyDrops`): it used to come on a 260-420 s timer at any time of day or night. Now, while the relay is down, it comes once a night, 40-180 s into the wave, and never by day. Once the relay is repaired (`objectiveRuntime.read().radioCall.repaired`, through `relayUp()`), the random timer stops.
- **The radio crate**: an `ammo`, `medical` or `hardware` pick (ChatGPT's `dw-game` `'radio-call'`, GP-55) for this run and day, made in prep before the alarm, calls GP-88's `spawnSupplyDrop({ x, z, contents, source: 'radio' })` at a clear spot 22.5-37.5 m from the mast (`radioCentre()`). With the chute's 2.5 m drift it lands 20-40 m out. `hardware` passes the event's drawn `blueprint` (it falls back to the cheapest turret blueprint he doesn't own). There's one radio crate a day, a repeated `receiptId` delivers nothing, and a pick for another day or run, or one made in the wave, is ignored.
- **The guard pack**: when it lands, zombies claw up 6-10 m round it (at least 8 m from him, on nav-valid ground, within the zombie cap), sized to the day. That's 2 on nights 1-3, 3 on 4-6, 4 on 7-9 and 5 from night 10, with one feral from night 4 and two from night 8. They're tagged `objectiveRole 'crate-guard'` and aren't part of the wave.
- **It waits for the alarm**: a radio crate doesn't time out (the random crate still lasts 90 s). If it's unclaimed when the alarm sounds, it's gone (`supply-drop` `expired`).
What the player notices: before the relay is fixed he gets one crate a night, mid-wave, and none in the day. After the relay is up, the crate comes when he calls it. He picks Ammo, Medical or Hardware at the HQ board, the plane flies over the radio mast, and the crate floats down 20-40 m from it. Two to five zombies claw out of the ground round it, so fetching it is a daylight fight. It waits for him until the alarm.
