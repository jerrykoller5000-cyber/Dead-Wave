## What changed (index.html)

- **The E prompt (#kioskPrompt) is floating text now.**
  - It is moved from #hudNotices into #hud and given the class `floating`: no yellow box, light text with a dark shadow, the key as a small yellow keycap.
  - It is projected every frame over what it belongs to: the kiosk, the HQ window, the HQ panel, the CIF, the armory, a door, the mortar. Anything else floats over his head.
  - Anything in brackets is stripped (`cleanPrompt`). "Skull window — bring skulls to turn in" is now "Skull window", and the turn-in no longer shows the ($) value.
  - The wave preview (ChatGPT's ui/wave-preview.js) still words the HQ panel's prompt through `hq-prompt`. It is now handed a stand-in element, so its words get the same cleaning and keycap.
- **Left HUD (CSS in `<style id="cl181Hud">`):** #modeBadge, #waveLine, #centerHud (#timeLine, #tacticalLine) and #prepChecklist are hidden. The elements stay in the DOM and keep updating, so nothing reading them breaks.
- **The SOLID badge** is not shown for `?solid` (the other dev mode badges are unchanged).
- **The Night N Complete card** is not shown. `showDawnSummary` plays the dawn music cue and publishes `dawn-summary` instead. The night record still runs. The "Day N cleared" banner, for a wave that ends without a finisher, is now the title alone for 2.2 s.
- **The stock notice** is one line, "New unlocks in terminal" (`shop.newUnlocks`, English fallback), for 3 s. The guns are still in `dataset.guns` and in the `stock-arrived` event.
