## Changed

- **Speed (game/horde.js `speedFor`, called in hordeJoin).** The pace roll is unchanged (GB-94). What it gives is now:
  - Walkers: type speed x pace x FAST 1.6, capped at CAP_RUN 0.9 x his sprint (PHYS.run 11.8, so 10.62 m/s).
  - Sprint band: 1.04-1.12 x his sprint (12.3-13.2 m/s), and that is also its ceiling.
  - A shambler walker averages about 5.8 m/s, up from about 3.7. Kinds keep their order: brute < shambler < feral.
    The quickest ferals reach the 90% ceiling.
  - Bosses, the spider and other kinds without a profile are untouched (cap Infinity).
  - baseSpeed and gaitMul scale with it, so GB-136's hop (75%) and crawl (35%) cut from the new speed.
- **Ceiling in updateZombies.** In the horde steering block, spd is capped at z.hzCap (x1.2 on a blood moon). A lunge
  (hzLungeT) and a sprint burst (sprintT) are exempt.
- **Roles (`nextRole`).** An even quota: exactly 3 flankers in every 10 joins (hordeState.roleAcc). Kinds without slots
  get no role.
- **The mass and its front (`massBearing`, in hordeFrame).** The bearing of the mass bodies chasing within 40 m. It is
  null with fewer than 3, or when they are spread all round him. Stored in hordeState.massAng / massN.
- **Slots (`roleSlot`, replacing pickSlot at the slot rethink).**
  - Mass bodies take the emptiest slot within +-35 deg of the front.
  - Flankers take the emptiest slot 80-125 deg off the front, on either side, within 150 deg of their own bearing.
  - With no front yet, or a mass body far off the front, it is pickSlot as before.
  - New `frontMoved`: a body with a role re-thinks at once when the front first forms or swings more than 30 deg.
    Without it, flankers kept the slots they took before there was a front (t205 caught this: 48% in band, now passes).
- **No more trickle (spawnWaveBatch, `refillReady`).** When the field is full (MAX_ZOMBIES 48), it used to retry every
  0.35 s and let one body out per death. Now it holds (fieldHold) until 14 are down, or 10 s pass. Then the next 14 come
  out as a burst. The hold resets at wave start. Horde sizes and the 48 cap are unchanged.
- TT: hordeDbg gains speedFor, run(), refill() -> {hold, t}.
