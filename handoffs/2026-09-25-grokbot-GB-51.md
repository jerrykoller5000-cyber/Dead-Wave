# grokbot - GB-51 Zombies idle - 2026-09-25
Changed: A zombie with nothing to chase now idles on its own clock: it breathes, sways, looks one way and then the other, and every 3 to 10 s shuffles a step (a foot lifts, the body turns a little). Each body rolls its own breath rate (1.6 to 2.6 rad/s), sway rate and phase, look timing and step timer, so a group never moves in step. The POI guards asleep at their post get the full idle pose, including a slow turn and a shuffle of at most 0.12 m round the post. A zombie held in the grace, or one whose speed is 0 (waiting), stands and idles instead of walking on the spot. The walk and the attack are unchanged.

## Detail
Before: an asleep POI guard skipped the whole walk loop and only turned slowly on the spot (a statue); a standing zombie in the loop swayed on z.bob, which runs at the same rate for every body; a body with speed 0 but not frozen (a spitter or screamer holding, one pinned) kept stepping on the spot, because the walk phase advanced whenever it was not frozen.

Now:
- zombieIdleState(z, dt) keeps a per-body clock: breath 1.6 to 2.6 rad/s, sway 0.6 to 1.3 rad/s with its own phase, a head target that alternates left and right (0.3 to 0.75 rad), sometimes back to the front, every 1.2 to 4.5 s, eased at the body's own rate, and a step every 3 to 10 s lasting 0.6 to 0.8 s (alternate legs, a turn of up to 0.45 rad, kept within 0.8 rad of where it was posted).
- Standing zombies in the walk loop blend the idle in and out over about 0.2 s (idle.amt), only while standing with nothing in reach and not swinging, winding up or reeling from a hit, so no pose snaps. The head turn goes back to rest when the body walks off.
- Asleep POI guards (poseSleepingGuard): the same clock drives the torso, head, arms, legs and hips directly, plus the turn and the small shuffle round the post. The far LOD (over 40 m from the camera) keeps the body's turn and shuffle but skips the joints, as before.
- t76: two or three guards over 4 s each sway (0.06 to 0.12 rad), breathe (hips 12 to 20 mm), and look about (0.29 to 1.05 rad); the closest pair differ by 0.033 rad on average; each has its own breath rate; they stay within 0.11 m. A forced step lifts a leg 0.35 rad and turns the body 0.2 to 0.4 rad. A zombie held in the grace idles (amt 1.00) without stepping on the spot, and a walker keeps its walk (idle 0.00).
