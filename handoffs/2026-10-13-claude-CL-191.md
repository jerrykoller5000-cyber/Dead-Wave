# CL-191 · Claude · The AI disclosure, rebuilt: 21.8 s, one baked video, and a load that waits for it

Jerry (2026-10-13, on CL-190): "The Intro is busted. Too long. The Soldier animation gets stuck and repeats. The soldier
just appears out of nowhere, no fade in or anything. The text and the transitions could use way more style to them.
GPT has made a new version of the soldier dance. The entire loading sequence from start to finish lags. ... Have him at
the bottom of the screen where the text is. Put the text in the center. The soldier is going to be a little smaller
about half his size. We are shooting for 20 seconds total."

## What was wrong (measured on Jerry's machine, in the Claude browser pane)

- **The lag.** The load is ~14.5 s there: ~7 s of world building (CPU), then ~5.7 s of post-processing setup, shader
  compiles and the warm frame, then ~1.9 s of warm-up fights — and all of the GPU half landed inside the disclaimer and
  the caracal. While shaders compile the GPU cannot present video frames, so both stuttered; after that the menu scene
  renders behind the opaque intro every frame.
- **Stuck and repeating.** CL-190 kept the alpha WebM on the song's clock from script; through those stalls it seeked
  to catch up, which shows as a freeze and a jump back.
- **It never ends in a hidden window.** CL-190's cue loop ran on requestAnimationFrame: with the window hidden it sat at
  "siren" forever, and the caracal with it (found in the pane: 20 minutes at "siren").

## What it is now

**One video**, `assets/disclaimer/disclaimer.mp4`: 1920×1080, 60 fps H.264 High + AAC 192k, 21.8 s, 15.8 MB, moov first.
Picture and sound are baked together (tools/disclaimer/, README there), so there is no runtime timing at all: the media
pipeline plays it hardware-decoded and off the main thread; nothing can drift, seek or repeat.

- 0.0–1.95 s: black, then the siren under hard red/blue wig-wag (2.2 flashes/s a side, under the 3/s photosensitivity
  line) through drifting smoke, the dashcam frame (corner brackets, ● REC, a running timecode) drawing in. The song's
  quiet lead-in rises under the siren; the siren's tail is pulled into the drop.
- 1.95 s, **the drop** (Been Warned 9.151 s = GPT's beat 0 = his frame 0): **ATTENTION!** slams in (Black Ops One, red,
  chromatic split settling, shockwave ring, red rules drawing out, camera shake, both lights flare). The dancer struts
  in as a dim silhouette; on beat 2 the spotlight snaps on with a flicker and he's lit — his entrance.
- From there the lights pulse on the beat (red/blue alternating, eighths during his floorwork), a lens streak crosses
  the screen as each line comes in, and the lines run **centred**, word by word **as they are spoken** (Whisper word
  timings): words rise out of a blur, born in the colour of the light that was on.
  - *This game was made entirely using* / **ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE.** letter by letter like a terminal, then a light
    sweep across it. The distant guy's "( you heard? )" is subtitled small, far left, where he is in the mix.
  - *Whether you like it...* / *...or it's just more* **SOULLESS AI SLOP...** (red, jittering chromatic, SLOP sagging and
    dripping) / *...is up to you.* — the rest steps back when that lands; "( you heard? )" again in the pause.
  - *But I do hope you are able to* / *have some* **FUN.** (amber, popping with sparks, then riding the beat).
  - The text clears for his handstand and backspin (~2.8 s, the lights go to eighths).
  - *Now go on!* / **GIT!** slams; 0.1 s after "Git!", on his ground freeze (19.279 s of his dance), the **rifle**: white

    dark, black.
- The dancer: GPT's GP-19906 (approved by Jerry), used as delivered — his full 1280×720 frame scaled 0.46 (about half
  his CL-190 size), at the bottom centre, no crop or tracking, his own travel; a faint reflection on the floor.
- The mix: voices -17 LUFS-ish, the song ducked 6 dB under them, the distant guy band-limited, panned left, echoed and

**Length: 21.8 s** — Jerry's approved 20-second dance plus the 1.95 s siren in front of it (the shot lands on his final
freeze). Getting to exactly 20 means cutting the first bar of his dance or the siren; one line in timeline.json either way.

## The load waits for the intro now (index.html, opening.js)

`DWOpening.gpuGate()` resolves when the opening leaves its video phase (disclaimer and caracal done or skipped), when
the window is hidden, or after 60 s whatever happens. The game awaits it right before `initPostProcessing()`: the world
build (CPU) runs under the videos as before, the GPU half (post, shader compiles, warm frame, warm-up) runs on the
title/loading screen. Cost: on Jerry's machine the menu comes ~5 s later than when the compile overlapped the videos,
in exchange for videos that play clean. `dismissForTesting()` and `?disclaimer=0` open it straight away (tests, packager).

## The player (assets/intro/disclaimer.js, disclaimer.css; index.html markup)

`<video>` in #disclaimer with the skip and a prompt. Plays with sound at the SFX volume; refused → "PRESS ANY KEY OR
CLICK TO BEGIN" (that key never skips); Esc/Enter/Space/Skip skip (sound out 0.2 s, picture 0.4 s); 'ended' or an 8 s
stall watchdog hands over (intervals, not rAF, so a hidden window gets there); a missing/undecodable file shows the one
sentence that matters as text for 4.5 s. `DWDisclaimer.{done, active, state, skip, dismiss, preview, video}`; the CL-190
`cfg/attach/songTime/timeline` hooks are gone with the live version. Play Dead-Wave.bat keeps CL-190's autoplay flag.
