## 1. The health pen (J17)

**Third person today.** H (when hurt, carrying, not akimbo — GB-258's ban, index.html:46328) starts the jab: `medPenT = MEDPEN_T (0.8 s)`, `medPenHealed = false`, the pen mesh shows, `playGesture('jab', 0.8)`, `AudioSys.medPen()` (index.html:46331–46334). The pen itself is a small built mesh (`makeMedPenMesh`, parented to the marine's **left grip** (`gripL || weaponMount`), needle-down rotation, index.html:23285–23289). The heal lands at **58% of the clip** (`medPenT < MEDPEN_T × 0.58` in the update, index.html:23297), the mesh hides as `medPenT` runs out (index.html:23293ff), the pen's own feedback is the HUD count and banner (the bar built at index.html:27720ff belongs to another system). In 3P another player (or the camera) sees: left hand brings the pen down to the thigh, jab, done — a gesture on the body's left arm (`playGesture('jab')`, the gesture system the roof survivors also use).

**First person today.** The pen mesh hangs on the **3P body's** grip. Stage 1's FP eye doesn't see the 3P arms doing their solve — it sees the view gun's own arms (viewmodel) holding the gun. So the FP experience of H is: gun stays up (no lower, no stow), a sound, a heal 0.46 s later, and — depending on how much of the 3P arm the FP camera catches at frame edge — possibly a disembodied gesture it isn't holding. **That is exactly Jerry's "looks bad in first person": the action exists, but it happens on a body the FP view doesn't own.** (Verified by construction, not by screenshot: `medPenHand`'s parent is the marine's grip, and nothing in viewmodel reads `medPenT`.)

**What the view arms need to show it.** A pen in the support (left) hand, gun lowered in the right:
1. The gun dips/lowers out of centre (the view pose system's "lowered" state — stage 1 has hip/ADS/reload paths; a `use` path is new but the same machinery).
2. The support hand comes up *holding the pen* — a **view-space pen prop** (copy `makeMedPenMesh`'s build into the view gun's part set, parented to the support wrist for the clip's duration — the same copy-by-parts trick as the mag).
3. The jab: support hand drives the pen down toward the bottom of frame (his thigh is below the eye; the read is "down and in, click, hiss", the heal at 58%), cap-click at the top of the beat.
4. Gun returns. Total still 0.8 s — **the timings don't change**, only who performs them.

**Hooks.** State: `medPenT` counting down from `MEDPEN_T` and `medPenHealed` (index.html:23285ff region) — the viewmodel update reads `medPenT > 0` → `use` pose with u = 1 − medPenT/MEDPEN_T; the heal beat at u ≈ 0.42 (58% from the end). Visibility of the view pen = `medPenT > 0.05` (mirror the 3P rule). The akimbo refusal already happens in `useMedkit` (index.html:46328) before any of this. Multiplayer: the 3P gesture stays for *other* eyes (co-op remote players see his body's jab as today) — the view arms are local-only, same split as the gun itself. ## 2. The HQ ladder (J28)

**Third person today.** `hqLadderAt()` gives the ladder (mid-west face, MU-15). Walking into it grounded with W: `climbingHq = true`, he's snapped to the ladder's x/z, rises at **2.4 m/s** (`player.position.y += dir × 2.4 × dt` in updateHqLadder), topping out onto the roof (`onHqRoof = true`, stepped 0.8 m in from the edge); S at the top climbs back down (index.html:27660–27690). `stepHqLadder(dir, dt)` is the test/debug driver of the same machine (index.html:27690). The *marine's body* during this: the pose solver puts him on the ladder (3P observers see a man climbing; hands' rung work is the pose system's business — no dedicated hand-over-hand clip was found in the ladder code itself).

**First person today.** The eye rises smoothly at 2.4 m/s facing the wall — and that is all: the view gun stays in frame (or hidden by other rules), no hands touch rungs, the gun doesn't stow. You float up a wall looking at cladding: Jerry's "no first-person ladder climb yet" — there is the *motion*, none of the *act*.

**What the view arms need.** The classic three beats:
1. **Stow:** on climb start, the gun lowers and goes to the sling — stage 1 already has the *concept* (hands change grips per pose); the ladder pose is "gun down/out of frame, both hands free". (In 3P the gun similarly shouldn't stay shouldered — owner's check.)
2. **Hands on rungs:** both view hands grip above, alternate reach-pull in time with the rise (2.4 m/s ÷ rung spacing ≈ a pull every ~0.15–0.25 s — the clip loops; hands must *contact* the ladder rails/rungs, which the view gun can do exactly because the ladder is at a known offset from the eye when snapped (his x/z are forced to the ladder's, facing fixed): this is the most authored-able FP animation in the game — the geometry relationship never varies.
3. **Top-out and return:** hands plant on the roof edge, gun comes back up as `onHqRoof` sets.

**Hooks.** State: `climbingHq` (bool) + direction (up/down, from the ladder update) + height progress — `(player.y − L.y)/(L.roof − L.y)` from `hqLadderAt()`'s own numbers. The viewmodel reads `climbingHq` → ladder pose (hands alternating by progress, phase from distance climbed, not wall-clock, so stopping mid-ladder freezes the hands on their rungs — a detail tests can assert). Sounds: rung grips could ride the metal step family (stepGround returns 'metal' on the roof, index.html:3547; ladder rungs are the same material read) — sound owner to say. Other ladders (the mast's, the tower's, MU-12/MU-17's mast ladder) share the *idea* but not this state machine — J28's ask is the HQ roof ladder; note the mast climb (`mastClimb`, index.html TT) as the second user when the pose exists.
