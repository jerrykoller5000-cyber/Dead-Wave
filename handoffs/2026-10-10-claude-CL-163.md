## The prompts
No artist, band or game names (ElevenLabs rejects them with bad_prompt; the test checks a list). The style is spelled
out instead: drop-tuned palm-muted guitars, modern heavy mix, an FM-synthesis lead (old PC sound-card tone), 8-bit
square-wave arpeggios, instrumental. Nights keep the CL-128 design per night (tempo, key, groove, story, from
docs/soundtrack.md) and are sent as a composition plan of the game's seven sections (stalk 8, dropA 16, riffB 16,
break 8, dropA2 16, bridge 16, climax 16 bars) at their tempo; music_v2/v2.5 enforce section durations, so a picked take
lines up with music.json's section director (bar timing still depends on the model holding tempo: check on import).
Every section's negatives include vocals (force_instrumental only works with a plain prompt). Everything else is a
prompt with force_instrumental and a length. Model music_v2_5 (2026-09-11; strongest on metal per ElevenLabs).
