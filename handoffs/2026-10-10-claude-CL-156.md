# CL-156 · Jerry's zombie sounds are in the game

Jerry finished his ElevenLabs zombie takes (Z-Sounds\04 - New Voices: 73 voice folders x 4 takes + 39 single files). All of them now play in the game.

## What was done
- Each take went into its folder under `z-sounds/` (the 112 CL-145 folders): `zombies/<type>/<state>/<type>-<state>-N.ogg`, and the single files into `footsteps/`, `impacts/`, `horde/`, `build-attacks/` and the zombie specials (bomber burst, screamer call, spider spit/acid, guardian roar/slam, cave guardian growl/screech/cave-groan/in-walls). Every one of the 112 folders has at least one take.
- Converted to Ogg Vorbis q5, mono (the take bank folds to mono anyway), 48 kHz: 134 MB of wav -> 8 MB.
- Leading silence trimmed (some leaper attacks started 0.4 s late), the silent tail cut to 0.2 s after the sound, so a take frees its voice slot sooner. Nothing else changed in the sound itself.
- Demon charge: Jerry's takes were 9 s with long silent gaps between the whoosh, snort and roar; gaps over 0.25 s were shortened (now 5.4-6.9 s). The prompt asked for 2 s, so shorter takes would fit the charge better.
- `z-sounds/index.json` written (331 files) for servers without /__zsounds.
- Jerry's originals in Z-Sounds\04 - New Voices are untouched.

## Checks
Camera (reload, then eval): index.json read, 112 folders / 331 takes listed and decoded, 0 failed; one take of each kind played (shambler idle, feral chase, brute attack, cave guardian hurt, demon charge, headshot, shuffle, distant horde, wood hit, bomber burst). takeFolderFor maps every new path to its own folder.

## Notes
- The camera's server returned 404 for /__zsounds (it predates CL-148); index.json covered it. Rewrite index.json (`node tools/zsounds-index.mjs`) when takes change if the game is served by anything but serve.py/serve.mjs.
