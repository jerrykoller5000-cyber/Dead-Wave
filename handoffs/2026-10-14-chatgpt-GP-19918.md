Causes and final implementation:
- GP19917 rotated an unconstrained shoulder-to-grip offset toward any cursor direction. Toward the feet it placed the grip in the pelvis; rapid changes could take it behind the chest. Clear grip positions stay above the belt and ahead of, or safely outside, the carrier; the cached pose eases on real time.
- Projecting the torso's head/up axis onto a nearly parallel barrel produced a roll flip. The new barrel-up vector comes from torso right, and a bounded pair of alternate elbow planes is evaluated only when actual palm contact misses. The original 1.3rad wrist and .85rad forearm caps remain; no hand detachment is concealed by relaxed limits.
- Floor correction moved only the palm vertically, which can push it across the shirt as the body rolls. The body support height now rises with the constrained hand. Wall correction likewise backs the whole shooter away and records physical contact instead of compressing the gun into the vest.
- The two-handed cup conflicts with the single sidearm's wide dive aim/reach. The final single pistol/revolver uses one hand in flat dives; the other arm has real-time balancing motion and relaxed fingers, closing around the blade for attacks. Dual pistols retain both grips and their attack selection; long guns retain their shoulder-stock solver.
- Standing blade clips include an overhead chop/body twist, and the legacy blade mount is separate from the articulated palm. The dive has its own cock/thrust/cut/recovery path and orientation; the handle is placed on the actual solved palm after wrist IK. Melee hit detection, duration and cooldown still use their original game-time rules.
- Gun hold and bullets share the safe aim point; sampled barrel/bullet disagreement stays under .012rad. Recoil reposing with holdWeapon(0) no longer advances real-time timers again.
