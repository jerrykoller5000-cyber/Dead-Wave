## 3. Camera shake each adds in first person

The view gun does not move the *world* camera (it has its own pass) — but the eye experiences: (a) the gun's spring motion itself (§1), (b) the **world camera's own walk bob** underneath (`walkBob`, index.html:21381/22343: sin stride × 0.05, decaying at 6/s) — so in FP, **two bobs stack**: the camera's (5 cm, world stride phase) and the view gun's (x .0065/y .0085 at 1.85 strides/m, scaled by speed from a 4 m/s reference) with independent phases; (c) recoil passed to the camera aim (the world's kick/recoil climb, shared with 3P) plus the view gun's rot spring overshoot; (d) event shakes (landmark deaths `addShake`, cave warnings, blasts) — world shakes that move the eye while the view gun's springs chase it a frame later. **The flamer:** its FP shake is not a VM number — it lives in the weapon's own handling (the stream's pushback/recoil in index.html; J59's "steady pushback" is CL-293's to add): today the flamer has no VM_POSES entry in the table above (not in :31–93), so it still uses the old FP (his own arms from his eyes) with the world's shake — which is why its violence reads different from the view guns'.

## 4. The three numbers most likely behind the vibration (J55)

1. **The underdamped springs (rot z 0.74, pos z 0.78 at k ~250).** Every shot, turn and landing injects an impulse; at z 0.74 the rot spring overshoots ~3–5% and rings for ~0.3 s. On an automatic (Wasp 14/s, minigun-class rates, akimbo), impulses arrive faster than the ring decays, so the gun *buzzes* around its target instead of settling — a vibration, not a sway. This is my first suspect because it's per-shot, always on, and scales with fire rate exactly as a "vibration when shooting/moving" complaint would. Test: tcl293's jitter bound with z raised to 0.9–1.0 in a branch — if the measured jitter collapses, that's the finding.
2. **Sway's look term fed by unsmoothed turn rate (0.022/rad·s⁻¹, clamp 0.07).** Turn rate is computed per frame from mouse deltas; at 60 fps a steady turn is a staircase of delta spikes, and the sway target inherits them unfiltered (no smoothing constant on the *input* — the springs then chase a noisy target, adding #1's ring on top). Signature: vibration *while turning the view*, worst at high mouse sensitivity/low fps. Fix direction: low-pass the turn-rate input (or derive sway from the smoothed aim the camera itself uses).
3. **The stacked bobs (world walkBob 0.05 m + VM bob y 0.0085 at a different rate/phase).** While walking in FP the eye gets the camera's 5 cm stride bob and the gun gets its own smaller, faster bob (1.85 strides/m at speed-scaled amplitude — at sprint 11.8 m/s the amplitude scaling from the 4 m/s reference matters: if the amplitude scales linearly it is ~3× the table values, ~2.5 cm of gun bob *relative* to an already-bobbing eye). Two oscillators at near-but-not-equal frequencies beat — read as judder/vibration while moving, worst sprinting. Fix direction: one bob (drive the gun's from the camera's stride phase, or zero the VM bob and let the camera carry it).

Runners-up (checked, less likely): breathe (0.32 Hz but ADS ×0 and 1.2 mm — too small); slideKick's 18 ms step (one-frame pop per shot, visible as *kick*, not vibration); land dip (event, not continuous); the spring integrator at low fps (k 260 with frame dt — if CL-293's round 0 finds jitter only under frame hitches, substepping the spring is the fix; the integrator at viewmodel.js:324 should be checked for dt clamping).
