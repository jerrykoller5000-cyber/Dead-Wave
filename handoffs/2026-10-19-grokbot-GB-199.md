# GB-199 - Grokbot's mission props, modelled in-house; the gun put away with the can - grokbot

Changed:
- studio/day-mission-props.js (new, LF): canvas textures only, shared materials, each maker a Group merged by material in index.html (mergeStaticKeepUV).
  - makeHikersBag: khaki canvas-weave body with a rounded top, front pocket, white patch with a red cross (front and back), zip, side compression straps and buckles, hanging loop (the rope ties at +0.30) and grab handle.
  - makeFrayedEnd: seven splayed strands at a cut. ropeMaterial: natural-fibre rope.
  - makeBough: tapered limb that fits any tree (length = the bag's reach, radius from the trunk), four side twigs, faceted leaf clumps and a leafy tip.
  - makeSnag: six faceted rocks and a jam of bleached driftwood, placed where the bag snags.
  - makeFuelPump: concrete plinth, red cabinet with a rounded top, dial face ("DIESEL", gauge, counter) in a steel bezel, globe, hand crank, black hose loop to a nozzle on its holster. Faces the depot.
  - makeJerrycan: NATO pattern (0.47 x 0.345 x 0.165 m), olive drab, welded seams, raised X on both broad sides with rims, three handles, spout with cap and cam lever.
  - makeGenerator: yellow open tube frame, engine block, alternator, red tank with filler cap, muffler, control panel (230V 50Hz 5.5kVA, outlets, OFF), recoil starter. By the HQ wall, panel out, x1.25.
  - makeDripDecal: dark pool with a rainbow sheen, random turn and size per drip.
- index.html: imports; the hikers' placeholders and the fuel placeholders swapped for these. The cut is now where the round crossed the rope (hikersRoundStep passes t): the length over the bough is shortened to the cut with a frayed end; a stub knotted to the bag's loop with its own frayed end falls with the bag (the old code hid the whole lower rope). Snag dressing on 'snag'. The can sits on the ground by its base. The held gun is hidden while he carries the can (weaponMount.visible and the relaxed arms include fuelCarried()) and comes back on drop, pour or blast. TT.hikersDbg().cut and TT.fuelDbg.gunShown for the tests.
- t264: checks the rope parts (frayed end shown, stub shown, upper rope still there). t267: the gun is put away carried and back down (waits for the pose pass; skipped with an INFO line if the page never shows the gun).
- No flatShading: the WebGPU node build threw 'reading context' in MeshStandardNodeMaterial.setupPosition with it (A/B: props stubbed = no errors). Facets come from split faces instead.
- Antigravity's two prop requests withdrawn (crew request).
