## How

**Adoption.** `marineBody()` runs `Studio.rigs.get('marine').create({ group: marine })` with `createBody(inst, marine/marine, { ground: entityGroundY at his height })`, and no pool. If it throws, he keeps today's reaction for the match, with one console warning.

**The blow.**
- `damagePlayer` works out where the blow came from: the attacker's mesh, or `info.from` for a blast.
- `marineBlowOf` turns it into a kind and m/s. Zombie melee with a mesh is blade, or crush for a heavy attacker (brute, tank, boss, colossus). `info.power` wins when given.
- `marineHit` refuses when he isn't grounded or `marineBodyAllowed()` is false (counted as `skipped`).
- Other damage (fire, acid, spider spit, birds, trees, the tower fall, a grenade's own blast) plays today's reaction only. A grenade's blast could be wired the same way as the bomber's later.

**The frame.** `updateMarineBody` runs after `holdWeapon(0)`: follow, update, apply. It logs events (last 40) for tests. Once he's down (`fall`, `down`, `getup`), it moves him by the body's drift. Standing, GB-50's slide already moves him.

**Why the knee goes when the body takes it.**
- Probing with `marineHitDbg`, a 3.4 m/s crush alone gives wake, hit, stagger, step, recovered: no fall.
- Through `damagePlayer` with GB-50's knee and slide on top, it went on to fall, land, down.
- Without the knee, with the slide kept, it is stagger, step, recovered: no fall, twice in a row.
- motion.js pins planted feet in the world and has no way to be told its host moved (the request to Claude).
