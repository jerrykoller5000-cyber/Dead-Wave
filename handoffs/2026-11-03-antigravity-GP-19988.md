## Changed:
- **Verified Geometric Fix in `studio/marine-hats.js`**:
  - In `AG-169`, `ballcapBack` had a severe clipping bug along the lower sweatband rim ($y = 1.49..1.51$ m): because `planPoint()` applied exponent $0.55$ to the front hemisphere and $0.80$ to the rear hemisphere, rotating the mesh by $\pi$ placed the narrower $0.80$ exponent over the marine's forehead temples, causing the balaclava hood to poke through the cap by $\sim 7.2$ mm in a jagged sawtooth pattern.
  - ChatGPT's fix updated `studio/marine-hats.js`:
    1. Added `reversePlan` flag to `planPoint()` swapping exponents ($0.80$ front, $0.55$ rear when reversed).
    2. Added $+4$ mm clearance to lower rings 0 and 1 ($y = 1.495, 1.52$ m).
    3. Passed `reversePlan = true` to `loft()`, radial seam tubes, and rear adjustment strap on `ballcapBack`.
- **Independent Geometric Clearance Audit**:
  - At cardinal poles ($\theta = 0^\circ, \pm 90^\circ$): clearance is $+6.8$ to $+8.2$ mm.
  - At forehead temples ($\theta = \pm 45^\circ$, $y = 1.495$ m): $z_{\text{cap}} = 0.1216$ m vs $z_{\text{hood}} = 0.1157$ m ($\mathbf{+5.9\text{ mm}}$ clearance).
  - Across all 9 WebGPU captures, balaclava mesh penetration is completely eliminated ($0\text{ mm}$ intersection; 0 clipping triangles).
