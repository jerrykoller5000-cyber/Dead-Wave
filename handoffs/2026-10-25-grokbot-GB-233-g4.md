Defects under 4, part by part (look calls, reported):
- Heavy turret (vs a real sentry gun such as an M2 on a remote mount): 1) The gun is a short brass stub, about a quarter of the body's width, poking out of a slot in a stacked-box body, so it reads as a robot or a mailbox, not a gun. On a real one the weapon is the biggest, longest part and the mount is under it. 2) Three stacked boxes with no visible pivot, so you can't see what turns. 3) Glossy dark-blue paint with hard white highlights on the edges (low roughness), where the art direction's painted steel is matte (rough 0.6-0.75) and olive or grey. 4) The brass barrel is the brightest thing on it, which pulls the eye to the smallest part. A rebuild with the gun dominant on a visible yoke is the bold fix; it's a model rebuild, so it's proposed, not done.
- Flame turret: 1) A plain red rounded tank (fire-extinguisher shape) with a short black nozzle, so the business end is tiny. A real field flame unit shows the fuel tank, pressure bottle, hose and a long wand. 2) The three dark feet sit apart from the base ring with gaps, so they read as loose rocks, not stakes. If they're ground anchors they want to touch the ring or have legs to it. 3) The tank's gloss (hard highlight) is above the painted-steel range.
- M240 tripod and the mine: no defect under 4. The m240 reads as itself (feed cover, carry handle, buttstock, tripod with cross-brace); the mine reads as a pressure mine with a lit fuze.
