## What the player notices
- **The rune door at the back of a warren's Deep** (E, beside it; the strongbox in front of it keeps its own E):
  - On an ordinary day: "It's warm. It's singing." (`quest.door.shut`)
  - On a silenced day, in a warren he hasn't cleared this run: "It's open. Somewhere." (`quest.door.elsewhere`)
  - On a silenced day, in a cleared warren (set piece dead, strongbox open): he goes through, straight into the heart (no fade, no walk down a long tunnel; he lands at the top of CL-112's tunnel).
- **In the heart:** the guardian (CL-78's chalk rig, at 1.25 scale on its boss body) stands between the source and the way in, and walks at him on the heart's flow field (2.6, 3.0 then 3.4 m/s by phase). No stir in here. The Hush keeps counting down.
  - **Its lunge:** about every 5 s (4.2, then 3.4 s), when he's within its reach plus 2.5 m, it rears up for 0.7 s (the rig's grab pose), then lunges in. If he's still inside its reach (3, then 4.2, then 5.6 m), it has him.
  - **Held:** the run's one kick-free, as topside and below (5 presses of E, the same "Kick free! (E)" prompt). Free, he loses 50 HP and his skull bag, and it's thrown back toward the source. With no kick-free left: 3.5 s, then the cave death, the run ends as `caveguard`, and he's taken up at the warren's mouth.
  - **The dead it calls:** between lunges, 2, then 3, then 4 at a time climb up out of the source's lip (shamblers and ferals, never more than 12 up). They pay nothing, like a nest's.
  - **Phases:** at two-thirds and at a third of its health, a roar and a shake. In the last phase a white column comes down every 9 s (the first 1.5 s in), until all six are down. Standing under one is a 30-damage blow, and it pushes him out. A fallen column is rock for him and the dead.
  - **The Hush runs flat:** the source roars and its light turns red, and nothing he does hurts the guardian. Going back up is the only move.
  - **Back up the tunnel** (walk off its top and back onto it): he's in his warren at the rune door, the Deep still cleared, its dead still dead. Next silenced day, the guardian has healed: a fresh fight.
  - **Killed:** the source's light goes out, "THE LAKE IS QUIET." on screen, and every dead below and in the valley drops where it stands. After 2.5 s he's up at his warren's mouth, and GP-70 gets `'quest' { kind: 'ending' }`, so the run is won with the true ending (GP-70's screen, CL-111's rune finish).

## Decisions I made
- **HP 3400:** an AK's sustained 153/s × 0.6 (the guardian's bullet column) × 0.7 (its armour), on target about 30% of the time, is about 19/s, so three minutes is about 3400. Jerry or GB-94 can retune it from play (`HEART.HP` in game/heart-fight.js).
- **The other numbers are mine:** reach 3/4.2/5.6 m, lunge every 5/4.2/3.4 s, 0.7 s wind-up, calls every 10/8/6 s of 2/3/4, a column every 9 s, the 30-damage blow from a falling column.
- **The guardian heals between attempts:** each entry is a fresh fight at full health. The quest state's `heart` still records the last entry's phase and health.
- **Through the door is a cut, not a walk:** he lands at the top of CL-112's 26 m tunnel. The return trigger arms only after he has stepped off that spot, so he doesn't bounce straight back.
- **The ending drops every dead at the guardian's death** (secret-quest §6), with no pay. GB-119's listener on `'quest' { kind: 'ending' }` will do the same for anything left, plus the rune pistol.
- **Godmode** (a dev tool) skips the hold, as topside and in the stir.
