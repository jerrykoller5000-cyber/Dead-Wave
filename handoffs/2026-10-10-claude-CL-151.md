# CL-151: the Training Ground's test builds: one button lays a standard set in front of the zombie gate (Claude, 2026-10-10)

For Jerry and ChatGPT's work on how the dead go at his builds: every test starts from the same layout.

**Look first:** `Claude outputs/training-test-builds.jpg` (in the game: the set laid, from the build room).

The HQ panel in the build room has a new button, **Lay out test builds** (hazard yellow, beside Clear all). It lays,
on the row just outside the gate's no-build zone (12 m from the gate, the walls on the zone's yellow line, facing it),
left to right as he faces the gate: a barricade, two sandbags, three walls, two runs of wire, a barricade; and a light
turret 6 m behind the walls. Pressed again (it then reads **Rebuild the test builds**), it takes away what is left of
its own last set, nothing else, and lays a fresh one. Free; the game's own placing rules apply (it goes through
placeBuildAt, so the zone, the doorway and the terminals refuse as usual; any refusal is kept in
`TT.trainingDbg.layoutRefused()`). Builds still go when he leaves (resetGame).

Materials for the build-hit sounds: wood (barricades, the tier-0 walls), sand, wire, turret.

## Files
- index.html: `TRAINING_LAYOUT` (kind, local x, z, edge) and `trainingLayout()`, `trainingLayoutCount()` beside
  trainingAlive; the panel gets `onLayout`/`laidOut`; debug `TT.trainingDbg.{layout, layoutPieces, layoutRefused}`.
- ui/training-panel.js (ChatGPT's): optional `onLayout`, `laidOut`; the button (keys `training.layout`,
  `training.layoutAgain`, `training.layoutHint`, English fallbacks for ChatGPT to word). ui/training.css: `.tp-layout`.
