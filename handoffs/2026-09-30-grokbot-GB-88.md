# grokbot - GB-88 (R5, P-59): the siege on night 18 made real - 2026-09-30

Changed:
- **Night 18's 'siege' trick now does something.**
  - At the alarm, `beginWave` sets `siegeNightNow` from the plan's `trick === 'siege'`, next to CU-77's `nightKindNow`. `resetGame` clears it.
  - That night, every brute and soldier the wave spawner makes gets the formerly dormant `'smash'` tactic. It's set after the cave role, so a role can't undo it, and the old tactic is kept in `z.siegeFrom`.
- **What smash does** (the existing targeting, unchanged):
  - The zombie goes for the nearest placed wall or barricade within 28 m, and hits any of his builds it bumps into.
  - With none in reach it comes for the marine. GB-103's defence seekers still take priority.
- **Brutes keep their weight:** the heavy hit and the 2.2 heft on builds come from `z.brute`, and `softStr` now reads `siegeFrom === 'tank'`, so a sieging brute still shoves through sandbags at 0.25.
- **Soldiers lose their weave and rushes that night.** They walk straight at the wall, or at him.
- **Unchanged:** the total, the mix, the pushes and the guardian ('boss', planned, D-13). There's one music plan (P-21).

What the player notices:
- On night 18, the brutes and soldiers that come within 28 m of his walls or barricades peel off and batter them instead of running past to him. The base takes a real beating that night.
- Without walls (or once they're down) they come for him as before.
- Soldiers stop strafing that night and come in straight lines, so they're easier to hit but reach the walls faster.
