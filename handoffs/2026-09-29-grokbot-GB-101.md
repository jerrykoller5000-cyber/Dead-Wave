# grokbot - GB-101 (R3, P-106): skills by doing, the combat counters - 2026-09-29

Changed: The six skills in `game/skills.js` (CL-88's spec, `docs/skills.md`) now earn XP from what the marine actually does. Each counter calls `addSkillXp` through one wrapper (`skillXp`), so the `skill-up` event, the toast and GP-76's panel all fire as they already did.
- **Stopping power** (`damageZombie`, on the kill): a one-shot kill pays 1, and a one-shot headshot kill pays 2. "One shot" means the first hit on a zombie at full health killed it (`_osSid`/`_osFull`, recorded per zombie). A turret kill pays nothing, and neither does a debug TT spawn (`z.ttSpawn`).
- **Quick hands** (`startReload`): pays 1 when he has fired since his last reload, the mag is at 25% or less, and a live zombie is within 8 m. Reloading a full mag, reloading with nobody near and reloading without firing all pay nothing.
- **Fleet foot**, two ways. (a) The chase: `updateSkillWatch(dt)` runs each tick after `updateZombies`. In a wave, while he's running (Shift) and the nearest live zombie within 15 m is closing on him faster than 0.3 m/s, it pays 1 per 10 s. (b) The roll (`tryRoll`): pays 1 when a zombie within 1.5 m is winding up or swinging at him.
- **Grenadier** (`explodeGrenade`): one blast of his (a thrown grenade, his launcher, his mortar, or a drum he set off, which `byMarine` now carries through `damageLandmark`/`destroyLandmark`) that kills 3 pays 1, and one that kills 5 or more pays 2. Bomber, mine and turret blasts pay nothing.
- **Vitality**: living to see dawn pays 2 (`startPrep`). A comeback pays 1: a zombie hit takes him under 25%, and he gets back over 50% within 30 s. That's once a night, and falls and other non-zombie damage don't count.
- **Scavenger** (HQ `deposit-complete`): 1 per 10 skulls banked, counted across the run.
- A fresh start clears the counters along with the XP (`skillSw`, `skillState()`).
TT: `skillDbg()` (the counter state), `skillWatchDbg(dt)` (steps the chase and comeback watch), and TT `spawnZombie` marks `ttSpawn`.
What the player notices: the skills now rank up on their own. Headshots, clutch reloads, running from the horde, close rolls, big grenade kills, comebacks, dawns and banking skulls each raise their skill, a toast says so ("Fleet foot · rank 2"), and the rank bonuses (damage, reload speed, move speed, grenades, cash) grow over a run. Nothing is bought any more.
