# claude — CL-213 bench --prof: where each frame's time goes (CU-156, taken over) — 2026-10-19

Changed:          `tools/bench.mjs --prof`. Over the measured seconds the game's own profiler (TT.profDbg, PROF in
                  index.html) is on, and the scenario's JSON line gets `prof`:
                  - `frameMs` (1000 / fps), `tickMs` (the sum of every PROF section: the JS the tick runs),
                    `outsideMs` (frameMs - tickMs: the browser, the compositor and the wait for the GPU);
                  - `top`: the 12 heaviest sections, mean and worst ms per frame;
                  - `spikes`: the first frames over 50 ms with their four heaviest sections;
                  - `gpuMs`: one frame's render passes on the GPU, from WebGPU timestamp queries.
                  three requests every adapter feature at init, so `timestamp-query` is on the device whenever the
                  adapter has it; the bench only sets the backend's `trackTimestamp` and resolves the queries every
                  frame from its own requestAnimationFrame. On the WebGL backend, or without the feature, gpuMs
                  says 'unavailable'. Nothing in index.html changed.
                  The printed summary adds one line, `frame = tick + outside, gpu`, and the 12 sections.
Files:            tools/bench.mjs (Cursor's; taken over under D-81 while Cursor was idle on CU-156 since 18:18Z).
Tests:            node --check. A headless run in the cloud (SwiftShader) did not get past the game's boot in 15
                  minutes, so the first real run is Antigravity's AG-86 part 2 on Jerry's GPU.
Not verified:     Every line of `--prof` on a real run. If it fails, the error says which step (`--prof: ...`).
                  WebGPU timestamps in Chrome are rounded to 0.1 ms, which is fine at this size.
Contract changes: none (a new flag; without it the bench is byte-for-byte the same line as before).

## Why

AG-82, AG-85 and AG-86 part 1: every night runs 31-35 fps on Jerry's 5080 at High whatever the draws (476-672) and
triangles (735 k-1.02 M). Night 20 stayed at 32.4 fps after every zombie came inside its budget (GP-19930). So the
limit is neither draw calls nor triangles; this says which section of the frame (or the GPU) it is.
