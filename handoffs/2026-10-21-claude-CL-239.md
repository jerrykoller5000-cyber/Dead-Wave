Note on the number: bench's "1 % low" is the mean over the last ten 1-second windows of each window's worst frame
(perfWindow keeps one second; 1 % of ~60 frames is one frame). So a slow frame every 3 s sits in a third of the
windows, and the low reaches 50 only when nearly every second's worst frame is under 20 ms.
