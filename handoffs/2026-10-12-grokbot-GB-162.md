# GB-162: Build room wear: minigun holes, old blood, acid, webs, burns (Jerry, direct ask)

Grokbot, 2026-10-12. Status: review.

## Asked
Jerry: "lets do the ones for the build room. Ground needs to be riddled with bullet holes from the miniguns on the wall. Dried old bloodstains around. Marks of all different kinds. Old acid splotches from the spitters. Old spiderwebs. Flame and burn marks. Not crazy but to the point where it definitely tells a story."

## Done
New module world/training-build-wear.js, `dressBuildWear`, called from buildTrainingGround next to the GB-159 wear.

**Kill zones.** `BUILD_WEAR.KILL` is four weighted zones where zombies would go down: just past the gate's hazard paint, the middle of the room, toward the doorway, and the west side. Marks are concentrated there and thin out everywhere else.

**Floor:** one 1440x1600 sheet at y 0.003, under the build-grid paint, the hazard stripes and the NO BUILD stencil.
- **Minigun fire:** about 1,500 holes, chips and gouges. The gun positions come from `containmentMounts` in game/training-containment.js, and each hole is stretched away from the gun that fired it (longer the farther away). There are 22 strafing arcs where a gun swept after a runner, and brass stains plus soot under each gun.
- **Blood:** about 26 dried brown and maroon pools with darker rims and spatter, some half mopped; drag trails hauled toward the gate; boot prints, two hand prints, and mopped streaks.
- **Acid:** 16 etched patches where the paint is eaten through, with yellow-green tide rings and pitting.
- **Burns:** blotchy scorched patches with char flakes, and one melted spot.
- **Misc:** claw scratches, scuffs, a long drag mark, grime heavier along the walls, and debris specks.

**Walls:** five sheets, 5.9 m tall, cut around the doorway and the gate. Each one falls into its wall's cutaway side, so it fades with the wall.
- Char streaks running up from the floor.
- Acid splotches and drip runs low on the wall.
- Blood spatter, runs and a hand print at body height.
- Low minigun misses and claw scratches.
- A soot fan and brass specks around each gun mount.
- Grime along the base.

**Cobwebs:** 9 quads sharing one texture: corners under the tile ledge, corners at the floor, and behind three of the gun mounts.

**Cost and limits:**
- New draws: 1 floor, 5 walls, 1 for the webs.
- Visual only: everything is left out of the collision boxes, and walk areas, zombie room and build lattice are unchanged.
- Nothing is over the range, and the 6 m test passes.
