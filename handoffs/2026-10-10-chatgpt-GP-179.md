## Behavior and tuning

- Demon fireball: player 17–34m away, 0.85s planted cast tell, 0.45s recovery, 5.5–8.5s cooldown, 24 raw direct damage. Existing close charge, melee and other active actions have priority; flame anchors follow actual hands, respect severed limbs and stop on death.
- Spitter footprints: every 0.75m walked, 0.65m hurt radius, six-second life. One shared player damage cadence (4 raw damage / 0.4s) prevents overlapping footprints multiplying damage. Stationary actors and teleports do not spam patches; water and another floor height are excluded. Trails survive the owner's death until they expire.
- Colossus throw: player 7–38m away, 1.65s planted reach/lift tell, 0.85s follow-through, 8–12s cooldown. Fixed ballistic target is dodgeable; direct contact is 80% of player maximum health before the existing armor/resistance/roll/god handling. In the real game, 100 health + 100 armor becomes 72 health + 48 armor after an 80-raw hit. Missed stones do not apply splash damage. Removed back stock replenishes after a visible delay.
- Swept contacts use the earliest cover/player hit, including thin geometry. Both abilities respect initial line of sight and midflight cover. Busy casts stay visible and animate outside the normal 40m joint LOD limit.
- Hazards are bounded to 32 projectiles / 96 footprints and pooled. Acid is one RGBA drawing batch; twelve idle demons plus twelve idle Colossus add 36 attachment draws. No added point lights. Epoch-guarded cleanup handles a lethal damage callback synchronously entering Training blackout without stale processing, duplicated release or stranded hazards.
