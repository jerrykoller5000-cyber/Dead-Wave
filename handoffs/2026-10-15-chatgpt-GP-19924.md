## Implementation

- Shoulder roots extend into the torso and actual shoulder sockets. Forearms flow into palms as one profile; bare shins flow into feet. The pelvic waistband overlaps abdomen and both hip sockets instead of ending as a separate oval.
- combat/zombie-joins.js applies a spatial blend once at bind time, using existing bones. Coincident vertices on opposing sides of a join receive the same blend in the socket, so they stay together through bending/twisting. There is no added animation callback, draw or per-frame CPU deformation.
- On limb loss, mixed weights across collapsed bones revert to their original rigid owner. Pool reset restores exact bind indices/weights. Hidden source meshes remain rigid and clone normally into the severed piece.
- Actual Guardians reuse core/skin-merge.js without changing it. Opaque visible body draws drop43->5 (Guardian) and41->3 (Caveguard); existing eyes stay separate. Final sculpt covers exposed elbow ends and provides continuous wrist/palm and ankle/foot profiles.
- Spider keeps rigid chitin and its dark sockets; the knee socket belongs to the articulated knee. Demon keeps the approved sculpture and receives the common joint deformation.
