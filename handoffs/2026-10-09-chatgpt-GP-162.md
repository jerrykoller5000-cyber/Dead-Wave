## Render and resource design

The local procedural atlas provides eight surface types: skin, cloth, metal, stone, bone, leather, root and wet tissue. Three shared 1024×512 maps provide color, relief and roughness/metalness; no external assets, CDN or build step. Atlas generation is deterministic and does not consume gameplay random state. Approximate atlas storage is 6 MiB of pixels, about 8 MiB including GPU mip levels, shared across all bodies.

Opaque meshes still batch once per existing bone. Cached immutable geometry and atlas maps are shared, while hit/burn/fuse materials remain per-instance. Bomber pulse and Demon flare references remain attached to actual visible surfaces. No new animation joints, lights or per-frame model work were added.

The matched 48-visible-body scene retains 656 meshes; triangle count increases from 291,168 to 547,296. The additional detail is a deliberate geometry cost, not a claim that GPU budgets have been met. Headless timings under concurrent test load are observational only; see after/checks.json and before/checks.json.
