# CL-352 · Every agent checks the board every 15 minutes: crew.mjs standby (D-147)

Jerry, 10 Nov ~20:15Z: "I am going to make them all autonomous to check the board in 15 minute increments. Give me a
universal agent copy and paste to make sure they do that and stay running and never idle until we reach the end of our
workload."

- `node crew/crew.mjs standby <agent> [--minutes N]`: returns at once with the agent's work (open requests to it, its
  next ready card, its own in-progress one included); else waits up to 15 minutes, looking every 20 s, and returns the
  moment something comes in. The same work reported in the last 15 minutes waits for something new instead (a card that
  waits on someone in words, not by id, would spin): remembered per agent in the OS temp folder. An idle card's "last
  seen" updates, so the director sees who waits. `--minutes` takes fractions for shells that stop commands sooner
  (Cowork's: about 3 minutes). crew/STANDBY-OFF (the director writes it when the workload is done, or Jerry says stop)
  ends it for everyone.
- Tested in a scratch copy: work at once; the same work again waits; a new request mid-wait returns it; STANDBY-OFF; an
  empty queue waits and times out. On the PC: Antigravity's AG-286 returned, then waited the second time.
