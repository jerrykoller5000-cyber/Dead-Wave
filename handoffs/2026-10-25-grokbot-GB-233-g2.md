Defects under 4, part by part (look calls, reported):
- AK (vs the AKM): 1) No gas tube. A real AK has the gas block about two-thirds of the way down the barrel and the tube above the barrel carrying the upper handguard; here the upper wood floats as a block over a bare barrel. 2) The magazine is straight. The AK's banana curve is its silhouette signature, and a straight box reads as a different gun. 3) The lower handguard is too deep: it reaches almost to the magazine's top, where a real one is a slim wooden sleeve under the barrel.
- M4 (vs the M4A1): 1) The magazine is a straight box; a STANAG has a slight forward curve. 2) The lower receiver is one slab, with no magwell flare or trigger-guard shape. 3) Materials are the shared mid-metal palette (group 1's finding).
- AK/AA12/sniper reloads from his eyes: the magazine well stays out of frame mid-swap, even with GB-228's cant. A deeper cant or a lower dip is a feel change, proposed.
- Shotgun (vs the 870): the receiver's humpback is a Browning Auto-5 shape on a pump gun. It reads fine at gameplay scale. Bead-over-receiver alignment at 0.165 below the bead is how a bead shotgun aims; kept.
