# CL-180 · Claude · Ladder climb: hands and feet on the real rungs

Jerry (with screenshots of the mast climb): "Hands and feet do not use the rungs of ladder like they should."

## What changed (index.html only)

- `ladderNow()` now says where each ladder's rungs are:

  | Ladder | First rung | Spacing | Count | Rail half width |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Mast | gy + 0.3 | 0.4 | 35 | 0.15 |
  | Watchtower | gy + 0.35 | 0.36 | 15 | 0.24 |
  | HQ | its foot + 0.3 | (HQ_H - 0.5) / 11 | 12 | 0.17 |

  It also gives how far in front of his line the rungs' face is.
- `ladderLimbs()`: every hand and foot holds a real rung, hand over hand and foot over foot.
  - When the lower of a pair falls below its reach, it moves to the rung just above the other's, once that is in reach.
  - Going down, the upper one goes to the rung just below the other's.
  - One hand at a time and one foot at a time. A move eases over about 0.13 s, faster on the faster tower ladder.
  - Standing still, nothing moves.
- Reach windows (`LADDER_GRIP`), measured from the marine's real joints (shoulders 1.04 m up, hips 0.58 m, legs 0.27 + 0.25 m):
  - hands 0.7 to 1.5 m above his feet
  - feet -0.12 to 0.6 m
  - his body hangs 0.08 m low on his arms and sits 0.24 m behind the rungs' face
- Feet: a two-bone leg solve puts each ankle over its rung with the ball of the foot on it and the sole level.
- Hands: the arm IK, with the grip point on the rung between the rails.
  - `ladderHands()` lays the hands on again at the very end of the marine's frame. The gun hold, knife, draw, grenade and idle layers ran after the pose and were pulling the arms off the rungs.
- The grip state resets when he leaves a ladder, and re-grips if the ladder is jumped along (the debug `climbMast`).
- Debug: `lightningDbg.limbs()` and `LADDER_GRIP`.

## Checked (cloud, headless)

Hand points and ankles were measured against the real rung positions while climbing the mast up and down, and the watchtower.
- Every limb that is holding sits on its rung: hands 0.00 m off the rung's height and 0.02 m from its face; ankles about 0.00 m over the rung's top.
- Only the limb in the middle of a move is between rungs.
- Standing still on the mast: all four exactly on rungs.
- Screenshots from the back and from the right side show the hands round the rungs and the feet on them.
- No page errors. Main script passes node --check.
