## What the player notices
- Who you rescue depends on the camp: the trapper's camp is Cpl. Okafor, the rangers' camp is Sgt. Brandt, the hikers' camp is Spc. Pike.
- They don't follow you. From the next morning they're on the HQ roof, up the ladder on the west wall. Each has a post in a corner: Okafor north-west, Brandt north-east, Pike south-east. Every post has a sleeping bag and a crate, and Brandt's also has the M240B on its tripod. The figures are stand-ins for now: the bare marine used at the fire, until CL-75's figures arrive.
- By day they lie on their bags. When the alarm goes they stand up, and until dawn they shoot at the dead within about 26 m of the HQ that they can see over the roof's edge. Brandt fires bursts of four from the M240B; Okafor and Pike fire single M4 shots. Dead right under the walls are out of their sight, so those are still his to deal with. Nothing goes after them.
- Climb up and stand next to Okafor and press E: once a day she heals him to full. Next to anyone else, or Okafor after the day's heal, E talks: their roof line shows as a banner until GP-95's talk card is in.
- Once Pike is back, every repair costs 75% (this is GB-91's trapper discount, now hers).
- Gone: the hikers' medic raising regen to 50% (it stays 40%), and the ranger's free turret by the HQ.
- A new run clears the roof.
