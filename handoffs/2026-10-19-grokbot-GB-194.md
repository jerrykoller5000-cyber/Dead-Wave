# GB-194 A cheaper tick for the far, idle dead at night 20 (Grokbot, 2026-10-19)
Changed: index.html, before updateZombies and at the top of its per-zombie loop:
- ZOMBIE_TICK { crowd 24, near 16, far 30, maxDt 0.25, ranged: spitter, screamer, military, spider, colossus } and zombieTickDt(z, frameDt, on).
- With 24 or more zombies (and not in training), a zombie that is not winding up, swinging, charging, leaping, spitting, screaming or ability-busy, is not a ranged kind or a boss, and is more than 16 m from its target ticks:
  - every 2nd frame;
  - every 3rd frame if it is behind the camera, in the fog, or more than 30 m away.
- The skipped frames' dt is summed into its next tick (capped at 0.25 s), so it covers the same ground. Each zombie gets a random phase, which keeps the load even.
- Everything else ticks every frame as before, and so does every zombie of a group under 24 (the tests' small spawns).
- In the loop, `const dt = zombieTickDt(...)` shadows the frame dt; 0 means skip this frame.
