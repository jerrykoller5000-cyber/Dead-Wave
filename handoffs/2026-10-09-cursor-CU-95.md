## How

`node tools/zombiesheet.mjs [out] [type ...]` (out defaults to review/zombie-types). It enters the Training Ground, puts the
targets away and hides the HUD, then for each type: spawns it in the build room (tagged trainingZ so the room's first-seconds
sweep leaves it), lets it move for 0.9 s so it is in a living pose, takes it out of the zombie list so nothing moves it again,
stands it on the range turned 25 degrees off the camera, and frames it by its own bounding box (30 degree lens, 17 degrees
down), so the 4.3 m colossus fits like the 2 m shambler. The marine is parked behind the camera.

These replace AG-53's (qa/shots/2026-10-08-AG-53), whose single shots mostly missed the zombie (the brute's shows only the
marine landing) and whose group shot had them all in one pile.

The maimed crawl (no legs) and hop (one leg) are states of these types, not types of their own, so they aren't shot.
