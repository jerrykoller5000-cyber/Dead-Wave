# GB-139 - GP-142's lines wired: build upgrades findable, the hold-G throw told - Grokbot, 2026-10-06

This follows up GB-133 (Jerry: "No way seen to upgrade build items") and GB-134 (G became a hold), using ChatGPT's
GP-142 copy.

## Changed
What the player notices:
- **Placing something else with the reticle on one of his own pieces** that has a next tier whose plans he owns, the
  place banner adds `build.message.upgradeAvailable`, e.g. "Wood wall can upgrade to Reinforced wood wall. Open B and
  choose Upgrade, first on the Structure wheel." It isn't shown with no plans, off the piece, with the Upgrade tool
  itself (that has its own line), or while dragging a line. It's checked every 250 ms, not every frame.
- **The first wall, door, gate, floor or turret he builds** (any build with an upgrade track) shows
  `tips.building.upgrade` on the big banner for 6 s, titled `tips.building.title` ("Building").
- **The first time he holds G** (when a hold starts) shows `tips.combat.grenadeHold` ("Hold G for a longer throw…"),
  titled `tips.combat.title`.
- Each tip shows once per browser profile (`localStorage['dw.tips.v1']`, like the coach's cards) and sends
  `publishUI('tip-shown', { id, key })`.
- The upgrade tool's old hard-coded lines now go through the existing keys: `build.message.upgradePoint`,
  `build.message.noUpgrades` ({name}) and `build.message.maxUpgrade` ({name}). The old English stays as the fallback.
- Input labels: the game has no remapping in index.html, so dwText fills {buildWheel}, {fire} and {grenade} from the
  defaults.
