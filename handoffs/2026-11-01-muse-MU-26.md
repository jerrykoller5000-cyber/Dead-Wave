## 0. What stage 1 built (the pistol's shape, in code)

`game/viewmodel.js` builds the FP gun by **copying named parts of the 3P model**: `gun = { …, slide: map.get(direct(ud.slide)), mag: map.get(direct(ud.mag)), … }` (game/viewmodel.js:242), and its own comment states MU-11 finding 3 — "the palm ride and the slide's grab work in the gun root's space: a magazine or slide deeper in the [hierarchy breaks it]" (game/viewmodel.js:239). What the code then does with those parts is pistol-shaped throughout:

- **The slide is the moving part.** On a shot: `gun.slide.position.z -= P.slideKick * k` (kick out-and-back over ~75 ms, game/viewmodel.js:569). In a reload: the rack (hand pulls the slide back `rack[0]→[1]`, hold, release at `rack[2]`, game/viewmodel.js:570–573). The support hand's `work` target *is the slide*: "name === 'work' && gun.slide" → grab point from the slide's local box (game/viewmodel.js:335–338).
- **The mag is the second moving part:** `reloadMagPose` copies `baseMagPos/baseMagRot` and hides it in the pouch window (index.html:25028ff); the support palm rides it (holdWeapon places the hand on the mag in 'hand' state).
- **One grip + one support:** hands come from `viewmodel.js:251–269` reading the posed 3P marine's wrist frames (per GP-19993's table note) — i.e. stage 1 derives FP hands from the 3P hold, it does not consume authored sockets.
- **Reload plans are per-gun data already:** `RELOAD_PLAN` (index.html:25004): pistol = mag beats + 'slide' rack; sniper = 'bolt'; **m4, ak, uzi = mag-only, no work beats** (`reloadPlanMag(0.12, 0.34, 0.62, [], 0.84)`; uzi 0.10/0.32/0.58, 0.80).

So stage 2's question per gun is narrow: does it have a copyable mag (yes, all three), does it have a copyable *work* part (no — all three have `slide: null`), and what must the builders name so the view gun can move something that isn't a slide.

## 1. The GW-4 (m4)

- **Parts the view gun must copy:** mag (polymer STANAG, curved, at gun-local (0, −0.16, 0.05), studio/weapon-longarms.js:137ff), charging handle (a static box at (0, 0.068, −0.195), :64), holo sight (glass + dot at z 0.045, :128ff), suppressor (gunSuppressor 'm4'), muzzle/flash (`gunEffects` at (0, 0.02, 0.98)), stock (telescoping, butt −0.505, matters for hip distance — spec §Evidence).
- **Direct children?** The mag is `K.group(0, -0.16, 0.05)` assigned `gun.userData.mag` with base pos/rot (studio/weapon-longarms.js:147ff) — a kit group parented to the gun root: **direct-child-shaped, satisfying finding 3 for the mag**. `gun.userData.slide = null` and `barrelAssembly = null` explicitly. **There is no bolt, bolt-release or charging-handle *node*** — the charging handle is anonymous geometry inside the receiver slabs; nothing can move it. GP-19993's proposed `work` socket (−0.038, −0.03, 0.11, the left receiver control at studio/weapon-longarms.js:68–74) is a *candidate press point* with no node behind it.
- **Grip/support today (3P):** WEAPON_HOLD at index.html:48824ff (spec cites :48828 for m4's grip + foregrip); handguard spans z .21–.70, so the proposed support z .36 (table §Gun-local sockets) sits on it. Grip local [0, −0.106, −0.076] per the table (copied from WEAPON_HOLD).
- **Sights:** `SIGHT.m4` iron pos [0, 0.134, −0.12] co-witnessed through the holo dot (game/sight-points.js:13); ads uses the holo window — the view gun must copy the holo glass + dot meshes (they're slabs/boxes in the builder, copyable as geometry, not named nodes — the view gun copies by part-mapping, so they need to be in the map: check they're reachable as direct children or accept whole-receiver copying for the sight cluster).
- **Reload beats & what moves:** mag out (0.12 reach, 0.34 pouch), mag in (0.62 seat), done 0.84 — **only the mag moves**; no rack/work beats. A real GW-4 reload ends with a bolt release or charging-handle rack on an empty mag; today nothing in the plan or model can do it.
- **Needs added:** (a) a named **charging-handle group** (and/or bolt carrier) in makeM4Model, direct child, `gun.userData.work` (or slide-equivalent semantics: slideKick → carrier kick is also the *firing* motion — a GW-4's bolt cycles every shot; stage 1's slideKick has no target today); (b) RELOAD_PLAN m4 work beats after seat (release press at the table's work point, or a rack at the charging handle (0, .068, −.195)); (c) VM_POSES.m4 + VM_SOCKETS.m4 from the table (hip z −0.70 for the butt).

## 2. The AK (ak / KR-7)

- **Parts:** long curved mag (`K.group(0, −0.06, 0.04)`, deeper curve profile, studio/weapon-longarms.js:239ff (mag at builder-relative :76)), charging handle **on the carrier, right side** (builder comment :16 — safety lever + charging handle; static geometry), leaf sight, wood stock (butt −0.502 per spec).
- **Direct children?** Mag yes (`gun.userData.mag`, same kit-group shape). `slide = null`, `barrelAssembly = null` again. The AK's signature reload motion — **rock the mag in, then rack the right-side charging handle with the support (left) hand reaching under/over** — has no node for either the carrier or the handle.
- **Grip/support:** WEAPON_HOLD ak grip [0, −0.126, −0.095], support [0, −0.025, 0.37] (table, from WEAPON_HOLD index.html:48824ff); the support hand leaves the handguard to rack — the pose set needs that travel (the table's `reload: 'ak'` clip name).
- **Sights:** `SIGHT.ak` [0, 0.0943, 0] low leaf (game/sight-points.js:14); ads.rear 0.70 (long stock, table).
- **Reload beats:** identical mag plan to m4 (0.12/0.34/0.62/0.84, no work beats). What moves: the mag only — and the AK mag should **rock** (rotate into the well, front lug first), which `reloadMagPose` can't express (it lerps the mag as a rigid seated/hand/pouch prop with base rotation restored — a rock is a rotation keyframe mid-seat; the pose system (viewmodel-poses.js clips) can carry it as a gun-path rotation, owner's detail).
- **Needs added:** named **carrier/charging-handle group** (right side), `gun.userData.work`; work beats in RELOAD_PLAN.ak (rack after seat); mag rock keys in the 'ak' reload clip; VM_POSES.ak (hip z −0.75, the biggest kick of the three).

## 3. The Wasp (uzi)

- **Parts:** mag *in the grip* (`K.group(0, −0.205, −0.0435)`, studio/weapon-sidearms.js:389ff (builder-relative :74)), top charging knob (built low in the sight channel — GB-235 moved it so the sights are usable), folding wire stock (butt only −0.088 behind the sight point when folded, per spec), sights tower.
- **Direct children?** Mag yes (userData.mag). `slide = null`: the Wasp is an open-bolt SMG — **its bolt is the firing motion** (it slams forward per shot), and there is no bolt node. The charging knob is the reload's work part (cock it after the mag), also anonymous.
- **Grip/support:** grip [−0.088…] table: grip [0, −0.088, −0.027], support [−0.055, −0.035, 0.055] — the support is *on the receiver* beside the grip (no foregrip; it's a machine pistol hold). This is the gun that breaks stage 1's assumption hardest in pose terms: support hand barely travels for the mag (grip-mag drops straight down into the palm).
- **Sights:** `SIGHT.uzi` [0, 0.156, −0.25] level with the rear tower (game/sight-points.js:11, GB-228/235); ads.rear 0.34.
- **Reload beats:** mag plan (0.10/0.32/0.58, 0.80), no work beats. Needs: cock the knob after seating.
- **Needs added:** named **bolt group** (for both shot-cycle and cocking — one node serves slideKick-equivalent and rack), `gun.userData.work` = the knob; RELOAD_PLAN.uzi work beats; VM_POSES.uzi (hip z −0.54, light kick at 14 rps — the table's note).

## 4. The pistol assumptions, collected (what stage 2 must generalise)

1. **"The moving part is a slide that kicks on every shot and racks in the reload."** → generalise to *a work part*: slide (pistol), carrier (GW-4), carrier+handle (AK), bolt (Wasp; sniper already says 'bolt' in RELOAD_PLAN). viewmodel's `gun.slide` field and the `work`-grab (game/viewmodel.js:335) should read `ud.work || ud.slide` — one seam, all guns.
2. **"The mag is in front of the grip and drops into the palm."** Wasp's is *in* the grip; AK's *rocks*. reloadMagPose's seated/hand/pouch states survive; the seat motion needs a per-gun rotation key (poses).
3. **"One grip, support on a forend."** Rifles: yes, but support *leaves* to work the action (AK under-reach, GW-4 press/rack) — the pose clips must include support-off-support beats (the table's reload clip names: carbine/ak/smg).
4. **"Butt doesn't exist."** Pistols have none; rifles put 0.49–0.50 m behind the grip — hip/ads distances in the table exist because of it. The view gun's near-plane behaviour with a stock in frame (shouldered in ADS: the butt should be *at/behind* the eye, invisible) needs the ADS camera check per gun (ads.rear values).
5. **"Sights are a slide-top notch."** GW-4 aims through a holo window (glass material — copied glass at FP scale is a render-order/transparency question for AG), AK a low leaf, Wasp a tower; SIGHT data covers all three already (game/sight-points.js:11–14) — GB-259 owns final sight pictures.
