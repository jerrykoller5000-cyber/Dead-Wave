# CL-227: the zombie skins' fixed-size skeleton; the far-shadow toggle keeps each mesh's own flag

**What AG-106's journal named (night 20 High, the 5080, CL-226's bench):** 40-43 pipelines built in 30 s, none
released, almost all with new shaders:
- 20-22 `zombie-skin` (skinZombieMesh, CL-138's one-draw body): a new vertex shader each, one on each of the 25-45 ms
  frames. three's SkinningNode writes the skeleton's length into the vertex shader (`referenceBuffer('skeleton.boneMatrices',
  'mat4', bones.length)`), and a body's skeleton is the joint groups its visible parts hang from, so the counts vary
  (10-17 at spawn in the menu, t994's probe; more variety in a fight: hidden parts drop their groups). Every new count
  was a shader and a pipeline built on the frame that zombie was first drawn. The fragment shader was reused every time,
  which is what a bone-count difference looks like.
- 18 `demon-fire-fists` shadow pipelines at once when demons came within 34 m: updateZombies' far-shadow toggle set
  `castShadow = want` on every mesh, so the fire fists, built not to cast (game/zombie-ability-visuals.js), started
  casting the moment a demon came near.

**Changed (index.html):**
- skinZombieMesh: after the bind and the join blend, the skeleton is rebound padded to 32 bones (64 for a rig over
  32) by repeating its last bone. Nothing is weighted to the padding; the join blend mapped the real bones first. One
  skeleton size, so one shader per body material, and the load's warm-up (every kind drawn once) covers them.
  Cost: up to 32 bone matrices a zombie a frame instead of 10-17.
- updateZombies' far-shadow toggle: `castShadow = want && own flag`, the own flag kept in `userData._castLod`, the
  field setZombieRenderLod already uses for the same purpose. Parts built not to cast (the fists, glow, eyes) never
  cast now.

**New test:** `tools/tests/t270.js`, real renderer only (`node tools/realtest.mjs t270 --soft`): ten kinds, two each:
every skeleton 32 or 64, at most two sizes, no weight on the padding, a posed vertex finite, no page errors. On the
test page (no SkinnedMesh in fakethree) it passes with a note.

Tests: t270 4/4 on the real renderer (cloud, --soft): 22 skins, all 32. Test page: t93 11/11; t75 15/16 with and
without this change alike ("nobody inside him: 0.040 m", a cloud-only red; it's green on Jerry's PC, AG-103).
Not verified: night 20 on the 5080 (AG-107: the bench and the pipelines journal again, expected 0-2 zombie-skin
pipelines and no demon-fire-fists shadows).
