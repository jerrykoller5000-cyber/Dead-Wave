## 1. Building in the build room (index.html `placeRefusal`; world/training.js `tg.build`)

B opens build mode in the Training Ground as outside. Where: the game's own lattice inside the build room's walls
(17 x 19 squares). Refused, with the reason on the build banner:
- the range and anywhere outside the room: "Build in the build room";
- the zombie gate's apron: "Too close to the zombie gate";
- the doorway's apron (three squares inside it): "Keep the doorway clear";
- the floor in front of the HQ panel and the skull window: "Keep the terminals clear";
- the infirmary bay: "That is the infirmary".
Everything else is the game's usual rules (one piece per square, stacking, not on him, cash, blueprints). The valley's
ground rules (the map's edge) are skipped in here; water, rough ground, trees and rocks never apply (flat floor).
Builds go when he leaves (resetGame, as before). Text keys `training.build.{outside,gate,door,terminal,infirmary}`
and `training.noBuildZone` have English fallbacks (ChatGPT's to word in strings.js).

## 2. The gate's no-build zone

Every square whose middle is within 5 squares (10 m) of the gate's opening: 55 squares, five rows deep in front of the
gate, rounding off at the sides. Painted in yellow-and-black hazard stripes (one mesh, the stripes run on across the
squares, a hair of bare floor between them so the lattice still reads), a yellow edge where it meets buildable floor,
and NO BUILD ZONE stencilled in front. The painted squares are exactly the refused ones (the test checks it).
Knobs: `TRAINING.GATE_CLEAR` (5), `gateHalf` (4 m, the gate's half width) in world/training.js.

## 3. The hangar

The build room's walls go up to 24 m (`TRAINING.HANGAR_H`): tiles to 6 m, steel siding above with a ledge between,
steel columns every 6 m under roof trusses, high-bay lamps hung from the trusses (the light switch puts them out with
the strips), a crane rail along the long walls, a dark roof deck. The range keeps its 6 m walls; its side of the
shared wall carries on in siding. Wall solids (rounds) stand the full height; the columns are solids too. Nothing up
there casts a shadow. The mortar's highest shell climbs about 21.5 m above the floor (T capped at 3.2 s:
2·T² + the muzzle), under the trusses' bottom chord (22 m).
