# CL-187 · Claude · The dead on their feet: ankles for every kind, the roll of each step, the lean of a start and a stop

Jerry (2026-10-12, after CL-186): "do an animation and motion pass on all the zombies."

What the strips showed (the game itself, headless, each kind walking at the marine from the side; review/zombies-CL187/):
the per-kind passes of the last days (CL-137's legs, GP-19850..19890's feral, leaper, spider, bomber, brute, military,
spitter, screamer and drowned) have given every kind its own stride, steering, tells and attacks. What was common to
all of them was the feet. GP-19852 gave seven kinds an ankle and a flat sole; the feral, demon, colossus, guardian and
cave guardian still walked on feet fixed to their shins (the toe in the dirt at the back of the stride, the heel in it
at the front, plainest on the military before GP-19864 and still on the feral), and a flat sole alone hovers through
the swing like a tray and lands flat. Nothing in any of them leaned into a start, a stop or a turn.

## game/zombie-feet.js (new; game/zombie-feet.test.mjs, 3 tests)

1. **Ankles for the rest** (`prepareZombieFeet`): the same split of the batched concept shin that GP-19852 does (by
   boot colour and height), into a `zombie-ankle` group at the joint — on bare feet (feral, demon, guardian, cave
   guardian) the pivot sits at the shin's foot so the cut is at the joint; the colossus's boots split like the
   others'. Stored under the same keys (`leg.userData.shamblerAnkle`, `shamblerFootY`) so every reset and the skin
   bind treat them alike. The leaper runs on GP-19856's own paws and the spider plants its own limbs: untouched.
2. **The roll of a step** (`applyZombieFeet`, after every per-kind pose of the frame): the heel lands toe-up, the
   sole is flat through the stance, the heel comes off the ground ahead of the toe (the leg is re-solved a touch
   higher so the ball of the foot stays down), the toe hangs through the swing. Where each foot is in its step is
   read off the leg itself — GP-19852's kinds each keep a private stride clock, so the foot's height over its ground
   and the way it is moving (back along the ground: planted; up and forward: swinging) are the one thing every rig
   agrees on; the kinds on the old sine gait use its phase. The kinds without a module also get their soles laid
   flat here. Off during attacks, hits, knockdowns, rises, charges, leaps, the brute's and military's moves, the
   drowned's grabs, the shambler's recovery, the spit and the scream (the modules own the legs then).
3. **The lean of a start, a stop and a turn** (`applyZombieLean`): every body leans into its own acceleration — in
   on a start, back on a stop, into a turn (the centripetal pull) — from its position's smoothed velocity, so the
   horde's shoving does not jitter the chest; a small dip of the hips and the head taking half back. Kinds with a
   bank of their own (feral, leaper, spider) get the forward part only.

Knobs, live: `TT.zombieFeetDbg.ZFEET` (toeUp 0.17, pushMax 0.42, pushFrom 0.62, hang 0.15, footLen, per-kind
scale `kinds.<kind>.k`; `on: false` is the old feet) and `TT.zombieFeetDbg.ZLEAN` (perAccel 0.012, max 0.12,
side 0.6, rate 5; `on: false`).
