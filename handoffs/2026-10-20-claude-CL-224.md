# CL-224: the desktop app's Rust crates in the notices (D-84)

The exe carries 246 Rust crates (Tauri and what it pulls in) on Windows, and their licences ask for their notices to
travel with the binary. NOTICES.txt only named Tauri.

Changed:
- `NOTICES-desktop.txt` (new, at the root, 212 KB): every crate in `desktop/src-tauri/Cargo.lock` for
  x86_64-pc-windows-msvc, grouped under its licence with the licence's full text (cargo-about 0.9.2). 98 MIT texts,
  Apache-2.0, Unicode-3.0, BSD-3-Clause, Zlib, and MPL-2.0 for five (cssparser, cssparser-macros, selectors,
  dtoa-short, option-ext; unmodified, source on crates.io, which is what the MPL asks of a binary).
- `NOTICES.txt`: the desktop paragraph points to it.
- `tools/package.mjs`: FILES ships it next to NOTICES.txt (so the installer's `game/` has both).
- `desktop/src-tauri/about.toml` and `about.hbs` (new): the accepted licences and the template; `desktop/README.md`
  says how to redo the file after a Cargo.lock change.

Tests: `cargo about generate` in the cloud on the device's Cargo.lock (identical); `node tools/package.mjs --list`:
1,446 files with it, 1,445 without; `node --check`.
Not verified: the installer with it (AG-104 builds it next).
