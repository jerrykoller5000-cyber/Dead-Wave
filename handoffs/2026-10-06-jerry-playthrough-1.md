# Jerry's playthrough 1 — 2026-10-06

His notes after his first full playthrough of the 1.0 candidate, verbatim (CL, 2026-10-06). The board's tasks for them are
CU-88 to CU-91, GB-133 to GB-138, GP-138 to GP-141, CL-125 to CL-130 and AG-52; the zombie rules are D-77.

> Tons of stuff for the board on the playthorugh:
>
> CIF: 1. "Your Gravewalker" view needs to pause rotation on default. 2. You should not see items inside CIF that you do
> not have currently unlocked. 3. NVG's do not need to be on gravewalkers head unless the helmet is currently being worn
> and NVG's are unlocked. 4. Gravewalker needs the stance where his arms are by his side instead of sticking out in front
> of him. 5. Need a full view of marine and a slight zoom on what is being customized. For instance when you are working
> on his boots you cannot actually see them with the current character view. CIF window needs an updated appearance.
> 6. Ability to change the "Lighting resistant" boot color in CIF.
>
> Minimap: Enemy indicator needs be more accurate in the direction of an enemy. Needs to show multiple enemy directions,
> indicator needs to be smaller. If the player has not gotten close or seen an enemy during the daytime the indicator
> does not need to show their location. The indicator will however pick up on the direction of enemies within 50m at
> night. Indicator sometimes lies about actual direction of enemies relevant to camera rotation.
>
> Map: Map needs camera direction as well as player direction like the minimap to keep players from getting too
> confused. Replace all the names on the map with a ? besides HQ until they are discovered by the player. Give the caves
> their real name on the map upon discovery. EX: Root Warren. Change HQ's name to FOB Threshold on maps.
>
> New weapon/Skulls processed unlock notification: Instead of the new weapon unlock being dead center it needs to appear
> on the left-Side of the screen under the Dead-Wave and Prep sreen almost as if was a dropdown extension of the panel.
> Same with skulls processed. We need to move as many notifications from the center to the left-side. Excluding
> killstreaks.
>
> Grenade and 40mm launcher: Grenade needs an throw arch that grows as the player holds the g button down. One quick tap
> will release the grenade close, three seconds will grow the arc to its max throw distance. If the arc is cutoff by
> terrain the arc will indicate that visually. For the 40mm launcher we will have an arc similar to the mortar.
>
> Gravewalker: Run and walk animation a little weird, he kinda bobs back and forth in a funny way right now. Legs and
> body rotation look goofy and a little rigid. Needs heavy breathing after running on idle.
>
> Kiosk: For Tabs like Weapon, the two sub-tabs need to be colored different and named different to draw attention to
> them. For instance; Weapons (Yellow) underneath GUNS (Green) Upgrades (Brown). This needs to be done for all tabs and
> subtabs.
>
> Armory: A little 3d rotatable view of your weapon just like the character rotation screen in CIF.
>
> Indicators: Need mission indicators on minimap and map.
>
> Radio Mast: Need to make it more obvious on how to repair and make the repair bar a little more noticeable when
> repairing. Make the supply pick up at radio mast in a separate location to the place where you repair as not to
> confuse players.
>
> Music: Better Music deserving special attention each night. Here is your inspiration:
> https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zjyQWpJc6qk,
>
> Hush and Warrens: Bug right now where you charge the hush, go up to the cave and press E to go down and nothing
> happens. Cannot sound the alarm after that.
>
> Zombies: Better models and animation for all zombies. They need to be more engaging visually and behaviorally. Zombies
> need to all be faster on average. Right now they are pretty slow. They need to reach up to 90% of players speed while
> sprinting. Sprinters need to outpace player on sprint. 70% of the zombies need to congregate while the other half
> attempts to flank the player. At no point do you really get the horde experience like you do in project zmboid or
> left4dead. I don't know if it is the pacing of the waves or what but you always feel like you are just facing down
> small individual battles instead of a mass of zombies. By day 20 it just felt like a slow boring trickle of zombies.
> Zombies with lower extremities blown off need to crawl and have a crawling animation. Zombies with all there limbs
> blown off need to die, right now I saw a bunch of nugget zombies crawling around which is funny but I think we should
> get rid of it. Zombies with one leg blown off need to hop towards player at 75% speed. aw
>
> SFX: We need unique sounds for the different zombies.
>
> Bugs: There is a bug where when you reload the launcher, 6 casing come out for every 1 casing. So there are like a ton
> of casings flying around. No way seen to upgrade build items.
>
