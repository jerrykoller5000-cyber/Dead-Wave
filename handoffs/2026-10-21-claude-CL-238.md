# CL-238: bench --prof journals the render objects three makes (measurement only)

AG-111's spikes that built nothing (no node build, no pipeline, no upload, no GC) are `r:render` 15-18 ms with
`zombies` 6-10 ms, about 175 frames (3 s of game time) apart in every run. That is not CL-219's sun-shadow cadence
(150 frames exactly), so CL-237 may not be what lifts the 1 % low; something on a ~3 s clock is.

A lead from a three-only probe (`qa/2026-10-21-CL-238/ro-probe.html`, headless WebGPU on SwiftShader): three r175 keeps
one render object per object, material, render context and lights node. Hiding or showing one light made a new render
object for every lit mesh (10 of 10); setting its intensity to 0 made none; a shadow redraw, a mesh hidden and shown, a
mesh removed and re-added and castShadow flips made none. A new render object makes its bindings and finds its pipeline
(no build when the shaders were seen before): CPU in `r:render` with nothing in the build or pipeline journals. So a
light that comes into the scene's light list and leaves it (a gun's muzzle light made after the light pool's last
`adopt` scan, which runs every 120 frames; a light under a top-level object that sceneCull hides) would look exactly
like these spikes.
