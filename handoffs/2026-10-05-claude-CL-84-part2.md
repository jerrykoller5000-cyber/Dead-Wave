# claude — CL-84 The marine's own animation (part 2: the reload) — 2026-10-05
Changed:          The magazine change for the mag-fed long guns (GW-4, KR-7, Breacher-12) now comes from the studio, as data: studio/clips/marine/reload-rifle.json, a hand path clip (a new studio format, dw-hand-path/1, read by studio/marine-reload.js). It keys where his support hand goes over the reload: from the forend to the magazine to strip it, out round the front of his body to a magazine pouch (the carrier's when he wears one, the belt's when not), back under the well, pushes the new magazine home with a slap, and back to the forend; and what the magazine does meanwhile (seated, drops, gone, in his hand, seated). The game turns the keys' named places into points every frame (the gun tilts and moves while he reloads), runs a smooth curve through them and the arm IK follows it; the fresh magazine rides in his palm from the pouch until it seats. Before, the hand went in straight lines (through the chest on the way to the belt pouch) and the new magazine floated up under the gun on its own while the hand was elsewhere. Part 1 (the walk and the run) is still waiting in review for Jerry's "good".
