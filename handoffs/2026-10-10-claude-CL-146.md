# CL-146: the hangar stops flickering through the walls; the security door answers its keypads (Claude, 2026-10-10)

Jerry: "The pillar and textures of the new hanger are bleed through the walls and they are tweaking out and shifting.
The other thing is that player need to use the keypad next to the security door to open and close it instead of going
directly to the door."

**Look first:** `Claude outputs/hangar-walls-keypad.jpg` (in the game: the range wall before and after, close up, the
siding above the range, both keypads).

## 1. The flicker (world/training.js, the CL-143 hangar block)

Why: the walls are single planes, and the wall between the range and the build room is one plane each way at x = -8.
The hangar's steel columns stood flush against the walls, so the back face of each column on that wall lay in the
same plane as the range's tiles. Two surfaces in one plane fight for the same pixels: dark stripes that crawl as you
move, at every column (z -6, 0, 6, 12, 18, 24). The roof truss chords ended in the same plane above the range walls
(behind the range's siding, 3 mm off), and the ledge between tile and siding had its back face on the wall too.

Now nothing in the hangar touches a wall's plane:
- the columns (body, flange, footplate) stand 6 cm off the walls and stop 3 cm under the roof; their solids are 0.54 m
  wide (were 0.6, touching the wall);
- the truss chords stop 10 cm short of the side walls; the purlins and crane rails 10 cm short of the end walls;
- the ledge stands 2 cm off its wall.
A test checks the columns' solids, that no box above the range walls ends in the shared wall's plane, and that
nothing of the hangar hangs out over the range at 6 m (my first pass dropped the ledge's turn and ran one 17 m out
across the range; caught in the pictures, fixed, and now tested).

## 2. The keypads (world/training.js, index.html `trainingActionTarget`)

The housings by the door (GP-167, one each side of the wall) are the keypads now: a 12-key face (red cancel, green
enter) under the status lamp. `tg.securityDoor.keypads` = `[{ side, x, y, z, front: { x, z } }]` (world; side -1 the
build room, +1 the range). The door's action ("Open/Close the security door", or locked) shows only within 1.1 m of a
keypad's front (60 cm out from the wall, beside the door); standing in the doorway offers nothing. Same toggle,
lock and sound as before (training.security).
