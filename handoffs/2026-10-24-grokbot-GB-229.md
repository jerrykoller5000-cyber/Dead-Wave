# GB-229 - grokbot: auto-reload on empty (N9)

- Jerry (N9): "Instead of having the player reload, let's have them auto-reload on empty. I've heard that complaint too many times from players."
- index.html: autoReloadDue(ammo, dt), defined just above startReload(), runs once a frame in tryFire (after the melee block, so every gun with a magazine, belt, tank or loader is covered). When the gun in hand is dry and has something to load (a spare magazine that holds more, or reserve), it calls startReload() AUTO_RELOAD_DELAY = 0.25 s after it ran dry, so the last shot's kick and the shotgun's pump play first.
- It does nothing while already reloading, in menus, in build or place mode, in the vault, unarmed, on the mortar or Watchman, or swimming, and it waits on the shotgun's pump. Nothing to load: no reload, and no extra click or banner (the trigger's own dry click and 'out of ammo' banner are as before). R still reloads early, unchanged. The view doesn't matter (it reads the gun, not the camera).
- New check t281: all 11 guns (pistol, revolver, uzi, m4, ak, aa12, shotgun, sniper, launcher, flamer, minigun) start their own reload within 1.2 s of game time when dry, and are loaded after; m4, shotgun and revolver again in first person; R at 10 rounds still reloads; dry with nothing to load, no reload (m4, shotgun).
