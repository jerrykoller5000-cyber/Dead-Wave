## What was wrong, measured

t222 puts the real cursor, through the real camera, on a still zombie's head (where its model's head really is), chest or
legs, from three or four sides at 6 and 14 m, and traces the round from the real muzzle along the game's aim against the hit
column the bullets use. Before (8 kinds, 4 sides, 2 ranges):

- Pointing at a head was a headshot 17 times in 64. The aim point was the middle of the camera ray's chord through the
  column; looking down at about 46 degrees the column's top cut the chord, so the point sank to chest height, and the round,
  rising from a muzzle lower than the head, went in lower still.
- A feral's or leaper's head leans out past its column: a cursor on it missed the column, the aim went to the ground behind,
  and the round dived into the dirt (5 of 8 leaper head shots).
- Pointing at the legs landed low 36 times in 48; at the chest, in the body 55 in 64.
- A shambler crossing at 3-6 m/s with the cursor kept on its chest was hit 7 times in 36 (pistol, M4, shotgun): a round
  takes 0.1-0.25 s to get there and the body has moved more than its own width.

## What changed

- rayPickZombie: the point is the middle of the column at the height the ray passes its axis, kept inside the column, and
  the head is also found as a sphere where the model has it (a hit there aims into the head slice). The aim is then raised
  or lowered so the round enters the column at that height (it enters short of the axis, on the slope from the muzzle).
- Lead: trackZombieMotion keeps each zombie's ground speed (eased, timed on simTime); a zombie under the cursor is led by
  speed x flight time (distance / the gun's round speed), capped at 2.5 m. Bullets and pellets only: not the launcher, the
  flamer or the saw, not while manning the mortar or the M240, winding up a grenade, or placing a piece.
- The reticle: four ticks at the radius, where the cursor is, of the cone the next round is thrown in (shotCone, the same
  formula aimDirWithSpread uses, moved out unchanged); a gold ring when the cursor is on a head with a clear line; an X on
  his hits (white, gold headshot, red kill). Hidden for the saw, the flamer, the mortar, building and the raised scope.

After, the same check (6 kinds, 3 sides, 2 ranges): head 36/36 headshots, chest 29 body + 1 head (a steep view where the
head covers the chest), legs 24/24 low, no round into the ground or past; crossing runners 16/16 hit; the launcher not led.

## Left as it was

- A cursor on open ground still aims at the ground under it (a round there goes into the dirt at the cursor). Aiming at
  chest height over open ground would forgive a cursor just short of a body, but it changes grenades, the launcher, the
  flashlight and building, which all read the same point; left for Jerry to ask for.
- Headshot rules, damage and spread values are combat's (Grokbot) and unchanged.
