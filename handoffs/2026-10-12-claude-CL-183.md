## How it plays

1. **Breakers.** E at the relay cabinet opens its breaker panel. Sato's tag hangs on it: "Tone on all channels 02:14. Pulled the fuse to make it stop. Didn't. — Sato, signals". Keys 1–4 flip the breakers. The order is 2, 1, 4 (02:14). A wrong breaker sparks, trips them all, and holds them hot for 1.6 s.
2. **Radio.** With power on, the panel turns into the relay's radio. A/D, the arrows or the mouse wheel tune it (0.1 MHz a step, Shift for 1 MHz). The band is 88–108, and the station sits somewhere new each run. The static thins and the signal bars light as you get close. Within 0.25 MHz for 1.4 s it locks in.
3. **Code.** A number station beeps out the locker's three-digit code, each digit as that many beeps, on a loop. The LCD fills in as you hear it. After one full round the code counts as heard.
4. **Reboot.** "Hold E — Reboot the relay" at the cabinet takes 20 s of holding. The time is kept if you let go. The big radio bar shows it with seconds left. The reboot's siren calls the dead: 3 zombies (plus 1 a night, up to 7) come from 30–40 m round the mast, then one more every 6 s of holding, up to 3 more.
5. **Locker.** The site is repaired, as before, and the locker unlocks its keypad. Keys 1–9 enter the code. A wrong code flashes WRONG. The right one flashes OPEN, then E takes the supplies, as before.

Panels close on E or Esc, or when you walk away. While a panel is open it takes the keyboard and the wheel, and the floating E prompt steps aside.

- **game/relay-puzzle.js (new):** the rules, with no DOM or three.js. `RELAY` (tuning), `createRelayPuzzle(rand)`, `stationPlan(code)`, `bootHoldSeconds(frac)`.
- **game/relay-puzzle.test.mjs (new):** 5 node checks.
- **ui/relay-panel.js (new):** the three panels: breakers with the tag, the radio (dial, signal bars, LCD), and the keypad. They live in #relayPanel inside #hud.
- **core/audio.js:** relayClunk, relaySpark, relayStatic(on, clarity), relayBeep(long), relayBoot(on) (the siren), relayKey(ok), relayOpen. All are synthesised; there are no new files.
- **index.html:**
  - The relay glue sits before updateRadioCues: relayPuzzle, relayOpen/Close/Action/Render, relayCallTheDead, updateRelay, and a capture listener for E at the cabinet or locker.
  - radioPromptText follows the puzzle's stage. CU-131's rule is kept: there is no banner while you walk up.
  - The radio bar shows the reboot, and a finished reboot reads "Relay up", not "paused".
  - getObjectiveInteraction reports the reboot's share as the hold's seconds (`bootHoldSeconds`), so the objectives' own 6 s repair and the runtime are unchanged.
  - objectiveReach blocks the locker ('locked') until the code is in.
  - New dev command `relay` (to the cabinet's front), and debug hook `TT.relayDbg`.
  - The puzzle resets with each run. A run restored with the radio already up skips it.
- **tools/tests/t204.js:** rewritten for the new flow (see Review).
