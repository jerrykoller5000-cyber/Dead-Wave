## Cause and correction

`makeBruteArmor` splits compacted concept geometry by exact vertex colors. GP-19970 changed brute steel from `#494a43` to `#7d817d` and the sign from `#8e3027` to `#424746`, but the partition matcher still recognized only the old pair. Grokbot's native repro found only `front` in `u.brutePlates`; `hitBruteArmor` then read the missing `group.rotation` or `group.matrixWorld` before damage was applied. The matcher now accepts both prior and current colors. Reset assigns durability only to groups actually present, and the hit loop treats an absent group as exposed without throwing. The original four-plate behavior remains covered by the nine passing Node tests.

The first native run used directional `dir` values and confirmed damage but the spawned brute's yaw mapped those hits to front/right. I tightened the final check with explicit `bruteZone: left` and `back` and asserted their durability fell. Final proof: left `30 → 10`, back `40 → 20`, blast front `45 → 15`, back `40 → 10`, left/right `30 → 0`; HP damage `45/9/9`. Each native camera run held the machine-wide game-test slot, one browser at a time, and released it on exit (rule 16).
