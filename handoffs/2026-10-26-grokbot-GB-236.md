# grokbot - GB-236 The grips, the Uzi's first (Jerry) - 2026-10-26
Changed:          The Uzi's grip is now a block the magazine runs up through, in proportion to the receiver, instead of a stick under it. Width 56 -> 78 mm (inner slab 46 -> 68) under the 98 mm receiver, a 0.80 ratio; the real gun's is about 0.75-0.8. Front to back 56 -> 88 mm (shapedGrip d 0.072 -> 0.12). It grew mostly rearward: the centre line moved 10 mm back (cz -0.02 -> -0.03), so the front of the grip is only about 5 mm closer to the trigger, which keeps 21 mm of finger room. The 44 x 36 mm magazine now has walls of about 20 mm round it and stays inside the grip on its reload path (the magazine itself is unchanged). The side panels, checkering and screws moved out to the new faces. The trigger guard and the trigger are unchanged. The hand's grip point (WEAPON_HOLD.uzi.grip) follows the new centre line, z -0.017 -> -0.027, so the palm stays on the grip.
                  The other guns, checked against their references side and back (by eye on the before sheets; grip width and depth against the receiver or frame): pistol (polymer frame, about a Glock's proportions): fine, no change. Revolver (wood, a round butt about as wide as the frame): fine. M4 (an A2-style grip, slim under a 80 mm lower, like the real one): fine. AK (a slim, raked grip; the real one is slim too): fine, though it could take a nudge thicker (a couple of mm); left alone. AA12, shotgun, sniper, launcher: in proportion, no change. Minigun and flamer: rear handles, not pistol grips; not judged here. The revolver and M4 side shots, and the AA12 back shot, came out black (the inspector's black-frame bug, already with Cursor), so those three views were judged from the other angle.
