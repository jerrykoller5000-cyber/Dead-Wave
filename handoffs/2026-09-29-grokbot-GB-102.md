# GB-102 — R3 P-109: rain puts fires out

Changed:
- Burning zombies: in rain the burn runs down `1 + 1.8 × wet` times faster, where `wet` is the shower's `weather.intensity`, 0 to 1. Ground fires already worked this way. In a downpour a 3 s burn is over in about 1.1 s.
- Spread: a burning zombie used to pass 1.6 s of fire to anything it touched. Now it passes `1.6 × (1 − wet)`, and nothing below 0.3 s. So a downpour lights nobody, and a light shower passes on a short burn.
- Campfires: in rain their light drops to `1 − 0.8 × wet` (a fifth in a downpour) and comes back after. The embers stop once `wet` is over 0.5. There is one puff of steam per shower as the rain takes each fire. Lanterns are under glass and unchanged.
- The cigarette:
  - Once `weather.intensity` is over 0.1 the marine doesn't reach for the pack. He can still be idly bored.
  - A lit cigarette hisses out when a shower starts: a small pale puff at his head, and the idle resets.
  - A butt dropped in the rain leaves no ember (`spawnGroundFire(..., cigarette)` returns early).
- What the player notices:
  - The flamer and fire traps are weaker in a shower: fires go out fast and don't jump through a pack.
  - Campfires dim to embers while it pours.
  - The marine won't smoke in the rain, and a lit cigarette hisses out when the shower starts.
