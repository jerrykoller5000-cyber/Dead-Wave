# CL-347 · More game tests at once on the PC, with the room (D-143)

Jerry, 10 Nov ~18:05Z: "If the bottleneck is available test slots I give you guys the authority to open as many as you
think is feasible without cooking the machine. We ran into an issue where we were crashing often last time but if you
have a good solution for it. Feel free to implement."

The October crashes came when the agents' game tests overlapped with nothing to stop them (15 Oct). Now the queue
itself decides, at each start, whether the PC has the room:

- `tools/game-test-slot.mjs` (Claude 2's, taken for this job): several leases (`holders`; `holder` mirrors the first
  for older readers and for a waiter started before the change). The first lease starts as before. A further one only
  while fewer than the PC's slots are in use (auto: memory, cores and the card via nvidia-smi, at most 3), 30 s after
  the last start, and with the room right then: memory for one more game (5 GB) plus 4 GB, cores under 75%, the
  card's memory free, the card under 80 °C. Jerry (his Play and Inspector launchers) and `--exclusive` run alone, and
  nothing new starts while he waits. A reclaimed lease (a crash or a killed session) drops the PC to one at a time for
  20 minutes. No readable graphics card (the cloud, the sandboxes): one, as before. `status` says how many and why the
  next one waits; GRANT log lines carry the numbers ("2 of 3; 30 GB free, ...").
- `tools/game-test-slot.config.json` (new): the numbers, to change without code (`"slots": 1` is the old rule).
- Tests: the 17 in game-test-slot.test.mjs pass unchanged; game-test-slot-multi.test.mjs (new) 13/13: three at once
  and the fourth waits, release frees one, a game per lease, Jerry alone and nothing new while he waits, --exclusive,
  memory/cores/VRAM/heat refusals, the stagger, the crash backoff, one lease per agent, the default of one, auto counts,
  the config, and an old waiter sharing the state. Real processes in the cloud: two `slot run`s at once, the third
  waited for a free one.
- `--review`: game-test-slot-browser-smoke.mjs finds its own lease among the holders (same checks). Passed in the
  cloud beside a second lease (the panel badge read "test slots: 2 of 2 in use").
- crew/panel.html and crew.mjs show every holder, the count and why the next one waits; the panel names a retired
  agent capitalised (the smoke check's "Cursor").
- AGENTS.md rule 16, the board's slot section, D-143 (docs/decisions.md and the board). FYIs to the crew; Antigravity
  to report the PC's status line (what nvidia-smi gave) after the commit job.
