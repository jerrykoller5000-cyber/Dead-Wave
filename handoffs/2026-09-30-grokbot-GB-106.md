# GB-106 — The Hush (Grokbot, 2026-09-30)

Spec: docs/specs/hollows.md §1 (CL-98). The way down is a stub until CU-71.

## What the player notices
- Once the relay is repaired, every morning (start of prep) he has one Hush charge.
- Standing at a cave mouth, the prompt says **E: Light the Hush**. Pressing E lights it: that mouth stops grabbing him, so he can walk in safely.
- Standing in a lit mouth, a second **E: Go down** takes him into the Hollows (today: a `hollow` enter event; CU-71 fills in the real trip).
- The charge is spent on the way down; the Hush's battery (480 s) starts counting while he is below.
- A lit Hush goes out if he walks more than 8 m away, when the alarm sounds, or when prep ends. The charge is kept if he never went down.
- Refusals: "no charge" (flat, also before the relay is repaired), a chalk-marked mouth ("chalk"), and while the alarm is sounding ("no time").
- He cannot start the wave (HQ alarm) while he is below.
