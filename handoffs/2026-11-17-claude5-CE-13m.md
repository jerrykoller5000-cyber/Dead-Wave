## What b2 does (game/zombie-feet.js groundFeet; everything else in the file is the J159b draft 3c561a2d)
- A sole within plantEps of the ground and going back under him takes a spot in the world and keeps it. It lets go only when the gait lifts the foot clear (plantHold x the band). The J159b draft let go on any lean, turn or forward drag and then faded the foot back along the ground: that fade was most of the skating.
- Reach: plantReach 0.3 x (thigh + shin) fore and aft, and what the hip's sideMax (20 deg) swings it to the side. Lengths are read off the bones every frame: nothing per kind, nothing hard-coded (the sim passes with legs x1.35, D-192/J162).
- Past its reach (or the hip's extension, or the knee) the foot STEPS: up stepLift, across to 0.85 of the reach on the far side, straight down onto a fixed spot, one foot at a time. A foot the gait drags forward along the ground while the other holds is lifted clear. If nobody holds the ground, the lower foot does.
- Sideways travel: the hip swings out or in (leg.rotation.z, taken off again first thing next frame), with the leg's toe-yaw coupling put back in the plane; the hips come down for a far plant (at most 12% of the leg). One read-back pass puts a held foot exactly on its spot.
- A sprint (Froude over 3: 4.7 m/s on a 0.74 m leg; the feral and the guardians) with a gait that lifts its own feet gets a flight: push-off, the body 7 cm clear, the next footfall takes the ground. Without this the hip pass dragged the swinging foot along the ground at 14 m/s.
