# GB-148 - Revolver stowed snug on the pack + a gentler weapon wheel tick (Grokbot)

Jerry, 2026-10-09 01:28 CT: "Revolver sticks out too much from pack and is not tight when holstered. Also could you change the weapon wheel sound to something more pleasant?"

Changed:
- **Revolver on the pack.** It was hung in the low pack holster at the pack's very edge (torso x ±0.19, z -0.39). Its grip pointed straight out past his side, 6.4 cm beyond his outline, and there were 8 cm of air between the gun and the pack. Measured on the real renderer.
  - New spot: `REV_PACK = { x: 0.115, z: -0.336 }`, with y 0.76, scale and tilt unchanged. The gun is turned so the grip points in toward his spine and the muzzle points down. The leather shell and loop follow it to the barrel side.
  - Now: 0.5–0.7 cm inside his outline. Air to the pack 0.7 cm (his left) and 1.2 cm (his right). Clipping 0 cm, or 1.0 cm in the AA-12/shotgun loadout. Same for every loadout tried, the full kit with the minigun included.
  - Only the revolver is moved. The pistol's low holster and the Uzi's holster are as they were, and `CARRY_FIT.revolver` is unchanged.
- **Weapon wheel tick** (the sound as the pointer crosses a gun).
  - Before: CL-135's dial detent, a bright click plus a blip of static. 81% of its energy was above 2 kHz, half of it above 4 kHz, at level 0.0548.
  - Now: a soft, rounded "tock". It is a dull knock around 620 Hz under a muted short click: 2% above 2 kHz, about 25 ms long, level 0.032 (about 4.7 dB quieter). Like the old one it is noise-built, so t223's no-tone and no-ringing rules still hold (ring 0.36 < 0.5).
  - Rate limit: at most one tick per 50 ms (was 35 ms). Ticks less than 120 ms apart (a fast sweep) play at 0.6 volume.
  - Volume still goes through the weapons bus and the SFX volume setting.
  - Selecting a gun still plays the CRT channel-change static Jerry asked for in CL-135. That sound is untouched.
