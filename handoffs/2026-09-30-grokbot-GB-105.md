# grokbot - GB-105 (R5, P-129, D-65): the hearing rule and the suppressor's cost - 2026-09-30

Changed:
- **The hearing rule.** Every gunshot is heard within its gun's radius: `SHOT_HEARING`, 35 m (pistol, Uzi) to 70 m (sniper); M4 45, AK 50. `heardShot(w)` runs on every shot, in `tryFire`'s gun branch and the minigun loop. A sleeping post guard inside the radius wakes its post with reason `'shot'`: the GB-43 posts and the GB-57 bounties.
- **A fitted can** (CU-68's `suppressor[w]`):
  - cuts the radius to x0.3 (`SUPPRESSED_HEARING_MUL`): the M4 goes to 13.5 m, inside the 18 m walk-up wake, and the sniper to 21 m;
  - makes its bullets and pellets carry x0.9 damage (`SUPPRESSED_DAMAGE_MUL`).
- **For P-139's stir meter:** `shotsHeard` and `lastShotNoise` are kept (TT `heardShotDbg`).
- In a wave the horde already homes on him, so the hearing rule itself only shows by day. In the night, the can's cost is the softer rounds.

- With a can he can shoot a post's neighbours and pass at 20 m without waking it (the sniper still carries to 21 m).
- Suppressed guns kill a little slower: about a tenth more rounds per kill, so more zombies reach him.
