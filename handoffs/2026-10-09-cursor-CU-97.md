## The arc

One thin light-cyan line (24 straight pieces of the real flight) from where the grenade will leave his hand to where it
comes down, and a small ring there; both green when the landing spot is among the dead. The mortar and the launcher keep
their yellow dots and wide orange ring. The materials are the dots' (no new shader on first use). The arc starts at
grenadeReleasePoint, the point the hand reaches at the release, not at his head; and the throw's bearing is now level, so
a cursor near his feet no longer shortens it: the hold alone sets the distance (3 m tap to 19 m at 1.6 s).

## The throw

GRENADE_ANIM: grab 0.16 s (the grenade in his hand at 0.11), up and back 0.14 s, then held behind his head while G is
held (drawn a little further back as the throw grows); G up: over the top 0.08 s, the grenade leaves at the top, the
follow-through 0.14 s, back to the gun 0.2 s. A tap throws as soon as the grenade is in his hand (about 0.2 s after G
down); a hold cut off (the window losing focus, the water, a menu) puts it back unused. The off hand is his right (D-75:
the gun arm is his anatomical left), the same hand as the skull-bag lob and the MedPen.

grenadeArm runs after the hold and the draw, and puts the hand on the beat's target by solveArmIK (as knifeArm does): the
pouch on his torso (the grenades hang on his hip), the rest by the way he faces (so the gun's lean and twist don't move
"behind his head"); the way up curves out in front of his shoulder rather than through his chest. While it plays it is
the gesture (gestureT), so the arm swing and the idle stand aside. A grenade appears in his hand from the pouch (the
pouch shows one fewer) until the release.

Measured on the real renderer (tools/grenadesheet.mjs, an M4 in hand): the hand from the gun (0.92 m up) to the pouch
(0.80 m), out in front of the shoulder on the way up, 1.41 m up and 0.14 m behind him wound up, 0.05 m from the release
point at the release, down across his front after.
