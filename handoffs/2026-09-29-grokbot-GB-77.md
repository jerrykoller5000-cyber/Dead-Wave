# GB-77 — R2 P-28: shoot a bomber in the crowd and the chain is his

Changed:
- When the marine kills a bomber (a shot, his blade, his fire, or a blast he set off), its blast is now his: every zombie it kills feeds his streak and pays at his streak's rate, and it chains on through other bombers the same way. Before, a bomber's blast counted as a "defence" kill: base pay, no streak.
- A bomber a turret, trap or wire kills, or one that walks up and goes off on its own, is unchanged: its blast still pays base and never feeds the streak.
- A bomber's blast never pokes a cave, whoever set it off (new `noPoke` flag on the blast), so shooting a bomber at a cave mouth doesn't bring the guardian out.
- The blast's damage to him, his builds and the house is unchanged.
- What the player notices: popping a bomber inside a pack jumps the streak counter by the whole chain (6 kills from one shot in the test).
