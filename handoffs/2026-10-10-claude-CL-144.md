# CL-144: the training targets twice the size, and a score by hits (Claude, 2026-10-10)

Jerry: "Can you make the training targets twice their current size and a scoring system based on hits?"
Asked: rings + distance.

**Look first:** `Claude outputs/training-targets.jpg` (in the game).

## The targets (world/training.js)

The plates are twice the size: 1.28 x 2.24 m (`TRAINING.TARGET_SCALE`). Their foot stays where it was (0.66 m up), so
the chest rings come at a standing man's eye line (~1.6 m) and a knife still reaches; a wider stand with three uprights,
a taller backstop (3.8 m). The silhouette is drawn at twice the resolution, from one description (`PLATE_ART`) that the
scoring reads too. Rounds now hit the silhouette only: through the plate's cut-away corners they go on to the backstop
(world/training-ballistics.js `traceTraining`, `tg.onPlate`).

## The score

| where | points |
| --- | --- |
| head ring | 10 |
| chest bullseye | 10 |
| chest ring | 5 |
| anywhere else on the silhouette | 2 |

times the lane: lane 1 (8 m) x1, lane 2 x1.5, lane 3 x2, lane 4 x2.5, lane 5 (25 m) x3 (`TRAINING.SCORE`,
`TRAINING.LANE_MULT`; rounded). A knife or a grenade scores the plate (2 x the lane). The values are printed by the
rings on the plates.

The HUD strip shows the score and the best; each hit pops under it ("+30 BULLSEYE 10 x 3", red for the bullseye and
the head ring, yellow for the ring, grey for the plate). The string runs while he is on the range; walking through to
the build room (or leaving) banks it, and the best of the visit's strings is kept for the session. `publishUI` events:
`training-target` now carries `zone, points, mult, total, score`; a new `training-score` { score, best } on banking.

New text keys with English fallbacks (ChatGPT's to word in strings.js): `training.hudScore`, `training.hudBest`,
`training.zone.{head,bull,ring,plate}`.
