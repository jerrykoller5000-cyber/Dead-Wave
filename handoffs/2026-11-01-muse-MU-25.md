## 2. Layout: a grid of 6 m cells, 3 m gaps, 3.4 m ceilings

`layoutWarren(theme)` (world/hollows.js:47) plans each warren on a 16×16 grid of **CELL 6 m** cells (`HOLLOW`, world/hollows.js:21): three depths with sizes [10,12], [8,10], [5,7] cells (the Deep counts its 2×2 chamber as four), connected by ramps (the depth drops). Runs between cells are **GAP 3** — a 3 m-wide opening in the cell walls; tunnel ceilings are **TUNNEL_ROOF 3.4 m**, chambers **CHAMBER_ROOF 6.5 m** with a 2×2 Deep chamber. Walls are the built rock (thickness WALL 1). The five themes (root, shale, iron, wet, hill — world/hollows.js:20) differ in look/set-piece/prop scatter (`WARREN_LOOKS`, world/hollows.js:31), not in this skeleton: same grid discipline everywhere.

**Where he snags (the J47 geometry):**

- **Movement below is nav-gated, not free:** the dead (and his own step checks in places) move on a nav grid at **NAV_CELL 1.5 m** (`navField/navStep/navOpen`, game/below-fight.js:182–249) — a step that isn't nav-open is refused or axis-slid (index.html:56865ff: try full, then x-only, then z-only). Axis-slide on a grid reads as *catching on corners*: every cell doorway is a 3 m gap in a 6 m wall, approached at an angle the slide resolves badly. This is the mechanical heart of "hard to move through".
- **3 m gaps, 3.4 m ceilings:** a 1.9 m marine with a gun up fits, but the camera (third person, behind/above) is the real casualty — tunnels at 3.4 m put the camera in the roof on turns; chambers at 6.5 m are the only headroom. Claustrophobia is dimensioned, not accidental.
- **Props in the runs:** set pieces and scatter sit *in* cells (knees, slabs at index.html:599/624 — the wet warren's water is knee-deep and *slows* him, hollows spec §3 via index.html:624), timber/roots/blades by theme; the iron wall's cut gap (world/hollows.js:484–497: bars with a walk-through cut) is a deliberate pinch. Alcoves (sleepers' nests) open off runs.
- **The ramps:** depth changes are ramps at cell edges (DEPTH_DROP 4 over a cell run) — the hikers' rope hangs at the iron one's head (world/hollows.js:513–522), the set-piece telling you this was climbed once.

**Lights and sight:** each theme has an ambient + fog colour (WARREN_LOOKS: ambient ~0x0a–0x12, fog near-black) — he sees by the Hush (his carried light; its charge is the warren's timer, MU-12 §2.8), glow points (the `glows` scatter, world/hollows.js:628 — theme-coloured glow orbs), lamps dropped by the dead (the hikers' headlamp set piece), and his flashlight. ## 6. What a walked descent and wider runs would touch

**A real descent** (a ramp/stair you walk, the light going): the warren would need a *surface-connected* entry: an entry shaft cell-chain from the cave mouth's real position down to depth 1 (today the warren floats at −400 under the *origin*, unrelated to the mouth). Options by cost: (a) **Keep −400, add a descent *inside***: entry room → a sloped run (2–3 cells dropping DEPTH_DROP×2) to the warren proper — the teleport lands him at the shaft top *inside the mouth area*… no, the teleport remains the seam, only hidden by a fade; (b) **Place the warren under its mouth** (origin = mouth xz, y down): then a true shaft is buildable — entry cell at mouth height, ramps down; this changes ORIGIN from a constant to per-cave (buildWarren already takes origin — game/below-fight.js:56 has `origin` as a parameter; the layout code uses HOLLOW.ORIGIN (world/hollows.js:21)) and everything seated relative to it (guardian mouths, cave hills above, MU-16's caveHillHeight ties the surface dome to the warren — a moved warren must move its hill). (b) is the honest build; the light "going" is the sky/fog blend over the shaft's first minute (the day clock's state vs the warren ambient — a lerp on descent progress, new). The Hush's two-E ritual compresses to one (the descent *is* the transition).

**Wider runs:** GAP 3 → 4.5–5 and TUNNEL_ROOF 3.4 → 4.5, chambers 6.5 stay the tall rooms: layout constants (world/hollows.js:21) + the nav cell (1.5 → wider runs don't need nav rescaling, but doorways in the nav get wider automatically since nav is derived from cells). What breaks: the nav field's cost (a warren is 16×16 cells at 1.5 m nav = ~64×64 — unchanged by GAP), the set pieces sized to 3 m gaps (iron wall gap, slabs), the wet warren's water (cell-wide, fine), camera headroom improves without touching camera code, and **the fights rebalance**: wider runs = longer sight lines below (the Hush's light radius unchanged, so darkness still rules past ~10 m; stealth economics survive width, but sleeper alcove distances re-tune). The snag fix is *not* width alone: the axis-slide (index.html:56865) at doorway corners wants funnelled door frames (nav opens diagonally at gaps) — a nav improvement independent of GAP.

**Both together** is CL-286's scope call; the cheap honest order is: widen + nav funnel first (it fixes J47's movement complaint inside today's teleport), descent second (it's the one that moves ORIGIN and re-seats the surface ties).
