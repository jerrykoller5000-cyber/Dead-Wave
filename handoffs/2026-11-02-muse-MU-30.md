## 3. The music along a sprint (CL-275 as built)

The score's calm is **time-pooled** (menu/dawn/day/dusk/night pools, core/audio.js:1222) with **place overrides** for exactly three places — graveyard r40, relay r35, hikers r30 (index.html:62443; enter at r, leave at r+12) — plus the wave suites at night, specials (guardian/heart/wanderer), and the forest/below beds under everything. So a sprint drop → HQ → west road → rangers hears: day pool → (no HQ place) → forest bed thickening → *nothing new at the rangers' gate* — the chain's farthest, most story-loaded camp is musically identical to the woods around it. The three places that *do* sing are the graveyard, the mast and the hikers: **the score already believes in place-music; it just hasn't been given the other places.** That is the cheapest flow lever in this whole map (§4, idea 1).

## 4. Ten ideas (each: place, beat, gate)

1. **Give the remaining story places CL-275 pools** (rangers r30, trapper r30, depot r30, tower foot r25): the gate *chimes* of arrival. Beat: each camp's own silence/sound (MU-23's events are the diegetic half; this is the score half). No new systems — three lines in the `at(...)` chain.
2. **The depot cough as the power gate's tell** (MU-23 §3.1): until the fuel log is read, dusk at the depot coughs. Gate stays the chain; the *advertisement* is sound.
3. **A trail-sign at each chain gate** (the next place's mark, carved): the gates today say their lock text at 2.2 m; a visible sign 20 m out (history-prop, no new system) turns backtracking into anticipation. Beats: Sato's hand at the mast gate, Clarke's at the ranger gate (he left the key *and* the directions).
4. **Make the river the trapper's real gate:** keep `trapper-fence`, but let the *first* crossing be the lesson — the ford runs fast after rain (weather exists): on wet days the ford is closed, the bridge is the way (flow, not lock). Beat: Okafor survived by respecting water.
5. **The west road milestone:** halfway to the rangers (the longest straight), the skyline pull of the camp + the first horseshoe on a post (iron foreshadow, two camps early). Gate: none — a *promise*, placed where MU-16's sightline rules say nothing else shows.
6. **Graveyard as the night-20 rehearsal:** its guards already wake like a wave's; let banking a skull there teach the alarm (a one-time coach line, ChatGPT's keys). Beat: the dead of Threshold first, the living later.
7. **The mast's guy-wire singing (MU-23 §3.2) as the storm gate:** storms already schedule (index.html:10461); the wires sing 90 s before — a *time* gate on the relay trip (go now or shelter). Beat: Sato listened to wires.
8. **Drop-in symmetry:** the landing clearing gets the run's only *departure* beat — Heron's night-20 extraction from the same clearing (the boat/plane shore is the dock today; Track S says Heron is a floatplane — the lake. Either way: end where he began, or at the water the story keeps pointing at. CL-296's call, flagging the fork).
9. **Hush charge as Below's soft gate per warren:** first dive shallow (charge only reaches depth 1–2), the vault's second lift (already a reward shape) extends it. Beat: Sato built it in stages; depth becomes earned, not binary.
10. **The phone (MU-23 §3.3) as the hikers' gate-keeper:** it rings the dusk after discovery and won't stop until the kit is taken — an audible *pull* replacing a marker, silenced by the mission itself. Gate: none new; the *reason to go now* is new.
