Changed: Jerry: "make the physics ragdoll system a toggleable feature in the Settings and off by default", then fix the chaos the integration brought (the player tweaking out, models crumpling, enemies teleporting, players and enemies stuck). Settings gains "Ragdoll · Physics reactions: on/off" (`tt_reactions`; off by default; turning it off mid-game lets every awake body go). Off, nothing of D-42 runs in the game: hits, deaths and the marine's blows play the code from before GB-65 to GB-67, and the cave drag plays the scene loaded through `sceneWithoutMotion` (the marine animated by the clips alone, as CL-64 shipped it). (2) A standing zombie's body comes along with its mesh (`body.shift` by the mesh's movement, as the marine's already did): the AI walks the mesh while the body stands, so the body was stretched between its planted feet and a host that had moved on, and toppled or slid the mesh (the teleports). Down, the body's drift moves the mesh as before. (3) Every reacting zombie's animation pose is kept before its body writes and put back before the next updateZombies (GB-98, done here rather than on the board at Jerry's ask): updateZombies writes partial Euler angles, so the body read its own written pose back as the animation and fed itself (the crumpling). (4) Failsafes: a live body awake more than 8 s (the marine's 6 s) is let go whatever state it is in, so nobody stays stuck reacting.
