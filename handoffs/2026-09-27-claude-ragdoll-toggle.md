(3) Every reacting zombie's animation pose is kept before its body writes and put back before the next updateZombies (GB-98, done here rather than on the board at Jerry's ask): updateZombies writes partial Euler angles, so the body read its own written pose back as the animation and fed itself (the crumpling). (4) Failsafes: a live body awake more than 8 s (the marine's 6 s) is let go whatever state it is in, so nobody stays stuck reacting.

## Part 2 — the megaswarm as the benchmark (Jerry, 2026-09-27, later the same day)
Changed: Jerry: hordes disappear crossing rivers and certain terrain; some zombies crumple and float or go inactive; make the megaswarm (`megaswarm` in the dev console, 500 shamblers) clean with Ragdoll off and on. Found in the code and fixed: (1) **the far LOD outlived the zombie.** Past 40 m a zombie's joints get `matrixAutoUpdate = false` (the far LOD). Killed out there, its corpse toppled rigid (the limb flail never applied), and `freezeCorpse`/`finishCorpse` only restore the joints they froze themselves, so the mesh went back to the pool with its joints still frozen and the corpse's pose stuck on them: the next zombie on that mesh walked without animating, in the dead pose ("crumpled", "inactive"), and cast no shadow. `resetZombieMesh` now wakes every joint and puts the hips back over the feet, `beginCorpse` drops the LOD so a far corpse animates its fall, and the LOD restores each part's own shadow flag instead of `true` for all. (2) **no reacting body in the water**: a body's floor is the riverbed while the mesh floats at the surface, so a hit in a river crumpled the body onto the bed under a floating mesh; `z.inWater` (swimming, or wading deeper than 0.35 m) skips the hit, lets an awake body go, and gives a death in water the old topple. (3) **swimmers ride higher**: a shambler paddling had its feet 1.15 m under the surface, head only above, so a horde crossing a river at night was all but under water and read as gone; now 0.95 m (head and shoulders out). The fog cull (GB-59) was checked and is consistent with what three draws (view-axis depth); nothing changed there.
New test: tools/tests/t98.js, the megaswarm benchmark: 160 shamblers spawned across a river from the player, watched for 20 s with Ragdoll off, then on with a shell into the front every 3 s, then 60 more from the pool. Nobody hidden inside the fog, under the ground on dry land, floating over it, frozen while near, jumping 4 m in a tick, standing still 8 s with a way to go, no live body awake 9 s, no NaN; pooled meshes come back with live limbs and hips over their feet. 20/0 here. The frame cost is reported as INFO (`--all-fails` now prints INFO lines for every test): headless on this 2-core box 67 ms a frame with 160 up, 74 ms with bodies; the GPU number is Jerry's to read off the FPS overlay with `megaswarm`.
Tests: t98 20/0, t93 11/0, t59 48/0, t36 22/0, t90 16/0.
Not verified: the river disappearance and the crumple/float on Jerry's GPU (headless found none of them before or after; the three fixes are what the code shows would do exactly that). The megaswarm's real frame rate.
