## Behavior and limits

- Blur derives from actual translation and wrapped root/head angular changes, not nominal speed. Static/slow poses stay sharp between rapid tics. Crossing ±π does not count as a full spin. Teleports and skipped intervals reset rather than streaking across rooms.
- Two skinned snapshots retain the actual previous pose, sharing source geometry without cloning the whole rig. Transparent silhouettes fade within115ms; main body stays opaque. No extra shadow passes, lights or full-screen postprocessing.
- Up to8 nearest visible living Ferals within26m get echoes (16 ghost draws normally; maximum32 if a body uses two material skins). Far LOD, dormant guards, climbs, crawling, hopping and knocked-down bodies are excluded. The optional ?skin=0 renderer keeps its original rendering without blur.
- Clears on zombie reset, Training clear/leave, game reset/end and domain change. Borrowed geometry/materials are never disposed by the effect. Runtime diagnostics are TT.feralMotionDbg; setEnabled(false) disables only the visual echoes for comparison.
- New Feral procedural accents are bounded and applied only to ordinary near locomotion/idle. Existing windup/swing, hit reactions, head tracking and dismemberment continue to control the rig. The generic older head twitch remains layered in.
