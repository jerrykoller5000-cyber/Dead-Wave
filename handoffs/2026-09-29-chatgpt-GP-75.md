## Implementation and evidence

The game had camera-relative directional travel already, but its pose first yawed Marine toward the chosen direction and then always pitched him forward. The new pose rotates around a horizontal axis perpendicular to the selected travel direction. That makes the top of his body tip into the movement vector while his torso keeps facing aim. The axis is recomputed from the fixed world travel vector as aim changes; the roll itself cannot change course when the camera or mouse turns. The reset check now includes side rotation, so a pure lateral roll cannot leave the pivot tilted.
