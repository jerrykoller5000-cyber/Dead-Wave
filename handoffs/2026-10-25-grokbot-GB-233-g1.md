- The whole gun palette (gunBody/gunDark/gunMid/gunAccent, index.html ~17861) uses metalness 0.28-0.45. The art direction says painted steel is dielectric (metal ~0, rough 0.6-0.75) and bare metal is 0.8-1 (rough 0.35-0.5), so these sit between the two. Moving them changes every gun's look, so it's a decision, not a fix.

- Revolver construction: the cylinder sits in a box frame with no crane or visible top strap, and the barrel is thick for the frame.
- The uzi reads as one flat dark grey (polymer grip and steel receiver not separated).
