## Design mapping

- Shambler/Feral: gaunt pale bodies, ragged tanks/shorts, dirty faces; Feral has hooked claws.
- Brute: red muscle, asymmetric scrap shoulder spike, striped breastplate, bracers/shin plates.
- Military: camo fatigues, patrol cap, plate carrier/pouches, radio pack/antenna and boots.
- Spitter/Bomber: distinct swollen acid belly and pressure-sac silhouettes; mouth ooze versus glowing belly cracks and gas sacs. The Bomber retains both fuse-animation material references.
- Screamer: stretched mouth, hanging tongue, rib details and magenta eyes.
- Drowned: slate skin, chest weights, weed and barnacles.
- Leaper/Spider: spinal ridge and long clawed forelimbs versus six plated articulated legs, dark joints and pink eyes.
- Colossus/Demon: hazard-sign siege armor versus swept horns, shoulder/back spikes and orange furnace slots. Demon charge flare is bound to the new visible material, including cached bodies.
- Guardian/Cave Guardian: pale bone sentinel with sensory brow/four eyes versus dark roots, stone slabs, moss, a shorter stone skull and two cold eyes. Existing jaw, finger and limb joints are retained.
