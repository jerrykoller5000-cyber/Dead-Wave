# GB-200 - fold the guns' still parts (grokbot, 2026-10-19)

## What
`index.html`, after `dressGuns(wardrobeState)` (the gunFurniture registration): new `foldGun(gun, stowed)` runs
`world/bake.js foldStatic` on every `weaponMeshes` gun (held), and on each carried copy as it is made (`carryCopy`
and the `fitCarriedGun` copy cache, `stowed = true`).
- Held gun: these stay as they are, together with everything under them: each Object3D in the gun's userData
  (bolt, trigger, mag, selector, cylinder, hammer, chain, barrels, slide, hand and muzzle anchors, flashes) and
  named parts (mods show and hide them). Glows, textures, and the furniture the camo repaints (gunFurniture
  materials plus any userData materials) are in `hold`.
- Carried copy: nothing on it moves, so its named parts fold too. Hidden fx are skipped, and the camo furniture
  stays in `hold`. A camo or look change rebuilds the copy, because the token changes.
- `carryGunCopy` itself is unchanged. Its other users (the rack, the roof crew, which disposes materials) get
  unfolded copies.
