# claude — CL-95 The guns' suppressors — 2026-09-29

Changed:
- Six guns each carry a suppressor that belongs on them (D-65):
  - M4: a rifle can with a collar and three rings.
  - AK-47: a thicker, lighter-metal can with a stepped nose.
  - Sniper: a long, fat can with a fluted mount.
  - Shotgun: a squared-off box can with cooling ribs.
  - Pistol and Uzi: slim cans.
- Each can is built hidden at the muzzle, over the flash hider or brake, and kept out of the mesh merge.
- `setGunSuppressor(gun, on)` shows the can and moves the muzzle point (`userData.muzzleLocal`) and the flash to the can's end. Off puts both back.
- A fitted can shrinks the muzzle flash to about a third.
- The AA-12 and the revolver have none.
- `SUPPRESSOR` in index.html holds each can's size, one line per gun.
- Nothing fits a can in play yet. P-127 (Cursor) sells them in the kiosk and calls `setGunSuppressor` on both guns of an akimbo pair. P-129 is the quieter shot.
