## Evidence and limits

- The Node test asserts the first cue can occur at 14.8 m, that a body cannot repeat inside a second, that a pack is spaced, and that daylight repeats are rarer. It also excludes sleepers, stationary, seen, distant and disabled candidates.
- The live approach probe held one shambler at 12 m until nightfall, then let it walk toward the marine through woods with NVG and flashlight off. The initial debug state reported `night: true`, `seen: false`, `cover: brush`; the two logged cues arrived while it was still 12 m and 6.51 m out. The browser reported no errors.
- The branch variant starts on tree-side cover at 12 m; the follow-up leaf footfall logged at 6.63 m. The browser again reported no errors. The hook maps the scheduler's `branch` event name to the audio engine's `snap` kind.
- Antigravity's AG-181 report (`qa/2026-11-01-AG-181/report.md`, `summary.json`) records real-GPU open-ground silence, pack pacing, a Night 20 mix pass, and ears-on scores of 5/5 for clarity, placement, character, mix, variety and timing. These are Antigravity's observations; this handoff's own game checks established the event timing and placement above.
- `review/GP-20005/performance.md` records the night-20 `?warnings=0` A/B runs. The first unprofiled pair was slower with warnings; a repeated profiled pair reversed it. After skipping the HQ's tree-free inner 9 m, an enabled profiled run reached 59.0 fps, 47.9 fps 1% low and 9.826 s ready time versus the paired disabled profile's 58.2 fps, 40.4 fps 1% low and 10.055 s. That is evidence of no consistent whole-frame or load-time regression, with run-to-run variance explicitly retained.
- Jerry's sound rule: both immersive (a dead body disturbing the woods at its actual position) and impactful (advance warning of a lethal approach). Sounds use AudioSys's FX bus, mute rule, the shared `sndAt` distance curve and camera-relative pan. The implementation generates no sound files or licensed takes.
- A first probe exposed my hook in `zombieLateStep`, outside the chase variables' scope; the browser stopped that update with a ReferenceError. I moved the hook to a small helper called from the first half of `updateZombies` before the split. The subsequent real-GPU probe passed with `errors: []`. The failed probe is not counted as verification.
