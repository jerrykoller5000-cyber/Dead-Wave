## What Jerry asked (22:45Z) and what was done

1. **The guardian.** `world/cave-guardian.js`: a built body (pelvis, three vertebrae, a deep chest and wide girdle, a
   long neck and a low skull with a heavy brow, six eyes in the cave's eye tint and an unhinging jaw full of teeth;
   arms two and a quarter metres long with clawed hands it knuckle-walks on; short digitigrade legs; bone spikes down
   the back, cave crust on the shoulders, weed off the forearms), coloured from the cave's own design. A two-bone IK
   puts the hands and feet where the beat wants them (the ground, an ankle, a body to carry), so nothing floats. The
   beats: `guardianGallop` (a bound on all fours for the chase, stride read off the distance run), `guardianRearGrab`
   (the pounce onto the leg), `guardianDragWalk` (for the mouth on three limbs, the leg held out behind),
   `guardianWalkUpright` and `guardianCarryThrow` (out with what is left, back over its head, thrown). Scaled 1.3:
   shoulders at about two metres.
2. **The pit's arms.** `world/pit-tentacles.js`: each arm is a tube rebuilt every frame along a Catmull-Rom curve
   through eleven control points, tapering, with a dark back, a pale sucker-dimpled belly and a run of the runes' cold
   light toward the tip. The grab is a helix: the first arm runs 2.2 turns round his shins, the second 1.6 round his
   chest, both blended out of the idle sway. The others stand up out of the hole and hook in over it, and everything
   goes down with him.
3. **The alarm.** `startLoopCine('alarm')`: the camera pulls back along its own line of sight to a shot 27 m from the HQ
   and 10.5 m up, looking at the tower, and holds (with a slow push) while `skyTo(SKY_NIGHT_AT, 3.4)` brings the night
   down, the strobes go and the eight flares go up and burst in frame; the rumble at 1.6 s; back to the marine at
   4.5 s, as the wave starts. No sun, no moon, no pan.
4. **The dawn.** On `wave-last-kill` the sky mode goes to `'dawn'` and `skyTo(SKY_MORNING_AT, 7)` runs on the wall
   clock through the finisher and into the next prep; startPrep no longer holds the night. `ui/dawn.js` is a banner:
   bottom right, the night's kills, skulls picked up and best streak, one line of what to do next, no buttons, no pause,
   gone by itself after 14 s (or with the run). `loopMorning` / `loopNextNight` stay as debug hooks. The old "DAY N
   CLEARED" card is skipped when the banner carries the numbers.
