# GB-151 Proper two-handed thumbs-forward pistol/revolver grip (Jerry, 2026-10-09 15:35 CT)

Jerry: "This picture shows what proper pistol grip looks like. Right now our player does not do that with the pistol or the revolver." (reference.png: a 1911 held two-handed, thumbs forward.)

Changed:
- What was wrong (I measured it on the real renderer, gun axes, metres from the grip anchor):
  - The hands in studio/marine-body.js are built with each thumb on the wrong side for a pistol grip. With the index finger on top and the wrist behind the grip, the firing thumb could only end up on the outside of the frame (+0.025 x).
  - The support hand never closed. Its wrist stayed at rest (gripA 0) and it hung beside and under the firing fist: 8.7 cm from it, its palm 13 cm from the firing knuckles, the thumbs 12 cm apart.
  - The firing wrist was bent 1.14 rad.
- The fix, in holdWeapon (index.html):
  - With a pistol or revolver in hand, both hands are drawn mirrored about their own middle line (new PISTOL_GRIP and mirrorHands). The fist channel, the grip point and every anchor stay where they were. The firing hand's channel sign flips with the mirror, in both the single-gun and akimbo calls.
  - The support hand now closes round the firing fingers: its wrist turns onto the grip (wrapG, a = handRollLW, the same path the long guns' support hands use).
  - WEAPON_HOLD pistol.fore and revolver.fore now point at the wrap: about (-0.037, -0.015, +0.018) m from the grip anchor. The revolver's sits a little lower so the support thumb stays under the cylinder.
  - CL-122's slide toward the support shoulder is skipped for the wrap. It would have moved the gun 3 to 8 cm, and the old cup never triggered it. The gun stays exactly where the hold puts it, so aim, muzzle, recoil and accuracy are unchanged (t229 checks this to 1.2 cm).
- After, pistol / revolver:
  - Firing thumb on the support side, high: (-0.028, 0.037, -0.006).
  - Support fist 4.4 / 4.9 cm from the firing fist, its palm 3.2 / 3.8 cm from the firing knuckles.
  - Thumbs stacked 2.6 / 2.8 cm apart on the support side.
  - Firing wrist 0.53 / 0.51 rad (was 1.14). Support wrist 0.42 / 0.44 rad.
  - Elbows about 56 / 53 degrees bent, roughly 2/3 extension. The hold is unchanged.
- Akimbo stays one-handed. Each fist is mirrored, high on its own grip, with the fingers across the front. Wrists: pistol 0.73, revolver about 0.9 rad.
- Firing: the support fist stays on (worst 1 mm). Reload: it leaves for the GB-145 plan and comes back to the wrap, at most about 1 m/s with no pop. Sprint keeps the same grip.
- GB-145, GB-148 and GB-149 code is untouched.
