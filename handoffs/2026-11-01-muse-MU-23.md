## 1. How ground cover and tree kinds are chosen today

- **Tree kinds:** `treeKindFor(x, z, h, moisture, slope)` (index.html:18213): pine on the alpine band (h > 15) or dry slopes, oak where wet (moisture > 0.52), autumn on dry mid slopes, birch the fallback (MU-17 §1). Kinds are picked per tree at scatter from *global* fields (height/moisture/slope) — nothing reads "which place is this".
- **Ground cover:** geometry kinds from world/ground-foliage.js (blade, fern, shrub, flower + stems — compact opaque plant geometry, GP-44), scattered and chunk-baked (MU-13 §2.6): tufts are chosen/placed by the foliage scatter in index.html from moisture/wetness, height, water distance and path clearance (trees keep 2.2 m off paths; cover also thins at sites/clearings through the clear functions). Flower pads are separately listed (`listFlowerPads`). Kinds today are by *ground*, not by *place*.
- **Clearings:** sites already clear cover (`clearFoliageInSquare` and the trapper/foliage clearing at build) — a clearing is an exclusion today, not a *planting*.

## 2. Where a per-place override would go

The override point is the scatter itself (single pass, seeded, before the chunk bake): for each tuft/tree candidate, after the global pick, test **distance to the nearest place-clearing centre** (the camps/depot/mast clearings are POIs with known centres — campsites, sheds.2, mast, and the new CU-205 positions) and, inside a place radius (the clearing's radius ~12–20 m), bias the pick: kind substitution (the place's signature kind replaces the global pick), density multiplier (thicker/thinner), and tint (the place's hue — foliage chunks carry per-vertex colour already, so a tint is a scatter-time colour write, free at runtime). Same for `treeKindFor`: add a place test *before* the global rules within a larger ring (~25 m), so a place's trees are its kind (pines at the rangers', say) fading to global at the ring's edge. Costs: one distance test per candidate at load (trivial); the runtime cost is **zero** (baked into chunks/batches as today). What must be decided per place (Claude's table): kind, density, tint — and the *gameplay* read (a place recognisable from its plants at 100 m is W3's wayfinding working as designed; the skyline pulls GP-20003 already do this at valley scale). Note the trap: the kit-tree pick and Pike's hide (MU-14) read *standing trees near a camp* — forcing kinds near camps doesn't change those readers (they take any kind), but a clearing radius bigger than the kit tree's 50 m search would change which tree is "nearest bank tree" — keep place rings ≤ ~25 m.

## 3. The six events: what exists, where the hook sits

### 3.1 The depot's generator coughing at dusk
- **Exists:** a generator *hum* (`AudioSys.generatorHum(volume, pan)`, core/audio.js:2106) played positionally when powered (`sndAt(F.gen.x, F.gen.z, 24)`, index.html:29977); the audio engine also has an engine-cough family (core/audio.js:2706 — "it coughs once and dies", the dry-tank start). Two generators exist in the fiction: the HQ's (fed by the depot's fuel, MU-12/15) and the depot's own dead one (Price's log lies by it). The event names *the depot's* — a dead generator coughing is a haunting detail: something tries to start it at dusk and fails.
- **Hook:** the day clock's dusk moment (`updateDayNight`'s dusk transition — the same moments the alarm cine and `duskBegan` use) + position at the depot's generator spot (fuel dressing at sheds.2, MU-12 §2.1) + the cough take (dry-start cough, already in the engine's vocabulary; positional `sndAt` pattern from index.html:29977). Gate: only once power is *not yet* earned? Or even after (the old generator still coughs)? Design call — I'd stop it once the fuel lead is done (its story is told). No new prop needed; the generator dressing exists at the depot.

### 3.2 The guy wires singing before a storm
- **Exists:** the mast has guy wires visually (wires to an anchor by the mast); a storm *system* with forecasting — `scheduleNextRain` (index.html:10461; grace to day 3, CL-262), wind spells (index.html:10250: `updateWindSpell`, gales), and the weather's own approach (intensity ramps). No wire/singing sound exists.
- **Hook:** the weather timer is the moment: when a storm is scheduled and ~60–90 s out (weather state knows; a "stormSoon" test beside `fogNightActive`-style queries), play a rising aeolian tone positioned at the mast (`sndAt` on POI.mast, audible on approach along the trail — the mast is a destination, MU-12). Sound: a synthesized wire hum is squarely in core/audio.js's synth vocabulary (the growl/breath synth family) — no pack file needed; pitch climbs with wind.gale. This is the storm's *herald*, complementing the existing first-raindrop reads. It also teaches "wires = the relay's place" (W3's rule that each event belongs to one place).

### 3.4 The rangers' radio squelching

### 3.5 The trapper's chimes stopping before the dead come

## 4. Common shape

Every event is the same three pieces: a **moment** (day-clock edge: dusk/dawn/stormSoon/discovery — all exist as state), a **place** (a POI spot via the `sndAt`/emitter patterns already used for the generator hum), and a **sound or silence** (the synth voices in core/audio.js cover wires/chimes/cough; the phone ring and any *recorded* texture are pack takes for ChatGPT). None needs a new system; the work is one trigger + one positional call each, plus two small props (phone, chimes). The design discipline is W3's own: one place each, and each must pass docs/sound-principles.md (immersive or impactful) — the chimes stopping is the one to prototype first, because if the *silence* doesn't land, the set needs rethinking, and it's the cheapest to test.
