## 1. Hit numbers over the targets (ui/training-hit-pops.js)
- The pop was `#trainingScorePop` (ui/training-panel.js, unstyled, so it sat in the top left). training.hud.set now
  gets `last: null`, so it never shows; the HUD bar at the top (targets down, score, best) is unchanged.
- On a scoring hit: a pop at the plate's top centre, 18 cm above it: '+points' and under it the zone (and xN for the
  lane). Placed every frame by projecting that point, so it stays over its target as he turns; it pops in (scale 0.6 ->
  1.15 -> 1), holds, rises 55 cm and fades over its 1.25 s. Gold for head and bullseye, amber for the ring, grey for the
  plate. At most 8 at once; elements are reused. Styles are injected by the module (no training.css change).

## 2. The spitter's trail (game/zombie-ability-runtime.js)
- It was a round blob every 0.75 m (a bubbly radial texture on a 1.6 m square at a random angle), full strength until
  its last 1.3 s, then gone; the oldest went at once when the 96 slots were full.
- Now each drop is a segment of one ribbon from that spitter's last drop to this one, 0.48 m wide: clear at the edges,
  a thick green body, a wet sheen down the middle, the same all along so the segments join without a seam. It holds
  for its first 1.2 s, then eases smoothly to nothing by 6 s while it narrows by 30% and dries to a dull olive.
  Same slots, same buffers, same 6 s, same damage (the record's x/z/y and radius are unchanged).

## 3. The knockout (index.html trainingBlackout / updateTraining, core/audio.js)
- `TRAINING_KO_HOLD` = 3.4 s: the wake doesn't start until it has run, so he lies in the black for 3.4 s plus the
  1.1 s it already had.
- `AudioSys.knockout(hold)`: a gain stage between the master and the limiter fades everything (music, zombies, guns,
  the room) to nothing (time constant 0.32 s); a tinnitus rings on its own path: two close tones at 6.18 kHz beating
  slowly, a faint 3.95 kHz under them, settling down after a second and fading out at the hold's end. Its level
  follows the SFX fader. `AudioSys.knockoutEnd()` brings the mix back over about 1.8 s as he comes round (and at once
  if he leaves during it).
- 0.4 s into the black, every skull lying in the room goes (`trainingClearSkulls`; not one he has picked up).

## 4. The gate (world/training-tunnel.js, index.html)
- A tunnel behind the gate: 14 m deep, the gate's width and height, unlit near-black walls, ceiling and floor, a
  black back, and 7 black see-through veils across it from 0.7 m to 4.3 m in. Every face points into it or at the room.
- The line: single file down the middle, the first 5.4 m back, each next one at least 1.5 m (more for big bodies)
  behind, each walking at 0.9x its own speed (1.3-3.6 m/s) so the stride matches the floor. They are not drawn deeper
  than 4.6 m (behind every veil), so they walk out of the dark already striding.
- The tunnel and anyone in it are drawn only while the camera is on the room's side of the gate wall
  (`cameraInRoom`): with the camera behind that wall (the GP-197 cutaway can put it there) neither shows.
