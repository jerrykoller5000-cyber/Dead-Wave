# grokbot - GB-233 The principles pass, Claude's rulings 1-3 (materials, recoil, reloads from his eyes) + ruling 6 (the lure) - 2026-10-25
Changed:          Ruling 1, materials: the shared gun palette is now dielectric where it is painted, parkerized or polymer (gunBody, gunDark, gunMid metalness 0.06-0.08, roughness 0.66-0.68; the barrels, gunAccent, 0.1 / 0.6; gunBodyVC too). Bare steel stays only where it wears or works (gunMetal 0.85 / 0.42 for the muzzle caps and the mortar's lip, steelMat and the blades as they were). The revolver has its own steel (revolverSteel 0x9aa1a8, 0.85 / 0.4), so the turrets' steelMat isn't touched. The Uzi's grip and handguard were already their own matte polymer (gunPoly 0.03 / 0.76); its receiver (gunBody) is now matte too, so they no longer sit a metalness step apart. The mortar's tube is matte olive phosphate (gunOlive) and its plate painted (0.08 / 0.7).
                  Ruling 2, recoil: every kick ramps in. The kick functions add their kick as before; deferKick() moves that step into recoilPending (and punchBodyRecoil into bodyPending), and decayRecoil() feeds it in with a 10 ms time constant while the old decay runs, so the peak lands 1-2 frames after the shot frame (about 20-35 ms) instead of on it. The step is scaled so the old peak sizes hold (pistol, shotgun and sniper stand). recoilRate() is lifted out of decayRecoil(). A kick counter makes the frame that fired re-place the hold (without it the first frame showed the mount's raw base pose, a 75 deg jump; found and fixed). The M4: 2-4 deg and about 1-1.5 cm at the peak (was 9-15 deg and 8 cm), the torso's share cut from 0.85 to 0.2, and back in about 100 ms (decay 12 to 26). The minigun: no buck per round; each round tops up a push value that becomes a fine tremor (20 and 23 Hz, under 1 deg) and a slow push-back of up to 2.5 cm.
                  Ruling 3, reloads from his eyes: the pistol, Uzi and revolver now cant like the long guns in first person (FP_RELOAD_CANT 0.40, with a small dip). The AK and AA12 cant deeper with a dip (FP_RELOAD_CANT_DEEP 0.62, y -0.07), and so does the sniper. Third person unchanged.
                  Ruling 6, the lure's -5 hp: not a bug in the game. Only damageBuild() takes hp off a build, and it calls removeBuild() on the same call when hp reaches 0 or below, which takes it out of `builds` and the scene. The lure's own life is a separate timer (lureT, 20 / 35 / 45 s by tier) that removes it when it runs out. A -5 can only be read off a build object that's already been removed, which my probe was holding on to. Nothing changed.
                  t277: the M4's "eye clears the top line" check now uses the optic rule (the eye is aimed through the holo's window, below the hood top). Claude approved this under rule 13 (before/after: qa/2026-10-25-GB-233/m4-ads-check.png).
