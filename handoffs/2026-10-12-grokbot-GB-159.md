# GB-159: Training Ground wear (Jerry, direct ask)

Grokbot, 2026-10-12. Status: review.

## Asked
Jerry: wear and a little history for the training facility, in five kinds: floor, walls, props, lighting and signs. Then, after seeing a screenshot: "Not bad, like the direction. Another fidelity pass, we need more variation. Start with the ground marks and get that right."

## Done
- `world/training-wear.js` (new): `dressTrainingWear(T, ctx)` and `WEAR`.
  1. **Floor:** a single 768x2016 canvas sheet, plus a chip overlay on the yellow line. Pass 2 changes:
     - Worn routes are soft, stamped blobs. Their width varies, they wander and break up, and the worn middle shows lighter concrete with a grimy fringe. Where routes meet (the line, the door, the lockers), the wear builds up on its own.
     - Each lane has its own amount of use (`WEAR.LANE_USE`): lane 2 is heavy and lane 4 is barely touched.
     - Boot marks no longer use rings. They are tapered heel and toe smears, partial sole prints, comma-shaped turn scuffs and thin rubber streaks.
     - Extras: specks, a dried spill with a tide line, a faint drag mark, and grit in the tile seams near the walls.
     - The line's chips are ragged flakes, denser on the lanes that get more use.
  2. **Walls:** the backstop is chewed up behind each lane. The tile above it has pocks and patches (two whites). The side walls get vents with grime streaks, a conduit, and rust bleeding from bolts.
  3. **Props:** three lockers, the middle one dented with its door hanging open; a cracked monitor taped in an X; a 7-hook rack with hook 5 missing; and a sagging cot with a stained mattress in the infirmary bay.
  4. **Lights:** one strip flickers in bursts every 4–13 s and goes out with the light switch. Two strips are dead, both on side walls.
  5. **Signs:** four faded safety signs with peeling corners, and a DAYS WITHOUT INCIDENT board where 47 was wiped and a 0 written in red marker.
- `world/training.js`: imports the module and calls it after the release gate, leaves the decals and strips out of the collision boxes, and has `update` drive the flicker. The build returns a `wear` field.
- The static parts merge into one draw call per material. The signs, board, monitor, locker doors and mattress share one texture sheet, and only the flickering strip keeps its own mesh.
- Visual only: the walk areas, zombie room, build grid, targets and stations are unchanged. index.html was not touched.
