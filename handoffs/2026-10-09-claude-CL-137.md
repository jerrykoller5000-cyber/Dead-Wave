## What changed (index.html, the walk cycle and the pose code in updateZombies; beginCorpse/updateCorpses)

1. **Legs that walk like legs.** A leg is back-most at sin = 1 and front-most at sin = -1; between those it is in the
   air, and now the knee lifts there (most at mid-swing, straight again for the heel to land) and stays near straight
   on the ground with a touch of give at mid-stance. Before, the shin bent by a constant through the whole front half
   (a stiff march) and the hips bobbed UP at double support, the wrong way round (now |cos|: highest as the legs
   pass). Per kind: a feral's knees come up high and fast, a brute lifts and plants, a shambler drags low; the limp
   leg (variant) swings short and stiff. The elbows bend as the arms come forward (a swing, not a pendulum), and the
   arm swing shortens with the stride at a creep. (`kneeK`, `kneeOf`, `walkAmt0`; with CL-136's `spdK`, the stride
   rate and reach follow the body's actual speed.)
2. **Its eyes are on him.** Inside 16 m (fading to 24) the head turns to the marine, yaw and pitch, within what a neck
   does (±1.15 rad, pitch ±0.5), easing in and out over whatever the walk and the idle gave it. It lets go while it
   screams, spits, swings, is hit in the head, or has no head. The first time one gets him in view inside 12 m it
   snaps its head to him (the twitch) and stiffens for a beat (`z.lookK`, `z.lookYaw`, `z.noticed`).
3. **The hit lands somewhere.** On top of the old lean, by where the round went in (`z.hitPart`, from the hit height
   and the round's direction against the body's own right, set in damageZombie): a head hit snaps the head back and
   to the side; an arm hit jerks that arm up and in with a twist; a leg hit buckles that knee, the body dips, and the
   stride hitches (cadence × 0.35 for the moment); a body hit twists the torso away from the side it came in on.
   0.45 s, a fast rise and a slower settle. Arms take a third of body-height hits, on that side.
4. **The attack commits.** Through the wind-up it draws back (hips -0.1), then lunges through the swing with its whole
   body (hips +0.28 along a sine, down a little, the front knee taking the weight) and settles after.
5. **How it goes down.** A headshot buckles it at the knees: a crumple, the knees folding over a quarter second (hips
   down 0.4 × scale, shins 1.75, torso and head over, arms hanging), then the fold over the knees with a smoothstep
   instead of the topple's drop. Kinds with legs crumple two times in five on any kill; a heavy blow (power ≥ the
   throw threshold) still topples and slides; crawlers, hoppers, the spider and the colossus topple. `c.style`,
   `killZombie` now carries `headshot`.
6. **`TT.slowMoDbg(seconds, scale)`** (and `TT.triggerSlowMo`): the game at `scale` speed for that long, for strips.

## The camera (tools/camera-rig.js, CAMERA.md)

- `live:<type>` / `live:<n>`: a zombie in the game right now, where it stands; `corpse` / `corpse:<n>`: a body down.
- `pre` on a batch or job shot: code run in the game right before that picture (`T`, `wait(ms)`), so a shot sets its
  own scene with no gap (a hit, a kill, the marine moved, slow-mo on). The strips above are `review/camera/jobs`
  jobs: `tools/` has none saved; the job files are in Claude's outputs (p1-scene / p2-react) if wanted again.
