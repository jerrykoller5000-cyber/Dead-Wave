- **Aboard:**
  - the extraction state becomes 'boarded', and `dw-game` `'extraction'` `{ phase: 'boarded', day, calledDay, hot }` is published;
  - then `endGame(true)` runs, held until any finisher camera or scripted kill is over;

- **The window stays open:** the last kill's dawn sweep doesn't close it. The boat waits into the next prep until he boards or the next alarm sends it away (GB-85's 'gone').
- **HUD prompt** by the boat: "Hold E — Board the boat", then "Boarding… N%".
- A run reset clears the hold.
