## How it plays now

1. **Sato's note** is a scrap of paper held down by a stone, somewhere 9–26 m from the cabinet. It is usually tucked by a tree or a rock, and it moves to a new spot each run. Up close the only clue is "E — Read". The breaker panel no longer shows the tag.
2. **Breakers:** 2, 1, 4, as before.
3. **Radio:** the panel shows only the dial and the signal bars. There is no code display and no notes. The station beeps the code (each digit is that many beeps), pauses for 4.2 s, and starts again.
4. **Reboot:** "E — Reboot the relay" takes one press. It counts down by itself and keeps running wherever he goes; it only pauses with the game. The bar shows the seconds left, and the siren calls the dead in:

   | Day | Length | Called at once | Then one every | Most in all | Kinds drawn from |
   |---|---|---|---|---|---|
   | 1 | 20 s | 4 | 6 s | 7 | day 2's mix |
   | 5 | 34 s | 8 | 5.1 s | 14 | day 6's mix |
   | 10 | 51.5 s | 13 | 4.0 s | 25 | day 11's mix |
   | 20 | 86.5 s | 23 | 2 s | 48 | day 20's mix |

   When it finishes, the radio counts as repaired wherever he is, and the bar reads "Relay up".
5. **The vault** is a small concrete store on a dock, 8 m or more from the cabinet on the first flat, clear ground round the mast. A ramp runs up to its roll-up door, with a keypad and a lamp beside the door and a "RELAY STORES" sign. The code at the keypad sends the lift up the shaft in its floor with the reward (3.2 s), then the door rolls up (1.3 s). Inside, the roof and front are cut away and the other walls drop to a stub, so the camera can see him. E there takes the reward.
6. **Rewards** depend on the day the vault is opened (game/relay-vault.js). It opens once a run, so the choice is between early and small, or late and big. Waiting also costs the radio's daily calls and the revealed sites.
   - Days 1–3, "Scraps": $75, 1 med pen, two packs for the gun in hand.
   - Days 4–7, "Stores": $150, 2 med pens, 2 grenades, every gun topped up.
   - Days 8–11, "Armoury": the best of AK, M4, revolver, shotgun or Uzi he doesn't own (or $220), plus $250, 2 med pens, 3 grenades, top-up.
   - Days 12–15, "Heavy stores": the best of AA-12, sniper, launcher, flamer or AK he doesn't own (or $450), plus $400, 3 med pens, 4 grenades, top-up, full armour (a plate carrier if he has none).
   - Days 16–19, "Black cache": the best of minigun, AA-12, launcher, sniper or flamer (or $800), plus $700, 4 med pens, 6 grenades, top-up, armour.
   - Day 20, the final day, "The red katana": the katana, plus $700, med pens, grenades, top-up and armour.
7. **The red katana** is the top of the blade line (knife, then machete, then katana). It goes on F like the other blades:
   - Reach 3.3 m, a ±90° arc, and no limit on how many it hits.
   - 240 damage a cut, every 0.34 s, using the heavy chop.
   - It isn't on the hit sheet (hitWeapon 'katana'), so the cut's own damage counts. The size scale still makes a colossus take a few cuts.
   - Worn, it sits at his left hip in a black saya, and the hilt hides while it's drawn.
   - Sounds: a lighter, ringing machete swing and hit, and a draw ring when he gets it.
8. **Spawns** (relaySpawnSpot) come up only on ground that is:
   - open, dry and gentle;
   - 26–40 m from the mast, and 26 m or more from any cave mouth;
   - clear of rocks, trees and props;
   - reached by the horde's flow field on a sensible walk (no more than 1.6 times the straight line plus 10 m), with no blocked cell beside it;
   - within 7 m of the mast's height.
