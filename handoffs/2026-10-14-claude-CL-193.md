# CL-193 · Claude · The shootdodge: V is a Max Payne slow-motion dive now

Jerry (2026-10-14): "Rolling with V serves not real purpose. I want to turn those into a multi-direction max Payne
style slow-mo shooting dive roll. Jumping in every direction in slow mo. The ability to move your character and
aimpoint mid-air. I understand this is going to be a crazy iterative process. So I do not expect it to be perfect off
the rip. But lets get started on it."

His calls, asked the same day:

- **Slow motion:** everything slows, including the zombies, the bullets, the dive and his rate of fire. Only the aim and
  the camera stay at full speed.
- **Damage:** in the air, a zombie's swing or lunge misses him; everything else still hurts.
- **Landing:** he stays down and keeps shooting until a move key or jump, or about 1.5 s.
- **Limit:** a slow-mo meter that the dive drains and that refills over time and with kills.

## What it does

- **V dives**, in the direction of the move keys (screen-relative, the same as walking; shouldered, W is where he
  aims). All eight directions work. With no key held, he dives along the aim.
  - The flight is long and flat: 7.6 m/s, a 0.6 m rise, about 5 m.
  - It is in bullet time (×0.28), roughly 2.5 s real.
  - The move keys steer him in the air (speed stays between 4.5 and 9 m/s).
- **He aims and shoots the whole way:**
  - The reticle and the shoulder camera run on real time.
  - The gun fires at the slowed rate, and its rounds fly slow.
  - The shoulder aim (right mouse) works in the dive, and its camera comes down with his body.
- **The body:**
  - The whole rig turns about the hips to lie along the dive, head first.
  - The chest turns toward the aim as far as a body lying that way can. Diving sideways he faces the target. Diving at
    it he is belly down. Diving away from it he is on his back, shooting past his boots.
  - It tips flat in about 0.16 s, comes down with the fall so it meets the ground, then slides.
  - Legs: long, one knee drawn up. The arms are the normal gun hold, which aims at the reticle from wherever his
    shoulders are.
  - studio/marine-dive.js `diveBasis`.
- **On the ground:**
  - He lies and can keep shooting.
  - A move key or jump gets him up (after 0.3 s). A key still held from the flight has to be pressed again.
  - Otherwise he gets up on his own after 1.5 s. Getting up takes 0.45 s, or 0.32 s with knee and elbow pads (the
    pads did the same for the roll).
  - Then 0.4 s of cooldown.
- **Damage:**
  - In the flight, damage from a zombie attacker misses: swings, lunges, the demon's charge, and the brute's, military
    and drowned attacks through their `dodging` hooks.
  - Blasts, fire, acid, spider globs, lightning and falls still land.
  - On the ground everything lands.
  - Known edge: the colossus's thrown stone carries its owner too, so it misses in the air as well.
- **The meter** (HUD, under armour: "SLOW-MO", amber, it glows while time is slowed):
  - A dive costs 0.08 up front and drains 0.1 per real second of slow motion: about a third of the bar per dive.
  - It refills at 0.04 per second, starting 1 s after the slow motion ends. A kill adds 0.06.
  - Under 0.15 the dive still goes, but at full speed.
  - A new run starts full.
- **The look:** a dark vignette and +6° of field of view in bullet time. On take-off, the old dodge sound and a
  heartbeat thump. On landing, dust, a thud and a small shake.
- **Kept from the roll:** it breaks the drowned's grip, and "Fleet foot" (GB-101) still pays for a dive within 1.5 m
  of a swinging zombie.

All the numbers are in one place: `DIVE` in studio/marine-dive.js.

## Where

- **studio/marine-dive.js (new):** the state machine (idle, air, down, up), the flight, the meter, the time scale and
  the lie of the body. Pure maths, no three.js.
- **studio/marine-dive.test.mjs (new):** 7 node tests.
- **index.html:**
  - The roll's state is replaced by the dive's. `tryRoll` keeps its name so tools/check-players.mjs still knows it, but
    it now launches the dive. Also new: `onDiveLand` and `updateDiveHud`.
  - In tick:
    - `realDt`, the bullet-time scale (the slower of it and the finisher's wins), and the idle step (cooldown, meter,
      ease-out).
    - A dive ends at once on a cinematic, a scripted kill, the ladder or a knock-down.
    - Real time for the shoulder aim, the mouse aim, the reticle spread, the zoom and the heading.
    - In the movement step, the dive owns his velocity.
    - The dive's gravity (10 instead of 18), no jump-tap cap, no footfalls.
  - In updateMarinePose: the dive's lie replaces the roll's tumble. Its cross-fade state is still 'roll'.
  - holdWeapon: the hold's pitch fades out as he goes flat (his chest already faces the aim).
  - The cameras come down with his body.
  - damagePlayer has the in-the-air rule; the zombie `dodging` hooks use `diveInAir()`.
  - registerKill tops up the meter.
  - TT hooks: `diveDbg()`, `setDiveMeterDbg(v)`, `DIVE`.
  - A guarded `AudioSys.setTimeScale(k)` call, made only when the dive's scale changes, for the audio to follow
    (asked of ChatGPT).
- **tools/tests/t253.js (new):** the dive in the game (24 checks), including a shouldered dive.
- **tools/tests/t128.js:** waits for a dive to finish between its two V presses (a dive outlasts the roll's 1.6 s),
  and before the Vitality part (in the air a zombie's damage misses). Its checks are unchanged.
