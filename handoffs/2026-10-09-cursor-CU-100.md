## What was wrong

Measured on the real renderer before any change (review/hands/before.json): the arm IK hit its targets (0.0 cm),
so the misses were in what it aimed at.

1. **The hand point is the face of the curled fingers.** _HAND_R (-0.01, -0.26, 0.12) and _HAND_L sit on the front
   of GP-163's glove (it spans z 0.034 to 0.124 in forearm space). Every grip's centre line was put there, so the grip
   ran along the front of the fist with the fist behind or under it (review/hands/markers/: red is that point, green
   the fist's middle).
2. **There was no wrist.** The hand was fixed to the forearm; CL-122's forearm roll (at most 49 degrees) was all it
   had. A support arm reaching a forend is nearly straight, so the forearm points along the barrel, and a fist on the
   end of it can't lie along the barrel at all (t195 on the real renderer: 135 -> 91 degrees off). The fist hung under
   the handguard, fingers pointing down it.

## What changed

- **A wrist** (studio/marine-body.js arm): a group at the top of the palm on each forearm, holding the glove and the
  bare hand exactly where they were built (identity at rest). userData.grip is the middle of the fist's channel,
  (ox, -0.262, gz + 0.008) in forearm space.
- **holdHand** (index.html): the hold puts that grip point on the anchor. The arm reaches for the wrist that does it
  (up to four passes, each moving the aim by what the last missed). The forearm rolls as before, and the wrist takes
  the rest of the turn, up to WRIST.max (75 degrees). Used for the firing hand (shouldered or not, akimbo's second gun
  too) and the support hand.
- **How the support hand wraps.** Up a vertical foregrip (the launcher, the flamer): index finger first, up the grip,
  wrist 56-60 degrees. On a forend or the pump: the way the arm brings it, index toward him, palm up, fingers curled
  up the side, wrist 35-44 degrees. Index first toward the muzzle wanted 100-115 degrees at the wrist, and the roll
  flipped side to side through 180, throwing the hand 9 cm off the Uzi while he walked.
- **Under the forend, not inside it** (foreDrop): a deep forend or pump buried the centred fist. The anchor drops to
  1.2 cm inside the forend's underside, read once off the gun's own mesh round the anchor: the M4 1.3 cm, AK 3.5,
  AA-12 3.3, shotgun pump 3.4, sniper 0.8 (gun space). Only the piece the anchor is on counts, no more than 4.5 cm
  down, so the sniper's folded bipod doesn't.
- **Reach.** The fist's middle sits 4 cm deeper in the hand than the hand point did. Guns not in the shoulder slide
  toward the support shoulder (CL-122's rule) at 0.52 m instead of 0.565, checked twice so the re-aim can't push it back
  out. A long gun in the shoulder can't slide, so the support hand goes back along its forend by what it fell short
  (at most WRIST.slideMax, 12 cm): the M4 up to 4 cm running, the AK up to 8, the sniper 5-12, all still on the forend.
  The pump and a vertical foregrip can't be slid along, so there the grip point eases toward the hand point by what it
  lacks (the shotgun running: 0.2 cm).
- **Holds left as they were.** The pistol's and revolver's cup round the firing fist. The chainsaw's front handle (the
  wrist's turn over the top of its arch would not settle; the old hold is 0.0 cm on it). The minigun (Jerry's order).
- **Nothing else inherits the wrist.** Everything that takes a hand after the hold aims _HAND_R/L as before: the
  reloads, the knife, the draw, the grenade, the idle. solveArmIK eases that wrist back to rest by its weight, and
  updateMarinePose starts every frame with both wrists at rest, so a cine or a death that skips the hold has them
  at rest. Through a reload the support hand fades to the hand point with the roll (handRollLW) that already faded.
