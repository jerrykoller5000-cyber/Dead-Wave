# GB-160: Training Ground old bullet marks pass (Jerry, direct ask)

Grokbot, 2026-10-12. Status: review. This follows GB-159 (handoffs/2026-10-12-grokbot-GB-159.md).

## Asked
Jerry: "Great job. Let us move on to old bullet decals on the wall next. Same thing." That means a variation pass on the old bullet marks behind the targets.

## Done (world/training-wear.js only)
- **One shared set of mark types (`markKit`)**, seeded so no two marks come out the same:
  - fresh holes: a dark core, a torn rim and cracks running out through the tile
  - spalled chips showing the grey under the glaze
  - shallow ricochet gouges with lead smeared off them
  - old holes puttied and sanded flush: some painted in mismatched whites, creams and greys, some left bare with sanding marks, some shot through again
  - cut-out squares patched with a board, showing seams and screws, some shot through since
  - soot and lead smudges
  - rust or dirt runs from a few holes
- **On the backstop rubber**, puttied holes show as dark rubber plugs a shade off the sheet instead of white patches.
- **Placement:**
  - Each lane gets as many marks as it is used (`WEAR.LANE_USE`: lane 2 heavy, lane 4 light).
  - Marks spread out from the plates' chest height (1.9 m) and thin out with distance, both on the backstop and on the tile above it.
  - There are wild strays across both surfaces and on the side walls, mostly near the far end plus a few near the firing line.
- **Resolution:** the backstop and the tile above it are drawn at 64 px/m (`WEAR.WALL_PX`), the side walls at 48 px/m.
- The side-wall decals are now built after the mark set exists.
- Cost: the same 4 wall decal meshes and materials as before, so no new draw calls. The texture sizes are 998x243, 1024x134 and about 1512x283 for each side wall. Nothing reaches above 5.9 m, and collision is unchanged.
