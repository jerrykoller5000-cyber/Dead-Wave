## 3. Root Cause Analysis & Detailed Findings for Cursor

### A. Why `placed 1 walls` instead of 10 walls
In CU-19, `commitBuildDrag()` was correctly wired into `tools/bench.mjs`. However, the drag coordinates chosen in `tools/bench.mjs` (lines 79–83):
```javascript
const p = TT.player.position;
const gx = TT.gridIndex(p.x), gz = TT.gridIndex(p.z);
TT.setPlaceMode('wall');
const c = TT.camera.position;
const tz = TT.gridCentre(gz + 3);
```
When the marine lands in front of the porch at `(0, -8.5)` (`gx: 0, gz: -4`), offsetting `gz + 3` targets `gz = -1`.
This drag line passes **directly through the HQ cabin**!

We inspected `TT.dragPlanNow()` during the drag:
```json
{
  "gx": 0, "gz": -4,
  "drag": { "gx": -3, "gz": -1, "edge": null },
  "plan": [
    "something in the way",
    "that is the cabin",
    "that is the cabin",
    "that is the cabin",
    "that is the cabin",
    "that is the cabin",
    "something in the way",
    null
  ],
  "placed": 1,
  "totalBuilds": 1
}
```
- 5 cells are refused with `"that is the cabin"`.
- 2 cells are refused with `"something in the way"` (terrain slope / cabin porch collision).
- Only 1 cell at the extreme end is empty and legal (`null`).

**Recommended Fix for Cursor:**  
Drag southward into open grass away from the cabin (e.g. `tz = TT.gridCentre(gz - 3)` instead of `gz + 3`).

---

### B. Why `fps: 0.0` is reported by `bench.mjs`
1. `tools/bench.mjs` opens `index.html?debug=1&raf=timer`.
2. When `?raf=timer` is set, `index.html` binds `requestAnimationFrame` to `setTimeout(..., 1000/60)`.
3. In Chrome, background and headless tabs throttle `setTimeout` to a 1000 ms clamp.
4. `TT.perfSnapshot()` / `perfWindow()` in `index.html` discards samples older than 1000 ms (`now - s.t > 1000`).
5. When throttled to 1 frame per second, `perfSamples` is empty or has at most 1 item when the 1-second interval polls, resulting in `fps: 0.0`.
6. When run without `&raf=timer` using native rAF, active frames are recorded continuously (`fps: ~4.5`, `worst: 224.3 ms` under SwiftShader CPU rendering).
