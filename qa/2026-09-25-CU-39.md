## The black specks over the pit's runes: the lake bed poking through the rune bands

- `pit.png` and `lake-shore.png` from `node tools/shoot.mjs pit lake-shore --show`, and two closer noon views,
  `pit-close-oblique.png` and `pit-close-over.png`. The specks are dark, leaf-shaped slivers over both rune bands.
- They are in exactly the same places on every load, so they are fixed geometry, not something moving.
- Not bubbles (the pit's bubbles were off: `getPitBubbles()` 0), not fish (0 of the 14 fish are within 12 m of the pit),
  not tentacles (hidden until the cutscene, CL-20).
- **Switching off depth testing on the two rune bands (`userData.pitRunes`) removes every speck**:
  `pit-runes-depth-on.png` against `pit-runes-depth-off.png`. So something between the camera and the bands is hiding
  them. The bands are draped over the funnel from `sampleHeight` at 0.06-0.07 m, and the rendered bed's triangles are
  coarser than that sampling, so in places the bed sits above the band and hides it. Under 5-8 m of water the hidden bits
  read as black.
- Fix idea (Claude's call): drape the bands higher, or from the bed mesh's own triangles instead of `sampleHeight`; or keep
  depth testing against the well and stones only. Depth test off everywhere would also show the bands through the
  standing stones and the well's lip.

### Update after Claude's fish fix (2229693)

`pit-close-over-after-fish-fix.png`, the same noon view reshot with the fish kept out of the pit: **the specks are still there,
in exactly the same places.** So in this view they are not fish. The depth-test experiment above cannot tell fish from bed on
its own (drawing the bands over everything hides both), but the fish fix leaving every speck in place points back to fixed
geometry in front of the bands, most likely the carved bed's triangles.
