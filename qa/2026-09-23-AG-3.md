## Detailed Observations

### 1. Side Aim on Low Pillar (GB-7)
- Placed a standalone pillar at corner (gx: -5, gz: -9).
- Positioned marine 3.5 m to the side at ground level.
- Set place mode to heavy turret and cast a shallow aim ray from eye height (py + 0.3) toward the pillar cap (`(pil.mesh.position.y + 1.6) - (py + 0.3)`).
- `getPlacePoint()` returned `{ hitType: 'pillar', valid: true }` without falsely snapping to rising ground grass.
- Calling `tryPlace()` mounted the turret directly onto the pillar cap (`slot: 'ptop'`, `isOnPillar: true`).

### 2. Roof from Ground Aim Inside Four Walls (GB-8)
- Constructed a walled 1-cell room with 4 perimeter edge walls around cell `(-1, -4)`.
- Positioned marine standing at ground level inside the enclosed room (`cy: -3.17`).
- Selected `floor` place mode and aimed ray upward from player eye level toward the top edge of the wall (`wallTopY = -1.0`).
- `getPlacePoint()` correctly resolved `{ pptLv: 1, valid: true }` matching elevated aim over the walled cell.
- Placed piece result: `{ count: 1, level: 1, posY: -1.0, isRoof: true, isBoardwalk: false }`.
- The floor cleanly roofs the enclosed space overhead, completely eliminating the previous regression where grounded players got unintended boardwalks at their feet.
