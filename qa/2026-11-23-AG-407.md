## 3. Three-Pass Visual Inspection & Polish Log

### Pass 1 Visual Audit Findings (caught by inspecting RTX 5080 renders)
1. **Reversed triangle winding** in `sculptSlabGeometry` and `sculptSpireGeometry` (clockwise `cos(a), sin(a)` ring produced inward normals, culling front faces so slabs appeared sunken/concave and spires showed hollow torn bottom skirts).
2. **Spire base grounding on steep slopes** needed a deep buried root skirt (`-0.28 * height`) plus a basal bedrock collar boulder.
3. **First People Ring Stone & Standing Stone UV/carving defects**: `buildMenhirGeometry` picked UV axis by vertex position (`|x| > |z|`) instead of face normal (`|nx| > |nz|`), causing horizontal corduroy streaks across wide monoliths, and `+Z` jitter bulged slightly through the Ring Stone's rune plane.
4. **Limestone erratic smoothness**: `creaseDeg: 40` and soft cuts made `01-valley-erratic-close` read too smooth/pale.
5. **Schist & Monolith texture periodicity**: regular `sin(v*58)` chisel stripes on `synthMonolith` and light domain warp on `synthSchist` created faint striping at large cave/menhir scale.
6. **Campfire hearth stones**: `'river'` style read slightly smooth; switched to heat-fractured soot-darkened `'granite'`.

### Pass 2 Verification (ALL 6 defects resolved)
- Fixed CCW outward winding in `sculptSlabGeometry` & `sculptSpireGeometry`, and added automated **positive signed-volume outward-winding assertions** to `world/rock-geometry.test.mjs`.
- Fixed `buildMenhirGeometry` normal-driven UVs + `flatFront: true` on carved First People faces; `17-first-ring-stone`, `23-first-ring-night`, and `30-standing-stone-clear` now render crisp, distortion-free, lichen-weathered stone with clean glowing runes and zero clipping.
- Verified all 5 valley geologies side by side in dry Noon (`sheet-4-hero-geologies-after.jpg`): speckled Sierra Granite, dark columnar Basalt, warm cross-bedded ochre Sandstone, multi-tiered Karst Limestone slabs, and organically warped silver-slate Schist are unmistakably distinct at a glance.
- Verified **four lighting states**: Noon (`01`–`06`, `25`–`32`), Dusk raking light (`07`), Night flashlight & glowing runes (`08`, `23`), and Overcast Rainstorm wetness=1.0 (`22`, darkened wet albedo + realistic damp specular sheen matching mud puddles).
