### #4 · [P2 · Visual / Sky Shader] Night-Sky Stars Render as Blocky Square Voxel Quads with Square Bloom Boxes
- **Owner:** `claude2` / `claude` (`index.html:2673–2678`)
- **Screenshots:** `qa/AH-6/webgpu-07b-night1-combat-1p.png` (clearest upward 1P sky view), `qa/AH-6/webgpu-06b-night1-alarm-cine.png`, `qa/AH-6/webgl-06b-night1-alarm-cine.png`, `qa/AH-6/webgl-09c-game-over-screen.png`
- **What happens:** Looking up at the night sky in 1st-person (`Digit5`) or during the Dusk/Alarm/Graveyard cameras shows every star as a sharp ~`6×6 px` square inside a large translucent grey square halo box (~`28×28 px`), bleeding over dark sky and cloud edges.
- **Root cause:** `buildSkyMaterial()` (`index.html:2673`) quantizes direction into cube cells via `const cell = floor(dir.mul(320.0));` with no intra-cell radial falloff (`fract(dir.mul(320.0)).sub(0.5)`). Each `1/320` rad (`~0.18°`) cell lights uniformly as a square pixel block and triggers square post-processing bloom (`mul(1.6)`).
