## 1. What Was Updated (Zero New Landmarks Added — 100% Existing Outer Models Upgraded)

Every single existing outer-world model in `world/outer-world.js` was rebuilt and paired with a dedicated procedural PBR texture & WebGPU TSL atmospheric depth-softening library (`world/outer-textures.js`):

### A. Atmospheric Softening, Optical Edge-Haze & Partial Occlusion (`world/outer-textures.js`)
- **14 live WebGPU TSL `NodeMaterial`s** (with automatic `MeshStandardMaterial`/`fakethree.mjs` fallback) syncing every frame to live horizon fog, sky mid-tint, sun/moon direction, dusk, storm/rain, night, and blood moon.
- **Triplanar surface breakup:** procedural seamless `DataTexture`s (`256×256` concrete/timber/rock/foliage pack, `256×256` directional whitewater cascade, `256×128` multi-octave Gaussian-feathered billowing alpine mist with exact `0` border alpha, plus reused cached `granite`/`limestone`/`monolith` rock PBR textures).
- **Distance desaturation & contrast compression** ramping past `r = 236..425 m` so outer models soften naturally through alpine air while near-wall pines stay dark, rich, and matched to the inner valley forest.
- **Grazing-angle Fresnel optical edge-softening ("atmospheric rim blur")** gently melting hard distant polygon edges into mountain haze without blurring the playable interior.
- **Multi-layer billowing valley mist & peak cloud drapes** (18 instances in 2 batched `InstancedMesh` draws) softly veiling structure foundations, river gorge footings, waterfall plunge pools, Ridgeline's cliff flanks, and the NE valley pass.

## 3. Two-Pass Visual Inspection Summary

- **Pass 1 Audit Findings:** Architecture, watchtower, trestle, waterfall, crag seating, and standing stones transformed immediately, but Pass 1 revealed 4 defects caught on inspection: (1) outer conifer whorl hex colors skipped `THREE.Color` sRGB-to-linear decoding, rendering near-wall pines pale minty sage; (2) atmospheric haze/emissive ramp began too close behind the wall (`r = 222 m`), softening near-wall pines and washing Ridgeline's front concrete too light; (3) one near-bank conifer partially crossed the left River Gate trestle sightline; (4) `6%` residual daytime linear white snow leaked into the far mountain range at Night (`20`).
- **Pass 2 Verification (All 4 Resolved):** Decoded conifer colors via `THREE.Color` with inner-valley-matched `leafShade` warmth/AO, calibrated radial haze ramp (`r = 236..425 m`) & gentler linear emissive veil (`<= 0.18`), deepened Ridgeline's concrete + added bold front-flank granite cliff buttresses, widened River Gate sightline clearance (`0.048 rad` for `r < 276 m`), and calibrated Night cordillera linear blend (`0.992`) + subtle moonlight rim. All 20 views across Noon, Overcast/Rain, Dusk, and Night — including 30°–34° telephoto stress zooms and ground-level player eye-height views — verified clean.
