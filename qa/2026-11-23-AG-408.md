## 3. Two-Pass Visual Inspection & Polish Summary

### Pass 1 Visual Audit Findings
1. Plain untextured `PlaneGeometry` valley mist showed visible rectangular glass borders in `02`, `03`, `06`, `10`.
2. Octahedron beacon meshes (`size: 2.8` + `2.8×` halo) rendered as oversized faceted red/orange geometric crystals on `13`–`16`.
3. Ridgeline's rectangular concrete plinth corner jutted over the steep northern peak (`03`, `04`), and Cordon Overwatch legs hung slightly on the steep western knoll (`09`).
4. Dark charcoal outer crags (`0x52575f`) dotted isolated on pale mountain slopes read too dark/scattered.
5. Three-tier 6-sided outer conifers read slightly geometric right behind the perimeter wall (`r = 226–260 m`).
6. Mid/far alpine ranges could stand taller through all 8 compass saddles for stronger mile-deep horizon layering.

### Pass 2 Verification (All 6 Findings Resolved)
- Replaced mist planes with a **Gaussian-feathered procedural `DataTexture`** (`makeMistTexture`) — zero hard borders in `02`, `03`, `06`, `10`, `18`.
- Replaced faceted beacon octahedrons with **photographic exponential radial-bloom `THREE.Sprite`s** (`makeLightGlowTexture`) — `14`, `15`, `17`, `18` now show crisp pinpoint red aviation blinkers atop Ridgeline, a warm amber lantern inside the Fire Lookout cab, and delicate twinkling amber pinpoints of the distant valley town through the NE pass.
- Rebuilt Ridgeline and Cordon Overwatch bases as **tapered battered octagonal summit bastions** carved into the peaks (`03`, `04`, `09`).
- Re-toned and contour-clustered outer alpine crags to match mountain granite (`0x767873` / `0x8f9088`), upgraded outer conifers to **4-tier staggered 7-sided evergreen silhouettes**, and elevated the Mid & Far alpine ranges (`peakMax: 312 m / 445 m`, still 180+ m inside `skyDome = 1350 m`).
