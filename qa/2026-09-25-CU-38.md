## What stands out

1. **Under 60 fps with the field at the 48-zombie cap** (nights 5-10): means of 50-54, 1% lows of 21-25, worst frames
   40-47 ms. The budget is 60 fps with 48 zombies (AGENTS.md rule 12). The overlay in the night-7 shot reads 788 draw calls
   against about 230 at the menu, with only one of the 48 near the marine: most are still walking in from the caves.
2. **Night 1 has the single worst frame of the run, 82 ms**, its only hitch, in the first seconds of the first wave. Likely
   another first-time compile (the first fight's gore or the risers climbing out) that the warm-up does not cover yet.
3. The finisher never hitched: worst 22-40 ms on every night.
4. The megaswarm bench (CU-30) held 59 fps with 500 zombies because they stand still in one clump; a real wave is 48
   zombies walking, pathing and animating across the map, which is the heavier case.

## What I could not check

- The marine was in godmode and did not shoot; the horde did not reach him in 25 s on the later nights, so "the fight" here
  is the approach, not a melee at the HQ.
- The card's numbers (1 kill, 0-1 skulls) reflect my script clearing the field, not a real night.
- No sound (muted window). Day-time prep between nights was skipped by Next Night.
