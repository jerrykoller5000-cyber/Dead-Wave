## 3. Analysis & Key Takeaways

1. **Impact of GB-28 (Far Zombie LOD):**
   - Disabling `castShadow`, pausing limb `matrixAutoUpdate`, and skipping crawl/gait updates for zombies beyond 40m has drastically cut the scene graph traversal overhead during massive horde scenarios.
   - In 500-zombie Megaswarm, over 80% of entities reside beyond 40m. The 65.5% drop in hitches directly confirms Grokbot's optimizations work as intended.

2. **Impact of CL-28 (Freezing Static World):**
   - Disabling `scene.matrixAutoUpdate` and freezing the matrices of ~900 batched tree groups/canopies and foliage chunks eliminated redundant world matrix calculations on static objects every frame.
   - Even during Day 5 combat with 221 scheduled enemies and ambient world rendering, hitches dropped by 57.1% (from 28 down to 12).
