  2. **Tank-top armhole & shoulder separation**: When arms swing forward/up in attack, the upper-arm bulb separates visually from the dark hollow tank-top armhole (`qa/AG-402/shambler/07-attack-close-side.png`, `qa/AG-402/shambler/06-attack-close-front.png`).
  3. **Thin uniform tube arms & flat hem step**: Arms lack forearm/elbow taper (`qa/AG-402/night12/02-n1-shambler-attack.png`); tank-top hem steps sharply to straight trousers with little waistline blending (`qa/AG-402/shambler/04-close-back.png`).
  4. **Blocky boot cuffs & inconsistent detail**: Boots read as thick brown blocks at mid-stride and attack (`qa/AG-402/shambler/07-attack-close-side.png`).
- **Q5 (Clipping / motion / death)**:
  - **Corpse head/arm floor sink**: Settled corpse reports `-361.9 mm` floor clearance with the head/neck area sunken or clipped below floor level while boots hover off the floor (`qa/AG-402/shambler/09-death-corpse.png`).
