`side-<weapon>.png` (its right side) and `three-<weapon>.png` (from ahead and above); `lineup.png` is the twelve
hand-held weapons together. The Watchman MG and the mortar are there set up (`m240`, `mortar`) and carried
(`m240Carried`, `mortarCarried`).
