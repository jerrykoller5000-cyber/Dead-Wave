# GP-20004 · Hikers' trail chain

## Quest-design questions

- **Why does it exist?** Rangers closed the trail after the hikers disappeared. Their chain still hangs across the camp approach; the ambulance crew kept its key with the radio log in the glovebox.
- **Why care?** The missing hikers and Pike's fate draw the player past a physical trace of the search, while opening the trail makes the cache and camp reachable.
- **How does it connect?** The medical convoy leads to the hikers' camp through the existing `ambulance-reached` event, `chain-key`, and `missing` lead; the site and its survivor remain governed by story-chain timing.
- **What changes afterward?** Taking the key and opening the chain clears the blocker permanently for that run and enables the missing lead's interaction. The gate remains shut if the player has not visited the ambulance and opened it.
- **What makes it memorable?** A worn TRAIL CLOSED board and slack metal chain interrupt the trail just before the otherwise inviting camp clearing. The key is found inside the ambulance beside the radio log, tying the two scenes together.

Placement follows world-design rules 2 (rangers' response), 8 (room to interact), 11 (trail guidance), and 12 (search aftermath). The blocker uses the existing chain mesh and the key uses the existing clipboard/key mesh. No new lead, reward, or story contract is introduced.
