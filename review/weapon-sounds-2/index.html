Weapon sounds, take two

CL-135, after Jerry's listen: the low end stays; the reloads, the guns' high end and the impacts are rebuilt from noise (steel, polymer, hands, dirt: no tones at all), and switching weapons is an old TV changing channel. Grey "before" is CL-134 (what you heard), gold "now" is this. Everything is straight out of the game's audio engine at the default volume. The whole thing in one file: weapon-sounds-2-reel.mp3 (timestamps in README.md).

Switching weapons

Before, the wheel ticked with the sound of a spent case landing and a change made no sound. Now the wheel ticks like a TV dial (a detent and a blip of snow) and every change of weapon is a channel change: the knob, a burst of static, cut, the picture on.

Reloads

Each weapon's whole reload as the game cues it: the hand to the pouch, the magazine out (and hitting the ground), the new one in and slapped home, the slide, bolt or handle, the settle at the end.

The guns

The boom and the sub are CL-134's, untouched. What changed is the action behind each shot (the slide, the bolt, the links), which rang like a xylophone, and the Wasp's bark, which was a little too much of a note.
