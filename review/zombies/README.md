# The dead (CL-129)

Real renderer (software GPU), day light. The lineups are the same kinds, before and after. Left to right in lineup 1: spitter, military, drowned, leaper, feral, shambler. Lineup 2: screamer, bomber, brute, demon, spider.

- `before-lineup1.png`, `after-lineup1.png`: the jaws hang open, the walkers hunch, rags hang from the hems.
- `before-lineup2.png`, `after-lineup2.png`: the bomber and the brute get the jaw; the screamer, the demon and the spider are unchanged.
- `close.png`: the faces from a couple of metres: the hanging jaw, the teeth, the dark of the mouth.
- `maimed2.png`: a crawler (right: flat, head up, hauling on its arms) and a hopper mid-hop (left, close to the camera, arms out).

The crawl, the hop, the reach, the twitch and the lurch are motion; they show best in the game.
