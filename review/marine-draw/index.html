The draw and holster moves. When he switches guns, his hand takes the gun back where it lives (his back, the chest holsters under his arms, the hip holster) and then draws the next one from where it lives and brings it up. The left hand lets go of the foregrip meanwhile. U is a holster alone; U again is a draw alone. Each strip is four moments of one switch, left to right. A shot cuts the move short, so switching never gets slower in a fight. It is better seen in the game than in stills: switch guns in prep and watch him. Your notes go in notes.md in this folder.
