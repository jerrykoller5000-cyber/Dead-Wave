The Hollows' sound · CL-101 · v1

What you hear when you go down into a warren. Each recording is the same 50-second walk, made by the game's own sound code. They are turned up so you can hear them on their own; in the game they sit under your footsteps and shots.

  1. 0 s, topside: the wind in the trees.
  2. 4 s, below, quiet: the wind is gone. The cave's low air, water dripping, and the Hush humming (it beats slowly).
  3. 16 s, the stir climbs (as if he's been shooting): a rumble grows in the rock. At half and at four-fifths the rock groans. From a third, the guardian moves in the walls: a scrape and some heavy knocks, off to one side.
  4. 30 s, the warning: the rock screams (grinding), the rumble full. In the game the music goes silent here.
  5. 38 s: knocked back to half (as after a kick-free).
  6. 41 s, the Hush dies: its hum winds down with a crackle.
  7. 46 s, back up: the wind again.

Below, the day's music is still there but far off, as if heard through the rock (quieter and muffled). A fight below plays its music as usual.
