The guardian boss on the cave guardian's rig · CL-78 · v1

The guardian you fight on nights 6, 12 and 18 is now the thing from the caves, in the Marrow's chalk colours, not a stretched zombie. It bounds at you on all fours, rears up with a clawed arm out to strike, flinches when hit, and rolls over when it dies. Its health, damage, speed and rules are the same. Stills can't show the motion: on a guardian night (or the dev console's guardian spawn) watch it move. Your notes go in notes.md.
