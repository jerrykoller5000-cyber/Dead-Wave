Fog Night · CL-76 · v1

Night 14: the lake breathes out. While its wave is on, a cold mist closes in to about 30 m, goggles down or not, and the sky and the ridgeline sink into it. It rolls in over five seconds at the alarm and lifts at dawn; the night keeps its own music. The same view from the HQ toward the treeline: a plain night, then Fog Night.
