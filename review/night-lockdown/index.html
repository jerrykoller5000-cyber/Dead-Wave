The night lockdown · CU-82 · v1

From the alarm to the morning the HQ is locked down: no kiosk, Armory, CIF, HQ panel or build menu. Every light on the HQ turns red while it is: the bay lamps, the flood, the corner strobes (red flashes in the alarm), the alarm cabinet's beacon and screen, the CIF and Armory signs, and the kiosk's screen.
