## 2026-09-29 - Jerry - v3
(Given in chat to Claude, written here in his words.)
The guardian running out and grabbing you does not look that bad. He just needs to drag you into the cave faster.
The marine needs no blood trail, but he needs to be shaking his head and pounding his fists as he is dragged in.
The animation of when the guardian grabs you when the marine tries to walk into the cave is poor: there are a few
frames where the guardian jumps into the air. No blood needs to be seen yet.
The cave needs more of a real depth to it. Instead of the guardian suddenly appearing and disappearing, he needs to
emerge from the darkness and be partially covered by it. The motion could be all round more fluid. It would be nice
if the guardian made a low growl.
