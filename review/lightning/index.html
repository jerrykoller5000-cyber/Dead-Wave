Lightning and the insulated boots · CL-92 · v1

Every shower is a storm now, and each storm throws five bolts. Most come down off in the trees: a white flash, the bolt, the crack and the thunder. Now and then one sets a tree near you on fire, kills the dead where it lands, or hits you for 70. A pair of yellow rubber boots waits at the foot of the radio mast; walk over them and you wear them for the run, and lightning can't hurt you. Try it from the dev console: shower, strike, strike me, strike tree, strike dead, boots. (The night shot is at the title screen's lighter light.) Your notes go in notes.md.
