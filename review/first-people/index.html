first people · CL-107 · v1

The valley's first people, told without words (docs/story.md §2, §7). The standing stones on the highest clear ground, each with one of the Pit's glyphs on the side that looks at the lake; the ring stone on the highest of them, the ring of eight carved round the dark; offerings at the hill barrow's door (cairns, a stone bowl of old ash, an antler, a stone with the crown). And the Marrow cave (the chalk one) sealed by their cracked carved door: he can't go in or poke it; the dead still squeeze out through the hole at its foot. The carvings are dark grooves by day and give the Pit's cold light at night; on a silenced day they go dark. Your notes go in notes.md.
