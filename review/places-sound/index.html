The caves and the pit: their sound · CL-82 · v1

What you hear near a cave mouth and near the pit, at night. The recording is made by the game's own sound code and turned up so you can hear it on its own; in the game it sits under everything else.

  1. 0 s, in the open: wind and crickets.
  2. 4 s, walking up to a cave mouth: the cave breathes. Air rushes in and out of it every few seconds, with a low hum under it, from the mouth's side. By day it is quieter. In the game the calm music also draws back a little here, and comes back as you walk away.
  3. 22 s: away again.
  4. 26 s, by the lake over the pit: a deep hum from under the water (you feel it more than hear it on small speakers), with the pit's rumbles on top as before. Once the signal is silenced, the pit is quiet.
