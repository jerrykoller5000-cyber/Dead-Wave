# The Watchman MG, carried and fired (CL-131)

Shots from the real renderer (SwiftShader in the cloud, not Jerry's GPU).

- `carried.png`: shouldered (T), five angles. The Watchman on his shoulder with the tripod's legs folded under the
  barrel, muzzle forward and up, his right hand up on it. The guns on his back are his own slung guns. Taken just before
  a last nudge: the gun now sits 2 cm further out and 3 degrees lower.
- `manned-tipped-up.png`: manned and aimed at a mark 5 m up and 10 m out. The gun tips on its own hinge; the tripod
  stays put. The spent cases and links are the small pieces under it.
- `ak-then-watchman.mp3`: the AK's report that the Watchman used to borrow (a 1.3 s burst), then two single Watchman
  rounds, then a 2.8 s Watchman burst. Its own report is deeper and heavier, with a bolt clack in front.
