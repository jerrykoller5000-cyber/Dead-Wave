The heart in the Marrow · CL-112 · v1

The secret's last room, far under the Marrow. A long tunnel slopes down into one big round cave. In the middle is the source: a glowing pool with the Pit's runes round it. Six stone columns hold up the roof. In Grokbot's fight (GB-92) the guardian can knock columns down; a fallen column lies across the floor.
