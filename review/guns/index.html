The guns · CU-81 · v1

Every weapon rebuilt: the twelve guns, the knife and the machete, and the M240B on its tripod. The parts are drawn from real outlines now (grips with a finger groove, stocks, magazines with their curve, turned barrels, muzzle devices and scope tubes), and the metal is lighter and less shiny, so the guns stop reading as black shapes. Each gun keeps its name, its grips and moving parts where they were, and its wood and polymer, so camo and the rune finish still go on. Old (v0) on the left, new (v1) on the right.
