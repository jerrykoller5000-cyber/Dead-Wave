The rabbit mound and the holy grenade · CL-93 · v1

Far out from every trail there's one mound with bones and a skull round its mouth. Shoot into it and a small white rabbit comes out, stares at you, then goes for your throat: one bite and your head is off. Bullets don't hurt it. The holy grenade sits in a little stone box behind the graveyard's dead tree. Walk over it to take it. When the rabbit is awake and near, G throws the holy grenade: a choir sings, the rabbit freezes, and the blast kills it. Try it from the dev console: holy gives you the grenade, rabbit takes you to the mound and wakes it. Your notes go in notes.md.
