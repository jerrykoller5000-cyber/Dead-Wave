The guardian's last fight: its music · CL-85 · v1

The fight in the heart under the Marrow, the only place the guardian can die, now has its own song. It is the same family as every night's music, slower and heavier, in a big stone cave. A low double heartbeat thumps under it, and the source rings high and cold over the quiet parts. It follows the fight: phase 1 the drops; phase 2 the second drop and the bridge; phase 3, as the columns come down, the climax. While the Hush is flat and nothing hurts it, the break.
