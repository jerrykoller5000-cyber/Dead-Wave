# GP-20130 — Training takes damage

Proposed for Jerry, J151 notes 61–62. The range becomes a place the player can damage, while the build room visibly protects its working equipment during an alarm. All damage lasts for this Training visit; leaving and returning restores the room. This adds no mission, reward or campaign consequence.

**Lights.** A shot into a fixture's bulb or a grenade blast with a clear path breaks that fixture: a short glass/arc reaction, then a dark damaged fitting. Other fittings keep working. The wall switch still switches every surviving light; it never repairs broken ones. Sunlight and the player's flashlight remain independent. Use the existing local-light budget; breaking a fixture removes its illumination candidate rather than adding another real-time light. Intact and broken fittings share batched geometry/materials.

**Terminals.** The CIF, supply terminal, Armory, range computer, HQ control console and skull-bank terminal each have an intact and wrecked state. The first damaging bullet or unshielded grenade blast triggers one brief screen flash/crack, falling glass or a loose hinged face, sparks and a power-down sound. Its screen stays dead, and E cannot open it; any already-open interface closes. Pellets and repeated hits cannot replay the destruction or duplicate purchases. A broken range computer ends its active drill without granting a completion. Door keypads, exit/reset controls and the recovery bed remain separate protected utilities, so destroying the CRTs cannot trap the player. No automatic repairs during a visit.

**Alarm shields.** The build-room console and bank get real painted-steel covers in guides attached to their existing housings. Sounding the alarm starts a roughly 0.5-second close, with a motor/seat clang; the deployment countdown releases zombies after the covers seat. A moving cover blocks rounds only where its metal actually is. The closed cover stops bullets, fragments and direct blast paths; no invisible immunity in front of an exposed screen. A terminal broken before closure remains broken. On a clear room, covers reopen. Keep their travel clear of the player, panels and access aisle; a blocked cover pauses deployment rather than cutting through a body.

**Infirmary.** Replace the present three-metre mesh enclosure on its two open sides with steel blast-shield panels on the same footprint. Retain its existing recovery rule: lower for the wake-up, and raise only after the player clears the bay by two feet. A grenade crossing into the bay gets an immediate catch/return beat: a gloved attendant's arm appears from a small protected service hatch and flicks it back out toward the thrower's position at release. Use the existing grenade grip/limits, not a new floating hand. The fuse keeps counting; there is no fuse reset, homing or extra explosive. A crossing is handled once, and a grenade already at detonation detonates normally. If the player is inside during recovery, return it outward through the clear opening instead of farther into the bay. The return is fast (target under 0.2 seconds) and visibly caused by the attendant.

**Implementation and proof.** ChatGPT owns state, interaction availability, bay behavior and copy; request World construction and Claude 2's hit/blast/grenade/alarm call-site loans. Proof: same eye/overhead cameras in four lights; real shots/blasts, open-menu destruction, duplicate pellets, obstructed covers, wake/exit, fuse expiry, repeated crossings and visit reset. Moving/broken geometry must match exact traces. Retain clipping/anatomy checks and compare load/frame times in an exclusive slot; every applicable model/motion score must reach four.

**Five questions.** Cause: the player's shots and grenades damage the training equipment. Stake: careless fire removes useful services and a misplaced grenade comes back. Connection: Branch kit protects its operations and medical bay. Consequence: the visit keeps the damage until re-entry. Memorable beat: the protected infirmary throws the player's grenade straight back.
