The valley's history in things · CL-108, CL-109 · v1

Everyone who came here died, and this is what they left. The PGB: a survey board at each warren cave, FOB Threshold's sealed door (LOCKDOWN, clawed through) and its motto, the Cordon's chained gate where the trail meets the wall. The hikers: their missing posters at the trailhead, their packs, rope and headlamp at the old mine. The rangers: their truck at their camp, the radio still on. Coldwater: the church's shell by the old cemetery, ruined footings with horseshoes over their doors, a chimney, two coffins' iron bands showing and one grave burst open from below. The trapper: his iron-ringed cellar hatch, horseshoes and traps. FOB Threshold's fall: brass, a dropped helmet, drag marks toward the nearest cave. Press E by a survey board, the mine's boards, the open grave or the gate to read its note. Your notes go in notes.md.
