# Zombie sound prompts for ElevenLabs (CL-145)

112 prompts. Each one gives four takes; all four go in the folder named under it, inside `z-sounds/`.

**Settings for every prompt:** Sound Effects, Duration set to the seconds shown (or Auto), Prompt influence about 0.6, Loop off unless it says loop.
MP3 is fine. File names do not matter; drop the takes in as they download. Delete any take that has words, music, or sounds wrong; two good takes are enough, eight is fine too (run a prompt twice).

**Start with:** shambler, feral, the impacts, footsteps `shuffle` and `barefoot`, and the horde. That is most of what you hear on a normal night.
