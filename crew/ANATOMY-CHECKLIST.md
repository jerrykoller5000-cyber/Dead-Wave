# Zombie shape checklist (D-190, J158)

Jerry, 17 Nov 2026 ~00:20Z: "You are free to come up with whatever. These are not based on exact references I just think basic structural anatomy come into play with creatures. Like we are looking for thing that are obvious. A player could easily point out how rudimentary the shape of the model is right now. The textures are not bad it's really just the shape."

So: you have creative freedom (no reference to match). The bar is that a player can't point out an obvious structural flaw. Textures are fine: don't redo them; fix the shape.
Judge every body on a flat black silhouette (front, side, back, three-quarter) beside the marine at the same scale, then on the four-light sheet.

## The masses
- Two main masses, the ribcage and the pelvis, joined by a spine that bends; the waist is narrower than both.
- The torso has depth: from the side the ribcage is an egg (chest forward, back curved), not a board; the belly and buttocks show.
- The head sits on a neck that rises from between the shoulders and leans forward; the neck goes into the trapezius, not straight into a flat collar.

## Shoulders
- The shoulder girdle (collarbones, shoulder blades) rides ON the ribcage; the shoulder joint is at the top-outer corner of the ribcage.
- Neck -> trapezius slope -> deltoid capping the joint -> upper arm is one continuous line. No knob sticking up, no step, no gap.
- At rest the upper arm hangs against the side of the ribcage (the armpit is closed); the arm comes out from under the deltoid, not off the torso's corner.

## Hips and legs
- The pelvis is wider than the waist; the hip joints are inside the pelvis, below and in from the hip bones.
- Thighs start at the pelvis' sides and taper to the knee; the inner thighs come close at the crotch (no box with legs at its corners).
- Knees and ankles line up under the hips; the feet are under the body's weight, not splayed outside it (a deliberate wide stance is a pose, not the body).

## Lengths (human default; any change is a deliberate type choice)
- About 7 to 7.5 heads tall; crotch at about half the height.
- Shoulders about 2 head-widths; hips a bit narrower for a man, as wide or wider for a woman.
- Fingertips at mid-thigh; elbows at the waist; knees at about a quarter of the height.
- Upper arm and forearm about equal; thigh a bit longer than the shin.

## Creatures (brute, colossus, guardian, demon, spitter, spider, leaper)
- The same logic with their own numbers: every limb goes INTO a mass and is wrapped by muscle at the joint; nothing is glued onto a surface.
- The weight must be carried: the centre of mass over the feet (or the feet/hands that carry it); heavy parts get thick supports.
- Exaggerate on purpose (a brute's traps, a feral's long arms) and keep it consistent left/right and front/back.

## The sheet
- Silhouette sheet first: flat black on white, front/side/back/three-quarter, the marine beside, BEFORE vs AFTER. Then the four-light sheet.
- The Director reviews; Jerry OKs the proportions before a body lands.
