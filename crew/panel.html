      How to read this panel
      • The top line says it in one sentence: who is working, who is waiting for the test slot, and whether anything needs you.
      • Needs you is the only list you have to act on: questions, agents to start or resume (press Copy prompt, paste it into that agent's chat), notes to look at, two agents in one file. "When you have time" is the slow stuff.
      • The cards are each agent: what it's on, for how long, its latest note and what's next. Waiting for test slot means its changes are ready and it is in line for the game.
      • Game-test slot is who has the game right now and who is next. Your own game or inspector always goes first.
      • Done today is every finished task with what changed, what nobody could check, and whether it's committed.
      • The folded boxes at the bottom are for when you want the detail. Nothing here changes anything: it only reads the crew files.
