HOW TO USE THIS FILE
--------------------
- Play one pack, then write your notes in that pack's section below. Rough notes
  are perfect. Short lines are fine.
- One thing per line. Start each line with a letter so we can sort them fast:

     B  = broken. Something doesn't work, or happens when it shouldn't.
     M  = looks like a mistake or a placeholder: a wrong or floating model,
          something missing, words that look unfinished.
     F  = feel. Too hard, too easy, boring, confusing, too long, not fun.
     P  = performance. A stutter or slow spot. Say where you were and what
          was on screen.
     I  = idea for later. It goes on the 1.x list and isn't built for 1.0.

- If you can, say WHERE (the place, and which day or night) and WHAT YOU WERE
  DOING.
- For a bug, three parts are enough: what you did, what happened, and what you
  expected to happen.
- Screenshots: drop them in this same folder (Night Packs) and put the file name
  at the end of the line.
- When a pack is done, tell me "check the night pack notes". I'll turn every
  line into a task for whoever owns it and tell you who has what.

Examples:
  B  Night 2: a shambler walked through the HQ's west wall. shot1.png
  F  Day 1: I didn't know the radio mast was the first thing to do.
  P  Night 3, at the dock with about 40 zombies: big stutter for a second.
  I  Would love a flare gun.


THE QUESTIONS FOR EVERY PACK
----------------------------
Only answer the ones you have something to say about.

 1. Did anything break or stop working?
 2. Did anything look wrong, unfinished or out of place?
 3. Did you always know what to do next? Where were you lost?
 4. Difficulty: too easy, about right, or too hard? Which part?
 5. Did it feel smooth? Any stutters or slow spots?
 6. Words: anything that read oddly, was misspelled, or said too much?
 7. Sound and music: anything too loud, too quiet, missing, or repeating badly?
 8. Controls: anything awkward or slow to respond?
 9. The best moment, and the worst moment.
10. Anything you'd want later (an I line).

A note on the console (~): jumping to a night marks the run as a test run, so it
won't earn badges. That's expected.


PACK 1: A FRESH RUN, NIGHTS 1 TO 3  (about 30 minutes)
------------------------------------------------------
Start: a normal new run from the title. No console.

Look out for:
- The first-run card that offers the Training Ground. It should only appear the
  first time.
- The orders card at the drop. Is the goal clear (hold out until night 20, when
  Heron comes)?
- Day 1: is it clear what to do during the day? The radio mast: Sato's note,
  the breakers, the station's beeps, the vault and what came up in it.
- The first alarm and the first horde.
- Day 3: Brandt's rescue at the watchtower (red flares).
- The supply sites as they are now (search them with E).
